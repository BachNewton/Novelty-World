Heritage through time

A throwaway mockup for reacting to, not building from. It explores the two directions you liked, symbols through time on the cards and an origins map in the person panel, with several variants of each, then a few extra ideas and a set of 3D ideas. Every variant has a label, so you can answer "I like A6 and B3".

Hover (or tap) any flag, coat of arms or chip to see which era it names.

Sample data  The people and how they're related come from the real tree fixture. Its heritage and origin fields are empty, so every birth year, heritage entry, birthplace, emigration year, mother tongue, recorded people, journey and story sentence here is invented to exercise the design. Some people are invented, such as Salvatore Santoro (italian/sicily), to give good examples. The symbols are real art only: the repo's verified files. No sample person is born under a symbol without verified art.
Whole-symbol rule, applied throughout. A flag, coat of arms or regional emblem is always shown whole: its true aspect ratio, uncropped, in its own colours (no tint, fade or filter), and nothing overlaps it (no badge, other symbol, label, line or arc). A share is shown by a symbol's size or by something beside it. Real art only: no symbol is drawn by us; each is the repo's verified file, checked against a specification or the historical record (see Sources), and no symbol without verified art is ever shown: the sample data avoids them instead. Variants whose core idea broke the rule (A5, A6, A7, D3) are marked dropped, and rule-abiding replacements carry new labels (A8, A9, D7).

SSources

Every symbol on this page, where its art comes from, and what it was checked against. Verified (repo) is the repo's own file in src/projects/family-tree/symbol-art/, with its full record in symbol-art.ts. The Grand Duchy of Finland's lion arms are shown in the form Finns drew themselves (Bomansson), not the Russian Empire's 1882 drawing. Every symbol here is the repo's verified art, with its record in SYMBOL_ART; sample data never picks a symbol without it (Sicily's own symbol before 1861, for one, isn't shown). Symbols used by the data model but not by this mockup (Bavaria, Prussia, Norway, France and others) aren't listed; the repo also has verified art for Wales, and Lebanon's flag and cedar flag.

Mockup only, with nothing wired to the app. Libraries: d3, topojson-client, world-atlas and three.js from cdn.jsdelivr.net, so it needs an internet connection. All symbol art is inlined: the repo's verified files; see Sources. The eras follow symbol-timelines.ts.
