# The people

Who everyone is and what has happened to them. Birthdays and every other date are in `timeline.ts`, and any age is computed from it with the date tool, never from this page.

## The Dorseys and the Novaks (Dayton and Helsinki)

- **Anna Dorsey,** from Dayton, Ohio, the only child of Walt and Ruth. She ran the front desk of a dentist's office for nine years, until it closed in March 2020 and reopened without her; that month she, Ben and Nora moved into her parents' house "for two weeks" so that Walt and Ruth never had to go into a store, and stayed until they left. She booked her parents' vaccine, filled in the forms to leave America in February 2021, asked her parents to come too, and was told no. She lives in Helsinki. Her mother named her, in a declaration made while well, as the one to decide her care, and Anna signed Ruth into the Montgomery County Sanctuary District as the only way anyone could ever get her out. Her Finnish permit for Ruth came through in November 2029, but the embassy appointment never happened.
- **Ben Novak,** Anna's husband, from Toledo, Ohio, a software developer. His parents, Dennis, who built Jeeps at the Toledo plant for thirty years, and Joanne, died of COVID in Toledo in December 2020, eleven days apart, before the vaccine; he said goodbye to his father on a nurse's iPad and watched their funeral on a laptop at the Dorseys' kitchen table, having stayed away to keep Walt and Ruth safe. He works for a company in San Francisco, from home; it has a dozen engineers in Helsinki, from a firm it bought (Mikko among them), and in autumn 2020 let its people work from any country where it had an office to pay them from, which is what made Helsinki possible. He asked to wait a year, and then, before he had been to see his parents' grave, put his passport on the table (he first stood at it in April 2024, on the family's first trip home), and was at its offices the week of the Bell Riots. He flew to Dayton when Walt fell, and stood by while Ruth nearly signed herself away.
- **Nora,** their daughter, two when they moved, so Finnish is her easy language and English the one she keeps for her grandparents. The Europa Mission launched on her fifth birthday, and she began her drawing of Europa that night in her mother's old orange crayon. **Theo,** their son, born in Helsinki. **Alma,** born in January 2027, whose name works in Finnish and in English.
- **Walt Dorsey,** Anna's father. He had polio at five, in 1952, in a ward where his mother could only stand at the window, and it left his left leg in a steel brace; in 1955 he lined up for the Salk vaccine anyway, against the kinds he hadn't had, and in February 2021, at seventy-three, for COVID's. He worked thirty-four years at Delco, a car-parts plant. He has followed spaceflight since John Glenn's orbit, when he was fourteen, and followed the Europa Mission in the paper. He declined Helsinki on the porch ("Who'd do the tomatoes?") and set the Sunday call at eleven, after church. Post-polio weakness took his good leg. He fell in March 2027 and lay seventeen hours before a neighbour's boy found him, never walked again, and died in a nursing home in Kettering in February 2030, when the power failed.
- **Ruth Dorsey,** Anna's mother, a piano teacher for forty years and an organist at her Lutheran church. Through the pandemic she taught over an iPad, playing a phrase and waiting for it to come back down the line, because the lag makes playing together impossible. Her left arm is Dolores's. She has schizoaffective disorder, which began in her twenties. In 1996 she spent eleven weeks in hospital, and a county worker checked on Anna's care every Thursday for a year. For nineteen years a monthly injection from her nurse, Dolores, kept her well, until the clinic closed in September 2026 and she relapsed. Her illness is written true: psychosis as it is (frightened, suspicious, the injection as poison, people as impostors), and Ruth, when well, as a whole person, proud, funny and difficult, never given wisdom or insight by her illness. On the shot again inside the District, she taught eleven children piano on a keyboard drawn on cereal boxes. Her last injection was in May 2030; Naomi Garber took her north that October.
- **Gene Kessler,** the Dorseys' neighbour across the street, Walt's age, who worked thirty years at the same plant and clears the Dorseys' walk after every snow. He remembers the Cutter batches of 1955, when his mother took him out of the vaccine line, and won't have the COVID shot; his wife, **Barb,** won't go first. He watches the sun through a shade-fourteen welding glass from the plant. In July 2024 he and Barb move to their daughter's in South Carolina, because Barb wants to watch the grandchildren grow up somewhere besides a phone: the first time she has gone first.

## Dayton's county and the District

- **Carol Flory,** a county clerk for twenty-three years and a trans woman, who took her name at thirty-five. When Ohio stopped letting her license say F, she lost her login and was moved to the District's visitors' window, where she kept its only record, a paper ledger. In the war, the Safety Board had her add a letter, H, to hold people who wouldn't pledge, and that is her guilt. She cut the ledger down to its "known as" column, to keep the residents from being sorted, and ended at a folding table on the levee, finding people by what they were called.
- **Joel Flory,** Carol's son, a sergeant in the Ohio Guard. He fired second at Gate 12A, helped his mother cut the ledger, and was shot by his own side in August 2030 after Gary Pruitt reported him.
- **Gary Pruitt,** the county caseworker, who makes every call he says he'll make, and who was ruined by his phone in the war.
- **Dolores,** Ruth's nurse at the county's mental health clinic on Wayne Avenue. She came from the Philippines at twenty-five, when American hospitals recruited nurses there, and kept giving Ruth her injection through the pandemic's lockdowns, at the house, masked, staying when her daughter left because "somebody has to stay and do the arms". She kept her license current after the clinic closed, restarted Ruth's injection inside the District, and went out of the gate in June 2030 to look for more of it. She hasn't been seen since.
- **Lucille Odom,** a former elementary school cook, who lost her food benefits to a work rule and lives in the District. She tied Ruth's ration card round her neck with her own shoelace, and later ran the District's kitchen.
- **Caleb Henderson,** the neighbours' boy, who carried the Dorseys' trash bins up and down the drive for nothing, found Walt, and later carried water in the District.
- **Naomi Garber,** Carol's sister, Old German Baptist, who won't say Carol's name, and who named herself responsible party for Ruth to take her north.
- **Miss Bea Yancey,** librarian and choir director, who hid both sides' banned books.

## Further away

- **Joy,** Dolores's daughter, two years younger than Anna, a nurse who worked a COVID floor in Dayton through 2020, holding up iPads for goodbyes. She left for a job nursing children in Toronto in February 2021, because she had stopped being able to feel for her patients and wanted to again. In Toronto she cared for a small child in a genetics lab, who grew up to be Khan.
- **Ben's colleague from Belfast,** a software developer at the same company, beside Ben in San Francisco while the Bell Riots ran on every screen. He grew up with what the Troubles did, and votes in Ireland's border poll in 2028. Not yet named.
