# The people

Who everyone is and what has happened to them. Birthdays and every other date are in `timeline.ts`, and any age is computed from it with the date tool, never from this page.

## The Dorseys and the Novaks (chapters 1 to 3, Dayton and Helsinki)

- **Anna Dorsey,** from Dayton, Ohio, the only child of Walt and Ruth. She filled in the forms to leave America in 2021, and lives in Helsinki. Her mother named her, in a declaration made while well, as the one to decide her care, and Anna signed Ruth into the Montgomery County Sanctuary District as the only way anyone could ever get her out. Her Finnish permit for Ruth came through in November 2029, but the embassy appointment never happened.
- **Ben Novak,** Anna's husband, from Toledo, Ohio, a software developer whose parents are dead. He flew to Dayton when Walt fell, and stood by while Ruth nearly signed herself away.
- **Nora,** their daughter, two when they moved, so Finnish is her easy language. **Theo,** their son, born in Helsinki. **Alma,** born in January 2027, whose name works in Finnish and in English.
- **Walt Dorsey,** Anna's father. He had polio at five, in 1952, and worked thirty-four years at Delco, a car-parts plant, on a steel brace. Post-polio weakness took his good leg. He fell in March 2027 and lay seventeen hours before a neighbour's boy found him, never walked again, and died in a nursing home in Kettering in February 2030, when the power failed.
- **Ruth Dorsey,** Anna's mother, a piano teacher for forty years and an organist at her Lutheran church. She has schizoaffective disorder, which began in her twenties. In 1996 she spent eleven weeks in hospital, and a county worker checked on Anna's care every Thursday for a year. For nineteen years a monthly injection from her nurse, Dolores, kept her well, until the clinic closed in September 2026 and she relapsed. Her illness is written true: psychosis as it is (frightened, suspicious, the injection as poison, people as impostors), and Ruth, when well, as a whole person, proud, funny and difficult, never given wisdom or insight by her illness. On the shot again inside the District, she taught eleven children piano on a keyboard drawn on cereal boxes. Her last injection was in May 2030; Naomi Garber took her north that October.

## Dayton's county and the District (chapters 2 and 3)

- **Carol Flory,** a county clerk for twenty-three years and a trans woman, who took her name at thirty-five. When Ohio stopped letting her license say F, she lost her login and was moved to the District's visitors' window, where she kept its only record, a paper ledger. In the war, the Safety Board had her add a letter, H, to hold people who wouldn't pledge, and that is her guilt. She cut the ledger down to its "known as" column, to keep the residents from being sorted, and ended at a folding table on the levee, finding people by what they were called.
- **Joel Flory,** Carol's son, a sergeant in the Ohio Guard. He fired second at Gate 12A, helped his mother cut the ledger, and was shot by his own side in August 2030 after Gary Pruitt reported him.
- **Gary Pruitt,** the county caseworker, who makes every call he says he'll make, and who was ruined by his phone in the war.
- **Dolores,** Ruth's nurse, who kept her license current after the clinic closed, restarted Ruth's injection inside the District, and went out of the gate in June 2030 to look for more of it. She hasn't been seen since.
- **Lucille Odom,** a former elementary school cook, who lost her food benefits to a work rule and lives in the District. She tied Ruth's ration card round her neck with her own shoelace, and later ran the District's kitchen.
- **Caleb Henderson,** the neighbours' boy, who carried the Dorseys' trash bins up and down the drive for nothing, found Walt, and later carried water in the District.
- **Naomi Garber,** Carol's sister, Old German Baptist, who won't say Carol's name, and who named herself responsible party for Ruth to take her north.
- **Miss Bea Yancey,** librarian and choir director, who hid both sides' banned books.
