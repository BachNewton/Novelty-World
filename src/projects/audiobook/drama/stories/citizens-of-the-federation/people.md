# The people

Who everyone is and what happens to them, as the outline has it. Birthdays and every other date are in `timeline.ts`, and any age is computed from it with the date tool, never from this page. Details a chapter settles when it is drafted are added here then.

## The Dorseys and the Novaks (San Francisco, Dayton and Helsinki)

- **Anna Novak,** born Dorsey in Dayton, Ohio, the only child of Walt and Ruth. Detail-minded: she has never met a form she couldn't get through. In 2020 she runs a dentist's front desk in San Francisco. She goes to her parents' house in March 2020 "for two weeks" to keep them out of stores, and stays until the family leaves for Helsinki in August 2022, having asked her parents to come, knowing it was now or never, and been told no. In Helsinki she stays home with Theo rather than spend years learning Finnish, and so can't become a citizen or bring her mother. At the eclipse she promises Walt never to let them put Ruth "in one of those places", and breaks the promise in April 2027 by using Ruth's declaration to get her the shot inside Dayton's Sanctuary District. In February 2030 she sends Dolores to sit with her dying father rather than take Ruth to Washington for her permit. She tells her children the truth.
- **Ben Novak,** Anna's husband, a software developer at Brynner (canon's Brynner Information Systems), on the team that builds its video player. Raised in Toledo by his grandmother, dutiful rather than warm, after his parents were killed in a car crash when he was three, a year after his older sister, Katie, died at nine of an inherited illness his grandmother never spoke of; she died while he was at college. He has never had anyone near who needed him, and can respect Anna's pull toward her parents without feeling it. When Brynner ends remote work outside its office cities, he moves to his team in Helsinki. At the Bell Riots he holds back a hostage's clip by the rule and never learns whether it mattered. When AI cuts his team, he and Declan are the two kept, and the family's permits ride on his job. He learns why his sister died when Alma is diagnosed. In Helsinki he leans on Antti Laine, a quiet Finnish dad he met at the daycare gate.
- **Nora,** born April 2019 in San Francisco, a baby when the family goes to Dayton and three when they move to Helsinki. The eclipse and the Europa launch on her fifth birthday light her love of space; she draws a space picture for Walt every birthday after, which he keeps on his fridge. At ten she tells her dying grandfather the crew are coming. She becomes a scientist, and is near Cochrane's work, not on his ship, at First Contact.
- **Theo,** born February 2022 in Dayton, six months old on the leaving day. The sea is his from his first ferry in Helsinki; he becomes a marine scientist, practical where Nora looks to the stars, and in the 2050s decides whether to use the organism from Io to heal the Baltic.
- **Alma,** born January 2027 in Helsinki. Diagnosed at six with the illness that killed Ben's sister; the era's cure can only cure by enhancing, so saving her makes her an Augment. In the Eugenics Wars she is an Augment in a war over Augments.
- **Walt Dorsey,** Anna's father. Polio at five, in 1952, in a ward where his mother could only stand at the window; one good leg and a steel brace on the other. Missed polio's vaccine by three years and is "on time" for COVID's. Thirty-four years at a car-parts plant; follows spaceflight. Says "everything's fine": a flaw out of love and out of pride, since needing help would mean he couldn't manage. Keeps going to the store himself in March 2020. Says no to Helsinki to spare Annie ("Who'd do the tomatoes?"), and sets the Sunday call at eleven, after church. Falls in April 2027 and lies seventeen hours before the neighbour's boy finds him. Dies in February 2030 in a cold nursing home in Kettering, on Dolores's phone at the hour of the Sunday call; his last words, "Tell Annie everything's fine."
- **Ruth Dorsey,** Anna's mother, a piano teacher and the organist at her Lutheran church. Schizoaffective disorder, begun in her twenties; an autumn in hospital in 2000, when Anna was eleven, and a county woman checking on Anna's care every Thursday for a year after. A monthly injection from Dolores keeps her well, and dulls her, with a slight tremor in her left hand she hides at the piano; her left arm is Dolores's. In 2020 she chooses to go without the shot to get her music back, sees the signs herself, asks for Dolores, and then writes a declaration at the bank naming Anna to decide when she can't ("When I am ill... Do not listen to me. Listen to this."). Plays the *Finlandia* hymn to Anna on Sundays, and never explains it. Relapses when her clinic closes in September 2026; inside Dayton's District from April 2027, on the shot again, teaching children on a locked, out-of-tune piano it takes a signed request to use. Off the shot after Dolores goes out of the gate in June 2030; taken north by Naomi in October; around 2032 she chooses whether to leave for Finland. Her illness is written true: psychosis as it is, and Ruth well as a whole person, proud, funny and difficult, never given wisdom by her illness.
- **Gene Kessler** and **Barb,** across the street. Gene worked the same plant as Walt, clears the Dorseys' walk after every snow, remembers the Cutter batches of 1955 and won't have the COVID shot; Barb won't go first. In 2024 they move to their daughter's, because Barb wants to watch the grandchildren grow up somewhere besides a phone: the first time she has gone first.
- **Caleb Henderson,** the neighbours' boy, who carries the Dorseys' bins down and back for nothing, and finds Walt.

## Dolores, Joy and Carol

- **Dolores,** from the Philippines at twenty-five, a nurse at the county mental health clinic on Wayne Avenue; Ruth's nurse for years, and a mother to Carol, whom she met at the clinic when Carol came for the counsellor's letter she needed to begin her transition, and didn't turn away. Falls ill in 2020. Keeps her license after the clinic closes; restarts Ruth's shot inside the District; in February 2030 sits with Walt while he dies, at Anna's asking, holding up her phone and changing arms; in June 2030 goes out of the District's gate to find more of Ruth's shot and isn't seen again.
- **Joy,** Dolores's daughter, born in Dayton the year her mother arrived; a nurse, and Carol's near-sister. Works a COVID floor in 2020, holding up iPads for goodbyes, and leaves for a children's hospital in Toronto in February 2021 because she has stopped being able to feel for her patients and wants to again. Later works at the Noonien-Singh Institute (canon's Toronto lab, where Khan was a small child in 2022), where Carol joins her.
- **Carol Flory,** a county clerk, a trans woman who took her name in 2013, when Ohio law printed her old name beside it in the newspaper. Raised Old German Baptist north of Dayton, cut off by her family except for what her sister Naomi carries from their mother; sings tenor with the Methodists on Salem Avenue. Dry, quick, courteous, a pen always on her. Believes a person's word about who she is counts. Takes Ruth's own signature in April 2027, knowing it isn't informed consent; tells Anna to use the declaration that exposes it; fired. Her two names go up beside her son's face in January 2029; she leaves for Joy in Toronto, giving Joel the District's ledger. Finds "Mom" under the last line of the narrow book Naomi posts her, and finds Ruth for Anna. Comes back around 2032 to testify, and must decide whether to accept an amnesty that frees the men who shot Joel. At the Institute she keeps the consent forms for children who can't consent.
- **Joel Flory,** Carol's only child, a sergeant in the Ohio Army National Guard; calls her Carol, because the other words didn't fit. On election night 2028 his unit is at the county elections board. At Gate 12A, bound by an oath to two masters who give opposite orders, he goes to keep his boys from firing, fires, and kills an airman; he says "I fired", never "I returned fire". Guards the District with his mother's ledger, writes "None available" on a colonel's list to save the residents, and is shot by his own side in August 2030.

## Dayton's county and the District

- **Gary Pruitt,** the county caseworker, who makes every call he says he'll make, writes down everything, and in the war reports Carol's son.
- **Lucille Odom,** a former school cook in the District, who ties Ruth's ration card round her neck with her own shoelace and runs its kitchen.
- **Naomi Garber,** Carol's sister, Old German Baptist (no television, nonresistant), who won't say either of Carol's names and carries their mother's "Mother asks if you're eating". In October 2030 she takes the old people with nobody north on her grain truck, Ruth among them, and posts the narrow book to Toronto without a letter. Planted before chapter 12.
- **Miss Bea Yancey,** children's librarian and choir director at Salem Avenue, who hides banned books and hums the note for hymn 534. **Ti Jak,** from Springfield's Haitian community, who goes east on the colonel's buses. **Dale Hutto,** Carol's neighbour, an engineer on the base. **Private Danny Ruhl,** in Joel's squad. **Airman Eli Kasprzak,** twenty, of Green Bay, killed at Gate 12A.
- **The Governor of Ohio** (the doctor of the 2020 briefings, real, never named), who sides with Philadelphia. Every act done under her authority in the war is fair, specific and plainly the book's own.

## The Toners (San Francisco and Belfast)

- **Declan Toner,** Ben's closest friend, seconded from Brynner's Belfast office to San Francisco until March 2020. The funniest person in any meeting; grew up on a street with a peace wall at the bottom of it. Kept with Ben when AI cuts their team. Beside Ben at the Bell Riots ("Rule's the rule"; "maybe it just works"). Comes to Dayton for the eclipse with Niamh.
- **Orla Toner,** Declan's wife, a nurse, who goes home in March 2020 to nurse in Belfast's hospitals. A lifelong nationalist whose patients depend on the North's free health service; in the border poll of November 2028 she votes no, and tells no one.
- **Niamh Toner,** their daughter, ten in San Francisco and adoring baby Nora; the border poll is her first vote.
- **Brendan Toner,** Declan's da, a retired bus driver, nationalist, widower, in the house with the wall at the bottom of the street. **Norman Kerr,** his friend from across the wall, unionist, whose brother Sammy the IRA shot on his milk round in 1976. Both carry real losses and real doubts.

## Further away

- **Renée Picard** (canon), a French astronaut, mission specialist on the Europa Mission's Shango, launched on Nora's fifth birthday and home on 13 February 2030 with something alive from Io that she believes may be someone. Canon: it later heals the oceans and cleans the air.
- **Khan** (canon), a small child at the Noonien-Singh Institute in Toronto in 2022, a young man there around 2033.
- **Chris Brynner** (canon), who founded Brynner and switched the District's network back on at the Bell Riots. **Dana Reyes,** who runs its trust and safety team. The District's people the public came to know (canon): Gabriel Bell, Michael Webb, Henry Garcia, Lee.
