# Chapter 2 purpose audit

A scene-by-scene check of "Responsible Party" against "no filler": what each scene is for, which beats carry that, and which don't. **Applied in `chapters/02/novel.txt`; done under the owner's handed-over judgement,** with the chapter rewritten as a narrated novel. Line ids are the old `chapters/02/script.yaml`.

The chapter's question, from the outline: *where does Ruth end up, and who gets to decide?* Its idea: *a form decides what a person is.* Form S-1 decides Ruth's name, her letter, her faith and her keeper; the state decides Carol's letter from her birth certificate; and two people get there first by writing themselves down: Ruth in her declaration, made in a good year, and Carol in the papers in her glove box. The chapter keeps its shape, the sections of the county form, read out of order because a clerk fills them in any order she likes (the opening now says so, so the jump from section three to six doesn't sound like a mistake).

## Breaking decided rules (fixed whatever else is agreed)

- **Interruptions by dash:** l013, l015, l037, l074, l091, l093, l110, l128. All told in prose: Ben starts to spell Dorsey and is thanked; Walt gets as far as "everything's"; Ben gets no further than "tonight".
- **Sitcom bits and performed quips:** "Hi, Purse" and "Purse, walk with me" (l012, l070) make a running gag of Ben at his expense. "I feel like a guy who got out of prison" (l052). "God knows, and He's got bigger problems" (l061). "He took the good car" (l038). "That's where I keep all the good ones" (l126). "I've got my doubts, but it's a hell of a tenor line" (l048). All cut. Carol's warmth now comes from what she does (refusing the signature, the pen, carrying Anna's words across the room, ticking "clergy").
- **Fragments as speech:** "It's what?" (l049) becomes "It's from Finland?"; Anna's "Yeah." (l055) becomes a silence the narrator describes.
- **The narrator's direction was "quiet sorrow" for every heading.** The stance is warm, curious attention; grave only for the fall, the District and the declaration.

## What the audit changes, in sum

- Cut: every interruption dash; "Purse"; the prison joke; "He took the good car"; "God knows"; "where I keep all the good ones"; the tenor-line quip; Pennsylvania; the Wright brothers; the can opener, the pallets and the thickened coffee.
- Rework: the Kroger call reported; "It's what?" and "Yeah." as prose; "dim" as seven syllables; "On Monday he would have told her his name".
- Sharpen the absurdity, told straight: the attorney's opinion followed to its end; the check mark that makes Ruth Jewish; the interviews nobody told the list about; Lucille's work rule; the log with no box for a nurse.
- Add: the time-jump line; the "known as" confide; Ruth's letter chosen before she arrived; Ruth writing past the form; Carol telling Anna she'll be the only one who can get Ruth out.
- Carrying lines, five: Ben hoping; "You write down who you are"; "I would like it in writing"; the file no one opens; one shoe loose.
