# Rewriting chapters 1 to 3

A working document for the rewrite the producer asked for after reading chapters 1 and 2. It holds what the rewrite needs that the story's standing files don't: the causes behind the producer's notes, the line notes, and the inputs. It is deleted, with the radio-play scripts, once the three chapters are rewritten and the producer has approved them.

## Order

1. The narrator's reference passage, approved by the producer: done, in `voice.md`. It is the Father's Day dinner, so chapter 1's dinner scene starts from it.
2. Chapters 1, 2 and 3, each through the `write-fiction` skill, with its cold read.
3. The threads pass across all three, against `outline.md`.
4. Delete `chapters/<nn>/script.yaml` and this file.

## Inputs

- `chapters/<nn>/chapter.md`, the current draft: what happens, and nothing more. The rewrite is a fresh draft, never an edit of it.
- `chapters/<nn>/script.yaml`, the radio play each chapter began as. Its `direction` on each line says what that line was meant to feel. The conversion to a novel dropped those feelings, so this is where to recover them.
- The line notes below, and the causes behind them.

## The causes

Six causes explain nearly every note. Fixing lines without fixing these brings the same notes back.

1. **Corrections were written into the prose,** a rule obeyed on the page: the time-jump rule became chapter 2's opening, the outline's thesis became the baby box paragraph, the example of a character's way of talking became that character's words, and edits left seams ("he can read a card, slowly" twice in one scene).
2. **Every rule landed on the narrator,** and several pulled against each other. The only way to obey them all was to state a fact and withhold its meaning, so the narration went clipped and oblique while the dialogue stayed good. `voice.md` replaces those rules.
3. **The emotion lived in the radio play's directions** and was dropped in the conversion.
4. **The writing was fitted to the voices:** paragraph caps, no foreign words, dialogue reshaped for the TTS.
5. **Nobody read it without the context.** The cold read now does.
6. **Facts and arithmetic were checked by nothing but the writer.** The date tool and online checks now do.
