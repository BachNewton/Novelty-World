# Chapter 3 purpose audit

A scene-by-scene check of "Known As" against "no filler": what each scene is for, which beats carry that, and which don't. **Applied in `chapters/03/novel.txt`; done under the owner's handed-over judgement,** with the chapter rewritten as a narrated novel. Line ids are the old `chapters/03/script.yaml`.

The chapter's question, from the outline: *how do you keep caring for people when the thing you work for falls apart?* Its idea: *a name against a category.* What people are called by those who love them can't be sorted; the letters a ledger keeps can. Every scene is a page of Carol's roll call, and each page sets a name against a letter: Mrs. Dorsey against D, Joel against the Hero and the Butcher, Naomi refusing either of her sister's names, Miss Bea and the Henderson boy against H. The cut and "Mom" answer it.

## Breaking decided rules (fixed whatever else is agreed)

- **The radio-play form:** every line is now the narrator's, with dialogue tagged. Each speaker is given a way of speaking on the page: Carol's clerk's precision and form language ("Here's how this goes", "That field's optional"); Joel's short, plain soldier's sentences, and "Carol", never a parent's word; Lucille's "baby" and orders; Naomi's plain, formal church speech ("That's the fare. We don't resist it."); Miss Bea's crisp relish and "dear"; Gary's kindness and "I always do"; the Henderson boy's "ma'am"; Ruth's formal "please".
- **Interruptions:** l034 ("Isn't this —") and Carol's rush at l107 are told in prose.
- **Stating what's coming:** "There will be books that give this date" (l030) and "There is no telling yet how any of this ends" (l140) are cut.
- **Catchphrases:** "You've got your sorry face on" (l108), a callback bit, is cut. "Same glove box" (l085), which needs chapter 2's line to decode, becomes "Same letter as mine", which also serves the idea.
- **A dry joke:** "Lucille Odom… calls them both the weather" (l002) is wry. Cut.
- **Contradiction:** "someone knocks on the glass" (l004) at a window the chapter says has no glass. Now the counter.
