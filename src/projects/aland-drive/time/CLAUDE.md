# Åland Drive: the day

The game's calendar and clock. How the light follows it is in `render/CLAUDE.md` (Light through the day). Paths are relative to the project's folder, `src/projects/aland-drive/`; the project's own `CLAUDE.md` there is the overview and says where everything else is written down.

The game keeps a calendar of its own (`time/calendar.ts`): Åland's year in 36 game days, three to a month, each game day `GAME_DAY_S` real seconds (`time/day.ts`: 25 minutes, the owner's choice), so a year passes in 15 hours of play. The world's time is one number, game days since the first of 2000's, running on from wherever it started on the world's clock (`sea/clock.ts`). Each game day stands for about ten days of its month: the real date it shows runs on through it, a month's days spread evenly over its three, and the sun and moon stand where they really would over Åland at the game's hour on that date (`time/sky-places.ts`: NOAA's solar calculator and the Astronomical Almanac's moon, turned to the grid, whose north is 6.25° west of true north), held by tests to Mariehamn's published sunrises and sunsets and to 2026's eclipses. Read at the date the game day started on, blended to the next, so no turn of a day, month or year moves the sky by more than a hundredth of a degree, which a test holds. The boats keep real time.

The world's time is saved in this browser (`getProjectStorage`) each game hour, and a page picks up where it left off; with no save it starts at the real date and time. Online, the host's time is the world's: each guest asks for it once it's in and takes it, behind a short fade so the sky doesn't jump in sight, and a guest who claims the world when the host goes already has it (see `net/CLAUDE.md`). A tool's or a test's page neither reads nor writes the save. `?date=2026-06-21` holds that real date and `?hour=22.5` starts the day at that hour, which runs on at the game's pace; a page moved by a link isn't saved and keeps its own time online, though as a host it hands it on. The dev menu's Day picks a month and its day (1 to 3), the hour, holds the time, or goes back to the real date and time now. The HUD over the mini map shows the month's name, the time, and the air's temperature where there is weather: which of a month's three days it is is the dev menu's, not the player's. The weather and the seasons hang off the same `GameTime` (`docs/TIME-AND-WEATHER.md`).
