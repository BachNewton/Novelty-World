# Åland Drive: dealerships

Walking round a dealership in first person, among its cars, as `ideas/cars.md` ("Dealers", "Inside a dealership") decides it. Paths are relative to the project's folder, `src/projects/aland-drive/`; the project's own `CLAUDE.md` there is the overview.

## The interior definition

**A dealership is data, never code in the system.** Each place that sells cars hands the system one `DealerInterior` (`dealership/interior.ts`), and the system reads nothing else about it: the yacht, the delivery hall, the premium showroom, the town dealer, the village garage and the used lot are all the same few fields. The art for one (`art/dealers/`, or a kit of its own) builds its room and returns this; a line in the registry (`dealership/registry.ts`) names it by its site. The definition is kept small and changes rarely: a field is added only when a room needs something the others can't do without, and never to describe one room's look.

**The room's frame.** Everything is in the room's own metres: +y up, the floor walked on at y = 0, and the axes of the dealership's exterior model, its street side +z. `origin` puts the room's origin in the exterior's frame (zero for a building, the car deck's height for a yacht), so the site's place and yaw (`DEALER_SITES`, `art/dealers/index.ts`) put the room where its building stands, which is what makes the view through its windows the right one. A yaw turns about +y, anticlockwise seen from above, and yaw 0 faces -z, as a car's nose does at yaw 0.

What it holds:

- **`name`**, shown at its door and on the maps. A dealership's name and `stock` are written once, in its listing (`art/dealers/listings.ts`), which its room spreads into its definition and the maps read without loading the room.
- **`room`**: the room's still art, one soup of the dealer kit's `Shell` (`art/dealers/shell.ts`), drawn in one draw with the room's light. A part with glow 1 shines by itself (a lamp's face, a sign). The room paints nothing outside itself: no backdrop of sky or sea past its windows or doors, since every opening shows the real island (see the windows).
- **`turntables`**: parts that turn, each a soup centred on its own axis, its `centre` on the floor and a whole turn in `turnSeconds`. A spot on one rides it.
- **`stock`**: which cars it sells, by the car list's own fields (`sim/cars.ts`'s entries: `makers`, `tiers`, `from`), every car matching all the fields given. `ideas/cars.md`'s dealer table, as rules: the yacht `from: harbour, tiers: A1, A2`; the delivery hall `from: harbour, tiers: A3, A4, B2, B3`; the premium showroom `makers: Tärna, Aurelle, from: dealer`; the town dealer `makers: Västan, Ödmark, Norrsken, from: dealer`; the village garage `makers: Klippa`. A car added to the list in a dealer's makers and tiers stands there with no change to the room, as long as it has a spot.
- **`spots`**: where cars stand, in the order the stock fills them (the car list's order, fastest tier first), so the finest place comes first. Each is the floor under the car's middle (its y the floor's height there: a plinth's top, a lift's deck, a turntable's) and the way its nose points. A room has at least as many spots as its stock has cars; the test holds every car of its stock clear of the walls, the solids and the other cars on its spot.
- **`floor`** and **`solids`**: what the player walks. The floor is the outline they stay inside; a solid is a box (its centre, half its size and its yaw) or a round post on that plan, anything that stands on the floor: a desk, a sofa, a pillar, a stand. A car on a spot is solid by itself, from its own footprint, so the art doesn't list cars. There is one floor level: steps and a raised deck are solids, or not walked.
- **`windows`**: flat panes, four corners in order round each. The island is drawn behind the whole room as it looks from the dealership's real place, at that moment's hour, season and weather, captured once on entering; a pane draws that view with a touch of glass over it. An opening with no pane (an open workshop door, the used lot's open sky) shows the island too, plainly. A window's frame and mullions are the room's art.
- **`viewFrom`**: where the island is captured from, the middle of the windows at the eye's height. The view is right from there and a little off as the player walks away from it, which at a window's distance is hard to see.
- **`light`**: the room's own light, never the sun. `ambient` (0 to 1, in `ambientPaint`) is what every surface gets whatever it faces: a delivery hall's bright even light is high, a yacht's evening low. Up to 16 `lamps` (`MOST_LAMPS`), each a `point` that shines all round, a `spot` down a cone about its aim, a direction from where it hangs (`[0, -1, 0]` straight down, never the point it shines at) and `coneDeg` the cone's whole width (a yacht's soft spots, a light over each car), or a `strip` from every point between its two ends (a workshop's tubes, a hall's rows of panels), each in a colour token, as bright as `power` at itself and half that at `reachM`. `mirrors` is what a glossy surface mirrors at a glancing angle: the floor's look, then the ceiling's. The cars are lit by the same light.
- **`door`**: where the player stands on entering, facing into the room, and how near it (`reachM`) they must come to leave.

Outside, the system reads the dealership's site (`DEALER_SITES`, `art/dealers/sites.ts`: its place in ETRS-TM35FIN metres, its yaw and its road) and its exterior's `entrance` (`art/dealers/exteriors.ts`: a point on the ground and a radius), which is where its door's marker stands on the island and its mark on the maps (`dealership/doors.ts`, `map/CLAUDE.md`). Its floor is at the height the placement stands its model (`render/dealers.ts`: the highest ground under its plot, or the water's surface for a site `afloat`, as the yacht's is), so the room and its exterior are one building.

**The rules a room keeps**, which `dealership/interiors.test.ts` holds for every dealership in the registry: the door, the view and every spot inside the floor; every car of its stock fits its spot, clear of the floor's edge, the solids and the cars beside it, and a turntable's car turns clear of what's round it; the door clear of everything; every car can be walked to from the door, near enough to reach, by a body that can't pass a turntable's whole sweep; at most 16 lamps; each window flat; a room within the dealer kit's triangle budget.

## The system

`dealership/walk-in.ts` is the walk-in, which the game holds (`render/game.ts` touches it in a handful of lines: it makes it, routes the actions through it first, hands it the frame while it holds the drive, aims and draws the capture, and adds its doors to the rings); everything else is in this folder.

- **The door.** Each dealership's door on the island is a ring of posts and a beam, a challenge's start ring (`render/challenge-marks.ts`) in the dealership's own colour (`dealer-mark`), round its exterior's `entrance` at its site, as big as a challenge's. In it, slower than walking pace, free of a challenge, the map, a menu and the free camera, the HUD offers the dealership (F or the d-pad's up, the challenge's button), and the press goes in.
- **Going in.** The drive stops as a paused menu stops it, and the page covers the view at once with the dealership's name and a spinner; the room is built only once the cover has faded in (`onDealershipCover`), so the press never reads as a freeze. The game's own frame draws the island six times from the dealership's real place (`view.ts`), one square image along each of the world's axes, so every system that culls by the camera culls for that face: two frames to settle, then six, under a cover. The faces are plain images, read by a shader that finds each pixel's face and place from the very camera it was drawn with. The room's scene is built meanwhile, and its cars' pipelines compiled asynchronously, each car's own (a car's material bakes its own numbers), so the room opens whole.
- **Every pipeline the room needs is built before the drive**: the room's material, the sky dome and the panes. The capture needs none: a pipeline is built for the kind of target it draws into, so each face is drawn into a target of the kind three draws the screen's scene into (half-float colour in the working colour space, its depth and its samples) and copied out to its image, and the world draws with the pipelines it already has. Any other kind of target compiled the whole world again at the start (`tools/shader-count.mjs`: 162 render pipelines against 90). three keeps one render context for every target of a kind, so that target shares the screen's, and is freed only on leaving. The room's light is one shader for every dealership (`room-light.ts`): its lamps are uniforms written on entering and summed over as many lamps as the room has, through the dealer kit's material (`lamps` lighting, `art/dealers/material.ts`). A car is lit by a sun straight down, as strong as the room's lamps, then by the ambient and the lamps reaching where it stands.
- **Inside**, the game's frame hands over to the walk-in once the stepping and the online world's sending are done, and only the room, its cars, the panes and a sky dome showing the faces are drawn: tens of draws and at most what a chase view costs in triangles, the cars' being most of it. The faces hold still while the player is inside. Looking down, a pixel of the view finds where its ray meets the ground and looks that way from the capture's point, so the forecourt doesn't slide as the player walks; far off and upwards the direction alone is right. The dealership's own model is left out of the world while the island is captured from inside it (`leaveOut` in `render/dealers.ts`), so its walls don't fill its windows; the map's buildings under its plot are gone once its tile is rebuilt.
- **Walking** (`plan.ts`): an eye 1.62 m up with no body, a circle of 0.3 m kept inside the floor and out of every solid and car by pushing it out of each, exactly, with no search. A car is solid where it stands, a turntable's as it turns, so the player walks up between its corners and is pushed aside by one sweeping round. WASD or the left stick walk at 1.7 m/s, Shift or a click of the left stick held jogs at 3 m/s, a mouse drag, the arrows or the right stick look; the controls are `input/controls.ts`'s (`walk()` and the `walk*` and `rev` bindings), shown in Settings. No touch.
- **At a car** (its footprint's edge within 1.2 m, or the one sat in), its card shows its maker, name, tier and measured figures from `garage/car-facts.ts`, as the Cars page reads them. R or RT held revs it on its own voice (and W from the seat), the revs climbing towards the throttle and falling to idle; the engine runs from the first rev or from sitting in it, and stops when the player walks away. C or RB sits in its driver's seat, its own cockpit (`art/cars/cockpit/`) built in its pivot and its dials reading the revs, the head turning 140° either way; again stands up beside the driver's door. F or the d-pad's up takes it: it becomes the player's car outside and is kept, as the Cars page's pick is. The way out is a ring in the dealership's colour on the floor, as wide as the door's reach, glowing brighter once the player stands in it; F or the d-pad's up there leaves, back in the car where it was left, which is the taken car if one was taken. A turntable turns its car, even with the player in it.
- **The used lot is an indoor hall**, walked the same way: a used-car hall with its office and windows on to its lot. It has no walk-in until that hall is drawn. An outdoor lot can't be one: the capture stands in for scenery seen far off through glass, and outdoors it would replace the very place the player is standing. Buying is taking everywhere: no money moves, and selling waits for a garage that holds more than one car.
- Other players aren't seen inside, and the Cars menu is as it was.

**The rooms are the art's own** (`art/dealers/interiors.ts`, `loadInterior`), built to the definition and held to it by `interiors.test.ts`. Until the tiles are rebuilt for the sites, the map's own buildings and plants under a plot still stand round the capture point, so a window can look on to a building the rebuild takes away.

**The probe** (`window.__alandDrive.dealership`) goes in at a site (`enterAt`), stands the eye (`standAt`), sits in a spot's car (`sitAt`), shows the captured island alone at the world camera's lens (`peek`), leaves, and lists the doors and what's inside (`doors`, `state`). `tools/dealership.mjs` walks into each and shoots it from its door, towards its windows, beside its first car and from that car's seat, and the window view held against the world itself from the same eye.
