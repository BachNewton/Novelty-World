# More places to find

An idea, not a plan: what the places to find (`map/places.ts`) left out, and why, for whoever adds the next ones. Nothing here is decided.

The first set is the forty places from `docs/HISTORY.md`, the modelled landmarks, three viewpoints and the two golf clubs. Positions below are scene metres from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), read from the pipeline's raw data, and each needs a note checked to `HISTORY.md`'s standard before it goes in.

## Left out on purpose

- **Jettböle**, the Stone Age seal hunters' camp: OpenStreetMap doesn't place it, so it needs a position from Ålands museum or kartor.ax first.
- **Over the water**: Vårdö church and Lövö, Kobba Klintar, Rödhamn and the pilots' museum at Degerby. The car can't reach them until there are ferries, though Kobba Klintar could be found from the shore at Lervik once Lervik has a position.
- **The ferry harbours**, Västerhamn and Långnäs: travel, not sights, so they belong with the ferries.
- **Collectible sets**: the post road's red posts (about 65), the 92 windmills, the midsummer poles. Forty pins already fill the map; a set reads better as a count than as a pin for each.
- **The second von Knorring statue** in Mariehamn (`-1941, 6855`), which sits on top of the Empress's memorial on the map, and the Finström one, which Finström church's note already covers.
