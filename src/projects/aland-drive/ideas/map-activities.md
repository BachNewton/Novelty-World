# Things on the map

An idea, not a plan: what could stand on the map beside the bucket-list challenges, the races and the places to find, so the island is worth exploring. Nothing here is decided or researched to a source yet, except where it leans on `docs/DRIVING-CULTURE.md` and `docs/HISTORY.md`, which carry their own.

## The owner's three tests

A map worth exploring has places that are:

1. **Interesting**: each is worth the drive.
2. **Diverse**: the kinds differ, so the tenth find isn't the first again.
3. **Spread across the whole map**: every part of the island has something, so no part is just road to somewhere else.

The third can be measured. `ideas/races.md` cut the island into squares of a kilometre and counted which roads a player who does everything has seen: the challenges and places to find show 32% of the road squares, and with the races 75%. The same squares can count any kind of find, so each new kind is judged by how much of what's left unseen it fills, as well as by the first two tests.

## Ideas

Each with what it is, where on the real map it would be, and why it might pass the tests.

- **Jobs, as Test Drive Unlimited had them.** TDU's car deliveries, hitchhikers, couriers and models: drive something or someone somewhere, with a reward for doing it cleanly. On Åland they could be fragile cases from Stallhagen's brewery at Godby (the game already measures jolts), the morning's catch to a harbour before the smokehouse closes, a car for a cottage guest off the ferry, or a lost visitor guided to the harbour, as Kobba Klintar's pilots once guided ships. They send the player to farms, quays and villages no race passes, which is what the gaps on the coverage map want.
- **Barn finds, as Forza Horizon has them.** An old car found in a barn and restored: an American car of the kind Eckerö's cruising club keeps, or a microcar of the Ålandsrally meet. A reason to drive down every farm lane, and cars for the garage once cars are owned. They would need more cars to find, of the island's own sorts.
- **Photo spots.** Stop at a real viewpoint and the view counts as a find: Getabergen, Orrdalsklint, Badhusberget, Storberget and the lookout towers. Cruising is the game's core, and a photo spot rewards stopping to look.
- **The stamp album.** Every find of every kind issues a stamp, drawn in code, on a page for each parish (`docs/DRIVING-CULTURE.md`'s "bigger" idea 1). It is the discovery log for everything above, and its pages show at a glance which parishes are still empty. Åland really does this: its own stamps since 1984.
- **Collectibles along the roads.** The post road's red posts, about 65 across the island, and a midsummer pole in each village. Small, many and evenly spread, they fill the space between the big places.
- **Timed moments on the world's clock.** Places worth being at an hour: the cruise between the harbours at 18:30, a lit ferry calling at Långnäs at 03:00, the Lemström bridge opening on the hour in summer. The world clock and the ferries under way are already in the game.
- **Roadside stops.** A café with Åland pancake, or a fuel station, as a place to stop and rest. The six fuel stations already stand on the map.
