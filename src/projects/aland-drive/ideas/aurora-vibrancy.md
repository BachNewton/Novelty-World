# Northern Lights with more show

An idea, not a plan: take it up once the seasons and weather have settled. The owner judged the current aurora fine as it is, and would like a stronger version later.

## What the aurora does now

`art/night/aurora.ts` draws it on clear, dark winter nights, as strong as the weather's `aurora` says and faded by cloud (`docs/TIME-AND-WEATHER.md`). It keeps to what Åland's latitude really shows: mostly a pale green arc low in the north, more often than the real once a month but still rare.

## The idea

A strength the game turns up for its own sake, stretching what is really seen from Åland the way the arcade-first rule allows:

- **More colour**: the reds and purples of a strong storm above the green, not only on the rarest nights.
- **Bigger**: reaching higher up the sky, and sometimes overhead as a corona.
- **More motion**: curtains that ripple and drift, and the brightening pulses of a breakup.

One scale from the real look to the show, so a night can sit anywhere between, set by how strong the night's aurora is, and perhaps a player's setting. A night's aurora stays the same for every player, as the weather does.

## Open

- Whether every aurora is enhanced, or only the strongest few nights.
- What the extra motion costs: it is one draw today, and should stay a matter of the sky's shader.
