# Landmarks out on the skerries

An idea, not a plan: nothing is decided or scheduled. It waits on the world reaching past Fasta Åland's roads, such as riding the ferries (see "Boats under way" in the project's `CLAUDE.md`, and `ideas/floating-origin.md`).

## The gap

Some of the game's most detailed landmark art stands where no car can get near it. Båtskär's six wind turbines by Nyhamn (`art/lighthouses/turbine.ts`) are built to be looked at up close: Enercon's green rings at the tower's foot, a door and landing, the nacelle's light and mast, the spinner and the blades' twist. But the nearest road a car can drive, the end of the road on Järsö, is about 5 km from the nearest of them, so a player only ever sees their far versions, and the near art is spent on the gallery. Nyhamn's mine tower beside them is the same, and so are the lighthouses on skerries no tile draws (Märket, Lågskär, Flötjan).

At 5 km and more the turbines still do their job, marking Nyhamn on the southern horizon, so nothing here is broken: the near art is just never seen in play.

## What would close it

- **Riding a ferry or an excursion boat.** The excursion boats already sail to Kobba Klintar and the outer islands, and the road ferries cross on their wires. A boat the player could ride, even as a camera that goes along rather than a car aboard, would take them past the skerries close enough for the near versions. A trip past Båtskär to Nyhamn is the natural one.
- **A boat of the player's own**, if the game ever gets one, would reach every skerry landmark.
- **A viewpoint**, the cheapest: a place to find on Järsö's southern shore, facing Båtskär, with the turbines as its view. It doesn't bring the near art into play, but it gives the far view a reason to stop and look.

When any of these lands, check the turbines' and the skerry lighthouses' near versions from the water, since nothing in the game has looked at them up close yet.
