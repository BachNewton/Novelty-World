# Åland Drive: the real driving culture

Research for the game's events, places and feel: what Åland's real driving, racing and car culture is, the roads it happens on, what the islands are known for beyond driving, and game ideas tied to those facts. Nothing here is decided. It is a menu for phase 6 and after. Facts carry their source and the date of the event or article; **[excerpt]** means only a search excerpt or a paywalled lead was readable, and "Not confirmed" lists what couldn't be checked.

The short version: Åland has a small, lively, volunteer motorsport scene that has **no permanent circuit and no drag strip**, so it borrows real public roads for a day. That suits this game exactly: its races are already on the roads the game has.

## What's real

### The club

- **Ålands motorklubb (ÅMK)**, founded 13 November 1962, about 300 members, all volunteer. Its first sport was car orientation (*bilorientering*). Sections today: folkrace, dragracing, rallysprint, MX and enduro, roadracing, karting. Fixed tracks at Vessingsboda, Lemland, a little over 20 km from Mariehamn. ([ÅMK, about](https://alandsmotorklubb.ax/om/))
- The chair is Ida Lindqvist, apparently the drag racer of that name. At 17 that racer told the new government she wanted a strip: ["a kilometre would do fairly well"](https://www.nyan.ax/familjen/dagens-alanning/en-kilometer-skulle-racka-hyfsat-bra/) (Nya Åland, 2015).

**Backrace.** A hill race for road and rally cars, on Norra Sundsvägen each autumn. It began in Geta, [on the road up to Soltuna](http://www.motorportalen.net/resultat) **[excerpt]** (2008, 2010), and [moved to a new municipality](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/backrace/) in 2018. **It isn't a speed race.** Each driver runs twice and [the smallest difference between the two times wins](https://www.nyan.ax/sport/gasen-i-botten-pa-arets-backrace/) (organiser Kaj Lindroth, 2026). In 2025 [a hundredth of a second decided it](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/backrace/). About 20 cars, mostly Swedish, and sausage with bread between runs. Oddly, the 2024 report speaks of [a record winning time](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/alands-motorklubb/), so scoring may have varied.

**Rallysprint.** Each year in Ytterby, one car at a time, 30–60 seconds apart, [each stage two or three times, fastest stage wins](https://www.alandsmotorklubb.ax/rallysprint/). The closure is [Tomtbergsvägen, Ljufviksvägen and the road joining them](https://www.regeringen.ax/sites/default/files/attachments/protocol/nr36-2022-enskild-i3.pdf), 09:00–19:00, in the same villages since at least 2016, and [52 crews](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/rally/) entered in 2015. The stage is about 3.7 km of gravel, and a best total of 2:18.0 by a Lancer Evo IX is given for 2023 [**[excerpt]**](http://rallysprint.motorportalen.net/). If both numbers hold, that is about 96 km/h on a gravel village road. A [Vessingsboda sprint](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/rallysprint/) also ran on the folkrace track in 2023, 18 drivers.

**Folkrace.** Cheap, entry-level rallycross on a track built to stay [under 80 km/h](https://www.alandsmotorklubb.ax/folkrace/), from the year you turn 15. The rule that matters: **any licensed rival can claim your car after the race** for 11,000 SEK, with a 400 SEK fee per bid. Jonas Ask [swept the 2025 premiere in a Saab](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/folkrace/).

**Cruising.** The real heart of it for this game.

- Oldtime Cruisers' car show at Ålandstravet, then [the traditional cruise between Mariehamn's Eastern and Western harbours from about 18:30](https://www.eckerolinjen.se/en/evenemang) **[excerpt]**, the wording being Visit Åland's listing, which has since gone. The 2026 date was 18 July, and Nya Åland ran a gallery the next day. The show spent [nearly 30 years in Badhusparken](https://www.nyan.ax/sport/oldtime-cruisers-tar-hastkrafterna-till-travbanan/) **[excerpt]** before moving; one report counts 300 vehicles by midday **[excerpt]**.
- [Eckerö Cruising Club](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/nyheter/vardagsliv/blankande-jankare-langtar-efter-varcruising): a garage of 17 shining American cars, a clubhouse smelling of waffles, and a winter of "longing for spring cruising" (2023). Eckerö also held a [vehicle show, Pärlor på hjul](https://www.nyan.ax/nyheter/lokalt/parlor-pa-hjul-med-spegelblank-plat/) (2009).
- **Storagatan on weekend nights.** Young people drive it towards the harbour with music up, in *raggarbilar* and *moppebilar*. Residents [complain](https://www.nyan.ax/insandare/sluta-terrorisera-vara-helgnatter-pa-storagatan/), police [weigh in](https://www.nyan.ax/insandare/oonskat-beteende-ar-inte-automatiskt-kriminaliserat/), and a [Nya Åland editorial](https://www.nyan.ax/ledare/lat-dem-roa-sig/) (9 July 2026) defends it: a quiet town risks being a dead one, and yesterday's raggarbil is today's moppebil.
- Police reported [tuned mopeds, one over 80 km/h in town](https://polisen.ax/-/pressmeddelande-12-16.07.2023) (July 2023).

### Local drivers

- **Ida Zetterström**, Åland's Top Fuel driver, moved from motorcycles to [dragsters](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/sport/motorsport/ida-zetterstrom-lever-sin-drom-over-500-kmtim), races the NHRA in the USA with [over 550 km/h](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/dragracing/) recorded (2025), and was [seventh in qualifying at the 2026 U.S. Nationals](https://www.nyan.ax/tag/motorsport/). Her car is ["The Flyin Fyfer"](https://www.nyan.ax/sport/ida-zetterstrom-vann-tavling-pa-dragrace-festival/).
- [Three Åland motorcycle racers](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/sport/pokaler-av-alla-slag-till-aland-i-dragrace/1363189) took trophies at Kjula Dragway (Aug 2026). With no strip at home, Kjula in Sweden is the [section's partner](https://www.alandsmotorklubb.ax/dragracing/).
- **Keke Rosberg, 1979.** His official site lists a [Viking-Line Ålands Rally](https://www.kekerosberg.com/rallying) with no car or result. It shows a real rally existed then. I found nothing else on it.

### The tracks that never happened

- **Åland Ring**, a motor park in Eckerö Kyrkoby: [environmental permit in 2013](https://www.nyan.ax/nyheter/lokalt/motorfolket-tycker-till-om-motorbanan/), [annulled in court, final by 2019](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/nyheter/hoppet-ute-aland-ring). Locals had doubted it for years.
- **The traffic safety centre at Vessingsboda**, known to this project as a plan, is **probably dead**. It won [up to €455,647 in grants](https://www.regeringen.ax/sites/default/files/attachments/protocol/nr26-2021-enskild-i1.pdf) (2021), but Ålandstidningen headlined [Ingen halkbana i Vessingsboda](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/nyheter/ingen-halkbana-vessingsboda) in November 2022: no funding, since skid training didn't become compulsory **[excerpt]**. Learners [can use Sweden's Gillingebanan](https://www.regeringen.ax/sites/default/files/attachments/protocol/nr26-2021-enskild-i1.pdf).
- **Maxinge**: ÅMK ran [Race Days there in 2016](https://regeringen.ax/sites/default/files/attachments/protocol/beslutslista-2016-07-08-webben.pdf), but closing the straight on road 2 was refused. A [cone race on "the airfield"](https://www.nyan.ax/nyheter/lokalt/motorfolket-tycker-till-om-motorbanan/) ran in 2013, which airfield not stated. That is the only airfield motorsport found.

### Winter

- **Ice roads are real and informal.** On 22 February 2026 the ice road to Simskäla, Vårdö, [broke under a fire brigade member](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/nyheter/varnar-foer-vak-pa-isvaegen-till-simskaela/1163153) driving slowly between its markers: thin at the hole, 30 cm right beside it.
- **Ice cars.** Nya Åland reported in 2007 that [children, some as young as eight, drive uninsured *isbilar* on the ice](https://www.nyan.ax/nyheter/lokalt/sa-blev-iskorning-laglig-for-unga/), legal because a 2004 change limited the licence requirement to roads. The picture was taken at Nabben. Whether that still holds under the 2023 traffic law is unconfirmed; a recent Ålandstidningen piece says police want the rules reviewed **[excerpt]**.
- Rule of thumb: [20 cm of ice for a car](https://www.nyan.ax/nyheter/bil-genom-isen-vid-jarso/), weak around bridges, piers, shoals and straits; a car went through at Järsö in 2018. [No motor vehicles](https://www.nyan.ax/nyheter/forbjudet-att-kora-bil-pa-vattentakter/) on the drinking-water lakes Långsjön, Markusbölefjärden and Dalkarby träsk.

### Rules of the road

- **The top legal speed on Åland is [90 km/h](https://regeringen.ax/sites/default/files/attachments/protocol/nr15-2022-enskild-i1.pdf)**, in the government's words in 2022. Yet 201 m of Streetrace reaches nearly 200. Speeders do make the papers: [145 km/h through Lemland](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/nyheter/korde-145-kmh-genom-lemland) (2022).
- **Plates:** [ÅL plus five digits since 2011, country code AX since 13 September 2025](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_registration_plates_of_Finland); that page disagrees with itself on blue or black lettering, so check a photo. Personal plates exist: the [government budgeted €727 a plate](https://www.regeringen.ax/node/53958) to reserve "Åland 1" to "Åland 10" for itself (9 November 2021).
- Words seen in print: *moppebil*, *raggarbil*, *jänkare* (American car), *isbil*, *mopel* ([a light electric vehicle](https://www.alandstidningen.ax/nyheter/mopelerna-farliga-eller-harmlosa) **[excerpt]**), *hoj* (motorcycle). No source for specifically Åland slang.

### Getting cars there

- Eckerö Linjen runs [Grisslehamn to Eckerö in about two hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecker%C3%B6_Linjen) and sells a [car and motorcycle package](https://destination.eckerolinjen.se/en/car-and-motorcycle-package-aland) built on the post road. The rallysprint carries its name, and it offered [a rally ferry trip](http://rallysprint.motorportalen.net/docs/eckerolinjen_rallysprint2010_resa.pdf) in 2010 **[excerpt]**.
- The Swedes bringing cars that are actually documented are **competitors**: most of the folkrace and backrace fields. A Swedish car day trip is unproven; the [standard day cruise](https://www.eckerolinjen.se/en/daycruises) doesn't land.

### What's absent

No circuit, no drag strip, no skid pan. No ice racing on ÅMK's list. No car museum among [Åland's museums](https://www.museum.ax/sites/default/files/attachments/page/24-0039%20Museer%20pa%CC%8A%20A%CC%8Aland_eng.pdf). No sign of A-traktor or EPA culture on Åland: searches found only Sweden, and the local equivalent looks like the moppebil. No motorcycle clubs found beyond ÅMK's sections. No motorway, and nothing legal over 90.

## Game ideas

Every idea rests on a fact above. **Use invented names for clubs, sponsors and shops**, as with the cars; real road and place names are the point and stay.

### Small: uses what exists

In priority order. All need only the road network, a start and finish, and the phase 6 timing that's planned anyway.

1. **Real stages as time trials.** Six out of the table: Ytterby gravel, Norra Sundsvägen, the Geta climb, and the post road's legs. Target times come from the real thing where known. Gives the explorer a reason to learn these exact bends.
2. **The 201 m streetrace, at all four sites.** Pre-stage lights, a tree, 1/8 mile, and a plaque: "Getavägen, record nearly 200 km/h". The roads are straight, so it costs almost nothing, and it's the instant challenge for two players meeting on a road.
3. **The regularity hill.** Two runs at Norra Sundsvägen, closest pair of times wins, with no speed target. It rewards the steady cruising the game is about, it's unusual, and it's true. A sausage stand at the top.
4. **Red posts on the Postrutten.** Collect every kilometre post across the island, about 65, with one line of history on some. Needs one small model.
5. **Storagatan after dark.** Harbour to harbour at walking pace with the windows down; the only "race" is being seen. Passing other players here at night is the social spot TDU's cruising needs, with the music as the reward.
6. **Your plate.** ÅL and five digits on every car, with a personal plate as a costly unlock in the shop.
7. **Follow the apple signs.** A chain of signs through the farm roads, leading to a harvest stall.
8. **The bridge opens on the hour.** At Lemström in summer the road closes for boats, by the game clock. Small, odd, and memorable: a real reason to choose the long way round.

### Bigger

In priority order.

1. **The stamp album as the game's discovery log.** Every find, a church, fortress, midsummer pole, red post, stage record, issues a stamp drawn in code, on a page by parish. It unifies the road discovery percentage, the landmarks and the regional art into one collection, and it's the thing Åland really does. The best answer to "why explore?".
2. **A club season on the real calendar.** One invented motor club with the real shape of the year: folkrace in April, cruising and sprint in July, streetrace in August, Potato Race and hill in October. Each is a small event at its real place. No new tech once the small ideas exist, and it gives the map a rhythm.
3. **Car orientation, the club's first sport.** The GPS and mini map go off, and you get a paper-style route card. It turns "getting to know the roads" into the skill itself, and the map code already knows every road and junction. It's also online-friendly: a shared card, first home wins.
4. **Vessingsboda and the claim rule.** A hand-built folkrace track and Motorstadion, with mixed dirt. Win too well and a rival may buy your car at the capped price, so the stakes rest on ownership. Needs a closed track and an off-road surface; the claim works best online.
5. **Winter: the ice road.** With the seasons of phase 7, marked ice roads open between islands, ice thickness varies, and a hole can open beside safe ice. Add a homemade ice car and a frozen bay for sliding. Few driving games have it, and it needs ferries or their ice-road replacement for the first real trip off the main island.
6. **Long Night Runs.** Mariehamn to Långnäs or Eckerö to catch a ship that really does call at night. Needs the day/night cycle and a ferry at the quay.
7. **Åland Ring, the track that was never built.** A fictional circuit at Eckerö Kyrkoby, with a wink. Last, because everything else here is real and this is the one thing that isn't, and it's a lot of hand work. But real Ålanders wanted it for years.

The through-line for feel: **90 on the sign, nobody around, a closed road once a year.** The drama is a shiny American car at 18:30, a sausage at the top of a hill, and a hundredth of a second, more than it is speed.
