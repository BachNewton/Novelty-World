# Åland Drive: the playground

`?playground`, a made-up map for trying how the cars drive. Paths are relative to the project's folder, `src/projects/aland-drive/`; the project's own `CLAUDE.md` there is the overview and says where everything else is written down.

`?playground` is the real game on a made-up map instead of Åland (`playground/layout.ts`), for trying how the cars drive without hunting the island for the right road: a long straight of asphalt and one of gravel side by side, one circuit laid twice, in asphalt and in gravel, a skidpad of each, a slab of bare rock, open grass all round and forest at the edges. The dev menu switches to it and back, and jumps to each part of it. It is one tile in the pipeline's own files and formats, built by `tools/playground.ts` into the site's public folder and committed, so the game reads it as it reads any tile, with nothing of its own changed, and a change to the layout is a rerun of the tool. It is a world of its own, its tile on the world's origin (the shaders read positions as 32-bit floats, so a world far from its origin draws less precisely), and what the game places by Åland's own positions rather than from tiles (the boats under way, sea marks, regattas and the places to find) is left out of it. A page in it drives alone. It's for feedback on feel; a change is still judged on Åland's real roads last.
