# Åland Drive: the racers

The AI drivers who race the player (`ideas/traffic-and-racers.md`, "Racers"; `ideas/races.md`, section 8), and the tool that is their ruler. Paths are relative to the project's folder, `src/projects/aland-drive/`; the project's own `CLAUDE.md` there is the overview and says where everything else is written down. The method behind this folder, as the `machine-learning` skill asks for it, is `ideas/racer-method.md`.

**The decided rules.** A racer drives the same car through the same `DriveInput` as the player, a full simulated car in the player's world (`sim/drive.ts`), so whatever it does a player could do too. It never gets more grip, power or luck than its car has, never speeds up out of sight, and has no rubber-banding: difficulty is only how well it drives. It recovers as a player would, by driving back to the road or, when stuck, by its own reset, at the player's cost in time. A race shows no time, only places.

- **The road as laid** (`laid-road.ts`): the baked road surface's sections, in a grid of cells, read at any place for the laid middle under it, its carriageway's and whole surface's reach to either side, and whether it is sealed. The GPS's line is OpenStreetMap's and parts from the laid road by a road's width and more in places, so everything a racer drives on, and everything a race measures (its grid and its lines, `tools/race-places.ts`), is read from the laid road, never the GPS line. `route-plan.ts` reads it along a route, every 4 m; where the GPS line strays further than a sample can see, the road is followed on from where it was last seen, only while the line runs on much as it did, since at a junction's apron the road turns and the gap is bridged instead.
- **The racing line** (`racing-line.ts`): the route's corridor, the laid middle with the carriageway's edges, bridged straight across the junction aprons where no section is laid, and in it the minimum-curvature line, the textbook racing line (outside, apex, outside): the least-squares answer to a banded linear system in the offset from the middle at every sample, bounded by the edges less the car's half width and a margin, solved by an active set (the unbounded answer, then every offset over an edge held on it, until none moves). The middle itself is the line a cautious driver keeps, and what the racing line is measured against. Both are pure functions of the route and the road.
- **The speed plan** (`speed-plan.ts`): the fastest speed at every sample from the car's measured limits (`sim/limits/`) on the ground under it, in the condition, on the tyres for it, at a share of its cornering grip and a share of its braking: the speed the bend allows on the grip the car holds at that speed, no lighter over a crest than keeps its wheels down (the road's height along the line says where it crests), no faster than the steering's lock at that speed turns the bend; then backwards, braking for every slower sample ahead with what the bend there leaves of the tyres; then forwards, no faster than the car's pull from its standing start. Pure arithmetic on a table: a plan costs nothing a frame.
- **The driver** (`driver.ts`): pure pursuit along the line, the steering every computer-driven car shares (`sim/autopilot.ts`), with a little steering towards the line by how far off it stands, softened at speed, and throttle and brake holding the car under the plan's speed a moment ahead. **The racer** (`racer.ts`) is that driver with a player's recovery: stuck, it backs out and tries again; stuck once more, off the road for good, on its roof or in the water, it presses its own R, back on its line at the nearest point. It runs the race's own rules for itself (`challenge/run.ts`), so its lap, its next line and its finish are read as the player's are, and its progress (lines crossed, and how near the next) orders the places (`challenge/standings.ts`). Its record is small and its drive is a pure function of it, so whoever runs it next online carries on from exactly there.
- **Difficulty** (`difficulty.ts`) is five named levels, each a share of the car's cornering grip and of its braking, the second a little higher. Their spread is a count the vetting tool makes, never a guess.
- **The field** (`field.ts`): the race's own car, which the player is handed, and the cars nearest it in pace of the kinds its ground suits (sports and road cars on a road race, the rally cars and four-by-fours off it), by the measured sprint to 100 km/h the tiers rank cars by. The racers drive the others.
- **In a race** (`grid.ts`, wired in `render/game.ts`): once the player's car is on its place, racers take the grid's other places, each in a car of the field, and the lights wait for them; they step before the world each step, held through the countdown as the player is; the game draws each as it draws another player's car. The HUD shows the player's place among the cars, and a finish's place is settled when the player crosses the line, since no racer behind can pass them then. A race's line comes from its file (`public/aland-drive/race-lines/<id>.json`, `race-line-file.ts`, written by `tools/race-lines.ts`): the line needs the whole route's laid road to draw and the game only has the tiles round the car, so it is drawn once from the built tiles for the widest car of the field, and every page and the vetting tool drive the very same line. A race of the page's own (a test's) draws its line from the roads loaded here. `?racers=<count>` and `?difficulty=<level>` in a link set the field for a drive.
- **The vetting tool** (`tools/race-vet.ts`, `vet.ts`): every race driven alone in the simulation, by the race's car or its whole field, in every condition on the tyres for it, from every place on the grid, on the GPS line (the par driver), the laid middle or the racing line, at shares of the grip, through the race's own rules, over the shared worker pool, and counted: finished or not and where it failed, each lap's time, metres past the carriageway's edge and off the whole surface, how far off its line it ran, hits, airborne steps, resets. It eases nothing. A clean finish is a finish with no stop, no reset and no hit, never further past the laid surface than a gate reaches. It is the ruler every change to a racer is read on, by exact difference, since a run is deterministic.
