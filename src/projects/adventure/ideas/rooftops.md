# Rooftops

An idea, not a plan: nothing here is decided.

A town can reward a player who likes platforming: up a stack of crates, onto a balcony, over an eave and away along the roofs. Nothing in how the player stands or collides rules it out:

- **Standing and blocking are separate.** The player stands on whatever a ray straight down finds among the pieces made to be stood on; collision only stops them walking through things, and an outline blocks only where it overlaps the player's own height. So a player up on a roof isn't stopped by the walls beneath them, and is stopped by a chimney beside them.
- **What it would take:**
  - Roof pieces among what can be stood on. The low teal roofs are gentle; the high red ones are steep, so decide whether a player stands on a slope that steep, or slides down it.
  - Ways up, placed as level design in the map editor: a balcony, stairs, a ladder, crates, a cliff beside a building.
  - A jump that reaches from those onto an eave, tuned so the climb takes a little cleverness.
- **To watch:** the camera up close among roofs, and whether the roofs' undersides or gaps between pieces show from up there.
