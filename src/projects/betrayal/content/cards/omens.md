# Omen cards

Drawn when an explorer discovers a room with an omen symbol. The haunt roll they trigger is in rules.md.

## Bite
- Deck: omen
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you draw this card, the player on your right makes a Might 4 attack against you on behalf of the unseen "something" (which then flees).
  - You defend against that attack normally, rolling dice equal to your Might.
  - This omen can't be dropped, traded, or stolen.
  - Make a haunt roll now.

## Box
- Deck: omen
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you draw this card, draw an item card and place it on the Box.
  - The item in the Box can't be dropped or traded.
  - If someone would steal the item in the Box, you may discard the Box instead.
  - On your turn, you may take the item out of the Box and put a different item you possess into it.
  - If the Box is empty, you may put an item into it.
  - The Box can't be stolen.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
  > Note: The card does not say what happens to an item taken out of the Box, or whether the item in the Box can still be used while it is there.
  > Resolution (community): an item taken out of the Box goes back to your ordinary possessions, and an item in the Box still counts as yours (an item that changed your traits is not "lost" by going in). A player reported a publisher answer that an item in the Box can't be used, and that swapping gives you use of the new item at the cost of the old; another player disputed it as not on the card, so whether a boxed item can be used stays open. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2534498, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2425410, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1658494
  > Note: The card does not say whether the swap or the "put an item in an empty Box" action is limited to once per turn.
  > Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found for the Box. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2425410, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1658494

## Cat
- Deck: omen
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: Companion
- Text:
  - Before one of your trait rolls, you may call on the Cat for luck: roll 1 die and apply the result below to that trait roll.
  - This omen can't be dropped, traded, or stolen.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
- Table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 0 | Subtract 2 from the result of that trait roll. |
| 1+ | Add 2 to the result of that trait roll. |

> Note: No per-turn limit is printed for the Cat.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. A single forum answer says the Cat can also help attack rolls, which is a separate question. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2492582

## Crystal Ball
- Deck: omen
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Once during your turn, after the haunt is revealed, you may attempt a Knowledge roll to look into the Crystal Ball, with the results below.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
- Table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 4+ | Search the item or event stack for a card of your choice, shuffle that stack, then put the chosen card on top of it. |
| 1–3 | Lose 1 Sanity. |
| 0 | Lose 2 Sanity. |

> Note: The card does not say whether the search includes the discard pile.
> Resolution (official): only the undrawn item or event stack is searched, not discarded cards. The 1st-edition FAQ also ruled the Crystal Ball can't search the room stack, which the 2nd-edition card already reflects. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Dog
- Deck: omen
- Set: base
- Label: Companion
- Text:
  - Gain 1 Might and 1 Sanity now.
  - If you lose custody of the Dog, lose 1 Might and 1 Sanity.
  - Represent the Dog with a small monster token placed in your room (use a token whose color differs from any other monsters).
  - Once during your turn, the Dog may move to any explored room up to 6 spaces away, using doors and stairs, then return. Before returning it may pick up, carry, and/or drop 1 item.
  - Opponents don't slow the Dog.
  - The Dog can't use one-way passages or rooms that require a roll.
  - The Dog can't carry items that slow movement.
  - This omen can't be dropped, traded, or stolen.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
  > Note: The card does not list which rooms count as one-way passages or rooms that require a roll.
  > Resolution (official): the Dog can't go through the Collapsed Room, Coal Chute, Gallery or Mystic Slide (one-way), the Revolving Wall (needs a roll), or the Mystic Elevator. Monsters don't slow it, and it can't carry a haunt item that makes its carrier spend two moves to enter a room. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

  > Note: the official list names the one-way rooms and the Revolving Wall, but not the rooms that take a roll to leave (Junk Room, Attic, Graveyard, Pentagram Chamber), the barrier rooms (Tower, Chasm, Catacombs), or the Secret Passage and Secret Stairs.
  > Resolution (project): the Dog doesn't go into a room that takes a roll to leave, or set out from one, since it couldn't get back to its holder without the roll; it may go into a barrier room by the side it comes in on, but never crosses it, as crossing takes a roll. It moves only by doors and stairs, as the card says, so it doesn't use the Secret Passage or the Secret Stairs, whose cards let explorers use them. This reads "rooms that require a roll" by what the Dog would need to do, and keeps its trip a simple out-and-back. Research: only the official FAQ list above.

  > Note: The card doesn't say where an item ends up after the Dog's trip, or what becomes of an item it carries when it is lost or its holder dies.
  > Resolution (project): the Dog's trip is one action that ends back with its holder, so it never holds an item between turns. An item it picks up (from an item pile, in a room it reaches) goes straight into its holder's possession when it returns; an item its holder gives it can be dropped in a room it reaches, onto that room's item pile. It doesn't trade with other explorers, since the card names only picking up, carrying and dropping. With nothing ever left on the Dog, losing it or its holder's death moves no item except as the usual rules say for the holder's own items. The one BGG answer on how the Dog works reads it the same way, as a six-room "boomerang" fetch. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/610370, https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Key
- Deck: omen
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Add 4 dice to trait rolls made to open, or take something from, a card or room tile (for example the Locked Room, Safe, or Vault).
  - You may pass through the Locked Room without removing a Lock.
  - At the start of each turn, if you are next to an unlocked door in the Locked Room, you may put a Lock token on that door.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
  > Note: No 8-dice maximum is printed on the Key (unlike Bloodstone and Spear). The card says "at the start of each turn", not "each of your turns", so it is unclear whether you may lock a door at the start of other players' turns.
  > Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found on either point. Players disagree on whether rolls may exceed 8 dice when a card gives no cap. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2501599, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1807098

## Letter
- Deck: omen
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you draw this card, you may immediately give one of your explorer tokens to another explorer.
  - On any of your turns, including the one you drew it on, you may move to that explorer's room; then you discard this omen and that explorer discards your explorer token.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
  > Note: The printed card does not say when the token is handed over, or whether you may move to that explorer on the turn you draw the Letter. The text above includes the official correction.
  > Resolution (official): the Widow's Walk FAQ (updated 12/6/16) corrects the card: you hand over the token immediately on drawing the Letter, and you may move to that explorer on any of your turns, including that one. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1700604/widows-walk-faq (the FAQ PDF, "Widow's Walk FAQ KB 12.6.16", linked from that thread; it is a longer version of https://media.wizards.com/2016/downloads/widows_walk_faq.pdf)

## Mask
- Deck: omen
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Once during your turn, you may attempt a Sanity roll to use the Mask, with the results below.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
- Table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 4+ | You may put on or take off the Mask. Putting it on: gain 2 Knowledge and lose 2 Sanity. Taking it off: gain 2 Sanity and lose 2 Knowledge. |
| 0–3 | You can't use the Mask this turn. |

> Note: The card doesn't say what happens to the traits it changed when you lose the Mask while wearing it.
> Resolution (project): losing the Mask while wearing it takes it off, reversing the change, because the trait change belongs to wearing the card and a card you no longer hold can't be worn.

## Rope
- Deck: omen
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - You may move up the Coal Chute, and from the Ballroom to the Gallery.
  - You don't need to roll to cross the Chasm.
  - You take no damage from the Collapsed Room.
  - You may move from the room with the Below Collapsed Room token into the Collapsed Room.
  - When moving by a dumbwaiter (dumbwaiter icon printed), you may move to any landing.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
  > Note: The dumbwaiter is shown as a small icon inline in the text, not a word; it refers to rooms bearing that symbol (Widow's Walk).
  > Resolution (official): the Widow's Walk rule sheet defines dumbwaiter rooms as the tiles with that symbol and lists the landings as the Basement Landing, any room of the Grand Staircase/Foyer/Entrance Hall, the Upper Landing and the Roof Landing. Source: Widow's Walk rule sheet, p. 2 (widows-walk-rules.md); https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2369803

## Skull
- Deck: omen
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Whenever you take mental damage, you may take all of it as physical damage instead.
  - Make a haunt roll now.

> Note: The card doesn't say whether damage taken as physical instead of mental counts as physical damage for other cards, such as the Armor.
> Resolution (project): it does: the damage is physical from then on, so the Armor takes 1 off it, because "take it as physical damage" makes it physical damage in every respect.

## Spirit Board
- Deck: omen
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Before you move during your turn, you may look at the top tile of the room stack.
  - If you use the Spirit Board after the haunt is revealed, the traitor may move any number of monsters 1 space closer to you. (If you are the traitor, you don't have to move those monsters.)
  - If there is no traitor, all monsters move 1 space closer to you.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
  > Note: "you don't have to move those monsters" when you are the traitor reads as the traitor's movement being optional; the card gives no further detail.
  > Resolution (project): moving the monsters is optional for the traitor: any number of them, zero included, 1 space closer, as the card says ("you don't have to move those monsters"). Stunned monsters can be moved this way (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/994104). Research: no ruling found. Forum threads on the Spirit Board settle only that the looked-at tile stays on top of the stack. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1872346, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/854312

## Vial
- Deck: omen
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Once on your turn, you may drink from the Vial: roll 2 dice and apply the result below.
  - Make a haunt roll now.
- Table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 0 | Take 2 points of physical damage. |
| 1 | Take 2 points of mental damage. Draw an item card. |
| 2 | No effect. |
| 3 | Gain 1 in any physical trait. |
| 4 | Gain 1 in each trait except Sanity. Lose 2 Sanity. |

> Note: The card does not say the Vial is discarded or used up after drinking, so it can apparently be used once every turn.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/boardgameexpansion/198452/betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill-widows-walk/forums/66
