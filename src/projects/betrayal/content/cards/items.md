# Item cards

Drawn when an explorer discovers a room with an item symbol.

## Adrenaline Shot
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Use before you attempt a trait roll: add 4 to the result of that roll.
  - Discard this item after you use it.

> Note: The card doesn't say whether an attack or defense roll counts as a "trait roll".
> Resolution (official): It doesn't. Combat is not a trait roll, so the Adrenaline Shot can't add to an attack or defense roll. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Amulet of the Ages
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you get it, gain 1 Might, 1 Speed, 1 Knowledge and 1 Sanity.
  - If you lose the Amulet, lose 3 Might, 3 Speed, 3 Knowledge and 3 Sanity.

## Angel Feather
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you attempt a roll of any kind, you may name a number from 0 to 8 and use it as the result instead of rolling the dice.
  - Discard this item after you use it.

> Note: The card doesn't say whether it can be used outside your own turn, such as on a defense roll.
> Resolution (official): It can. The Angel Feather, the Lucky Stone and the Skull omen are the only cards a player may use outside their own turn. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Armor
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Every time you take physical damage ("not just once per turn"), reduce that damage by 1 point.
  - This item can't be stolen.

> Note: The card doesn't say whether it also reduces an effect that tells you to "lose" Might or Speed, rather than to take physical damage, or whether it stops an attacker from stealing your other items.
> Resolution (official): It does neither. The Armor reduces only physical damage, such as combat damage. It doesn't apply when a card or room tells you to lose Might or Speed, and it doesn't stop an attacker who beats you by 2 or more from stealing another item instead. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

> Note: The card says only that the Armor can't be stolen. The rulebook says an item that can't be traded can't be stolen, but not the reverse, so it is unclear whether the Armor can be traded.
> Resolution (official): The card is correct and the card wins over the rulebook: the Armor can't be stolen but can be traded, dropped and picked up. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Axe
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: Weapon
- Text:
  - When you make a Might attack with this weapon, roll 1 extra die (maximum of 8 dice).
  - You can't use another weapon while using this one.

> Note: The card doesn't say whether the extra die also applies when you defend.
> Resolution (official): It doesn't; the bonus is only for attacking. Using a weapon is optional, and an explorer carrying several weapons chooses which one to use in each attack. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Bell
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:

  - If you lose the Bell, lose 1 Sanity.
  - Once during your turn, after the haunt is revealed, you may attempt a Sanity roll to use the Bell (results below).
- Table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|

## Blood Dagger
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: Weapon
- Text:
  - When you make a Might attack with this weapon, roll 3 extra dice (maximum of 8 dice). If you do, lose 1 Speed.
  - You can't use another weapon while using this one.
  - This item can't be traded or dropped.
  - If it is stolen, you take 2 dice of physical damage.

> Note: The rulebook says only a tradable item can be stolen, yet this untradable card says what happens when it is stolen. The card also doesn't say whether you may carry it without using it.
> Resolution (official): It can be stolen, and you take the 2 dice of physical damage when it is, whether or not you ever used it. Using it is optional: if you don't add its 3 dice to an attack, you don't lose the Speed. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1701137

## Blueprint
- Deck: item
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you roll for your movement, or roll to enter, exit or cross a room, you may choose the result instead.
  - You may travel between rooms that have a dumbwaiter (the card prints the word followed by the dumbwaiter icon).

> Note: The card doesn't say what the move between dumbwaiter rooms costs. The Widow's Walk rule sheet's dumbwaiter rule moves you only to a landing, for 1 extra space of movement.
> Resolution (unresolved): No official, designer or community ruling found. The only BGG answer touching it says traitors use dumbwaiters like other explorers, which doesn't settle the Blueprint's cost. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2534498

## Boomstick
- Deck: item
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: Weapon
- Text:
  - When you draw this card, place 2 item tokens on it.
  - Discard a token to make a Speed 7 attack instead of a Might attack.
  - When no tokens are left, discard this item.
  - You can't use another weapon while using this one.

> Note: "Speed 7 attack" is not defined on the card. Other cards' "Might 4 attack" means rolling 4 dice, so it probably means you roll 7 dice in place of your Speed, but the card doesn't say so. Unlike the Revolver, it also doesn't say what the defender rolls or what kind of damage the attack deals.
> Resolution (unresolved): No official, designer or community ruling found; the BGG Rules forum has no thread on the Boomstick.

## Camcorder
- Deck: item
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you draw this card, place triangular Knowledge Roll tokens on it, one for each explorer.
  - An explorer who has the Camcorder may take a Knowledge Roll token to gain 1 Knowledge.
  - You may not take a Knowledge Roll token if you already have one.
  - When no tokens are left on the card, discard this card and all of those Knowledge Roll tokens.

> Note: The card writes the token name as both "Knowledge Roll token" and "Knowledge roll tokens". It doesn't say whether taking a token is limited to once per turn. "All those Knowledge roll tokens" seems to mean the tokens explorers have already taken, and the card doesn't say whether discarding them takes back the Knowledge they gave.
> Resolution (unresolved): One BGG answer says discarding the tokens doesn't take the Knowledge back and that each explorer can take at most one token, but no other answer confirms it and there is no official or designer ruling. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2529368

## Candle
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you draw an event card, roll 1 extra die (maximum of 8 dice) for that event's trait rolls.
  - If you have the Bell, the Book and the Candle, gain 2 in each trait.
  - The first time you lose any one of those 3 items later in the game, lose 2 from each trait.

> Note: The Book isn't in the items deck. It's presumably the Book omen.
> Resolution (official): The 1st-edition FAQ calls the Book an omen that is also an item, so the Book omen is the third of the set. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

> Note: The card doesn't say whether an event that makes you fight counts as one of "that event's trait rolls".
> Resolution (official): It doesn't. Combat is not a trait roll, so the Candle adds no die to an attack or defense roll. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

> Note: The card doesn't say whether the set bonus comes back if you complete the set again after losing it.
> Resolution (project): it comes back each time you hold all three again, and only the first loss after that takes it back, because the card's bonus and loss are tied to holding the set, and a set rebuilt is a set held.

> Note: The card doesn't say which rolls count as "that event's trait rolls": only those made while the event is read, or also later ones, and whether another explorer's roll for your event counts.
> Resolution (project): the extra die goes to every trait roll you make for an event you drew, for as long as that event is in play, and never to another explorer's roll, because the card ties the die to your drawing the event and to your rolls.

## Dynamite
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Instead of attacking, you may throw the Dynamite through a connecting door into an adjacent room.
  - Every explorer and monster in that room that has both Might and Speed traits must attempt a Speed roll (results below).
  - Discard this item after you use it.
- Table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 5+ | Take no damage from Dynamite. |
| 0–4 | Take 4 points of physical damage. |

> Note: The card doesn't say what physical damage does to a monster, which normally isn't damaged but stunned or killed when defeated.
> Resolution (official): A monster that fails the roll is treated as if it had been defeated in physical combat, so it is stunned or killed as its haunt says. A monster that can't be attacked physically, such as a ghost, or that starts the haunt without a Might or a Speed, is unaffected. The traitor can't ignore the Dynamite, since it is an item, not an event. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Idol
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Once per turn, before you make any trait, combat or event roll, you may rub the Idol to add 2 dice to that roll (maximum of 8 dice).
  - Each time you do, lose 1 Sanity.

> Note: The card says "once per turn", not "once during your turn", and doesn't say whether a combat roll includes a defense roll on another player's turn.
> Resolution (community): It can be used when you defend, since it isn't a weapon, and "once per turn" allows one use in each player's turn. Two independent answers agree, with no official ruling. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1909175

> Note: The card doesn't define an "event roll".
> Resolution (project): an event roll is any roll an event card has you make, its damage dice included; the haunt roll and an item's own dice are not, because the card names the source of the roll, not its kind.

## Music Box
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - Once per turn, you may open or close the Music Box.
  - While the Music Box is open, any explorer or monster with a Sanity trait that enters the Music Box's room, or starts its turn there, must make a Sanity roll of 4+. If it fails, that explorer or monster is mesmerized by the music and its turn ends.
  - If an explorer or monster carrying the Music Box is mesmerized, it drops the Music Box. If the Music Box was open when dropped, it stays open.

> Note: The card doesn't say whether the explorer carrying an open Music Box is affected by it.
> Resolution (project): they are affected like anyone else, including in each room they enter with it, because the music fills the room the box is in and the carrier is in that room.

> Note: The card doesn't say what happens to an explorer mesmerized when it isn't their turn.
> Resolution (project): they only drop the Music Box if they carry it; there is no turn of theirs to end, because the card's "its turn ends" can only cut short a turn being played.

> Note: The card says only that an open box dropped by a mesmerized carrier stays open.
> Resolution (project): the Music Box stays open through every drop and trade until someone closes it, because open is the box's own state and the card gives no other way to close it.

## Pickpoket's Gloves
- Deck: item
- Set: base
- Label: none
- Text:
  - When you are in the same room as another explorer, you may discard this item to take any item that explorer is carrying.

> Note: The title is printed "PICKPOKET'S GLOVES", a misprint of "Pickpocket's". It's kept as printed here. Haunt 100 (Let's Play a Game) names the card "Pickpocket Gloves".
> Resolution (official): The name is a misprint: Haunt 100 spells it with "Pickpocket", and the open-source card data (tkindy/betrayal) uses "Pickpocket's Gloves". Search for the card under either spelling. Source: https://github.com/tkindy/betrayal

> Note: the card says "take", not "steal", so it is unclear whether items and omens that can't be stolen (the Armor, the Box, the companions) are protected from it, and whether the Blood Dagger's "if it is stolen" damage applies.
> Resolution (project): taking with the gloves is stealing, without the attack. The gloves can take any item or omen the other explorer could lose to a steal: not the Armor, not an omen marked "can't be stolen", but the Blood Dagger (its holder takes the 2 dice of physical damage) and any tradable omen or haunt item token, unless a haunt forbids stealing. The take counts as the steal action for that item this turn. Otherwise a single discarded card would undo every "can't be stolen" line in the game, which those lines exist to prevent; the one BGG answer on omens (thread 610370) also lets the gloves take whatever the stealing rules allow, and thread 1414426 treats the take as a steal for the one-action limit. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/610370, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1414426
