### Who knows whom and greatest fears
- Heather Granville — knows Jenny (Heather's older sister, Caitlyn, is Jenny's friend), Flash and Professor Longfellow (from school), and Vivian (a long-time friend of Heather's mother). Greatest fear: that she isn't actually perfect after all.
- Jenny LeClerc — her only real friend is Caitlyn, Heather's older sister. Knows Ox (grew up a few doors apart on Mulberry Lane) and Madame Zostra (from the library). Greatest fear: being trapped in a crowd or lost out in the open.
- Ox Bellows — has known Jenny since childhood on Mulberry Lane; met Professor Longfellow at Greenwich University; has known Father Rhinehardt all his life and confesses to him. Greatest fear: the dark.
- Darrin "Flash" Williams — knows Jenny from the neighbourhood; has known Madame Zostra all his life (he is her nephew); Zoe is his little cousin, whom he has met only a couple of times. Greatest fear: being caught by the "Not Good Thing" he feels coming after him.
- Vivian Lopez — friend of Heather's mother, Sarah; knows Madame Zostra and Father Rhinehardt as customers of her bookstore, Something Written; babysits Missy Dubourde at least once a month. Greatest fear: fire (and her fascination with it).
- Madame Zostra — knows Vivian and Father Rhinehardt from Vivian's bookstore; Flash is her nephew; sees Jenny regularly at the library; Zoe's mother comes to her for tarot readings. Greatest fear: death, especially her own (seeing it in the cards).
- Missy Dubourde — knows Peter from school and Father Rhinehardt from Sunday school; lives in Brandon's neighbourhood and Brandon delivers her family's paper, but she doesn't really know him. Greatest fear: dead things coming back to life and hunting her.
- Zoe Ingstrom — Flash is her cousin, but she doesn't know him well; her mother visits Madame Zostra for tarot readings; her family sometimes camps with Brandon's family. Greatest fear: the boogeyman.
- Peter Akimoto — earns extra money looking after Professor Longfellow's yard; knows Missy from school. Greatest fear: being trapped somewhere with no escape.
- Brandon Jaspers — sometimes sees Zoe's family when camping; delivers the newspaper to Professor Longfellow and to Missy's family. Greatest fear: puppets, particularly clown puppets.
- Professor Longfellow — knows Ox, Flash and Heather from the university; Brandon is his paperboy; Peter mows his yard and does other chores. Greatest fear: losing everything he has and proving no better than his father.
- Father Rhinehardt — knows Vivian and Madame Zostra from the Something Written bookstore; knows Ox from hearing his confessions; knows Missy from Sunday school. Greatest fear: going mad.

> Note: Small inconsistencies are as printed: Heather's entry says she knows Professor Longfellow "from school" while his says "from the university"; Missy's entry says she doesn't really know Brandon although they share a neighbourhood. These are flavour, not rules.
