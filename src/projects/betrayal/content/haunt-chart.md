### How to use the chart
- Find the row for the room where the haunt was revealed and the column for the omen card that revealed it; the cell is the haunt number.
- Look that haunt number up in the traitor table to find out who becomes the traitor.
- Entries marked * pick an explorer by a trait value. Tie-break for those (footnote): if two explorers tie in that trait and one of them is the haunt revealer, choose the haunt revealer. If neither is the haunt revealer, choose the one closest to the haunt revealer's left. (This footnote is printed under both halves of the traitor table.)

> Note: the footnote is worded for a tie between two explorers; applying it to a tie among three or more (revealer wins if tied, otherwise the tied explorer nearest the revealer's left) is interpretation. It also says nothing about how its first clause fits entries marked "(except for the haunt revealer)"; presumably the revealer is simply not eligible there, but that is interpretation.
> Resolution (community): both readings are the accepted ones. A tie among any number of explorers goes to the revealer if the revealer is one of them. Otherwise it goes to the first tied explorer going left (clockwise) from the revealer. In entries marked "(except for the haunt revealer)", the revealer is left out of the comparison entirely, so the footnote's first clause never applies, even when the revealer's trait value is the same. Several independent BGG answers agree, and the 3rd-edition rulebook words the tie-break as "the player closest to the haunt revealer (in turn order)". Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2326732, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1645119, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2936819
- "(except for the haunt revealer)" means the haunt revealer is not eligible for that selection.
- Some entries name an explorer followed by "or" and a trait rule (e.g. "Father Rhinehardt (Gardening) or highest Sanity*").

> Note: the page does not explain these entries. The likely reading (interpretation, not printed) is that the named explorer is the traitor if in play, and otherwise the trait rule applies.
> Resolution (community): that reading is the accepted one. If the named explorer is in the game, that explorer is the traitor; if not, the trait rule picks the traitor. The 1st-edition chart uses the same format. Only one BGG answer states it outright, but nobody challenged it and other posts take it for granted. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/577854, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1135420, https://web.archive.org/web/20060614170302/http://www.wizards.com:80/AvalonHill/rules/BHotH_Traitors_110405.pdf

> Note: these pages do not explain the parenthetical after a named explorer (e.g. "(Gardening)"). It appears to be that explorer's hobby, which would identify the character card side; that reading is mine, not printed.
> Resolution (community): it is that explorer's hobby, the story reason the haunt picks them, and it has no rules effect. The 1st-edition chart spells one out as "(gardening expert)", and every hobby on the 2nd-edition chart matches one in explorers.md. Each name appears on only one side of one card, so the hobby isn't needed to identify the explorer. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1281294, https://web.archive.org/web/20060614170302/http://www.wizards.com:80/AvalonHill/rules/BHotH_Traitors_110405.pdf
> Note: these pages contain no other instructions beyond the two tables and the * footnote; the chart's row/column lookup is implied by its layout.
> Resolution (unresolved): not a rules question (the lookup is explained in the rulebook, p. 15), so there is nothing to settle; not researched.

> Note: entry 49, "Heather Granville or highest Knowledge*", is printed without a hobby in parentheses, unlike the other named-explorer entries.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling or erratum found. The 1st edition printed the entry the same way, without a hobby, so this is not a slip introduced in the 2nd edition. Since the hobby has no rules effect, its absence changes nothing. Searched: the 1st-edition FAQ and Traitor's Tome, the Widow's Walk FAQ and every thread in the BGG Betrayal rules forum. Source: https://web.archive.org/web/20060614170302/http://www.wizards.com:80/AvalonHill/rules/BHotH_Traitors_110405.pdf
> Note: the book does not say what happens when the named explorer in a "Name or ..." entry is the haunt revealer; the entry is printed as above with no exception.
