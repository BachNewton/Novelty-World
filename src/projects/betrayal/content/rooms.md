# Room tiles

Every room tile of the base game (44 tiles, plus the three-room Entrance Hall / Foyer / Grand Staircase starting tile) and the Widow's Walk expansion (20 tiles). Each tile is described with its name reading upright; edges are top, right, bottom and left in that orientation.

- **Floors** come from the tile's back. With Widow's Walk, any tile that may go on the upper floor may also go on the roof (printed on the Roof Landing tile), even where the back doesn't show the roof.
- **Windows** follow the rulebook's list of rooms with windows (p. 23) and the Widow's Walk glossary update, which adds the Drawing Room and Sewing Room. Window-like art on other tiles is decoration.
- **Outside** marks the rooms that count as outside when a haunt refers to outside rooms (the Widow's Walk glossary update in widows-walk-rules.md, which gives the first full list; the base rules call the Patio outside).
- **Symbols** are the card symbols: event (spiral), item (bull's skull), omen (raven). **Marks** are other printed marks: an asterisk after the name (a special room, see rules.md), the Widow's Walk dumbwaiter icon, and the "?" symbol (draw a card of any type).

## Locked Room
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Floors: Roof, Upper, Basement
- Doors: top, right, bottom (the walls are lined with many decorative doors and hatches, but only these three sit in the yellow door frames at the middle of an edge; the left wall has none)
- Windows: none
- Symbols: event
- Marks: none
- Text: Place a Lock token on every door of this room. To go in or out through a door that has a Lock, try a Knowledge roll of 3+ to take the Lock off.

> Note: the tile doesn't say whether, after failing to remove one door's Lock, you may try another door's Lock in the same turn.
> Resolution (community): one Lock roll per turn in all. The rulebook bars making the same roll twice in a turn, and the Locks are one room's effect, so a different door is not a different roll. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2200229

## Drawing Room
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Floors: Roof, Upper
- Doors: top, right, bottom, left
- Windows: top
- Symbols: none of the three card symbols; a yellow question mark instead (see Marks)
- Marks: yellow "?" symbol (any card type); dumbwaiter icon
- Text: When this room is discovered, draw 1 card, of whichever type you choose.

> Note: if you choose an omen here and the haunt roll starts the haunt, neither the base haunt chart nor the Widow's Walk chart has a Drawing Room row, so the chart gives no haunt.
> Resolution (official): discard room tiles from the room stack until you find one with an omen symbol, place it in the house, and use that room to pick the haunt. The FAQ doesn't say where in the house the room is placed. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

## Rookery
- Set: Widow's Walk
- Floors: Roof
- Doors: right, left
- Windows: none
- Symbols: omen
- Marks: none
- Text: When this room is discovered, look through the room stack, pick a room tile, and place it in the house. Then shuffle the stack.

## Gymnasium
- Set: base
- Floors: Upper, Basement
- Doors: right, bottom
- Windows: none
- Symbols: omen
- Marks: none
- Text: Once per game, if your turn ends here, place your explorer token here and gain 1 Speed.

> Note: the printed tile has no explorer-token step. The Widow's Walk FAQ corrects the Chapel, Gymnasium, Larder and Library tiles to add "put your explorer token here and" after "if you end your turn here", and the text above includes it. "Once per game" is per explorer: each explorer may gain from this room once per game, and the token marks who already has.
> Resolution (official): the Widow's Walk FAQ errata adds the token step; the 1st-edition FAQ rules that each character gets each of these rooms' boosts once per game, and the Widow's Walk rules list this room among those that take an explorer token. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2016/downloads/widows_walk_faq.pdf, https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

## Vault
- Set: base
- Floors: Upper, Basement
- Doors: top
- Windows: none
- Symbols: event, item, item
- Marks: none
- Text: You may try a Knowledge roll of 6+ to open the vault and empty it.

> Note: the tile doesn't say what "empty it" gives you, or how many cards. The tile shows an event symbol and two item symbols, and the rulebook's symbol rule would have the discoverer draw all three.
> Resolution (community): the two item symbols are the vault's contents. Discovering the Vault draws only its event card; the explorer who opens it draws 2 item cards, and the Vault Empty token goes on the tile. The 1st-edition FAQ says that on the turn you discover the Vault you draw an event card and may then try to open it, and BGG players describe opening it as drawing two item cards. The 1st-edition tile text is the same ("open and empty the vault"). Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf, https://daviau1.rssing.com/chan-5960803/article58898.html (BGG thread "Purpose of Vault Empty token?")

## Gallery
- Set: base
- Floors: Upper
- Doors: top, bottom
- Windows: none
- Symbols: omen
- Marks: none
- Text: You may choose to fall down to the Ballroom, if the Ballroom is in the house. If you do, take 1 die of physical damage.

> Note: the tile doesn't say whether falling to the Ballroom costs a space of movement, or whether you can fall with no movement left.
> Resolution (official): falling spends no movement. The 1st-edition FAQ, answering a question about climbing up with the Rope, takes as given that going down from the Gallery or the Collapsed Room doesn't take a move, and the 2nd-edition rulebook says the same of the Collapsed Room (p. 7). It follows that you may fall during your move even with no movement left, and keep moving afterwards with whatever movement you had. Climbing back up costs a move. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

The rulebook says the stairs reach the Foyer through a secret door, but not which frame that is, nor whether the other frame can join a second basement room.
> Resolution (community): the frame at the head of the stairs, beside "Leads to and from Foyer", is the secret door to the Foyer and takes no tile; the tile joins the basement only at the foot of the stairs. The 1st-edition tile printed only that one door, and the 2nd edition added the frame at the head of the stairs.
