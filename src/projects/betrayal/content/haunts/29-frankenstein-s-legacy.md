# 29 — Frankenstein's Legacy

Summary: Following an old lab notebook, the traitor has brought a stitched-together corpse to life, and Frankenstein's Monster hunts the heroes.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Put the large circular Frankenstein's Monster token in either the Research Laboratory or the Operating Laboratory. If neither room is in the house, search the room stack for either tile, put it on the upper level with the Frankenstein's Monster token on it, then shuffle that stack.

### Monsters
- Frankenstein's Monster — Speed 3 · Might 8
  - It must always move at full speed toward the closest hero. If several heroes are equally far away, the traitor decides which one it moves toward.
  - It must make an attack during its turn if it can.
> Note: Only Speed and Might are printed for Frankenstein's Monster.
> Resolution (official): the Monster has no Sanity or Knowledge, and the rulebook bars attacking with a trait the opponent doesn't have, so Sanity attacks (for example with the Ring) can't be made against it. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf (rulebook, Make an Attack)

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- When Frankenstein's Monster makes an attack roll, it adds 2 to the result. It gets no bonus when defending.
- Frankenstein's Monster is immune to Speed attacks (such as those using the Revolver and Dynamite).
> Note: This contradicts Secrets of Survival, where the heroes throw Torches with a Speed attack.
> Resolution (designer): the immunity is meant to cover only other Speed attacks, such as the Revolver and Dynamite. Torch throws still work, and they hit without stunning. This is the designer's clarification, not printed errata, so the rule text is left as printed. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/572058 (reply by Bruce Glassco), https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1503814, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1722092
- If the traitor beats a hero by 2 or more with a Might attack, the traitor may steal a Torch token from that hero instead of dealing damage. (See "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook.) A successfully stolen Torch is automatically destroyed.
> Note: "If you beat a hero" — "you" could mean the traitor's explorer, the Monster the traitor controls, or both; the book does not say.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The 1st-edition revised tome has the same wording. Searched: BGG haunt 29 threads (572058, 1722092, 305246, 1503814, 100335, 1371041, 1782209). Source: http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes consider the Monster an abomination and will try to kill it. They also know the Monster is vulnerable to fire.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside 5 pentagonal item tokens to represent Torches.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0. It tracks Torch hits.

### Rules

#### How to Kill the Monster
- There are two ways to kill Frankenstein's Monster.
1. Death by Fire
   - Go to the Charred Room, Furnace Room, Pentagram Room or Kitchen to find and light a torch. Put a Torch token on that explorer's character card.
   - There is no limit to the number of Torches the heroes can find, but each explorer can carry only one at a time.
   - A hero in the Monster's room, or in an adjacent room with a connecting door, may attempt a Speed attack to throw the Torch at the Monster.
     - If the hero defeats the Monster, it takes one Torch hit: advance the Turn/Damage track by 1 and discard the Torch token. This attack does not stun the Monster.
     - If the Monster defeats the hero, the hero loses the Torch; return it to the pile of set-aside tokens.
   - Each hero may throw only one Torch per turn.
   - The Monster is killed once it has taken Torch hits equal to the number of players.
2. Death by Falling
   - The Monster always has to move toward the closest explorer it can attack.
   - Lure it into the Tower or the Chasm. A hero may then attempt a Might roll of 6+ in one of those rooms to push the Monster to its death.
   - A hero may attempt this roll once during his or her turn.

> Note: "Pentagram Room" is printed here (not "Pentagram Chamber", the name used elsewhere).
> Resolution (unresolved): no errata found. The 1st-edition revised Secrets of Survival and the 2020 reprint also say "Pentagram Room"; the Pentagram Chamber is the only such tile. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf, https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf
> Note: Only 5 Torch tokens are set aside, yet the book says there is no limit to the number of Torches; presumably a discarded Torch token returns to the set-aside pile and can be found again.
> Resolution (community): yes. A thrown or destroyed Torch goes back to the pool and can be lit again, because the book says there is no limit to the number of Torches the heroes can find. One well-referenced answer went unchallenged, and the asker accepted it. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1722092
> Note: The book does not say what a tie on the Torch Speed attack does, or whether the Might roll requires the hero and the Monster to be in the same room.
> Resolution (unresolved): no haunt-specific ruling found. The general rule is that on a tied attack no one is hurt, but no source says whether the hero keeps the Torch. One answer reads the push roll as made on the hero's own turn, in the Tower or Chasm where the Monster has been lured. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf (rulebook, Make an Attack), https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/305246

### Heroes win when
- The Monster is dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has animated Frankenstein's Monster and will command it to kill the heroes to test its strength.
- The Monster is very tough and best avoided, but it has one weakness: fire.
