# 66 — The Cardinal Sins

Summary: The traitor, Cardinal Sinn, means to be elected Pope by winning the votes of Electors and reaching the roof; the heroes must win the Electors over first or kill him.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- Raise the traitor's traits by a total of 5 points, split as they like (e.g., 2 Sanity and 3 Knowledge).
- Put the traitor's figure on any landing.

### Allies
- Electors (small monster tokens; no stats printed on this page). Each Elector has a color tied to a sin, as shown in the Electors table.
  - Electors are neutral voters, won over by either side; they never attack.

### Rules
#### How to Denounce a Sin
- Once during their turn, when the traitor is in a room with an Elector, instead of attacking they may denounce the sin of that Elector's color. Steps:
  1. Denounce the hero closest to the traitor (the traitor chooses if there is a tie), in a booming, holy voice, with "I excommunicate you because of your sin of" followed by the name of the sin.
  2. Attack that hero using the trait listed next to the Elector's sin in the Electors table.
  3. If the traitor deals damage to that hero, the traitor also steals a random item card from the hero.
  4. If the traitor dealt damage to the hero, put the Elector on the traitor's character card.
- If the traitor has the Ceremonial Robe card, they may instead pick the two closest heroes and attack both of them the same way. Even if damage is dealt to both, the traitor gains only one Elector.

#### Electors
| Elector | Sin | Attack using |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Wrath | Might |
| Orange | Gluttony | Sanity |
| Yellow | Sloth | Speed |
| Green | Envy | the hero's lowest trait |
| Blue | Greed | the hero's highest trait |
| Purple | Pride | Knowledge |
| Magenta | Lust | your highest trait |

> Note: "your highest trait" (Magenta/Lust) means the traitor's own highest trait.

#### Special Attack Rules
- Until the traitor has four or more Electors, the traitor cannot take mental or physical damage.
- Before that point, though, a hero may steal an item card from the traitor whenever that hero would otherwise have dealt the traitor 2 or more points of physical damage with an attack.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or
- The traitor is on the roof (to send up the white smoke) and has four or more Electors on their character card.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to stop the traitor becoming Pope.
- Electors (small monster tokens) are around the house, each with a vote in the election; the traitor must win them over by punishing the heroes for their sins.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Put one small monster token of each color face down, representing seven Electors, in rooms the heroes do not currently occupy.
- No two of these rooms may be adjacent, even between floors, unless that is impossible; in that case come as close to that as you can.
### Rules
#### How to stop the Cardinal from becoming Pope
- The heroes need a majority of the Electors to vote against the traitor.
- When you are in a room with an Elector that has not yet voted, you may attempt a Sanity roll of 6+ to gain that Elector's vote. On a success, take the Elector token off the room tile and put it on your character card.
#### Special attack rules
- Unless the traitor has four or more Electors, the traitor cannot take mental or physical damage. You can still make attacks to steal item cards.
- If you die, give all of your Electors to the traitor.
### Heroes win when
- The heroes have a total of four Electors, or
- The traitor is killed.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is Cardinal Sinn, who thinks the heroes have been very sinful.
- To become Pope, the traitor needs four Electors and must get to the roof.
> Note: The rule text frames the goal as a "majority" of Electors; with seven Electors, four is that majority, matching the win condition.
