# 95 — Nanny, Interrupted

Summary: The traitor has become the explorers' ghostly Nanny, who is shrinking the heroes back into children and wants them rounded up in the Nursery; the heroes must all escape to outside the locked front door.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but has turned traitor.
- If any of your traits are below their starting values, raise them to their starting values.

### Rules
#### Special attacks
- You can't make normal physical attacks. Instead, on your turn you may make one of the following attacks. None of them can hurt you.
- **"Reminisce about days gone by":** Against a hero on your floor who isn't yet a baby, make a Knowledge attack. If you succeed, deal mental damage to the hero. If any of the hero's traits drops to the skull symbol, the hero becomes a baby.
- **"Throw the baby over your shoulder":** When you are in a room with a baby on your turn, or when a baby tries to leave your room on any turn, you may make a Speed attack against that hero. It deals no damage. If you win, you pick up the hero: put their figure on your character card. You can carry several heroes at once.
- **"Use the baby's middle name":** When a baby is in your line of sight, or you are carrying one, you may sternly say the hero's full name from the Full Names table followed by "Go to your room!", then make a Sanity attack, adding 1 die to your roll for each baby you are holding. If you win, place the baby in the Nursery.

#### Special damage
- Whenever you take damage, you must also drop, if possible, a number of item cards, omen cards and/or heroes (carried babies) equal to the amount of damage you take.

#### Special movement
- You tower over the kids: add 1 die to your Speed when moving.
- You have a key to the front door: you can enter or leave the Entrance Hall through that door.
- You can leave the house by spending 1 space of movement to jump out of a window or an outside room on any floor. If you leave from a room on the upper floor or the roof, put your figure outside, next to the room on the same side of the house that is closest to the front door. Jumping from the roof deals 5 dice of physical damage, from the upper floor 3 dice, from the ground floor 1 die. You take no damage if you have the Rope. The list of windows and outside rooms is in this expansion's rule sheet.

The FAQ adds the sentence after the first sentence of the first Special Movement Rules bullet (its wording says to put "your explorer token" outside; this haunt uses figures). Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
- Once outside the house, you can walk around the perimeter of the ground floor. Each outside edge of a tile counts as its own room.
- From outside, you can enter a ground floor window or outside room by spending 1 space of movement.
- Line of sight passes through windows and outside rooms on the ground floor, as if the perimeter areas were rooms.

### Traitor wins when
- All heroes are in the Nursery and have all been turned back into babies.

### What you know about the heroes
- The naughty children are out of bed and must be returned to the Nursery to sleep. They seem to have grown up, but they'll be child-sized again soon. They'll certainly try to escape, but once caught they'll realize their Nanny knows best.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside the Item Pile tokens.
### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- At the start of your turn, take 1 die of physical damage and 1 die of mental damage. If you start your turn in the Nursery, take 2 dice of each type instead.
#### Special damage rules
- When you take mental damage, take all of it to Knowledge. When you take physical damage, take all of it to Might.
- When you take Might damage, raise your Speed by the amount your Might went down.
- If any of your traits drops to the skull symbol, you are a baby for the rest of the game. As a baby:
  - You can still move at least 1 space even if your Speed reaches the skull symbol.
  - You get a 0 on trait rolls for any trait that is at the skull symbol.
  - You can no longer take damage.
  - You may only communicate in baby talk.
#### Special attack rules
- If you are a baby, you may attack the Nanny using Speed.
  - If you lose, you take no damage, but the Nanny picks you up.
  - If you win, the Nanny takes no damage, but drops a number of carried items, omens, and/or heroes equal to the difference between the rolls. You may throw any of them into any adjacent room or rooms if you like. For freed heroes, this doesn't count as exiting the room.
  - (The book then repeats: if you lose, you take no damage, but the Nanny picks you up.)
- If the Nanny has picked you up, the first thing you must do on your turn is attack the Nanny to make her drop you. You may use your normal Might attack, or the Speed attack above even though it deals no damage. If you defeat her, put your figure in any adjacent room and lose 1 Speed. If you don't defeat her, your turn is over.
> Note: The book doesn't say which trait this escape attack uses. Only babies are explicitly allowed to attack the Nanny with Speed, and only babies are described as being picked up, so this is presumably that Speed attack, but it is printed only as "attack the Nanny." The text above carries the official correction.
> Resolution (official): errata. Either: your normal Might attack, or the baby's Speed attack, though the Speed attack deals no damage. The FAQ adds this sentence after the escape-attack sentence. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
#### Special movement rules
- You can spend 1 space of movement to crawl out a window or outside feature from the roof (taking 5 dice of physical damage), an upper floor (3 dice), or the ground floor (1 die). If you leave from a room on the upper floor or the roof, put your figure outside, next to the room on the same side of the house that is farthest from the front door. You take no damage if you have the Rope (or if you are a baby, obviously). This expansion's rule sheet lists the windows and outside rooms.
  > Note: the printed rule doesn't say where a hero lands when crawling out from the upper floor or the roof. The second sentence above is official errata.
  > Resolution (official): errata. You land outside next to the room on the same side of the house that is farthest from the front door (the Nanny lands closest to it). The FAQ adds the sentence after the first sentence of the first Special Movement Rules bullet (its wording says to put "your explorer token" outside; this haunt uses figures). Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
- Once outside the house, you can walk around the perimeter of the ground floor; each outside edge of a tile counts as its own room. You can enter a ground floor window or outside room by spending 1 space of movement. Line of sight passes through windows and outside rooms on the ground floor as if the perimeter areas were rooms.
- You can hide in dumbwaiters: spend 1 space of movement to move from a room into its dumbwaiter, and put your figure on the dumbwaiter symbol [dumbwaiter symbol]. While in a dumbwaiter you roll 2 additional dice on defense. When you leave the dumbwaiter, you can spend 1 space of movement to re-enter the room, or 2 spaces of movement to move to the landing either one floor up or one floor down.
- If any of your traits is at the skull symbol and you need to roll that trait to enter, cross, or exit a room feature, you automatically succeed. Nothing stops a baby who wants to get somewhere dangerous.
#### Special item and omen rules
- Not counting cards that cannot be dropped, you can carry at most one item card or omen card for each point of Might you have (minimum of one). If you have more than that, you must drop the excess in an Item Pile.
### Heroes win when
- None of you is carried by the Nanny, and all of you are outside the front door, next to the Entrance Hall, which is locked.

### What they know about the other side
- The Nanny is a strict, orderly caretaker with odd magical powers. She wants her naughty children (the heroes) rounded up back in the Nursery.
