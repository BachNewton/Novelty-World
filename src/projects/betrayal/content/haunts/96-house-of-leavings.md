# 96 — House of Leavings

Summary: The heroes have been pulled into an alternate-dimension version of the house, rearranged and haunted by trapped spirits and possibly an imagined Minotaur; they must all gather in the Abandoned Room to escape.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but has turned traitor.
- Put your figure and the large circular Demon Lord token (representing the Minotaur) in the Grand Staircase.
- Set aside small yellow monster tokens (representing Claw Marks), one per explorer.
- Reduce your Speed to its lowest value above the skull symbol.

### Monsters
- Minotaur (large circular Demon Lord token) — no stats printed
  - The Minotaur doesn't get a monster turn; it moves only as described under "On each hero's turn".
  - The Minotaur can't attack the heroes.
  - When a hero attacks the Minotaur, it defends using your Sanity value (see Special attacks).

### Rules
#### On each hero's turn
- At the end of each hero's turn, if that hero is not within line of sight of the Minotaur, that hero takes 1 die of mental damage.
- After the hero has checked whether the Minotaur is within their line of sight, you may move the Minotaur to any room within **your** line of sight.
- You may put a Claw Mark in the room the Minotaur just left. You have only a limited number of Claw Marks, and once a Claw Mark has been placed in a room it can't be moved.

#### Special attacks
- You and the Minotaur can't attack the heroes.
- The heroes can make Sanity attacks against the Minotaur, which defends using your Sanity value. If the Minotaur defeats the hero, it deals mental damage to the hero, and in addition you can move that hero to any room within that hero's line of sight.
- Your traits can't drop below their lowest value above the skull symbol. If a trait dropping to the skull symbol would kill you, that trait instead drops to its lowest value above the skull symbol, and you move to the Upper Landing.

#### Special movement
- Every room you discover counts as "new" to you and must be discovered normally, as though you had never visited it.
- If an explorer draws the Abandoned Room from the room stack while discovering a room, and it would be placed adjacent to a room with a Claw Mark, shuffle the Abandoned Room back into the room stack and draw another. If the explorer draws it again immediately afterward, place it regardless of where the Claw Marks are.
> Note: The book doesn't say how a room you discover "as new" interacts with rooms already on the board; it only says such rooms must be discovered normally.

#### Special omen rule
- You can't pick up, steal or otherwise carry the omen that started the haunt.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What you know about the heroes
- They are trying to find their way out of the house. You have a Minotaur to stop them — or perhaps you don't.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Put all heroes' figures in the Entrance Hall.
- Put the undiscovered room tiles back in the box; you won't need them.
- Set aside the Abandoned Room tile.
- Pick up all discovered non-landing room tiles and split them into two stacks of equal size. Shuffle the Abandoned Room tile into one of the stacks, then put that stack under the other one, so the Abandoned Room is somewhere in the bottom half of the combined pile.
- Put the traitor's explorer token on the omen that started the haunt.
### Monsters
- Minotaur — no stats printed on this page
  - Heroes are not sure it really exists. See Special attack rules for the Sanity attacks heroes may make against it.
### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- Only the hero carrying the omen that started the haunt can attack the traitor.
- If you keep the Minotaur in sight (even right next to you), it won't scare you to death. You can make Sanity attacks against the Minotaur if it is in your line of sight. If you defeat the Minotaur, you can move it to any room within your line of sight.
#### Special movement rules
- Every room you discover counts as "new" to you and must be discovered normally, as though you'd never visited it.
- The Minotaur may leave Claw Marks (small yellow monster tokens) around the house. If an explorer draws the Abandoned Room from the room stack while discovering a room and it would be placed adjacent to a room with a Claw Mark, shuffle the Abandoned Room back into the room stack. If an explorer draws it immediately after that, place it regardless of where the Claw Marks are.
> Note: "Draws it immediately thereafter" is not defined further; read literally it means the very next room draw (by any explorer) after the reshuffle.
### Heroes win when
- Any hero ends their turn with all living heroes in the Abandoned Room.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor wants to keep the heroes from escaping the house and may have conjured a ghostly Minotaur to stop them — or so the heroes think; it's hard to tell what is real.
