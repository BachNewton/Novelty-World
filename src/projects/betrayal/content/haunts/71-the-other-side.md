# 71 — The Other Side

Summary: The heroes are Ghosts who have always lived in this house; the traitor is trying to drive them out, and they must kill the traitor so it joins them in the afterlife.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Set aside matching explorer tokens for each hero: one each with four or five heroes, two each with three heroes, three each with two heroes.
- Raise your Sanity and Knowledge to their maximum values.
- Ghosthunting equipment: for each hero, search the item stack and its discard pile for an item card from this list, checking in this order: Chalk, Device, Ceremonial Robes, Locket, Blueprint. If a hero holds the item you are looking for, take it from them. Then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.

### Rules
#### How to Put a Ghost to Rest
- To put a Ghost to rest, take something precious from that Ghost and deliver it to a room with an omen symbol [raven symbol]. You must do this at least four times in total, and at least once for every Ghost.
- When you steal an item card that belongs to a Ghost, put that hero's explorer token on the item card. On your turn, you may carry that item to a room in the house with an omen symbol [raven symbol]; put that Ghost's explorer token on the room to show the Ghost has been put to rest there. The Ghost then lowers its traits to their lowest values.
- A room that already has an explorer token on it cannot be used to put another Ghost to rest.
#### Special Attack Rules
- You may attack Ghosts only with Sanity or Knowledge. On your turn, you may attack each Ghost in your room once.
- Whatever trait you attack with, a Ghost damaged by your attack loses only Sanity.
- When you defeat a Ghost, you may gain 1 Sanity or 1 Knowledge and take one of the Ghost's items. This includes items that cannot normally be lost, dropped, or stolen.
- If a Ghost attacks you with Speed, Might, or Knowledge and wins, you take no damage unless your Sanity is at its minimum value.

### Traitor wins when
- You have put all the Ghosts to rest.

### What they know about the other side
- The spirits of the house (the heroes, who are the Ghosts) are unsettled and angry. They may try to hurt you and will not let you leave. You only get out alive by appeasing the Ghosts of the dead who control the house.

> Note: This page never states outright that the heroes are the Ghosts; it follows from "that hero's explorer token" being "the explorer token for that Ghost", and the heroes' book says so directly.
> Note: The rest rule says the item goes "to a room with an omen symbol", while the step-by-step bullet says "to a room in the house" without repeating the omen requirement. The text above carries the official correction.
> Resolution (official): errata. The room must have an omen symbol; the FAQ changes the bullet to "a room in the house with an omen symbol". Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
> Note: "at least four times, and at least once for each Ghost" versus the win condition "put all the Ghosts to rest"; the book doesn't spell out how these two counts combine.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Any hero who has no item card draws one from the item stack.
- After that, if the heroes together have fewer than four items, the heroes draw one item each (in turn) until they have four items among them.
  > Note: The book says "each hero draws one until you have four items among you"; the drawing order is not specified.
### Rules
- The heroes are Ghosts.
- At the end of your turn, if you did not attack that turn, gain 1 Sanity.
#### Special attack rules
- You may attack the traitor using any trait. The traitor defends with the same trait.
- If you attack with a mental trait, the traitor takes mental damage.
- If you take mental damage, you must lose it from Sanity instead of Knowledge.
  > Note: Printed as "you must lose Sanity instead of Knowledge"; it reads as applying to mental damage (you may not put it on Knowledge). It does not say whether physical damage is affected. The text above carries the official correction.
  > Resolution (official): errata. Only mental damage must come off Sanity; physical damage is taken normally. The FAQ changes the sentence to "If you take mental damage, you must lose Sanity instead of Knowledge." Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
- You cannot die. If damage would drop any of your traits to the skull symbol, lower that trait only to the lowest value above the skull. You may not voluntarily take any action that would lower your traits further.
#### Special movement rules
- You are incorporeal and immune to negative room effects.
- During your movement, you may lose 1 Sanity to pass through any wall into another previously discovered room, as though the two rooms were connected by a door.
#### Special item and omen rules
- You may not discard, drop, trade or steal item cards or omen cards.
  > Note: the printed rule doesn't forbid discarding, so a Ghost could discard an item such as the Dynamite and leave the traitor too few items to win. The text above carries the official correction.
  > Resolution (official): errata. Ghosts can't discard items or omens either; the FAQ changes the paragraph to "You may not discard, drop, trade, or steal item cards or omen cards." Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
### Heroes win when
- The traitor is killed (and has joined you in the afterlife).
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is trying to remove the heroes from their home. The heroes have special ways to stop the traitor because they are Ghosts.
