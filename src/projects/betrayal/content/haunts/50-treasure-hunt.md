# 50 — Treasure Hunt

Summary: A dead man's will leaves his fortune to whoever is clever enough to find it; there is no traitor, and every explorer races (and fights) the others to be the single winner.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor; every player is a hero.
- All the heroes compete to find the hidden treasure.
- The rest of this haunt's rules are in the Secrets of Survival.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- This haunt has no traitor; every player is a hero. All hope to find the hidden treasure, but only one can win.
- The haunt revealer takes 20 small red monster tokens numbered 1 to 20, turns them face down, and mixes them.
- The haunt revealer places one token face down in each room in the house that has a symbol [raven symbol] [horned skull symbol] [spiral symbol]. These tokens are Clues to finding the treasure.
- If there are more eligible rooms than tokens, the haunt revealer spreads the Clue tokens as evenly as possible between floors.
- If there are more Clue tokens than eligible rooms, set the unused tokens aside.
- During the haunt, place a Clue token face down in each newly discovered room with a symbol, after the room has been discovered and any event, item, or omen draws have been resolved.
### Rules
#### Searching for Treasure
- An explorer may search for the treasure in any room that contains a face-down Clue token. To do so, spend 1 space of movement and make a Knowledge roll in that room.
- Add to the roll the total number of Clue tokens the explorer currently has, plus any bonuses granted by specific Clue tokens they are carrying. Traps (tokens 17–20) do not add to the roll.
- An explorer may search several rooms, or the same room several times, during the same turn.
- Consult this table for the result of a search:

| Die Roll | Search Result |
|---|---|
| 0 | Ouch! You tripped. Take 2 dice of physical damage. |
| 1–3 | Nothing. Try again. |
| 4–12 | Take the Clue token and turn it face up. Consult the Clue table for its effect. |
| 13–15 | The house begins to collapse! (see House collapse below) |
| 16 + | You find the treasure! |

#### House collapse (search result 13–15)
- The first time this result is rolled, turn over an unoccupied room tile on the upper floor so its back is showing.
- Each later time, turn over a room adjacent to a room that has already collapsed. The adjacent rooms don't need connecting doors.
- If an occupied room collapses, all explorers in that room are killed.
- Once the entire upper floor has collapsed, start collapsing the ground floor, beginning with the Grand Staircase. Use pentagonal item tokens to mark the Grand Staircase, Foyer, and Entrance Hall as collapsed.
- If the entire first (ground) floor collapses, everyone in the basement dies.
- If the Mystic Elevator's roll would take it to a floor with no uncollapsed rooms that have open doorways, it does not move.
> Note: The first collapse must be an unoccupied upper-floor room; the book doesn't say what happens if no upper-floor room is unoccupied. Later collapses may hit occupied rooms.

#### Clues
- Most Clues give hints about where to look. Whenever an explorer collects a Clue token, consult the Clue table to see whether it grants a bonus in future searches.
- Some Clues are risky and can kill the explorer if they fail the Knowledge check. A few are traps: they grant no bonus and instead have a harmful effect.
- An explorer may keep searching for the treasure in a room even after that room's Clue has been found.
- Explorers can trade Clue tokens just like any other items.
- An explorer who loses a Clue token, through trade or theft, can no longer use its bonus unless they get it back.

> Note: Bonuses like "+7 Knowledge" are printed as bonuses to the search roll's result in the named rooms (the search is a Knowledge roll); "Add your Knowledge/Might/Sanity to the roll" means adding that trait's current value to the search roll total. The book doesn't say whether clue 20's two taken tokens are looked at or added face up, or what happens to the puzzle box token itself if the hero solves it.
> Resolution (community): This note's reading is the majority view. A "+X Knowledge" clue adds X to the result of the search roll rather than adding dice, and "add your Might/Sanity/Knowledge" adds that trait's current value to the result. Most answers across several threads agree, while a minority read "+X Knowledge" as extra dice. #### Special Attack Rules
- Explorers can attack each other and can steal Clue tokens. (See "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook.)
- Damage taken in combat can never reduce any trait to the skull symbol. Damage from other sources, such as traps, can still kill an explorer.
### Heroes win when
- An explorer finds the treasure. Only one hero can win.
