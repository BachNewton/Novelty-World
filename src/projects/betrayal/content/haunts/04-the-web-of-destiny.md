# 4 — The Web of Destiny

Summary: A giant Spider has trapped the haunt revealer in a web and filled them with eggs; the heroes must free the captive, destroy the eggs and escape the house.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- With 5 or 6 players: your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- With 3 or 4 players: your explorer has been eaten by the Spider. Drop all your items and remove your figure from the game.
- Place the large circular Spider token in the room of the explorer who has the Bite card.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip on 1; it tracks time.

### Monsters
- The Spider — stats depend on the turn shown on the Turn/Damage track:

| Turn | Speed | Might | Sanity |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | 2 | 5 |
| 2 | 1 | 2 | 5 |
| 3 | 2 | 4 | 5 |
| 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 |
| 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| 6 | 6 | 7 | 5 |
| 7+ | 6 | 8 | 5 |

  - Mandatory action each turn: the Spider must move toward an explorer who is not the haunt revealer and attack if it can.
  - Neither you nor the Spider can attack the haunt revealer unless the eggs have been destroyed.

### Rules
- The haunt revealer is stuck in a web and infested with giant spider eggs, and cannot move.
#### You Must Do This at the end of each of your turns
- Advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Once per Spider attack, you may reroll every die that came up blank. Example: rolling 4 dice for the Spider with 2 blanks lets you reroll those 2 dice, but only once.

### Traitor wins when
- The Spider's eggs hatch (on turn 9), or
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The haunt revealer is caught in a sticky web, infested with giant spider eggs, and cannot move.

> Note: the haunt revealer's condition (stuck, cannot move) is printed only under "What You Know About the Heroes"; it is repeated under Rules here because it constrains play.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling needed, and none found. The heroes' setup in Secrets of Survival also states the condition ("can't move"). The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome puts it in the same section. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf
> Note: with 3 or 4 players the traitor's explorer is removed, yet the traitor still advances the track "at the end of each of your turns"; the book does not say more about how the traitor's turn works without an explorer.
> Resolution (official): the 2nd-edition rulebook (p. 17) says a traitor removed at the start of the haunt still gets a turn after all the heroes, and that the traitor's player still controls the monsters. So the traitor's player takes a traitor turn with no explorer, advances the track, then runs the Spider's monster turn. On exactly when the track advances, one BGG user reported a publisher email saying it is after the monsters' turn; that report is unverified. Source: https://instructions.hasbro.com/api/download/F3148_en-us_avalon-hill-betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill-second-edition-cooperative-board-game.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1475878

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The haunt revealer is caught in a sticky web and is now the "trapped explorer". The trapped explorer can't move, but can still try to destroy the web by attacking it, and can also use or trade items.
- If the Medical Kit hasn't been found yet, any hero who gets the chance to draw an item card may instead search the item stack and take the Medical Kit card, then shuffle the item stack.
- Set aside triangular Might Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
- The trapped explorer is infested with giant spider eggs, which will eventually hatch.
### Rules
#### How to Destroy the Web and Eggs
- While the eggs have not been destroyed, none of the trapped explorer's traits can drop to the skull symbol.
- Heroes destroy the web by making Might attacks against it. The web defends with Might 4.
  - If the attacker defeats the web, put a Might Roll token in that room instead of dealing damage.
  - If the web defeats the attacker, the attacker takes no damage.
  - When the room holds Might Roll tokens equal to the number of players, the web is destroyed and the trapped explorer can move and act normally.
- A hero who has the Medical Kit and is in the same room as the trapped explorer can attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to destroy the eggs. A hero who has the Healing Salve can use it to destroy the eggs with no Knowledge roll.
> Note: The Healing Salve sentence follows the same-room rule but does not restate it, and does not say whether the Salve is used up.
> Resolution (community, weak): BGG posters read the Salve as also needing the same room as the trapped explorer. One group ruled so, another poster agreed, and a third proposed rewording the text so both items share the same-room condition. No ruling found on whether the Salve is used up. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1320785, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2619849
#### How to Exit the House
- Once the trapped explorer is free and the eggs are destroyed, heroes can leave the house.
- A hero can attempt a Knowledge roll (picking the lock) or a Might roll (breaking the lock) of 6+ to open the front door in the Entrance Hall. On a success, that hero draws an event card and ends their turn.
- On later turns, every hero still alive can exit the house from the Entrance Hall by spending 1 space of movement.
### Heroes win when
- The trapped explorer has been freed, the eggs have been destroyed, and at least one hero exits the house.
### What they know about the other side
- A huge Spider has awakened. It wants to protect the trapped explorer until the eggs hatch.
