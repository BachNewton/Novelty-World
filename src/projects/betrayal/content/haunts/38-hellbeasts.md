# 38 — Hellbeasts

Summary: The traitor has unleashed a swarm of burning Firebats that scorch anyone sharing a room with them; the heroes must perform an exorcism to banish them.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- Gather small red monster tokens to represent Firebats. Put a number of them equal to half the number of players, rounded up, in the room where the haunt was revealed.

### Monsters
- Firebats — Speed 3

### Rules
#### You Must Do This...
- On the monster turn, when the traitor rolls for how many spaces the Firebats move, add as many new Firebat tokens to the room where the haunt was revealed as the result of that roll.
- Firebats cannot move on the turn they appear.

#### Special Attack Rules
- Firebats cannot attack or be attacked.
- After all the Firebats have moved, roll 1 die for each Firebat token that is in the same room as one or more heroes. Every hero in that room takes physical damage equal to the result.
- The Armor card can prevent only 1 point of that damage.
- Firebats do not slow heroes moving through their room.

> Note: The book does not say whether the 1 die per Firebat is rolled separately for each Firebat (each hero in the room taking each result) or summed per room; read literally, each Firebat's die damages every hero in its room. It also does not say whether the Armor's 1-point limit applies per die or per monster turn.
> Resolution (community): Two independent answers read it literally: after moving, the traitor rolls one die per Firebat, and each Firebat's die damages every hero in its room. This is not an attack. No ruling was found on whether the Armor's 1-point limit applies per die or per monster turn. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1664605, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/76312

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes' bodies contain the blood the Firebats need to breed.
- The heroes will try to find a way to stop the traitor.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Perform the Exorcism
- The heroes must make a number of successful "exorcism rolls" equal to the number of players before the Firebats kill them all.
- Each exorcism roll needs a specific room or item and is either a Sanity roll or a Knowledge roll. A hero can make only one exorcism roll during their turn.
- Sanity roll of 5+: while in the Chapel, Crypt, or Pentagram Chamber, or while carrying the Holy Symbol or Ring.
- Knowledge roll of 5+: while in the Library or Research Laboratory, or while carrying the Book or Crystal Ball.
- After each successful exorcism roll, put a Sanity Roll token or Knowledge Roll token (matching the trait used) on the room tile or item card used for that part of the exorcism.
- Once any hero has successfully used an item or room for an exorcism roll, no hero can use that item or room for an exorcism again. (Example from the book: after a successful Sanity roll in the Chapel, no one can use the Chapel again.)
- When the heroes have placed a number of these tokens equal to the number of players, the Firebats are banished.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Firebats cannot attack or be attacked.
- Firebats do not slow heroes moving through their room.
- The traitor tells the heroes how much damage they take for being in a room with one or more Firebat tokens.

### Heroes win when
- The heroes successfully perform the exorcism and drive the Firebats from the house.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor commands a swarm of Firebats and wants every hero dead.
- The Firebats cannot attack, but they burn heroes who stay in a room with them.
