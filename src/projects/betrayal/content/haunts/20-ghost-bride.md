# 20 — Ghost Bride

Summary: A ghost bride has chosen one of the explorers as her new groom; the heroes must find her real groom's body and her wedding ring and bring both to her in the Chapel to lay her to rest.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Put a small magenta monster token (representing the Ghost Bride) in your room. You move it on the monster turn.
- The Ghost Bride chooses as her groom the hero carrying the Ring. If that hero is female, she instead chooses the oldest male hero. Announce who the chosen groom is.
  - If there are no male heroes, pick a male character at random from the box and put his figure in the Entrance Hall. That character cannot move or take any actions during the scenario, but he can be attacked.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0. It tracks time once the wedding starts.
- If the Chapel is not in the house, search the room stack for it and put it in the house, as hard for the heroes to reach as possible. Then shuffle the room stack.
### Monsters
- Ghost Bride — her traits depend on the total number of players:

| Players | Speed | Sanity |
|---|---|---|
| 3–4 players | 4 | 6 |
| 5–6 players | 5 | 7 |

  - She can move through walls as if they were connecting doors, but not through floors or ceilings.
  - She cannot be damaged or stunned by any means except Sanity attacks from the Ring.
  - If she attacks and is defeated, her traits are not reduced.
### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Ghost Bride makes Sanity attacks, which inflict mental damage on anyone except the chosen groom.
- When she attacks the chosen groom, the mental damage she deals lowers his Might, as follows:

| Mental Damage | Might |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | −1 |
| 3–4 | −2 |
| 5+ | −3 |

#### How to Get the Ghost Bride Married
- Kill the chosen groom. He then becomes a ghost under your control, and he drops all his items, the Ring included.
- Put the chosen groom and the Ghost Bride in the Chapel. This starts the wedding.
- At the end of each of your turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number, starting at 1. The wedding is complete on Turn 3.
### Traitor wins when
- The Ghost Bride marries her chosen groom.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to stop the wedding.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If the Crypt is not in the house, search the room stack for it, place it in the basement, then shuffle the room stack.
- Place a pentagonal item token in the Crypt. It represents the Corpse.

### Rules
- The heroes must learn the groom's name, find and dig up his buried body, and bring the Corpse and the Ring to the Chapel.

#### How to Stop the Wedding
- The steps below must be completed in the order listed. Each hero may attempt only one step per turn.
  1. Knowledge roll of 5+, made while in the Bedroom, Dining Room or Library, or while carrying the Book (the bride's old diary). Success means the real groom's name is figured out.
  2. After step 1: Knowledge roll of 4+ in the Crypt. Success locates the real groom's body.
  3. After the body is located: Might roll of 4+ in the Crypt. Success disinters the body.
  4. After the body is disinterred: take the Corpse token and carry it to the Chapel.
     - While carrying the Corpse, entering a room costs 2 spaces of movement.
     - The Corpse token can be traded to another explorer the same way an item is passed.
  5. Carry the Ring to the Chapel. The body and the Ring may reach the Chapel in either order.
- When both the Corpse and the Ring are in the Chapel, the Ghost Bride appears there and is laid to rest.
> Note: The book does not say which hero must make each roll; any hero who meets the location/item condition appears able to attempt a step, one step per hero per turn.
> Resolution (official): Not answered for this haunt specifically, but the official Avalon Hill FAQ (written for the 1st edition, last revised 2005) rules generally that information one explorer learns by a Knowledge roll (its example is a monster's name) is known to every explorer, and that a player may make only one roll of a given scenario category per turn. So any hero who meets a step's location or item condition can attempt the next step, whoever completed the earlier one. No haunt-specific ruling found in BGG Haunt 20 threads. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20151204092838/http://www.wizards.com:80/default.asp?x=ah/faqs/hoth

#### Special Attack Rules
- The Ghost Bride makes Sanity attacks.
- Nothing can damage or stun her, with one exception: Sanity attacks made with the Ring.

### Heroes win when
- They have found the wedding ring (the Ring card) and the real groom's body (the Corpse token) and presented both to the Ghost Bride in the Chapel, before she marries their friend.

### What they know about the other side
- The Ghost Bride believes one of the explorers is her new groom.
