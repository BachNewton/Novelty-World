# 91 — Burn out the Darkness

Summary: The traitor (and possibly several traitors) has become the living Darkness, set on snuffing out the fires the heroes spread through the house to purge it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor. There may be more than one traitor; if so, you are a team of traitors.
- You are now Darkness, and attacking the Darkness is pointless. Remove the clips from your character card. Whenever you need to make a trait roll, your result is 5.
- Discard all your cards.
- Darkness is quiet: for the rest of the game you may only speak in a whisper.
> Note: The Traitor's Tome doesn't say how extra traitors are chosen; the heroes' Setup (Secrets of Survival) covers it.

### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- Once per turn you may attack a hero in your line of sight, using the inkiness of the hero's soul. Make a Sanity attack; your result is 5. If you defeat the hero, the hero takes mental damage. If you don't, your turn ends.
- When a hero would die for any reason, that hero instead joins the Darkness and becomes a traitor, following the rules in the **Right Now** section.
- Monster tokens represent Fire. They do not take turns.

#### Special movement rules
- You can no longer discover rooms.
- Darkness is fast but unpredictable. Each turn, to find your number of spaces of movement, roll dice equal to the highest number next to Speed on your character card, then subtract the number of Fire tokens in your room.
- Face-up Fire tokens count as opponents for the purpose of movement.
- In any room you are in during your turn, you may make one attempt to suppress the Fires there: roll 1 die for each Fire token in that room. If the result is not blank, remove the token if it is face-down; if it is face-up, flip it over.
- Treat face-down room tiles as if they have doors on every side and do not block line of sight. You may spend 1 space of movement to move through a wall into an adjacent room as if the rooms had connecting doors.
> Note: Rooms become face-down when the heroes' Fires destroy them (see How to Burn Down the House in the heroes' rules).

### Traitor wins when
- On your turn all the Fires are extinguished and there are still at least as many face-up rooms as there are traitors, or
- All the heroes are Darkness.

### What they know about the other side
- The Darkness frightens the heroes. They will try to purge it the way ignorant mobs always have: with fire.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- This haunt can have multiple traitors. The hero to the traitor's left chooses a player (other than a traitor) to join the heroes; then the traitor chooses someone to join the traitors. Repeat until every explorer is either a traitor or a hero. The traitors leave the room together.
- If any floor has fewer than five rooms, draw room tiles for that floor and put them into play until every floor has at least five rooms.
- If the Stairs from Basement is not yet in play, search the room stack for it, place it in the house, then shuffle that stack. **For this haunt, the Stairs from Basement is considered a landing.**
- Each hero takes 15 small monster tokens of one color: green, orange or red (each represents that hero's own Fire). Each hero puts one of their monster tokens face-down in their own room.
- The hero to the traitor's left takes the first turn.

### Rules
#### How to Burn Down the House
- When you enter a room, you may put one of your Fire tokens there, face-down.
- At the start of your turn, the Fire spreads: put one of your Fire tokens face-down in each room connected to a room containing one of your face-up Fire tokens, unless that room already has one of your color.
  - Besides normal door connections, the Roof Landing connects to the Upper Landing, which connects to the Grand Staircase; and the Foyer connects to the Stairs from Basement.
- Then destroy each non-landing room that has one of your Fire tokens face-up: remove all figures and tokens from the room (Fire tokens on it go back to their heroes), and flip the room over.
- After flipping those tiles, every hero in a face-down room must put their figure in the nearest face-up room and attempt a Speed roll. Each such hero whose result is lower than 2 plus the number of spaces they moved takes 2 dice of physical damage.
- Finally, flip all your face-down Fire tokens face-up.
> Note: "Remove any figures and tokens on the room ... and flip the room over. Return any non-Fire tokens and figures." The book doesn't say where the non-Fire tokens and figures are returned to; heroes are then moved by the next step, but the destination of other figures and tokens is unclear.

#### Special Movement Rules
- Treat face-down room tiles as if they have doors on every side and don't block line of sight.

### Heroes win when
- Every room in the house is burned.
> Note: Landings are never destroyed by fire (only non-landing rooms are), so "every room" presumably means every non-landing room.

### What they know about the other side
- The Darkness will keep spreading out from the house unless stopped, and fire is the only way: the heroes must burn the house down to truly purge it.
- Not everyone escaped the Darkness, and those it took will try to drag the heroes into it.
