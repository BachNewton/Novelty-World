# 16 — The Phantom's Embrace

Summary: The traitor has trapped a Girl in the basement under a Phantom's guard and set a ticking bomb; the heroes must rescue her, then defuse the bomb or get out of the house with her.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- The explorer who has the Girl loses her, and she flees. Set aside the Girl card and a small magenta monster token (representing the Girl), and adjust that explorer's traits to match.
- Set aside a small purple monster token to represent the Phantom.
- Set aside at least 20 "distinctive tokens" (red or orange monster tokens work well).
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0. It tracks time.
### Monsters
- The Phantom — Might 6 · Sanity 5
  - The Phantom appears each time the explorers discover a basement room with an event [spiral symbol] or omen [raven symbol] symbol. When such a room is discovered, put the Phantom token and the Girl token in it, then put one distinctive token in that room.
  - After the Phantom appears, a hero can attack it. If the Phantom is defeated, it is killed and the explorers gain custody of the Girl. Otherwise it escapes with the Girl: set both tokens aside. It shows up again (you put both tokens back) the next time a hero discovers a basement room with an event or omen symbol.
  - If the entire basement has been explored and the Phantom is still alive, at the start of the monster turn choose any basement room and place the Phantom and the Girl there.
  - The Phantom never goes to the same room twice until every basement room has a distinctive token.
### Rules
- The heroes cannot use a special attack to steal the Girl.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Phantom does not attack, but it can defend itself. If it defends successfully, it flees.
#### You Must Do This ...
- At the end of each of your turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number, then roll dice equal to that number. If the result meets the target for the number of players in the table below, the house blows up.

| # of Players | House Blows Up on: |
|---|---|
| 3 | 8+ |
| 4 | 7+ |
| 5 | 6+ |
| 6 | 5+ |

> Note: The book does not define "otherwise" precisely in the Phantom's escape rule; read literally, the Phantom escapes with the Girl whenever it appears and is not defeated (including when no hero attacks it). It also does not say whether a Phantom that "flees" after defending successfully takes the Girl with it.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found on either point. BGG threads on this haunt only settle side questions (the Phantom is killed, not stunned, when defeated; discovering its room still ends the hero's move; the Revolver can't be used against it). Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2204186, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1968616
### Traitor wins when
- You blow up the house before the Girl escapes, or
- All the heroes are dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to save the Girl and themselves.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Rescue the Girl
- First the heroes must find the Phantom and rescue the Girl; then they must find and defuse the bomb, or get out quickly.
- The Phantom (and the Girl) appear in the next basement room a hero discovers that has an event [spiral symbol] or omen [raven symbol] symbol. The traitor places their tokens in that room.
- Instead of drawing a card for that room, the hero must attack the Phantom.
  - If the hero defeats the Phantom, the hero kills it and gains custody of the Girl.
  - If the Phantom defeats the hero, the hero takes damage normally and the Phantom escapes with the Girl (both tokens are removed from the house). The Phantom shows up again in the next basement room a hero discovers with an event or omen symbol.
- If the entire basement has been explored, the traitor chooses any basement room and puts the Phantom and the Girl there. The Phantom never moves to the same room twice.
#### How to Defuse the Bomb
- Once during your turn, you can attempt a Knowledge roll of 7+ in the room where the Phantom was defeated, to defuse the bomb.
#### How to Escape from the House
- A hero can attempt a Knowledge roll (picking the lock) or a Might roll (breaking down the door) of 6+ to open the front door in the Entrance Hall.
- If the roll succeeds, that hero draws an event card and ends his or her turn.
- On later turns, all heroes can move 1 space out the front door from the Entrance Hall to escape.
### Heroes win when
- The heroes rescue the Girl, and then either defuse the bomb or escape from the house with the Girl, before the bomb blows up.
> Note: The book does not say whether every hero must escape, or only the hero with custody of the Girl.
> Resolution (unresolved): no reliable ruling found. A single BGG answer (by a frequent rules answerer) reads it as all heroes, including the one with the Girl, having to escape; nobody else weighed in, and a separate thread asking the same question went unanswered. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1968616, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1163936
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has summoned a Phantom to guard the Girl. She is hidden somewhere in the basement.
- The traitor has set a trap: a bomb is ticking away, and the heroes don't have much time.
