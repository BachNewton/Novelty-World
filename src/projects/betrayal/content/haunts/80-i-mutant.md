# 80 — I, Mutant

Summary: The traitor is a mad scientist whose lab monkeys (the heroes, now mutants) have broken loose, and must recapture them with the help of lab assistants.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- For the rest of the game, call the heroes "Monkeys".
- If the Research Laboratory is not in play yet, search the room stack for it, place it in the house, then shuffle that stack.
- For each Monkey, put one small round monster token of a different color in the Research Laboratory. These represent Assistants.
- Put Obstacle tokens, representing Electronets, in different rooms of the house. The number of Electronets equals the number of explorers.

### Monsters
- Assistants act on the monsters' turn. Each color is a different Assistant with its own stats and ability:
- Red — Speed 3 · Might 5 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 2
  - When the red Assistant defeats a Monkey, you may move that Monkey through a doorway into an adjacent room.
- Orange — Speed 5 · Might 3 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 2
  - The orange Assistant may move through false doors.
- Yellow — Speed 2 · Might 2 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 5
  - The yellow Assistant may attack or defend using Knowledge. If it succeeds, it deals no damage; instead put an Electronet in the yellow Assistant's room and capture that Monkey.
- Green — Speed 1 · Might 3 · Sanity 5 · Knowledge 3
  - The green Assistant may teleport to any room in the house instead of moving.
- Blue — Speed 3 · Might 4 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 3
  - If the blue Assistant is defeated in an attack, the Monkey takes damage equal to the amount by which the Monkey's roll exceeded the blue Assistant's roll.
- Purple — Speed 3 · Might 4 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 3
  - The purple Assistant adds 2 dice to its attack rolls. Instead of dealing damage when it wins, it steals an item card from the hero if it can; you gain that item.
- Magenta — Speed 3 · Might 4 · Sanity 4 · Knowledge 1
  - The magenta Assistant cannot be stunned.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- During your turn you may put one Electronet in a room you occupied during that turn.
- Each of your Assistants may do the same during the monsters' turn (place one Electronet in a room it occupied that turn).

#### How to capture a Monkey
- When a Monkey enters a room containing an Electronet, it must attempt one roll of 5+ for each Electronet in that room, choosing Might, Speed, or Knowledge.
- If any of those rolls fails, the Monkey is captured: tip the hero's figure over.
- For each roll that succeeds, remove one Electronet from that room.
> Note: The book does not say whether the Monkey picks one trait for all its rolls or may pick per roll, nor in what order failures and removals resolve when some rolls succeed and some fail.

#### How to control your Assistants
- Assistants may move their full Speed; they do not roll for movement.
- Assistants may move captured Monkeys. An Assistant in the same room as a captured Monkey may spend 2 spaces of movement to bring the Monkey and/or any Electronets along to the room where the Assistant ends its turn.
- An Assistant defeated in an attack is stunned as usual. When it stops being stunned, replace its token with an Assistant of a different color.
> Note: The book does not say whether the replacement color must be one not currently in play, or where the unused color tokens come from (setup places one per Monkey, so some colors start unused).

#### Special attack rules
- Monkeys cannot be killed. If an attack would kill a Monkey, each trait that would drop to the skull symbol instead drops to the lowest value above the skull symbol. The Monkey is captured instead: put an Electronet in its room and capture the Monkey.

#### Special movement rules
- A captured Monkey does not count as an opponent for the purposes of moving past an opponent.

### Traitor wins when
- You have captured all of your Monkeys.

### What they know about the other side
- The Monkeys have been turned into mutants and will try their best to ruin your experiment.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Place a plastic clip on the Turn/Damage track at twice the number of explorers. The track counts the damage remaining to the Research Laboratory.

### Rules
#### Getting Captured
- The traitor has placed Electronets around the house to capture heroes.
- A captured hero tips their figure over.
- While captured, on your turn you can't move and can't take any action other than mutating and dropping an item.
- At the start of your turn, remove one Electronet from your room. If no Electronets remain in your room, you are no longer captured: stand your figure back up.

#### Mutating
- At the end of your turn you may mutate: lower any traits by 1 or more in total, then raise one other trait by the total number of points you lowered.
- You then gain an extra power for the rest of the game, chosen by your highest trait (if traits tie for highest, you choose among them):
  - **Speed:** You can slip through walls. You may spend 1 space of movement to pass through a wall into a discovered room on the other side. You may spend 2 spaces of movement to ignore an Electronet in a room you enter.
  - **Might:** Once on your turn, you may pick up and rotate the room you are in, so that it matches at least one door. You can't do this if it would separate a room, or a set of rooms, from the rest of the house.
  - **Sanity:** You may ignore any room text, and you may control the Mystic Elevator at will. Also, once during your turn, you may attempt a Sanity roll; remove one Electronet from your room for each die that results in a 2.
  - **Knowledge:** Once on your turn, you may draw an item card.
- You may have only one of these powers at a time. If your highest-valued trait changes by the end of one of your turns, your power changes to match it.
> Note: The book says "lower any traits by 1 or more, and raise another trait"; it does not say explicitly whether the lowered points may come from several traits at once. "Any traits" (plural) suggests they can.

#### How to Destroy the Research Laboratory
- If you start your turn in the Research Laboratory, you may, instead of moving or attacking, smash the lab equipment: attempt a Might roll. For each die that results in a 2, lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number.

### Heroes win when
- The Turn/Damage track is lowered to 0, destroying the Research Laboratory.

### What they know about the other side
- They have been betrayed by an unhinged scientist who wants to make them the subjects of strange research.
