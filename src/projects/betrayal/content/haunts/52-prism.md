# 52 — Prism

Summary: The house turns out to be a moving death-trap prison built by experimenters; the heroes must work out the safe sequence through it and reach the exit room before the experiment ends.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer is still in the game but has turned traitor and is no longer in the house. Remove your figure from the house and discard your cards. You now control the prism and its system of Traps.
- Roll 2 dice to get a sequence from the Mathematical Sequences table. Do not reveal the roll to the heroes.
- Place the 24 Obstacle tokens (representing Traps) face-up, in any order, one in each room that is not a landing. If there are not enough rooms, set the extra tokens aside.
- On a piece of paper, choose a room holding a token whose number is in your sequence to be the exit room. Write down that token's number and hide the paper somewhere on the table.
- Pick up the rooms the heroes are in and move some or all of them to new positions in the house. If a hero is in the exit room, do not move that room. New positions may be on any floor, but doors must connect where possible, and there must be a path from the exit room to the landing on its floor.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 6. It tracks time.

#### Mathematical Sequences
Roll 2 dice and use this sequence.

| Roll | Sequence | Numbers |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Threes | 3, 6, 9, 12, 15, 18, 21, 24 |
| 1 | Fibonaccis | 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21 |
| 2 | Fours | 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24 |
| 3 | Primes | 2, 3, 5, 7, 11, 13, 17, 19, 23 |
| 4 | Powers of 2 | 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 |

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- Lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number. When it reaches 0, the haunt ends.
- After lowering the track, pick up the rooms the heroes are in and move them anywhere else in the house, connecting doors where possible and leaving a path from the exit room to the landing on its floor. If a hero is in the exit room, do not move that room.

#### Triggering the traps
- When a hero moves into a room with a Trap token, check whether that room is trapped:
  - If the token's number is in your sequence, the room is safe: flip the token to its unnumbered side.
  - Otherwise, remove the Trap token from the house and roll a die. The trap's attack is made against your traits even though you are not the one being attacked. On a result of:

| Result | Effect |
|---|---|
| 0 | The hero makes a Knowledge attack. If you win, the hero takes 1 point of mental damage and ends all movement. |
| 1 | The hero makes a Speed attack. If you win, the hero takes 1 damage to a trait of the hero's choice and ends all movement. |
| 2 | The hero makes a Might attack. The hero may not use weapons on this roll. If you win, the hero takes 1 point of physical damage and ends all movement. |

> Note: You have no figure and discarded your cards, but you still roll with your own traits for trap attacks. The book does not say what happens if the hero wins the trap attack (presumably nothing, since you cannot take damage).

#### Special attack rules
- You cannot take damage.

#### Special movement rules
- If any Obstacle tokens were set aside at the start of the haunt, put one in each new room as it is discovered.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes have died, or time runs out before the heroes find the exit room.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes will try to work out which room is the exit room and reach it before time runs out. If any hero is in the exit room at the end of the experiment, the heroes win.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The heroes leave the room (the play area) while the traitor sets up; the traitor stays with the house.
- When the heroes return, they look at the house, which will have changed.
- Each hero should get a piece of paper; taking notes is recommended, to work out the sequence needed to stay safe and find the way out.

### Rules
#### Special movement rules
- Entering a room that has an Obstacle token may trigger a Trap. The traitor tells you what happens.
- When you discover a room with a [spiral event symbol], [horned-skull item symbol], [raven omen symbol] or [question-mark symbol], you may choose to ignore the symbol and not end your turn there.
- If you explore on a floor that has no room tiles left, draw the next room tile for any floor and place it.

### Heroes win when
- Any living hero is in the exit room, at the heart of the prism, when the experiment ends.

> Note: the heroes' book does not say when the experiment ends, which room is the exit room, or what the Traps do; those are in the traitor's book.

### What they know about the other side
- The house is a deadly prison designed to confuse and trap the people inside.
- Every puzzle has a solution: its builders must have made a safe route through the hazards for themselves. The heroes must work out how to reach the exit room and escape in time.
- The traitor is obsessed with mathematical sequences, and has used that obsession to decide where the Traps go and which room is the exit room.

> Note: the printed book doesn't tell the heroes that the Traps and the exit room follow a mathematical sequence, nor how to tell which room in the sequence is the exit. The last bullet above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. The heroes must work out the sequence, and the exit room, from the traitor's actions; the FAQ adds the sentence about the traitor's obsession with mathematical sequences to the end of What You Know About the Bad Guys.
