# 59 — The Fleshchild's Alchemical Mandate

Summary: The traitor is revealed as a Fleshchild, an alchemist's homunculus sent to collect a piece of each hero; the heroes must hold onto their Flesh and kill it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer is not still in the game, but something much like you is. Flip your character card and set your traits to the starting values on the new side, then raise each trait by the number of heroes.

### Rules
#### How to harvest Flesh
- Each hero starts with one small orange monster token (representing a pound of Flesh) on his or her character card.
- When you start your turn in a room with one or more Flesh, you may pick up one Flesh and put it on your character card.
- Flesh counts as an item and can be dropped or stolen, but not traded.

#### Special attack rules
- When you defeat a hero who has Flesh on his or her character card, the damage you deal to that hero increases by 1.

#### Special movement rules
- When you add Flesh to your character card, you may immediately move up to a number of spaces equal to your Speed.
- When you start your turn in a room with an orange monster token, you may pick up one Flesh and put it on your character card.

> Note: The second movement rule repeats the harvesting rule (Flesh is the orange monster token).

### Traitor wins when
- You have all the Flesh on your character card, or all the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes know the legends of Zosimus Alchemista's fleshchildren, and now that your true form is revealed they know exactly what you are. You must collect samples from each of them for your master.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Put one small orange monster token (it represents a pound of Flesh) on each hero's character card.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- At the start of your turn, if there is no Flesh on your character card, lose 1 from each of your traits.
#### How to save yourselves
- When the Fleshchild takes damage from an attack, it drops all the Flesh it is carrying.
- If you have no Flesh on your character card and you end your turn in a room with Flesh, you may pick up one Flesh and put it on your character card.

### Heroes win when
- The Fleshchild is dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is a Fleshchild, the homunculus creation of an evil alchemist (Zosimus Alchemista), and its mission is to collect a bit of each hero.
