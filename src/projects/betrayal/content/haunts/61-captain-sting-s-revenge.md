# 61 — Captain Sting's Revenge

Summary: A treasure-map hunt opens a portal to the Underground Sea, and the traitor's explorer is killed and replaced by the pirate captain who has come with her crew to haul the house's loot back to her Treasure Chest.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer is dead. Remove their figure from the house and discard their cards.
- Put the large circular Pirate Queen token (Captain Sting) in the room where the traitor's explorer was.
- If the Underground Lake is not yet in play, search the room stack for it and place it in the basement, then shuffle the room stack.
- Put the Box (the Treasure Chest) in the Underground Lake.
- Put small magenta monster tokens (Pirates) in the Underground Lake, one per hero.

### Monsters
- Captain Sting — Speed 5 · Might 8 · Sanity 5 · Knowledge 4
- Pirates — Speed 4 · Might 3 · Sanity 4 · Knowledge 2

### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- When the traitor (Captain Sting) or a Pirate brings a Piece of Loot to the Treasure Chest, roll 1 die. On a 1 or higher, the Piece of Loot goes into the Treasure Chest.

#### Special Attack Rules
- Captain Sting may steal an item card in any attack she is part of, whether she attacked or was attacked, and whether she wins or loses.
- A Pirate needs to deal only 1 point of damage to steal.

#### Special Movement Rules
- Captain Sting moves using her Speed and does not roll to move.
- Pirates (Captain Sting included) can carry only one Piece of Loot at a time. While carrying a Piece of Loot, they move 1 fewer space on their turn.

> Note: This half does not define what a Piece of Loot is or where it comes from; that is presumably in the heroes' book (Secrets of Survival).

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or
- The traitor has secured a number of Pieces of Loot equal to the number of heroes and returned them to the Treasure Chest.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes win if the Treasure Chest in the Underground Lake is destroyed.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Place pentagonal item tokens, representing Pieces of Loot, equal to the number of heroes, in any rooms of the house, one token per room.
- If possible, put one Piece of Loot token on each floor.
### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- Once during your turn you may attack the Treasure Chest, but only with a Might attack. The Treasure Chest has Might 4.
- Each time you win such an attack, put a Might Roll token on your character card.
- Once the heroes together have accumulated four Might Roll tokens, the next successful Might attack destroys the Treasure Chest.
- Captain Sting can be stunned using normal attack rules, but she does not take damage.
#### Special movement rules
- Heroes can pick up Pieces of Loot.
- An explorer carrying a Piece of Loot has 1 fewer space of movement per turn.
### Heroes win when
- The Treasure Chest is destroyed.
### What they know about the other side
- Captain Sting, a pirate leader, has killed their friend; her crew is loyal to her.
- The Pirates win when they have returned all the Pieces of Loot to the Underground Lake.
> Note: "After the heroes have accumulated four Might Roll tokens" does not say whether the four tokens are counted across all heroes or per hero; the plural "heroes" suggests a shared total.
