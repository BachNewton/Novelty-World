# 9 — The Dance of Death

Summary: A Dark Fiddler's music is luring everyone to dance to their deaths in the Ballroom; the heroes must defy it from the Pentagram Chamber with the Holy Symbol before they fall under its spell.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- This haunt starts with no traitor, only heroes. Each hero may become a traitor during his or her turn. Do not read this half unless your explorer has turned traitor.
- When you turn traitor: raise any trait that is below its starting value back up to its starting value.

### Rules
#### You Must Do This On Your Turn
- Every turn you must make a Might roll:

| Die Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| 3+ | No effect. |
| 0–2 | You can't move this turn. Lose 1 Might. |

- Unlike the heroes, you do not have to make a Sanity roll at the start of each turn when you lack the Holy Symbol.

#### Special Attack Rules
- You cannot make Might attacks; all your attacks are Speed attacks instead.
- If you beat a hero by 2 or more with a Speed attack, you may steal one item from that hero instead of dealing damage.

#### Destroying the Holy Symbol
- First steal the Holy Symbol from whichever hero has it (see "Special Attack Rules" above). Then end your turn in the Chasm, Furnace Room or Underground Lake to destroy it.

> Note: the rules for how a hero becomes a traitor are not on this page; they are in the Secrets of Survival half (see Heroes below).
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling needed, and none found. This is a layout note; the revised 1st-edition books split the text the same way. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf

### Traitor wins when
- The Holy Symbol has been destroyed.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to stop the music.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- This haunt starts with no traitor, only heroes. Any hero may become a traitor during their own turn.
- If the Pentagram Chamber and the Stairs from Basement are not in the house, the haunt revealer searches the room stack for them and places them in the basement. They can be connected however the players choose, but the Pentagram Chamber must be placed as far from the haunt revealer as possible.
- Do the same for the Ballroom, connecting it to a ground-floor room of the players' choice.
- Then shuffle the room stack.
- Set aside triangular Sanity Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
> Note: "Do the same for the Ballroom" presumably applies only if the Ballroom isn't already in the house, and presumably carries the "as far from the haunt revealer as possible" placement too; the page does not spell either out. The book says "You can connect these rooms as you choose" without naming which player chooses.

### Rules
#### How to Banish the Fiddler
- The heroes must complete the steps below, in order. A hero can attempt only one step per turn.
  1. The hero carrying the Holy Symbol moves to the Pentagram Chamber. That hero can't voluntarily give the Holy Symbol to anyone else.
  2. A hero in the Pentagram Chamber with the Holy Symbol can attempt a Sanity roll of 5+ to defy the Dark Fiddler. The hero doesn't have to be carrying the Holy Symbol, as long as the hero is in the same room with it. On a success, put a Sanity Roll token in that room.
  3. When the Pentagram Chamber holds Sanity Roll tokens equal to the number of explorers at the start of the haunt, the heroes have banished the Dark Fiddler.
> Note: Setup sets aside Sanity Roll tokens equal to the number of players, while step 3 counts "the number of explorers at the start of the haunt"; these appear to be the same number.
> Resolution (official): they are the same number. The 2nd-edition rulebook (p. 16) says a count "equal to the number of explorers" includes explorers who have died, so it stays at the player count from the start of the haunt. Avalon Hill's 1st-edition FAQ rules the same way. Source: https://instructions.hasbro.com/api/download/F3148_en-us_avalon-hill-betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill-second-edition-cooperative-board-game.pdf, http://web.archive.org/web/20151204092838/http://www.wizards.com:80/default.asp?x=ah/faqs/hoth
#### You Must Do This
- At the start of each turn, a hero must attempt a Sanity roll of 4+ to resist the Dark Fiddler's lure, unless the hero is carrying the Holy Symbol.
  - Fail while in the Ballroom: the hero goes insane and is now a traitor (and reads this haunt in the Traitor's Tome).
  - Fail in any other room: the hero takes 1 Sanity damage instead. If that drops Sanity to the skull symbol, the hero does not die but becomes a traitor (and must read this haunt in the Traitor's Tome). Otherwise, the hero must move toward the Ballroom by the shortest route through discovered rooms, and may take other actions normally along the way.
- If a hero's Sanity drops to the skull symbol for any other reason during this haunt, that hero also goes insane and becomes a traitor.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Dark Fiddler can't be attacked.
### Heroes win when
- They banish the Dark Fiddler.
### What they know about the other side
- Somewhere in the house a Dark Fiddler is playing music that eats at the mind. A dance of death has begun in the Ballroom.
