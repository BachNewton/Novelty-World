# 19 — A Friend for the Ages

Summary: The traitor is kept alive by a cursed portrait in the Gallery that takes all of his or her injuries; the heroes must gather Paint and repaint the portrait to break the spell, or kill the traitor.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Raise any of your traits that are below their starting values back to their starting values.
- Then repeat the following once per hero in the game (as many times as there are heroes): find the trait that is the least above its starting value and increase it by 1. A trait at its starting value counts as 0 above it. If traits are tied, you choose among them.
### Rules
#### You Can Do This ...
- After you finish moving on your turn, you may search the room stack, choose a room tile, and place it in any legal place. Then shuffle the stack.
#### Your Portrait
- You must not look at your portrait. Whenever you enter the Gallery, or start your turn there, you must attempt a Sanity roll of 4+. On a failure, take 1 die of mental damage. This damage ignores your general immunity (below).
#### Destroying Paint Tokens
- The heroes will place Paint tokens in the house at the right times. Paint tokens can be picked up, dropped, traded and stolen like normal items, but the Dog cannot carry them. Each explorer can carry only one Paint token at a time.
- If you are carrying a Paint token on your turn, you can destroy it instead of making an attack.
#### Special Attack Rules
- You cannot be harmed by normal means. Events, room features and damage cannot reduce your traits, except where this haunt's rules say otherwise. You can still take damage from entering the Gallery, as described above.
- When you gain or lose an item, your traits go up or down normally, as the card describes. Exception: you take no damage if the Blood Dagger is stolen from you.
- An opponent can steal an item from you during physical combat. (See "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook.)
### Traitor wins when
- You destroy at least three Paint tokens, or
- All the heroes are dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to kill you by repainting your portrait.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Take pentagonal item tokens (representing cans of Paint) equal to the number of heroes + 2.
- Also set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal in number to the number of heroes.
- Place the Paint tokens in these rooms: Attic, Abandoned Room, Collapsed Room, Patio, Statuary Corridor, Storeroom, Wine Cellar. Place one Paint token in each room.
  - If more of these rooms are in play than you have Paint tokens, place the tokens in the rooms farthest from any explorer.
  - If you have more Paint tokens than suitable rooms in play, set the extra tokens aside. Each time an eligible room is discovered, put a Paint token in it.
### Rules
#### How to Repaint the Portrait
- Paint tokens can be picked up, dropped, traded and stolen like regular items, but can't be carried by the Dog. Each explorer can carry only one Paint token at a time.
- A hero in the Gallery who carries a Paint token can attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ on his or her turn to repaint the portrait. On a success, discard the Paint token and place 1 Knowledge Roll token in the room. A hero can't do this more than once per turn.
- When the heroes have placed Knowledge Roll tokens in the Gallery equal in number to the number of heroes when the haunt began, the spell is broken.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The traitor can't be harmed by normal means. If a hero defeats the traitor in physical combat by 2 or more, the hero can steal an item from the traitor. (See "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook.)
- Exception: when a hero wearing the Amulet of the Ages defeats the traitor in combat, the traitor's traits take damage as normal.
### Heroes win when
- The heroes break the portrait's spell by repainting it, or the traitor is dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is protected by a mystical portrait, which absorbs all of his or her ills and infirmities. The traitor is trying to protect it at all costs.
