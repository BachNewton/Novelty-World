# 72 — Man's Worst Enemy

Summary: A hellbeast bound into a dog seeks to possess or destroy the explorers; the heroes can only hurt it by playing fetch with the Dog and striking the Beast with what it brings back.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Discard the Dog card. Put a small red monster token, representing the Beast, in your room.
- If you have the Girl, the Cat, or the Madman, set those cards aside and adjust your traits to match. If that would drop any trait to the skull symbol, set it to its lowest value above the skull instead. Use small monster tokens of different colors to represent these companions; put each token in a different room up to 4 spaces away, and put a matching monster token on the card for reference. The companions stay in those rooms and can be picked up by the heroes.
- Set the Turn/Damage track to 6 with a plastic clip. It tracks time for possession.

### Monsters
- The Beast — Speed 4 · Might 4 · Sanity 6

### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- At the end of each of your turns, lower the Turn/Damage track by one. When it reaches 0, you may possess a hero (see Possession). Then reset the track to 6.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Beast makes Sanity attacks and deals mental damage. When it reduces a victim's mental trait to the skull symbol, that trait stays at its lowest value and the victim is possessed (see Possession).
- The Beast cannot be attacked through normal means: no one can attack it directly.
- You, and any other possessed victims, can attack, be attacked, and use cards as normal. If any of your traits drops to the skull symbol, you are knocked unconscious and removed from the game.
- If a hero has the Cat card, the Beast must attack that hero if it can.
#### Possession
- A possessed hero becomes a traitor. A hero can be possessed in two ways:
  - The Beast possesses a victim with its mental attack (see Special Attack Rules).
  - When the Turn/Damage track reaches 0, the Beast may automatically possess the hero with the lowest Sanity. On a tie, you choose which tied hero is possessed. If the player with the lowest Sanity is protected in some way, the Beast possesses the player with the next highest Sanity instead. Whether or not the Beast possesses a hero, reset the Turn/Damage track to 6.

### Traitor wins when
- Every hero is either dead or controlled by the Beast.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to destroy the Beast.

> Note: "you are rendered unconscious and removed from the game" — the book does not say whether this counts as death for the heroes' side.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside the matching explorer tokens for each hero.
### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- The Beast can attack you, but you cannot attack it directly. You can fight it only through the game of fetch (below).
- The Beast responds to the Bell. Once during your turn, if you are in the Beast's room, you may use the Bell to attempt a Sanity roll of 5+. On a success, the Beast is stunned ("playing dead").
- A hero accompanied by the Cat cannot be possessed. If the Beast would deal damage to you, you may discard the Cat card instead of taking the damage.
  > Note: "Accompanied by the Cat" presumably means carrying the Cat card; the book does not define it further.
- You may attack the traitor or a possessed hero normally.
#### How to play fetch
- Once during your turn, while in the same room as the Beast, you may attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+. On a success, you send the Beast to a room with an item symbol [item symbol] up to 6 spaces away.
  - If that room already has a weapon in it, the Beast brings that weapon to you, and you may immediately make an attack against the Beast using it.
  - If that room has no weapon, the Beast draws an item card and brings it to you. If the item is a weapon, you may immediately make an attack against the Beast using it.
  - If you attack with a weapon on the turn it was fetched and deal damage to the Beast, take one of your explorer tokens.
- When the heroes together hold a number of explorer tokens equal to 1 more than the number of explorers who started the haunt, the Beast is slain: its spirit is banished and its victims are no longer possessed. The Dog remains, though, and the Beast will return.
- After that, the next time you play fetch and get a weapon, you must attack the Dog with the weapon it brings you. If you do not, you lose all your explorer tokens.
  > Note: The book does not say what happens when the Dog is attacked or how this leads to the win; the win condition is "you kill the Beast". The traitor's book may clarify.
### Heroes win when
- You kill the Beast.
### What they know about the other side
- A hellbeast bound to a puppy wants to consume the heroes' minds or destroy their bodies; if they don't act quickly, they will all become slaves of the Beast.
