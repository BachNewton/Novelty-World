# 51 — Director's Cut

Summary: The hero is a film director whose fellow explorers have become bumbling actors; the director must recover item 10 (the missing document of the Vision) and get it, with the Vision, to the Theater.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorers are still in the game but have turned traitor. (This haunt has more than one traitor; the text addresses them collectively.)
- Each explorer discards all weapon item cards and weapon omen cards, then draws the same number of non-weapon item cards.

### Rules
#### Special item and omen rules
- Whenever you draw a weapon from the item stack or the omen stack, discard it and draw a replacement from that same stack.

#### How the item tokens work
- Some rooms hold Props (pentagonal item tokens), each with a number on it.
- When you enter a room with an item token, look at its number and place it in front of you number side-down.
- You may hold only one item token at a time. If you pick up a second one, look at both, keep one, and leave the other number side-down in the room.
- The token's number identifies which item it is (see the table). When you use the item, reveal its number to the other explorers.

| Token | Item | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Powerless Chainsaw | Deal 2 dice of physical damage to another explorer and 1 die of physical damage to yourself, then end your turn. |
| 2 | Sawed-Off Shotgun | It's not loaded, but you can trip people with it. Make a Speed attack. Whoever wins takes physical damage equal to the difference. |
| 3 | Flash Powder | Make a Knowledge attack. This attack deals no damage. If you win, the other explorer drops all item tokens and item cards. You may examine them and pick one up. |
| 4 | Fog Machine | Make a Knowledge attack. This attack deals no damage. If you win, move to an adjacent room and put the Smoke token in the room. Leaving that room now requires succeeding at a Sanity roll of 5+. |
| 5 | Hook for a Hand | Make a Might attack. Add 1 die to this roll, and drop one item card, item token, or omen card. |
| 6 | Plastic Skeleton | Make a Might attack against an explorer in an adjacent room. On that explorer's next turn, the explorer must succeed at a Speed roll of 5+ to exit the room. |
| 7 | Screechy Violin | Deal 2 dice of Sanity damage to another explorer and 1 die of Sanity damage to yourself. |
| 8 | Clapperboard | Draw an event card, then either keep it or give it to another explorer. You may do this once during your turn. |
| 9 | Dove-Summoning Capguns | They're not loaded. Make a Speed attack. This attack deals no damage. If you win, move the explorer you attacked to a discovered room up to three rooms away. |
| 10 | The Plan | If you are in the room with the Director, take the omen card that started the haunt and the face-down card under it, without looking at it. |

> Note: Sawed-Off Shotgun says "whoever wins takes physical damage equal to the difference" — read literally the winner takes the damage. That is what is printed; it may be intended as a joke (the gun trips people) or an error.
> Note: Hook for a Hand does not say who drops the card. It reads as the attacker paying a cost for the extra die, but the book does not state it outright.
> Note: Flash Powder makes the loser drop "all item tokens", though an explorer may hold only one item token at a time.
> Note: Fog Machine says "move to an adjacent room" without naming who moves; read literally it is the attacker. It does not say how long the Smoke token's 5+ Sanity requirement lasts.

#### Special attack rules
- The Director is accompanied by several Production Assistants. You may make a Knowledge attack against a Production Assistant in the same room as you.
- You cannot attack the Director while a Production Assistant is in your room.
- If you stun a Production Assistant, you may send it to any room on your floor that has no explorer in it. If you lose, the Director may move your figure "in the same manner".
- If an attack deals at least 2 damage to an actor or to the Director, and that target holds an item token or the omen with the face-down card, you may take that instead (of dealing the damage), dropping any item token you already hold. Do not look at the event card.

> Note: "instead" is not explicit about what it replaces; the natural reading is the damage.

### Traitors win when
- One of you holds the Director's Vision in the Theater with no other actors present. The Director then gives that player instructions that may let them win; all the other traitors lose.
- If you kill the Director, you lose and the Director wins.

### What they know about the other side
- The hero is the Director of the film "The Haunting of Hell Hotel", who thinks it is as good as "The Cabin in the Woods". Each traitor is an actor on the set who must kill all the other actors.
- Every weapon collected so far is a useless prop.
- The Director holds an omen card that conceals the Vision for the film: a face-down event card. Getting that card may let you impress the Director and keep your job.
- The Director's Production Assistants are also your opponents. If you attack the Director, a Production Assistant steps in and defends with Knowledge.

> Note: "a Production Assistant will interpose, using Knowledge to defend" sits alongside the rule that you cannot attack the Director at all while a Production Assistant is in your room. How the two combine (e.g. which Assistant interposes, and from where) is not spelled out here.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Take the pentagonal item tokens numbered 1 to 10 (they represent Props) and place them randomly in different rooms, number side down.
  - Each floor must get at least two tokens.
  - No token may go on the floor that has the Theater.

> Note: the book says both "at least two tokens on each floor" and "none can be on the floor with the Theater"; read together, "each floor" means each floor other than the Theater's.

- Place small magenta monster tokens (they represent Production Assistants) in your room, as many as there are traitors.
- Draw an event card (it represents your Vision) and place it face down, under the omen card that started the haunt, in the center of the table. Do not look at it.

### Allies
- Production Assistant — Knowledge 5
  - They are your entourage. They do not act in the monster turn.
  - If you are not stunned, you may choose to have them move with you.
  - You cannot move them if you leave their room without them, but you can take them along again if you pass back through the room they are in.
  - They never attack. They defend for you whenever you are attacked while they are in your room (see Special attack rules).

### Rules
#### Special movement rules
- When you enter a room that has an item token, you may look at it.
  - If it is item 10, you may take it, and you also take the omen that started the haunt.
  - If it is any other number, put it back where it was.
- If you reach the Theater carrying item 10, you win.
#### Special attack rules
- A traitor may make a Knowledge attack against a Production Assistant in the same room.
- If a traitor would attack you while a Production Assistant is in your room, the traitor must attack the Production Assistant instead.
- Whatever trait the traitor attacks with, the Production Assistant defends with Knowledge.

### Heroes win when
- You have item 10 and the Vision and you are in the Theater.
- Ending: when you, or a lone actor, is in the Theater and has the omen that started the haunt, you move to the Theater if you are not already there. The person holding the Vision then reveals it and acts out the event "in a highly dramatic fashion", and the explorer with the event card wins the game.

> Note: the book is unclear on who holds the Vision. It starts face down under the omen in the center of the table; the only rule that moves it is taking the omen when you find item 10, which suggests the Vision travels with the omen, but this is never stated. The ending also lets "a lone actor" (a traitor) who reaches the Theater with the omen end the game, with "the explorer with the event card" winning; the two win statements ("you win" on reaching the Theater with item 10, and the ending procedure) are not reconciled.
> Note: the instruction to "weep once at the brilliance of it all, applaud lightly" is part of the ending procedure but is flavor, not a game action.

### What they know about the other side
- The other explorers (the traitors) are actors in your horror movie, "The Haunting of Hell Hotel", and they are ruining it.
- The omen that started the haunt conceals the document of your Vision for the film, which you must follow exactly as written. Your assistant director lost the document, and you must find it.
