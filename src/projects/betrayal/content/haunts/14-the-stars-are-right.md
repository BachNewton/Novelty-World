# 14 — The Stars Are Right

Summary: The traitor is helping a cult gather sacrifices to summon its god in the Pentagram Chamber; the heroes must collect every can of Paint in the house and throw them into the chamber to desecrate the pentagram first.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Put small purple monster tokens, one per other player (the number of tokens equals the number of other players), in the Pentagram Chamber. They are Cultists.
### Monsters
- Cultists — Speed 4 · Might 4 · Sanity 4
  - Cultists can carry items and explorers' corpses. Items a Cultist carries do not affect its traits and do not give it abilities.
  - Cultists cannot carry Paint tokens.
### Rules
#### How to Summon the God
- To summon your god, you must reach a total of 13 "sacrifice points" by bringing sacrifices into the Pentagram Chamber, scored as follows:

| Sacrifice | Points |
|---|---|
| Explorer's corpse | 4 |
| Girl, Madman, or Dog | 2 |
| Any other tradable omen or item | 1 |

- Item and omen cards you sacrifice are removed from the game and kept in a separate pile. Track sacrifice points on a piece of paper.
- You can sacrifice the Girl, the Madman or the Dog when you hold that card and are in the Pentagram Chamber.
- Paint tokens cannot be stolen, carried or sacrificed by you.
#### Special Attack Rules
- A Cultist can steal an item from an explorer, the same way explorers can. (See "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook.)
- When an explorer is killed, tip that figure over in its room to mark the corpse.
- A Cultist can pick up a corpse and carry it like an item, but while carrying one it spends 2 spaces of movement to enter a room.
- You (the traitor) can also carry corpses, with the same restriction as Cultists. Take the explorer's figure while you carry its corpse.
### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or
- You summon your god.
### What they know about the other side
- The only way the heroes can stop you summoning your god is to desecrate the Pentagram Chamber. They will do it with cans of paint (Paint tokens) hidden around the house.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside pentagonal item tokens (representing cans of Paint) equal in number to the number of players.
- Put one in each of these rooms, in this order ("as many as you can"): Kitchen, Larder, Junk Room, Storeroom, Research Laboratory, Attic.
- If there are more Paint tokens than rooms, you may put more than one token in a room, again in that order.
- If none of these rooms are in play, search the room stack until you find one of them, put it into play on a legal floor, put all the Paint tokens on it, then shuffle that stack.
> Note: The book does not define "as many as you can". Interpretation: it is limited both by the number of Paint tokens and by which of the listed rooms are in play, and leftover tokens go into rooms already used, again in the listed order. The search-the-room-stack rule applies only when none of the listed rooms are in play.
> Resolution (community): Matches the interpretation above: put one Paint token in each listed room that is in play, in order; extra tokens start the list over (a second token in each); search the room stack only when none of the listed rooms are in play. One detailed BGG answer, unchallenged and accepted by the asker; no conflicting answer found. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1103039
### Rules
#### How to Desecrate the Pentagram
- The pentagram is desecrated by finding the Paint tokens and throwing them on the floor of the Pentagram Chamber.
- Pick up the Paint tokens scattered around the house. A hero can carry only one Paint token at a time.
- A hero can throw a Paint token into the Pentagram Chamber from an adjacent room with a connecting door. Doing this counts as 1 space of movement.
- Every Paint token in the house must be thrown into the Pentagram Chamber to desecrate the pentagram.
#### Special Attack Rules
- If a hero is killed, tip that figure over in its room to mark the corpse.
- A Cultist or the traitor can then pick up that corpse like an item and carry it. (The dead hero's player takes the figure to show it is being carried.)
- While the traitor or a Cultist carries a corpse, it uses 2 spaces of movement to enter a room.
### Heroes win when
- The heroes desecrate the pentagram before the god is summoned.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is working with a fanatic cult. The Cultists are trying to summon their god by bringing sacrifices into the Pentagram Chamber.
- They can sacrifice items and a few specific omens, along with the explorers' corpses.
- The heroes don't know how many sacrifices are needed, so they must stop the Cultists as quickly as possible.
