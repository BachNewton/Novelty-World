# 89 — One of the Master's Affairs

Summary: Stranded travelers in the house of a mad doctor who builds monsters from body parts (with the traitor as his hunchbacked servant) use their discovery of time-warping to complete tasks that unlock the front door.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Put a plastic clip at 10 on the Turn/Damage track. It tracks your spaceship's countdown.
- Gain 1 in each of your traits.
- If the Theater, Tower, and Underground Lake are not in play yet, search the room stack for them, place them in the house, then shuffle that stack.
- Once those rooms are in the house, place the monsters in rooms according to the Denizens table: Doctor Frank N. Sense, his creation Monty the Monster, his girlfriend Cornucopia, Cornucopia's boyfriend Hedley, and the maid Fuchsia.

### Monsters
#### Denizens
| TOKEN | MONSTER | STARTING ROOM | TRAITS USE YOUR . . . |
|---|---|---|---|
| Doctor | Doctor Frank N. Sense | Operating Laboratory | Knowledge |
| Frankenstein's Monster | Monty | Gymnasium | Might |
| Pirate Queen | Cornucopia | Master Bedroom | Sanity |
| Head | Hedley | Larder | Lowest trait |
| Small magenta token | Fuchsia | Dusty Hallway | Speed |

> Note: This page searches only for the Theater, Tower, and Underground Lake, but Secrets of Survival setup searches for the Ballroom, Dusty Hallway, Gymnasium, Larder, Master Bedroom and Operating Laboratory, so every Denizen's starting room ends up in play.

### Rules
#### You must do this on each hero's turn
- The heroes will be doing strange tasks in the Theater, Tower, and Underground Lake to keep the house's denizens busy.
- At the end of each hero's turn, if that hero neither scored a victory in one of those tasks nor attacked a monster during that turn, lower the Turn/Damage track by one number.

#### How the house's denizens function
- Each monster's value in all four traits equals the value of one of your traits, as given in the Denizens table. The monster attacks with that trait, dealing physical or mental damage as appropriate.
- Monsters do not roll for movement.
- A stunned monster goes back to the room it started in. It does not return to normal until you enter that room on your turn.
- Add 1 die to your trait rolls for each non-stunned monster in your room.
- You can't be damaged while you are in a room with a monster that is not stunned.
> Note: The book does not say how far monsters move or who moves them (presumably you, on the monsters' turn). "Lowest trait" for Hedley is read as your current lowest trait.

### Traitor wins when
- The Turn/Damage track reaches 0 and you blast off to the stars with at least one hero still in the house, or
- All the heroes are dead.

> Note: the printed win condition doesn't cover every hero dying. The last bullet above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. The traitor also wins if all the heroes are dead; the FAQ changes You Win When to add it. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

### What they know about the other side
- You intend to carry the heroes off to the planet Tinselvania to be experimented on. They take you for nothing more than the Doctor's servant.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Each hero takes a pentagonal item token (representing Time) and a blank piece of paper.
- Set aside a triangular Speed Roll, Might Roll, Sanity Roll and Knowledge Roll token, plus the Fountain token.
- If the Ballroom, Dusty Hallway, Gymnasium, Larder, Master Bedroom and Operating Laboratory are not yet in play, search the room stack for them and place them in the house. Then shuffle the stack.
- Put the heroes' figures in the Ballroom.

### Rules
#### How to Thwart the Bad Guys
- The heroes must complete the tasks below before the doors to the Entrance Hall swing open.
- Different heroes can complete different parts of each task. Each hero may attempt a task once per turn, trying to score a victory.
> Note: "Attempt a task once a turn" can mean one task attempt per turn in total, or one attempt at each task per turn. The tasks are in three different rooms, so in practice a hero is usually in reach of only one. Owner to rule.
- Each time you score a victory, stun one of the monsters and tell the traitor to put it in the room where that monster started the haunt.
- **Stage a farce:** In the Theater, a hero must attempt a Speed, Might, Sanity or Knowledge roll of 3+. When a hero fails one of these rolls, that hero puts the matching triangular Trait Roll token on the Theater. Each Trait Roll token can be put on the Theater once, and each counts as a victory. If the roll succeeds, nothing happens.
- **Climb the radio tower:** Three times, a hero must be in the Tower and attempt a Might roll of 3+ to put the top room tile of the room stack under the Tower tile. If the roll fails, the hero takes 1 die of physical damage for each tile already under the Tower. Each room tile under the Tower counts as a victory.
- **Synchronize your swimming:** Two heroes in the Underground Lake must each roll 4 dice on one of their turns, aiming for all dice to show the same number.
  - Either hero may discard an item card or omen card that can be dropped to let both heroes reroll any number of dice. This may repeat until the heroes give up or all the dice match.
  - All dice matching counts as a victory; mark it by putting the Fountain in the Underground Lake.
- When all eight victories are scored (4 triangular Trait Roll tokens on the Theater, three room tiles under the Tower, and the Fountain token in the Underground Lake), the front door unlocks. If you can get there, you can escape.
> Note: The game's token sheet has no Speed Roll token (it was left off the printed sheet), so the Speed failure in Stage a farce has no matching token.
> Resolution (official): Use an item token to mark a failed Speed roll in Stage a farce, or print the replacement token sheet the publisher supplied. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2016/downloads/widows_walk_faq.pdf
> Note: For Stage a farce the book says a victory comes from FAILING the trait roll, and that "If you succeed, nothing happens." This is as printed (fitting a farce), not a transcription error.
> Note: "Two heroes ... must roll 4 dice each on one of their turns" doesn't say whether both roll on the same hero's turn; it reads as both rolling during one of their turns.

#### How to Warp Time
- At the end of your turn, you may discard your Time token to write on your paper your current traits (noting exactly where each clip is), your current room, and your current event, item and omen cards. This is a Time Slip.
- At the start of any later turn of yours, you may move to the room on your Time Slip and reset your traits and cards to match the slip. This can mean taking a card from another explorer or searching a stack or discard pile; shuffle any stack you search this way. Then tear up your Time Slip.

### Heroes win when
- All living heroes exit through the front door of the Entrance Hall, which starts locked (see How to Thwart the Bad Guys).

### What they know about the other side
- The Doctor has built a monster from the body parts of the last people who stopped here on a rainy night.
- The Doctor's servants are preparing for some big event, and the heroes don't want to be around when it happens.
