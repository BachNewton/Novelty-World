# 1 — The Mummy Walks

Summary: The traitor has woken a Mummy that means to marry the Girl; the heroes must learn its true name from the Book and banish it first.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Place the large circular Mummy token and one pentagonal item token (the Sarcophagus) in your room.
- If you hold the Girl card, you lose it and any bonuses it gave; set the card aside.
- Place a small magenta monster token (the Girl) in any room on the same floor as the room where the haunt was revealed, at least 5 tiles from the Mummy. If no room on that floor is at least 5 tiles away, place it as far from the Mummy as possible on that floor.
- When any explorer enters the room with the Girl token, that explorer's player takes the Girl card.
- If you get the Girl, you may hand the Girl token to the Mummy while you share its room; the Mummy then has custody of the Girl.

> Note: the book says the Girl goes "at least five 5 tiles away" (the number is printed twice); read as 5 tiles.
> Resolution (designer (revised 1st edition)): 5 tiles. No 2nd-edition errata exists, but the designer's revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome (2005), whose rule here is the same, prints the number once: "at least five tiles away". Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/84488

### Monsters
- The Mummy — Speed 3 · Might 8 · Sanity 5
  - Movement: if the Mummy's movement roll is 0 or 1, it may instead use a secret passage to move to any space in the house.
  - It can carry items and can have custody of the Girl; neither the Girl nor the items change its traits.
  - You may transfer items and the Girl to the Mummy when you are in the same space as it.
  - Mandatory action: during its turn the Mummy must attack one explorer in its room, if it is able to.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Mummy attacks with Might, but the damage it deals is Speed damage until the opponent's Speed is at its lowest value. That attack cannot reduce Speed to the skull symbol. Once the opponent's Speed is at its lowest value, further damage from the Mummy is Might damage, until the opponent dies.
- If the Mummy beats an opponent by 2 or more on an attack, it may steal one item from that opponent instead of dealing damage. It can take custody of the Girl the same way.
- The Mummy is immune to Speed attacks (for example those made with the Revolver or Dynamite).

### Traitor wins when
- The Mummy has custody of the Girl, is carrying the Ring or the Holy Symbol, and has brought them back to the room with the Sarcophagus; or
- All the heroes are dead.
- If neither the Ring nor the Holy Symbol is in play, then the next time you discover a room with an omen symbol [raven symbol], search the omen stack and take either of those two cards; then shuffle the omen stack.

> Note: "returns with them to the room with the Sarcophagus" — the Mummy's own arrival with the Girl and the Ring or Holy Symbol is what counts; the book does not say whether the Mummy must have left that room first.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. Searched the official 1st-edition FAQ, the Widow's Walk FAQ and the BGG threads on this haunt (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2754915, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1246968, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/133256, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/76311, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/953936). None of them asks whether the Mummy must first leave the Sarcophagus room. The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome uses the same "returns with them" wording. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to banish the Mummy back to the land of the dead.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside 2 triangular Knowledge Roll tokens.
- The traitor loses the Girl card and any bonuses it gave. In its place, the traitor puts a small magenta monster token (representing the Girl) in any room that is on the same floor as the room where the haunt was revealed and at least 5 tiles away from the Mummy. If no room on that floor is at least 5 tiles away, the traitor places the token as far away as possible on that floor.
- When an explorer enters the room the Girl token is in, that player takes the Girl card.
### Rules
#### How to Banish the Mummy
- If the Book is not yet in play, the next hero to discover a room with an omen symbol does not draw normally: that hero searches the omen stack, takes the Book card, then shuffles the omen stack.
- A hero banishes the Mummy by speaking its true name from the Book. This is done through the steps below, in order. Each hero can attempt only one step per turn.
  1. Attempt a Knowledge roll of 6+ to discover the Mummy's true name. The roll can be made only in one of these rooms:
     - the room with the Sarcophagus (studying hieroglyphics);
     - the Research Laboratory (scanning the archaeological team's notes);
     - the Library (researching the Mummy's history).
     On a success, take a Knowledge Roll token.
  2. On any turn after a hero has discovered the name, a hero who possesses the Book can attempt a Knowledge roll of 6+ to look up the Mummy's name and learn the banishing spell. On a success, take a Knowledge Roll token.
  3. Once the heroes have two of these tokens, a hero must carry the Book to the Mummy's room. Any hero in the same room as both the Book and the Mummy can then try to defeat the Mummy in Sanity combat; doing so completes the spell that banishes it forever.
- The Mummy is immune to Speed attacks (for example, attacks with the Revolver or the Dynamite).
> Note: The book prints "a hero must Carry the Book" with "Carry" capitalized; nothing on this page marks it as a special term.
> Resolution (designer (revised 1st edition)): it isn't a special term. The designer's revised 1st-edition Secrets of Survival prints the same instruction in lower case ("carry the Book to the same room as the Mummy"), and no source treats it as a keyword. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060110122045/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/84488
> Note: Step 3 says a hero must carry the Book to the Mummy, but then lets "any hero in the same room as both the Book and the Mummy" make the Sanity attack; the attacker need not be the one carrying the Book.

- They banish the Mummy before it marries the Girl.
