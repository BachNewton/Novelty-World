# 27 — Amok Flesh

Summary: A blob of flesh hatched from the Crystal Ball is spreading through the house and absorbing explorers, and the heroes must study it and mix a formula to destroy it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Set aside at least 20 small orange monster tokens. Together they represent one single, growing Blob taking over the house.
- The player whose explorer carries the Crystal Ball card discards it.

### Monsters
- Blob-People — Speed 2
  - A Blob-person cannot attack, be attacked, draw cards, use the Mystic Elevator, or discover rooms. It cannot carry or use items.
  - It moves during its player's turn and can pass freely through rooms that have Blob tokens.
  - At the beginning of the monster turn, put a Blob token in any room that a Blob-person occupies. The Blob does not expand from that room until it is connected to the greater Blob.
> Note: Blob-People have only a Speed value printed.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found, and none is needed: the haunt already says Blob-people can't attack or be attacked, so only their Speed is ever used. Searched: BGG haunt 27 threads (2732130, 1349790, 634411, 110356, 1223647, 1240832, 1068421, 2433581, 618750). Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1349790

### Rules
#### The Blob
- On the first monster turn, the Blob engulfs the room where the Crystal Ball was and every room adjacent to it through a connecting door. Put Blob tokens in those rooms.
- On each monster turn after that, the Blob expands into each adjacent room through a connecting door; put Blob tokens in those rooms.
  - The expanding Blob can use every movement option, including doors, stairs, and special effects such as the Coal Chute, Collapsed Room, Secret Stairs, Secret Passage, and so on. It cannot use the Mystic Slide.
  - The Blob can also expand up to or down from the Coal Chute, Gallery, and Collapsed Room.
  - If the Blob enters the Mystic Elevator, the Elevator no longer moves.
- After the Blob has expanded into all adjacent rooms, roll 1 die. On a roll of 2, the Blob expands a second time.
- If the traitor runs out of Blob tokens, take tokens from inner rooms that explorers cannot reach.
- Blob tokens do not count as individual monsters. They do not attack and are not affected by things that affect monsters.
- Anyone in a room with a Blob token (the traitor included) immediately becomes a Blob-person and discards all items and omens. Put a Blob token on that explorer's character card to mark him or her as a Blob-person. That player now controls the Blob-person, and the player's new goal is to help the traitor win (that player reads this haunt in the Traitor's Tome).
- The Blob is not affected by the Bell or the Spirit Board.
> Note: "The room where the Crystal Ball was" is not further defined; it reads as the room of the explorer who was carrying the Crystal Ball when the haunt was revealed.
> Resolution (community): it is the room where the haunt revealer drew the Crystal Ball. Only the Crystal Ball triggers haunt 27, so the revealer holds it in that room, and several answers place the first Blob there. The 1st-edition revised Secrets of Survival says the same: whoever is in that room should move away, because the Blob starts there. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2433581, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1068421, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1223647, http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are either dead or turned into Blob-people.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to destroy the Blob by finding objects that will hurt it and throwing them into it. The traitor can try to kill the heroes or try to steal those objects.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The player whose explorer carries the Crystal Ball card discards that card. Anyone in that room should move away quickly, since the Blob starts growing there.
- Set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
- Set aside the same number of triangular Sanity Roll tokens.

### Rules
- A hero who ends up in a room with a Blob token is turned into a Blob-person, whose new goal is to help the traitor win.

#### How To Destroy the Blob
- Examining the Blob: once during his or her turn, a hero in a room adjacent to a Blob token (with a connecting door) may attempt a Knowledge roll of 3+. On each success, put a Knowledge Roll token on that hero's character card.
- Finding the Blob's weakness takes successful examining Knowledge rolls equal to the number of players. When the last of these succeeds, set all those tokens aside again.
- After the weakness has been found, the heroes must make the chemical formula that kills the Blob. It needs ingredients equal to the number of players.
- Searching for an ingredient: once during his or her turn, a hero may attempt a Knowledge roll of 3+ in any of these rooms: Attic, Conservatory, Furnace Room, Gardens, Library, either Laboratory, Junk Room, Kitchen, Larder, Storeroom, an open Vault, and the Wine Cellar.
  - After each success, put a Knowledge Roll token on that hero's character card to stand for the ingredient found.
  - Then put a Sanity Roll token in that room. That room cannot be used for this roll again.
- Throwing an ingredient: a hero in a room adjacent to the Blob (with a connecting door) may spend 1 space of movement to throw an ingredient into the Blob. Move a Knowledge Roll token from that hero's character card to the Blob token's room.
- Once ingredients equal to the number of players have been thrown into the Blob, the entire Blob is destroyed.

> Note: "either Laboratory" is printed as-is; it presumably means the Research Laboratory or any other laboratory room tile in the game.
> Resolution (official): the game has exactly two laboratory tiles, the Research Laboratory and the Operating Laboratory (haunt 29 names both), so "either Laboratory" means one of those two. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf
> Note: The book does not say whether a hero may both examine the Blob and search for an ingredient in the same turn; each is limited separately to "once during your turn". The ingredient search is only possible after the weakness is found.
> Resolution (unresolved): no haunt-specific ruling found. The 1st-edition FAQ limits each kind of scenario roll to once per turn, which would allow one examine roll and one search roll in a turn, but no source applies this to haunt 27. ### Heroes win when
- They destroy the Blob.

### What they know about the other side
- The Blob is spreading.
- A hero who ends up in a room with a Blob token becomes a Blob-person and from then on helps the traitor win.
