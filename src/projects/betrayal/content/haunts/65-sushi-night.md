# 65 — Sushi Night

Summary: The traitor turns out to be a Merperson who floods the house from the basement up, hunted by heroes who see a meal, and wins by flooding the whole house or by drying out into a human again.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor (a Merperson).
- Raise the traitor's Speed to its maximum value.
- Set aside three of the traitor's explorer tokens.
- Put the Fountain token in any room in the basement, and put the traitor's figure in that room.
- Starting with the Fountain's room, put face-down small monster tokens (Flood) in rooms, one per room, as many as there are explorers.
- Once every room on a floor has been flooded, any Flood tokens not yet placed may go on the next floor up.

> Note: Placement is "starting in the room with the Fountain"; the page does not say whether the remaining tokens must go in rooms adjacent to already-flooded rooms. "Number of explorers" is printed rather than "number of heroes", so it presumably includes the traitor.

### Rules
#### You Must Do This After Each Explorer's Turn
- After each explorer's turn, put a Flood token on a room that is not flooded.
- After the turn of the explorer to the traitor's right, flood three rooms instead.
- Rooms must be flooded on the lowest floor that is not yet fully flooded. If that floor becomes fully flooded and the traitor still has Flood tokens to place that turn, they may start flooding the next floor up.

#### Special Movement Rules
- If the traitor moves into a room without a Flood token, they must stop moving.
- If the traitor ends their turn without having been in a room with a Flood token during that turn, put one of the set-aside explorer tokens on their character card. Once there are three explorer tokens on it, the traitor's fins have dried into legs and they become a human.
- Once a floor is completely flooded, any room later discovered on that floor is also flooded.

### Traitor wins when
- The entire house has been flooded and the traitor can escape to the ocean, or
- The traitor transforms back into a human (the heroes then have no reason to hunt them).

> Note: The page does not say exactly how the traitor "escapes to the ocean" once the house is flooded; it may be immediate.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are hunting the traitor, and they look hungry.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### Special movement rules
- While in a flooded room (a room marked with a face-down monster token), you roll 2 fewer dice on Speed rolls and have 2 fewer spaces of movement.
- You always roll at least 1 die and can always move at least 1 space.
### Heroes win when
- The Merperson is dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The house is flooding and the traitor has turned into a Merperson, which is formidable and hard to catch in the water.
- The heroes must hunt down the traitor before the house floods completely and the Merperson slips back into the ocean.
