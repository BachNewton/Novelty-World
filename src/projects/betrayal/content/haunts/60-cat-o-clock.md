# 60 — Cat O'Clock

Summary: The traitor is filling the house with Kittens on behalf of The Cat; the heroes must soothe the Kittens and feed The Cat a Cat Food dinner so it takes a nap.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- The traitor raises both physical traits (Speed, Might) by 2 and lowers both mental traits (Sanity, Knowledge) by 2. A trait that would drop to the skull symbol stops instead at the lowest value above the skull.
- Set aside six triangular Sanity Roll tokens.
- Place a large circular Cat token (The Cat) in the room where the haunt was revealed.
- Place small red monster tokens (Kittens), each in a different room: 2 Kittens with two or three heroes, 3 Kittens with four or five heroes.
- Roll 2 dice twice and look up the Cat Name table: the first roll picks the first name, the second roll the last name. The traitor must call The Cat by this name from then on.

#### Cat Name
| Roll | First name | Roll | Last name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Dorothy | 0 | Buttons |
| 1 | J Johnny | 1 | Cuddles |
| 2 | Mittens | 2 | Mittens |
| 3 | Precious | 3 | Tubbsman, Esq. |
| 4 | Barnaby | 4 | Fuzzy-Fuzz |

### Monsters
- The Cat — Speed 0 · Might 3 · Sanity 6 · Knowledge 6
  - To leave the house through the front door, The Cat must succeed at a Might roll of 6+. It may try this roll once on each of its turns.
  - Its Speed and Might grow with the Kittens (see Your New Feline Friends).
- Kittens (no stats printed)
  - See Your New Feline Friends.

> Note: The Cat has a printed Speed of 0; its only movement comes from the per-turn bonus below. No stats are printed for the Kittens.

### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- On their turn, the traitor may spend 1 point of movement to try a Might roll of 3+ to open a window or drawer in the room they are in.
- On a success, put a Sanity Roll token in that room (marking that its window or drawer has been opened) and put a Kitten in that room.
- Once all six Sanity Roll tokens are in the house, the traitor instead picks one of them and moves it to their current room.

> Note: The book does not say outright whether a room that already holds a Sanity Roll token can be used again; the token is described as showing that a window or drawer there "has already been opened".

#### Your New Feline Friends
- Kittens do not slow heroes' movement the way normal monsters do. Instead, a hero who enters a room with a Kitten takes 1 point of physical damage. A soothed Kitten does not deal this damage.
- On the monsters' turn, each Kitten may move 4 spaces through the house, but two Kittens may never be in the same room.
- When a Kitten enters a room with any heroes in it, it deals 1 point of physical damage to one hero in that room.
- At the start of The Cat's turn, count the unsoothed Kittens in the house, divide by 2 and round down. The Cat's Speed and Might are increased by that number for the rest of that turn.

#### Special attack rules
- If the traitor is attacked, the traitor may put a Kitten in any room that does not have a Kitten in it.

> Note: This rule is printed directly under The Cat's stat block, but it is addressed to "you" (the traitor), like the rest of the traitor's book. Read literally it triggers when the traitor is attacked; it may be meant to trigger when The Cat is attacked.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or
- The Cat leaves the house through the front door in the Entrance Hall.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to soothe the Kittens so they forget about world domination.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside pentagonal item tokens equal to the number of heroes; these represent Ingredients.
- Also set aside one pentagonal Item Pile token; it represents Cat Food.
- If the Kitchen is not in play yet, search the room stack for it and place it in the house, then shuffle the room stack.
### Rules
#### How to soothe the Kittens
- A hero can stun and soothe a Kitten by succeeding at a Sanity roll of 4+.
- A soothed Kitten stays soothed for the rest of the game (this is unlike normal stunning, which wears off).
#### How to calm the Cat
- Heroes must collect Ingredients around the house. Once during their turn, a hero in a room with an omen symbol [raven symbol] may attempt a Knowledge roll of 3+ to find an Ingredient.
- When Ingredients equal to the number of heroes are brought to the Dining Room, Kitchen, or Menagerie, the Cat Food is complete: replace those Ingredients with the one Cat Food token.
- To stop The Cat, the heroes must bring the Cat Food to The Cat and convince it to eat dinner. This takes a successful Sanity attack by a hero against The Cat.
- For each other hero in the same room, the attacking hero rolls 1 additional die on that Sanity roll.
### Heroes win when
- They convince The Cat to eat dinner (the successful Sanity attack above).
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is trying to bring as many Kittens as possible into the house.
- The heroes must soothe the roaming Kittens and make the Cat Food dinner that calms The Cat.
