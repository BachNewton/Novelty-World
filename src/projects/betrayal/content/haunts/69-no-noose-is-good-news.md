# 69 — No Noose is Good News

Summary: The traitor is possessed by the spirit of an Old West Hangman who wants everyone in the house hanged; each hero must win their own gallows game (a game of hangman) to be protected.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- Get as many pieces of paper as there are heroes in the house, and draw a Hangman game gallows on each.
- Choose a theme for the Hangman words and phrases. They must be titles, names, or common words and phrases, and the theme must be told to all players. Example: "Movie Titles."
- In secret, write down a word or phrase in that theme for each hero, at most four words and twelve letters in total.
- Under each gallows, write the blanks (not the letters) for that hero's word or phrase, leaving a space between words.
- Speak in a Wild West voice for the rest of the game.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Instead of attacking, the traitor may try to hang a hero: a Might attack against any hero in the traitor's room, in which neither side can take damage. If the traitor wins, a noose goes around the hero's neck. Until the hero breaks free, that hero may not guess any letters and rolls 2 fewer dice against attacks.
- If the traitor is in the Roof Landing, the Upper Landing, or the Foyer, they may instead make a Speed attack against a hero on the floor below them.
  - Trace a path from the traitor's landing to the hero's room using both landings.
  - Roll 1 fewer die for each room between the traitor and the hero, not counting either of their rooms.
  - There must be an unbroken string of unlocked doors between the traitor and the hero.
  - Example: on the Roof Landing, attacking a hero in a room connected to the Upper Landing, the traitor rolls 1 fewer die because the Upper Landing lies between them.

> Note: The page does not say what a successful Speed attack from a landing does (whether it places a noose, deals damage, or both). How a hero breaks free of the noose is presumably in Secrets of Survival.

#### How to Play the Gallows Game
- Once on each of their turns, a hero may guess a letter in their own word or phrase.
- If the guess is right, the traitor writes that letter in every blank where it appears.
- If not, the traitor draws a body part hanging from the gallows, in this order: head, body, left arm, right arm, left leg, right leg. Each time a body part is drawn, that hero takes 1 die of physical damage.
- If a hero's Hangman drawing is a complete person (six wrong guesses) and the traitor is in line of sight of that hero, that hero is killed.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- Every hero is on the traitor's execution list, and they will play Hangman to try to win back their lives.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to play the gallows game
- On your turn, you may spend all of your movement to guess one letter toward solving your own individual gallows game.
- For each incorrect guess, the Hangman draws a body part on the gallows and you take 1 die of physical damage.
- For each correct guess, the Hangman must fill in that letter in every position where it appears on your Hangman card and show the progress to all heroes.
- When a hero solves their game, that hero is protected from the gallows.
#### How to break free of a noose
- The Hangman may use a noose to prep you for the gallows.
- If you are caught in a noose at the start of your turn, you may attempt a Might roll of 4+ to break free.
### Heroes win when
- All living heroes are protected from the gallows.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is possessed by the spirit of an Old West hangman, who will not rest until everyone in the house is hanging from a noose.
- Heroes who play the gallows game better than the Hangman will not hang.
> Note: The two halves disagree on the damage for a wrong guess: the Traitor's Tome says the hero takes 1 die of physical damage each time a body part is drawn, while Secrets of Survival says 1 point of physical damage. The text above carries the official correction.
> Resolution (official): errata. It is 1 die of physical damage, as in the Traitor's Tome; the FAQ changes Secrets of Survival's "1 point of physical damage" to "1 die of physical damage". Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
> Note: The two halves also frame the guess differently: the Traitor's Tome allows a guess once on each of the hero's turns, and Secrets of Survival says the guess costs all of that hero's movement.
> Note: The heroes' half does not say what being caught in a noose prevents, or what happens when a gallows drawing is completed; those are presumably in the Traitor's Tome.
