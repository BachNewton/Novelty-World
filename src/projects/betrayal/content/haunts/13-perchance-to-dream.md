# 13 — Perchance to Dream

Summary: An explorer has fallen into an unwakeable sleep and its nightmares are escaping into the world; the heroes must wake the dreamer with the Holy Symbol before too many Nightmares get out.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Tip your explorer's figure over in the room it is in when the haunt is revealed. Your body is asleep: it cannot move or take any actions.
- Drop all your items.
- If you have the Dog, the Girl or the Madman card, set it aside and adjust your traits to match. This adjustment cannot kill you: if it would, reduce the affected trait to its lowest value above the skull symbol instead.
- Put small purple monster tokens, one per player (the number of tokens equals the number of players), into the room with your sleeping body. They are Nightmares.
- Secretly count the "escape rooms" in the house:
  - Rooms with outside-facing windows count: Grand Staircase, Master Bedroom, Bedroom, Chapel, Dining Room.
  - Rooms that open to the outside also count: Conservatory, Entrance Hall, Gardens, Graveyard, Patio, Tower, Balcony.
  - A room still counts as an escape room even if it is placed so its window is false (faces another wall).
  - If there are fewer escape rooms in the house than there are players, search the room stack and add rooms from the lists above to any appropriate floor until the number of escape rooms equals the number of players. Then shuffle the room stack.
  - Write down the number of escape rooms and keep it secret from the other players.
- Set aside escape tokens equal to the number of escape rooms in the house. Any distinctive set of tokens (for example, item tokens) can serve.
### Monsters
- Nightmares — Speed 5 · Might 4 · Sanity 4
### Rules
#### How Nightmares Escape
- A Nightmare in an escape room escapes from the house by spending 1 space of movement.
- Once a Nightmare has escaped from a room, no other Nightmare can escape from that room. Put an escape token in the room to mark it as used.
- Escape rooms discovered after the haunt starts can also be used by Nightmares to escape, but they do not raise the total number of escapes you need.
- Whenever a Nightmare is killed or escapes the house, you can unleash another Nightmare: put a Nightmare token into the room with your figure. Tokens are reused as needed.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Nightmares can attack explorers using Might, but the damage they inflict is mental instead of physical.
- If a hero attacks a Nightmare and defeats it, the Nightmare is killed instead of stunned. If a Nightmare is defeated while it is the attacker, it is stunned as normal.
### Traitor wins when
- The number of Nightmares that have escaped equals the number of escape rooms that were in the house when the haunt was revealed (the number you wrote down). When this happens, show the heroes the number you wrote down.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to wake your physical body.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Sanity Roll tokens equal in number to the number of players.
- Set aside the same number of triangular Might Roll tokens.
### Rules
#### How to Wake the Dreamer
- The Holy Symbol must be brought to the dreamer's room and used to wake that explorer:
  1. Carry the Holy Symbol to the room with the dreamer's body in it.
  2. As long as a hero in that room carries the Holy Symbol, any hero in the room can attempt a Sanity roll or a Might roll of 5+ to wake the dreamer. After each success, take a Sanity Roll or Might Roll token (matching the trait used). The dreamer awakens when the heroes have tokens equal in number to the number of players.
  3. The Smelling Salts card can't be used to awaken the dreamer.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Nightmares inflict mental damage instead of physical damage.
- If a hero attacks a Nightmare and defeats it, the Nightmare is killed instead of being stunned.
### Heroes win when
- The heroes wake the dreamer before an unknown number of Nightmares escape into the world. (The traitor knows that exact number.)
### What they know about the other side
- The dreamer's subconscious mind has released Nightmares into the house. If enough Nightmares escape the house, the heroes lose.
- The heroes don't know how many need to escape, so they must stop as many as they can. The traitor has written that number down, based on rules in the Traitor's Tome.
