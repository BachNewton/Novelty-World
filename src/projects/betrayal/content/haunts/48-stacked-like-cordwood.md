# 48 — Stacked Like Cordwood

Summary: The traitor is in league with Crimson Jack, an unkillable supernatural serial killer; the heroes must find the one cursed weapon that can end him and learn how to use it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- Place the large circular Crimson Jack token in the Entrance Hall, right next to the front door.

### Monsters
- Crimson Jack — Speed 3 · Might 3 · Sanity 3
  - When defeated, Crimson Jack is not stunned and takes no damage; instead he is temporarily removed from the house. At the start of the traitor's next turn he returns: put his token in the Entrance Hall again.
  - Each time Crimson Jack returns, add 1 to each of his traits (write it down on paper).
  - Aura of fear: at the start of each hero's turn, if that hero is in Crimson Jack's room, the hero must make a Sanity roll of 3+; on a failure the hero loses 1 point from a mental trait and 1 point from a physical trait.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are looking for a weapon that can kill Crimson Jack.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Might Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
- Set aside an equal number of triangular Knowledge Roll tokens.
### Rules
#### How to Find the Cursed Weapon
- There is a cursed weapon in the house: the Axe, the Spear, the Blood Dagger, or the Sacrificial Dagger ("your choice").
> Note: The book says "(your choice)" without saying who chooses or when; read literally, the heroes pick which of the four is the cursed weapon. When searching, the hero likewise takes "a cursed weapon of your choice".
> Resolution (community): The heroes choose exactly one of the four weapons to be the cursed weapon, and they may choose one they already hold. Several independent answers agree. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1947196, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1585853, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/613682
- If the heroes don't have the cursed weapon, they must search for it. It is in one of these rooms: Library, Chapel, Vault, or Attic.
  - Once during their turn, a hero in any of those rooms may attempt a Knowledge roll of 3+ to find it. The Vault must be open.
  - On a success, search the appropriate stack for a cursed weapon of the hero's choice, take it, and shuffle that stack.
> Note: "the appropriate stack" is not named; the Spear is an omen card while the Axe, Blood Dagger and Sacrificial Dagger are item cards.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found on which stack to search. Searched the official 1st-edition FAQ and BGG threads on haunt 48 (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1947196, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1585853, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/613682, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1227225, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1735750). One answer adds that the heroes may also get the chosen weapon by any other means, such as a card that searches a stack (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1585853).
- The heroes must then work out how to use the cursed weapon against Crimson Jack. A hero in the same room as the cursed weapon may attempt a Might roll or a Knowledge roll of 5+ to study it. On a success, put a Might Roll or Knowledge Roll token (matching the trait used) on that hero's character card.
- When the heroes have collected tokens equal to the number of players, they understand how to use the cursed weapon to kill Crimson Jack.
- From then on, if any hero defeats Crimson Jack using the cursed weapon, Crimson Jack is permanently killed.
> Note: The book doesn't limit how often a hero may attempt the study roll per turn.
> Resolution (official (1st-edition FAQ)): Once per turn per hero. The 2nd-edition rulebook's Task Rolls rule (p. 13) allows only one roll toward a haunt task per turn, even if a different trait could be used, and the official 1st-edition FAQ says the same. BGG answers apply this to haunt-specific rolls: you can make a given type of roll only once per turn. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1301975, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2449430
#### You Must Do This at the beginning of your turn
- Crimson Jack gives off an aura of fear. At the beginning of their turn, a hero in the same room as Crimson Jack must succeed on a Sanity roll of 3+ or lose 1 point from a mental trait and 1 point from a physical trait.
### Heroes win when
- They find the cursed weapon and use it to kill Crimson Jack.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is working with Crimson Jack, a supernatural serial killer.
- Jack can be permanently killed only with a cursed weapon hidden in the house. If he is defeated any other way, he will return stronger than ever.
