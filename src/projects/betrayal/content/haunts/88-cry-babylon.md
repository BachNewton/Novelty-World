# 88 — Cry, Babylon!

Summary: The traitor has become Nebuchadnezzar, living avatar of the god Marduk, and the heroes must kill him with the help of the house's protective spirit, a Lammasu, which demands sacrifices.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Reset your traits to their starting values, then raise each trait by the number of heroes.

### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- When you are defeated, instead of taking damage you may reduce one of your physical traits by 1.
- When you kill a hero, gain 1 in each of your traits.

#### Special movement rules
- If you enter the room with the Lammasu, you immediately lose 1 from each trait.
- The Lammasu is then destroyed, but it materializes again in a room on another floor. You choose which floor; the heroes choose the room on that floor.
- The heroes must place it in a room at least 3 spaces from any hero and on a landing, if possible. Otherwise they place it as far from the heroes on that floor as they can.
> Note: The Lammasu's initial placement is not on this page; Secrets of Survival sets it up (the Cat token, on a floor other than the traitor's). This page says "3 spaces" where Secrets of Survival says "3 rooms". "At least 3 spaces away from any hero and a landing if possible" could also be read as "at least 3 spaces from any hero and from a landing"; the reading above (the room should be a landing) is the more likely one.

#### Special omen rules
- You cannot possess the Cat card.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes will try to stop you, the reincarnation of Nebuchadnezzar, from fully manifesting as the Babylonian god Marduk.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside the Cat card.
- Put the Cat token (representing the Lammasu) in a room on a floor other than the traitor's floor. If possible, put it in a room at least three rooms away from every hero, and on a landing. Otherwise put it as far from the heroes on that floor as possible.

### Allies
- Lammasu (the Cat token) — no stats printed in either book.

### Rules
#### How to Enlist the Lammasu's Aid
- The Lammasu, the house's protective spirit, is the heroes' ally, but it helps only in exchange for sacrifices.
- When you end your movement in the Lammasu's room, you may discard an item card or omen card (other than the Cat card) to put the Lammasu in the traitor's room. The traitor then loses 1 from each trait.
- If you do this, you may also take the Cat card from wherever it is.
- The Lammasu is then destroyed, but it materializes in a room on another floor. The traitor chooses the floor, and you place the Lammasu: if possible, in a room at least 3 rooms away from every hero and on a landing; otherwise as far from the heroes on that floor as possible.
- At the end of any turn in which you don't sacrifice a card to the Lammasu, set aside the Cat card if you have it.
> Note: Neither book says what holding the Cat card does in this haunt beyond the card's own text, or whether the Cat card counts as a card that can be sacrificed on a later turn (it is excluded only from the sacrifice that brings the Lammasu).

### Heroes win when
- They kill the walking god their friend has become.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has become Nebuchadnezzar, the living avatar of the Babylonian god Marduk, and wants to gain power by absorbing the heroes' souls.
> Note: "Put it in a room that is at least three rooms away from any hero and a landing if possible" could mean the room must be a landing, or must also be three rooms away from a landing. Read here as "on a landing".
