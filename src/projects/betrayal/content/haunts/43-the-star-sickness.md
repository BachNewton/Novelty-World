# 43 — The Star-Sickness

Summary: An alien virus from a meteorite is spreading among the explorers; a hidden traitor is infected and the heroes must brew and administer a serum before everyone turns into Aliens.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt uses the hidden traitor rules on page 17 of the rulebook.
- All players read this haunt in the Secrets of Survival booklet.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- This haunt uses the hidden traitor rules on page 17 of the rulebook. Everyone reads this booklet (Secrets of Survival), but one player is a traitor.
- Take a set of small monster tokens of one color, numbered from 1 up to twice the number of explorers, and mix them. Each player takes two, looks at them, and shows them to no one. Whoever has the token numbered 1 is the traitor.
- More traitors may appear as the haunt goes on. A player who becomes a traitor stays a traitor even if they later lose the token numbered 1.
- If the Research Laboratory is not in the house, the haunt revealer searches the room stack for that tile and places it in a legal position, then shuffles the room stack.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 1. It tracks time.
- Set aside a pile of triangular Knowledge Roll tokens and a pentagonal item token to represent the Serum.
### Monsters
- Aliens — Speed 4 · Might 6 · Sanity 4
  - Aliens are not affected by the disease, and cannot trade or be traded items or tokens.
  - Aliens are treated as monsters for movement and for taking damage.
### Rules
- Anyone holding the traitor token cannot reveal it at any time.
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- The first time each turn that you occupy a room with another explorer, the two of you must give each other one of your face-down monster tokens, chosen at random.
- If there are several other explorers in the room, exchange with only one; it must be an explorer you haven't exchanged with before, if possible.
- You can make only one exchange during each of your turns.
> Note: "The first time each turn that you occupy a room with another explorer" can be read as forcing an exchange every turn that two explorers keep sharing a room.
> Resolution (designer): Bruce Glassco, who playtested this haunt, said that reading was not his intent and that the rule was worded poorly. The exchange should trigger the first time each turn an explorer *enters* a room with another explorer, so explorers who stay together do not keep re-exchanging; to get another exchange, an explorer must leave and come back. This is a forum statement, not printed errata, so the rule text above is unchanged. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/649827
- If you receive the token numbered 1 in one of these exchanges, you permanently become a traitor, even if you trade that token away later.
#### The Haunt Revealer Must Do This
- At the end of the haunt revealer's turn each round:
  - Advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number.
  - Roll a number of dice equal to the turn number. This is a Might physical attack against every explorer in the house; each explorer resists with Might as normal.
  - Traitors are **not** immune to this attack. There is no upper limit to the number of dice rolled.
#### Creating the Serum
- Once per turn, an explorer in the Research Laboratory may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+. Add 1 die for each other explorer in the room, and 1 more die if the Book is present. On a success, put a Knowledge Roll token in the room.
- Explorers can also search for herbs in the Conservatory or Gardens, if those rooms are in the house. At the end of their turn in either room, an explorer may attempt a Knowledge roll of 3+. On a success, they take a Knowledge Roll token (representing herbs) and place it on their character card.
  - An herb token is treated as an item: it can be dropped, traded, or stolen.
  - An explorer may search for herbs every turn and may carry more than one Knowledge Roll token at a time.
- It is possible to collect more Knowledge Roll tokens than the number of players.
- Creating the Serum requires collecting, in the Research Laboratory, as many Knowledge Roll tokens as the number of explorers. At least one token must have been discovered in the lab, and at least one other must come from collected herbs. Whoever creates the Serum, or brings the last token to the room, takes the Serum token.
- An explorer carrying the Serum can inject it into themself, or into another willing explorer in the same room, by spending 1 space of movement.
- Once injected, an explorer who is not a traitor:
  - no longer exchanges face-down monster tokens with other explorers;
  - is no longer attacked by the alien disease (the haunt revealer's end-of-round attack).
- Any injected explorer whose traits are below their starting values has them restored to their starting values. (Unlike the two effects above, the book doesn't limit this to non-traitors.)
> Note: The book doesn't say what happens when a traitor accepts a willing injection (by spending 1 space of movement, not through combat): only the two effects above are explicitly withheld from traitors, and only the unwilling and transforming-traitor injections are said to kill.
> Resolution (community): The few answers agree that the Serum kills any traitor it is injected into, however it is given. An infected explorer should therefore never accept a willing injection, and the heroes have to inject them by force (a Speed attack). Players also note that the heroes must inoculate the traitors too, killing them, to meet their win condition. The support is thin: one unchallenged answer that the asker accepted, plus agreeing remarks in a second thread. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/779985, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/649827
- An explorer carrying the Serum can inject an unwilling explorer or an Alien in the same room by defeating that character in physical combat using Speed. Doing so kills the traitor or Alien. A traitor killed by injection is permanently dead and does not transform into an Alien.
- An explorer carrying the Serum who is in the same room as a transforming traitor can inject that traitor by spending 1 space of movement. This also kills the traitor permanently.
#### Special Attack Rules
- In this haunt, all explorers can attack each other.
- If a traitor dies for any reason other than being injected with the Serum, that player announces that they are a traitor and that a transformation to the third stage is happening.
  - The transforming traitor skips their next turn.
  - On that player's following turn, the explorer becomes an Alien and drops all their items (including Knowledge Roll tokens).
> Note: The rules say the Serum is injected into "an unwilling explorer or an Alien" and that "Doing so kills the traitor or Alien"; it does not say what happens if the unwilling explorer injected this way is not a traitor.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. Searched the official 1st-edition FAQ and BGG threads on haunt 43 (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/779985, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/649827, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2449430, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1939148, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1332962, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1268621). None of them discusses forcibly injecting an explorer who turns out not to be a traitor.
### Heroes win when
- The Serum has been created and all living explorers have been inoculated.
### Traitor wins when
- All the explorers are dead, or have become traitors or Aliens; then the traitor or traitors win.
- If the traitors win, the player who most recently became a traitor loses instead.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has been infected with an alien virus and seeks to turn everyone else into Aliens.
