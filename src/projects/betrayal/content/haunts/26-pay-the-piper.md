# 26 — Pay the Piper

Summary: The traitor has called swarms of Rats into the house and is heading to the Pentagram Chamber for a rat ritual; the heroes must kill every Rat or stop the traitor first.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- If any of the traitor's traits are below their starting values, restore them to their starting values now. Then add 1 to each trait.
- Set aside small red monster tokens (Rats) equal to twice the number of players. Put one Rat token in each unoccupied room that has any of the symbols [spiral symbol], [bull's-head symbol] or [raven symbol] (event, item or omen).
  - If there are more Rat tokens than eligible rooms, the traitor may double them up in rooms of the traitor's choice.
  - If there are fewer Rat tokens than eligible rooms, the traitor decides which rooms get them.
- Set aside 5 triangular Sanity Roll tokens.
> Note: The book shows only the three icons, not their names; the event (spiral), item (bull's head) and omen (raven) names are the standard symbol meanings.
> Resolution (official): confirmed. The rulebook glossary names the spiral as the event symbol, the bull's head as the item symbol and the raven as the omen symbol. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf (rulebook, Glossary)

### Monsters
- Rats — Speed 3 · Might 2 · Sanity 1

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- A defeated Rat is killed instead of stunned.
- Rats in the same room may attack together as a group: add their Might scores together for one large attack against a single target (maximum of 8 dice). A group of Rats that attacks this way and loses does not take damage.
- While in the Pentagram Chamber, the traitor cannot be affected by the heroes in any way. Neither the Rats nor the heroes can enter the Pentagram Chamber.

#### How to Complete the Ritual
- Move to the Pentagram Chamber. Once there, the traitor is safe while working on the ritual.
- While in the Pentagram Chamber, the traitor may attempt a Sanity roll of 3+ to perform the ritual. On a success, put a Sanity Roll token on the traitor's character card, and put a Rat token (if any are available) in a room adjacent to the Pentagram Chamber; that room does not need a connecting door.
- The number of successful Sanity rolls needed to complete the ritual depends on the number of players:

| Number of Players | Rolls Needed |
|---|---|
| 3–4 | 5 |
| 5–6 | 4 |

> Note: The book does not say how many ritual rolls the traitor may attempt per turn.
> Resolution (official): one per turn. The 2nd-edition rulebook says you can't attempt the same roll more than once per turn (its example is the Vault). The 1st-edition FAQ adds that a scenario's rolls of one kind can't be made more than once per turn. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf (rulebook, Attempt a Die Roll), http://web.archive.org/web/20151204092838/http://www.wizards.com:80/default.asp?x=ah/faqs/hoth

### Traitor wins when
- The traitor either kills all the heroes or completes the ritual.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to kill all the Rats in the house, which would stop the traitor from completing the ritual.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Before the traitor places Rat tokens in the house, any explorer in the Pentagram Chamber is moved to a room adjacent to it. That room does not need a connecting door.

### Rules

#### Special Attack Rules
- Inflicting damage on a single Rat kills it.
- While the traitor is in the Pentagram Chamber, the heroes cannot affect him or her in any way.
- Neither the Rats nor the heroes can enter the Pentagram Chamber.

### Heroes win when
- They destroy all the Rats, or
- They kill the traitor before he or she reaches the Pentagram Chamber.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is performing a rat ritual in the Pentagram Chamber.
- The ritual can only be stopped by killing every Rat in the house quickly.
- Swarms of Rats may attack together.
