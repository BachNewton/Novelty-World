# 87 — Sibling Rivalry

Summary: The traitor is possessed by two prank-loving sibling spirits and splits into two bodies, setting deadly pranks around the house after locking their old headmistress in the basement.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- The other side of your character card is your second Sibling. Your trait clips stay where they are, which may give different trait values. Your trait values cannot change from these values until the Headmistress is found.
- Put your explorer token (representing your second Sibling) in the house as far away from your figure as possible.
- Put the large circular Doctor token (representing the Headmistress) in any basement room.
- Set aside Obstacle tokens (representing Pranks) equal to twice the number of explorers.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- Each turn you must alternate which Sibling you move. At the start of each of your turns after the first, flip your character card.

#### Special movement rules
- If the heroes catch you and defeat you in combat, they may drag you along with them to be punished. At the start of your turn you automatically slip free and can move freely.

#### Special trait rules
- Your trait values cannot change until the spirit of the Headmistress is released from the basement.
> Note: Setup says the values are fixed "until the Headmistress is found", while this section says "until the spirit of the Headmistress is released from the basement". These may be the same event; Secrets of Survival doesn't define either.

#### How to pull a Prank
- Move to any room in the house and succeed at a Sanity roll of 3+ to put a Prank in that room.
- The number of Pranks in the house at once may not exceed twice the number of heroes.
- When a Prank is triggered, return it to your Obstacle pile; you may set it again later in another room.
> Note: Setup sets aside twice the number of explorers, but the in-house limit is twice the number of heroes.

#### Triggering Pranks
- When a hero enters a room with a Prank, roll 2 dice and describe the prank you set. On a result of:

| Result | Prank | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Duck! | Hero makes a Speed roll of 3+; on a failure, takes 1 point of physical damage. |
| 1 | Ouch! | Hero makes a Might roll of 4+; on a failure, takes 1 point of physical damage. |
| 2 | Ew! | Hero makes a Sanity roll of 3+; on a failure, stops moving. |
| 3 | Sticky! | Hero makes a Knowledge roll of 4+; on a failure, drops a random item or omen and can't pick it up again this turn. |
| 4 | What the? | Hero makes a roll of 5+ using any trait; on a failure, takes 1 point of mental damage. |

### Traitor wins when
- Your pranks have caused the deaths of all the heroes.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are uninvited guests who interfere with your pranks on the Headmistress, though they also give you new targets. You have shut the Headmistress, who keeps trying to stop your games, in the basement, and you don't want the heroes to free her.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside five triangular Sanity Roll tokens.

### Allies
- The Headmistress (the large circular Doctor token, placed in a basement room by the traitor) — no stats printed in either book.

### Rules
#### How to Perform the Exorcism
- Only the Headmistress knows how to perform the exorcism, so the heroes must lead her to a cleansing room.
- On your turn, you can pick up the Headmistress when you enter her room, move her token along with your figure, and leave her at any point during your movement.
- With the Headmistress in the Bathroom, Chapel, Graveyard, Organ Room or Solarium, you may attempt a Sanity roll of 6+ to conduct the ritual. If you succeed, put a Sanity Roll token in that room.
- Once the ritual has been performed, each Sibling must be cleansed by a hero, on that hero's turn, while the Sibling is in a room containing a Sanity Roll token. When that happens, remove that Sibling and the Sanity Roll token from the house.

> Note: the printed book doesn't say how the Headmistress moves, and says a Sibling is cleansed "by taking it to" a room with a Sanity Roll token, leaving open whether a Sibling already in such a room can be cleansed. The second and third bullets above carry the official corrections.
> Resolution (official): errata. You enter her room, then carry her with you as far as you want; and a hero can cleanse a Sibling whenever the hero is in a room with that Sibling and a Sanity Roll token on the hero's turn, without dragging it there. The FAQ adds the movement sentence after the first sentence of How to Perform the Exorcism and rewrites the first sentence of its second bullet. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

#### Special Attack Rules
- If you attack and defeat a Sibling, you may, instead of dealing damage, grip the Sibling by the ear and drag it along as you move.
- Until the caught Sibling leaves your room, any hero entering that room may automatically catch it and drag it by the ear.
- If the Headmistress is in your room, add 2 dice to your attack and defense rolls against the Siblings.

### Heroes win when
- The evil spirits of the Siblings are cleansed through exorcism.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes' friend has been possessed by Siblings in the house who play deadly pranks.
- The heroes must find the spirit of the Headmistress, who is trying to complete her exorcism of the Siblings but needs a living person to help her.
> Note: Neither book says how the Headmistress is found or released, how she moves, or how the heroes "lead" her. Owner to rule.
> Resolution (official): partly answered. A hero picks her up on entering her room and carries her during their movement (see How to Perform the Exorcism). The FAQ doesn't say how she is found or released. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
