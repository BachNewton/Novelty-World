# 6 — The Floating Eye

Summary: Aliens allied with the traitor are mind-controlling heroes and marching them aboard their Spaceship; the heroes must disable the Spaceship before it leaves with their friends.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- With 3 or 4 players, set aside one small orange monster token (an Alien). With 5 or 6 players, set aside two.
- Place the Alien token(s) in the room where the haunt was revealed, and a pentagonal item token (the Spaceship) there too.
- Put your explorer's figure on the Spaceship token. Your explorer is out of the game, together with all that character's items and omens, waiting to be transported.

### Monsters
- The Aliens — Speed 4 · Might 6 · Sanity 6

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Mind control: instead of a Might attack, an Alien can make a Sanity attack against every hero in its room, each resolved separately.
  - If a hero defeats the Alien in this special attack, neither side takes damage.
  - If the Alien defeats a hero, the hero takes no damage but comes under that Alien's control.
- Controlled heroes: on a controlled hero's turn, you move that hero toward the room with the Spaceship token. The controlled hero does not have to make the die rolls or take the damage that room tiles require. Controlled heroes cannot attack or take other actions.
- A controlled hero who has reached the Spaceship's room boards it at the start of their next turn, and is then out of the game.
- The heroes have a secret way to free one another from Alien control. Aliens can control a given person only once: once freed, a hero is immune to the Aliens' mind control.
- Aliens are immune to Speed attacks (such as those using the Revolver or Dynamite).
- A stunned Alien still keeps control of any heroes it has beaten in Sanity combat.

### Traitor wins when
- Every hero is either dead or on the Spaceship.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to avoid being abducted, and they have a way to free themselves from the Aliens' control.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Might Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- If a hero is under an Alien's control, the traitor moves that hero toward the room with the Spaceship. At the start of the hero's next turn after entering that room, the hero boards the Spaceship and is out of the game.
- A hero under an Alien's control can't attack or take any other actions.
#### Special Attack Rules
- A hero can free another hero from an Alien's mind control by attacking and defeating that hero, with normal combat or with Sanity combat using the Ring.
  - If the attacker defeats the controlled hero, that hero takes only half damage (rounded down).
  - If the controlled hero defeats the attacker, the attacker takes damage normally.
  - Once freed, a hero can't be controlled again.
- A hero can attempt a Might roll of 5+ to damage the Spaceship. On each success, put a Might Roll token next to the Spaceship token. When the Spaceship has Might Roll tokens next to it equal to the number of players, it is disabled and the heroes win.
- Aliens are immune to Speed attacks (for example, attacks with the Revolver or the Dynamite).
> Note: The Might roll against the Spaceship states no location requirement and no per-turn limit; presumably the hero must be in the Spaceship's room, but the page does not say so.
> Resolution (official / community): the per-turn limit is official. The 2nd-edition rulebook (pp. 12-13) forbids attempting the same roll more than once per turn and allows only one task roll per turn. The location is community, and sources disagree. Most BGG answers require the hero to be in the Spaceship's room, while one later post says the roll can be made anywhere. The same threads treat it as a trait roll, not an attack, so a hero may also attack that turn. ### Heroes win when
- They disable the Spaceship so it can't take off with their friends.
### What they know about the other side
- Aliens have a mind-control ability they can use to manipulate heroes, and they can use it on all the heroes in a room at once, so heroes should stay spread out where they can.
