# 53 — Till Morning Light

Summary: During an annual night of legal killing (the Surge), killers outside try to get into the house and the traitor wants to let them in; the heroes must survive until morning.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer is still in the game but has turned traitor.
- Set aside the small blue monster tokens (representing Surgers) numbered from 1 to 9.

### Monsters
- Surgers — Speed 3 · Might 6* · Sanity 3 · Knowledge 3
  - *A Surger gets +1 Might during any attack for each other Surger in the same room (maximum of 8 dice).

### Rules
#### How to let the Surgers into the house
- During your turn, in each ground floor room that has a door not connected to a room, you may roll 3 dice, with 1 fewer die for each hero in that room. On 3+, put a Surger in the room.
- If you are in the Entrance Hall and have the Key, you place that room's Surger automatically, without rolling.

> Note: The book does not say how many times per turn this can be done, or whether "in any ground floor room" requires you to be in that room (the Entrance Hall clause suggests you must be there). The text above carries the official correction.
> Resolution (official): errata. You may try once in each such ground floor room during your turn, so at most one Surger per room per turn, and you need not be in the room. Being in the Entrance Hall with the Key places that room's Surger without a roll. The FAQ rewrites How to Let the Surgers into the House to say "in each ground floor room". Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

#### You must do this on the monsters' turn
- At the end of the monster turn, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number. When the track reaches 5, the haunt ends.

#### Special movement rules
- Surgers cannot get through a Lock unless there are three or more Surgers in total on either or both sides of the Lock. If so, remove the Lock from the door.
- Surgers cannot use dumbwaiters [dumbwaiter symbol].

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 1. It tracks time.
- If the Panic Room is not in play yet, search the room stack for it and place it in the house, then shuffle the room stack.
- Put a Lock token on the door to the Panic Room.

### Monsters
- Surgers (no stats printed in this book)
  - See Special attack rules for how heroes remove them.

### Rules
#### How the Surgers get in
- The traitor can try to let a Surger into the house in any ground floor room that has a door not connected to another room.
- Heroes in that room make the traitor's attempt harder.
- The traitor starts with the Key, which makes it much easier to open the front door. The book suggests the heroes might want to take the Key from the traitor.
- Heroes can get through the Lock on the Panic Room door; the Surgers may have more trouble doing so.
#### Special attack rules
- When a hero attacks a Surger and wins by 3 or more, the Surger is removed instead of stunned.

### Heroes win when
- Morning arrives and you are still alive. Morning arrives when the Turn/Damage track reaches 5.

> Note: the heroes' book does not say how or when the Turn/Damage track advances, what roll the traitor makes to let a Surger in, or how the Lock is passed; those details are in the traitor's book.

### What they know about the other side
- It is the Surge, an annual festival of violence. The people outside want to kill the heroes, and the traitor thinks letting them in is a good idea.
