# 32 — Lost

Summary: The traitor, an alien, has carried the house off to its own dimension, whose corrosive air is slowly killing the heroes; they must play the right song on the pipe organ to send the house home.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- Set aside every room tile played in the house so far, except the starting tiles and any occupied rooms. Remove any Secret Passage, Secret Stairs or Wall Switch tokens that connect to a room that was set aside.
- If the Organ Room is not in the house, search the room stack for it and place it in the house attached to a starting tile of the traitor's choice.
- Shuffle together all the set-aside tiles plus all undrawn tiles from the room stack and the discard stack.
- The occupied room tiles stay in the house. Move each of them so it is next to the starting tile of its own floor.
- Set aside 5 triangular Knowledge Roll tokens.

### Rules
#### How to Prevent the Heroes' Escape
- The traitor can try to make the transporter controls "human-proof" by attempting a Knowledge roll of 4+ while in the Chapel, the Game Room, either Laboratory, or the Pentagram Chamber.
- On a success, put a Knowledge Roll token in that room.
- Each of those rooms can hold only one of these tokens.
- Tell the heroes that each Knowledge Roll token placed subtracts 3 from their Knowledge rolls to activate the transporter.

> Note: The book does not say how often the traitor may attempt this roll (for example, once per turn). It lists five eligible rooms (Chapel, Game Room, either Laboratory, Pentagram Chamber) and sets aside 5 tokens, which matches "either Laboratory" being read as two separate rooms.
> Resolution (official (1st-edition FAQ)): The general rules settle it. The 2nd-edition rulebook bars attempting the same roll more than once per turn, and the official 1st-edition FAQ adds that a haunt-specific roll may be made only once per turn even in different rooms. So the traitor gets one human-proofing roll per turn. Nothing addresses the room count: the revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome has the same "either Laboratory" wording. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf, https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to reactivate their dimensional transporter in the Organ Room and send the house back to their home.
- The heroes take damage every turn from the planet's poisonous atmosphere.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### You Must Do This
- At the start of each hero's turn, roll 2 dice. That hero loses the result from any one trait or any combination of traits.
#### How to Return the House
- The pipe organ in the Organ Room is a dimensional transporter.
- A hero in the Organ Room may attempt a Knowledge roll to play the right song. Each hero may try to play the organ once during their turn.
- The house goes home if the result reaches the target for the number of players:

| Total Players | Result Needed |
|---|---|
| 3 | 15+ |
| 4 | 16+ |
| 5 | 18+ |
| 6 | 20+ |

#### Clues
- Heroes can explore the house to find clues. A clue, once found, benefits every hero who tries to play the organ. Each clue can be found only once.
- Add 1 to the organ result for each room in the house with an omen symbol [raven symbol].
- Add 2 if the hero playing the organ has the Music hobby.
- Add 2 once the music books in the Library have been found: once during their turn in the Library, a hero may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ to find them.
- Add 2 once a hero in the Game Room succeeds at a Sanity roll of 5+ (studying the exotic trophy animals to work out which dimension this is).
- Add 2 once a hero in the Tower succeeds at a Knowledge roll of 5+ (locating the new planet from the stars).
- Add 2 for as long as the Madman is in the Organ Room.
- Add 2 for as long as the Book is in the Organ Room.

### Heroes win when
- The house is returned to the heroes' own dimension.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is an alien who has transported the house to its home dimension.
- The corrosive atmosphere will slowly kill the heroes.

> Note: The book does not say how often the Game Room and Tower rolls may be attempted (only the Library search says "once during your turn in that room"). The Music-hobby bonus applies only while the hobby holder is the one playing; the book phrases it as a bonus "if you're playing the organ and have the Music hobby", so it is per-player rather than a found clue.
> Resolution (official (1st-edition FAQ)): Each clue roll may be attempted at most once per turn. The 2nd-edition rulebook bars attempting the same roll more than once per turn, and the official 1st-edition FAQ applies the same limit to haunt-specific rolls. No ruling was found on the Music hobby: the revised 1st-edition Secrets of Survival keeps the same per-player wording, and the only forum answer just says Professor Longfellow's Gaelic Music hobby counts.
