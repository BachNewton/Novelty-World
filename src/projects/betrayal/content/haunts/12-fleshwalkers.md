# 12 — Fleshwalkers

Summary: Each hero's evil doppelganger arrives through the front door; with no traitor, the heroes must survive and destroy all the Evil Twins, which only the Crystal Ball's holder can truly kill.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor, only heroes.
- All the heroes must cooperate to destroy their Evil Twins.
- If your hero is killed, you can still control your Evil Twin and kill other heroes.
- The rest of this haunt's rules are in Secrets of Survival.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Place small monster tokens (representing Evil Twins) in the Entrance Hall, equal in number to the number of players. Use tokens of different colors, each corresponding to a specific hero.
- The player to the left of the haunt revealer goes first.
### Monsters
- Evil Twins — no printed stat line; each Evil Twin's trait values equal its counterpart hero's trait values at the start of the haunt.
  - Those traits don't change.
  - Evil Twins can't carry items or take custody of companions.
### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor; every player is a hero.
#### The Evil Twins Must Do This . . .
- The Evil Twins act on the monster turn, which comes after the haunt revealer's turn.
- An Evil Twin always rolls for movement as a monster and moves toward its matching hero by the shortest possible route.
- If an Evil Twin ends its turn in a room with any hero, it attacks.
- If it is in a room with more than one hero, it always attacks its own counterpart if possible. Otherwise, choose randomly which hero it attacks.
- If a player's hero is killed, that player takes control of that hero's Evil Twin and uses it to attack other explorers.
#### Special Attack Rules
- If you don't possess the Crystal Ball when you attack or defend against your own Evil Twin, you lose 1 additional point in each of your traits, no matter who defeats whom. If your Evil Twin defeats you, you also take damage normally. If you defeat your Evil Twin, you stun it as normal.
- If you fight someone else's Evil Twin, you do not lose the additional point.
- If you possess the Crystal Ball, you kill your Evil Twin if you defeat it.
- If you defeat another hero's Evil Twin, you stun it, "unless" you possess the Crystal Ball and that hero is dead; in that case you kill the Evil Twin when you defeat it.
- The holder of the Crystal Ball can attack stunned Evil Twins. A stunned monster defends itself with its regular number of dice, but inflicts no damage if it rolls higher than its attacker.
- On your turn, you may take the Crystal Ball from another hero in the same room if that hero is willing to let you have it.
### Heroes win when
- Your hero is alive and all of the Evil Twins are dead.
> Note: Written per hero ("your hero is alive"); the book does not say further how this applies to players whose heroes have died and who now control an Evil Twin.

### What they know about the other side
- There is no traitor, just heroes. The Evil Twins are just like the heroes, but they want to kill them.
