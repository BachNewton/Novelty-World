# 15 — Here There Be Dragons

Summary: The traitor has summoned a fire-breathing Dragon into the house; the heroes must gather the Antique Armor, Shield and Spear and slay it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Put the large circular Dragon token in the Entrance Hall.
- Put a pentagonal item token (representing the Shield) in the Chasm or the Crypt.
- Put a pentagonal item token with a different number (representing the Antique Armor) in the Catacombs or the Underground Lake.
- If neither room for an item has been discovered yet, place that item in whichever of its two rooms is discovered first.
- Get out the Turn/Damage track and a plastic clip, but do not mark a number yet. It is used to track damage to the Dragon.
### Monsters
- The Dragon — Speed 3 · Might 8 · Sanity 6
  - Use the Turn/Damage track to record the damage heroes inflict on the Dragon. When its damage equals the number of players, the Dragon is killed.
  - Taking damage does not lower the Dragon's traits.
  - The Dragon is immune to Speed attacks (for example, those made with the Revolver and the Dynamite).
  - An explorer using the Ring can damage the Dragon with a Sanity attack.
### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- On its turn, the Dragon can attack twice: once with "fire breathing" and once by "biting". The two attacks need not happen at the same time, and they can come in either order.
- Fire Breathing: every explorer (you included) in the Dragon's room, or in a room adjacent to it, must attempt a Speed roll. An adjacent room counts only if it has a connecting door to the Dragon's room.

| In room with the Dragon | |
|---|---|
| 4+ | No damage. |
| 0–3 | Take 4 dice of physical damage. |

| In an adjacent room | |
|---|---|
| 4+ | No damage. |
| 0–3 | Take 2 dice of physical damage. |

- Biting: a Might attack.
- Toughness: whenever the Dragon is defeated, it takes 2 less damage.
### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to slay the Dragon.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Slay the Dragon
- The Dragon is so tough that the heroes need the Antique Armor (a pentagonal item token), the Shield (another pentagonal item token) and the Spear card to have much chance of success. The Antique Armor and Shield are both somewhere in the basement.
- Antique Armor:
  - A unique item, different from the Armor item card. You can't wear the Antique Armor and the Armor at the same time.
  - It can't be stolen with a special attack.
  - Putting on the Antique Armor, or taking it off and giving it to another character, uses a full turn: you can't move or do anything else during that turn.
  - While wearing it, you take 5 less physical damage from attacks.
  - It is heavy: you move 1 fewer space during your turn while wearing it. (You can always move at least 1 space every turn.)
  - It doesn't protect you against fire or heat damage.
- Shield:
  - While carrying the Shield, you are immune to fire and heat damage.
  - It is very heavy: you move 1 fewer space each turn while carrying it. If you both wear the Antique Armor and carry the Shield, you move 2 fewer spaces each turn. (You can always move at least 1 space.)
  - Every hero in the same room as the hero carrying the Shield is immune to the Dragon's fire breathing attack.
- Spear:
  - In addition to the Spear's Might bonus, when you attack or defend against the Dragon with a Might attack, add 4 to the result of your roll.
#### Special Attack Rules
- If you are hit by the Dragon's fire breathing attack, you can choose to discard an item and take 2 less physical damage. You can do this with several items at once, each one reducing the damage taken by 2.
- The traitor keeps track of the amount of damage the heroes inflict on the Dragon and announces when it has been slain.
### Heroes win when
- The heroes slay the dragon.
### What they know about the other side
- The Dragon wants to kill all the heroes. It can breathe fire and bite.
- The heroes will need special weapons and armor to defeat it.
