# 23 — Tentacled Horror

Summary: A creature lurking at the house's heart reaches through its rooms with Tentacles to drag the explorers to it and eat them; the traitor, already dead, controls the creature.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer is dead. Remove the figure from the game and drop all of the explorer's items.
- Set aside numbered pairs of small orange monster tokens (Arms) and small green monster tokens (Suckers), as many pairs as there are players. Each Arm/Sucker pair is one Tentacle.
- Put one Arm token in any of the following **Tentacle rooms**: Furnace Room, Conservatory, Organ Room, Underground Lake, Gardens, and Chasm.
  - If fewer Tentacle rooms are in play than there are players, search the room stack and take Tentacle rooms until there are as many as there are players, then connect the new rooms on appropriate floors. Shuffle the room stack.
  - No tokens can be saved for later.
> Note: The wording is "Put one Arm token in any of the following Tentacle rooms"; together with "as many [rooms] as there are players" and "you cannot save any tokens for later", this reads as one Arm token per Tentacle room until all Arms are placed.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found on whether two Arms may share a Tentacle room. Community answers only confirm one Arm/Sucker pair per player, traitor included. The 1st-edition revised tome has the same structure, with Roots in place of Arms. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1919508, http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf
- Put a Sucker token in each room that has an Arm token.
> Note: The book does not say outright that the Sucker placed must be the one numbered to match that Arm, but later rules refer to each Sucker's "matching Arm".
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. Players treat each Arm and Sucker as a fixed numbered pair (one Tentacle), but nobody addresses the setup wording directly. The 1st-edition revised tome says "For each Root, put a Tip in the same room". Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1919508, http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 1 to track time.

### Monsters
- Suckers — stats depend on the turn, as on the Turn/Damage track:

| Turn | Speed | Might | Sanity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Turn 1–2 | 2 | 4 | 7 |
| Turn 3–4 | 3 | 5 | 7 |
| Turn 5–7 | 3 | 7 | 7 |
| Turn 8+ | 4 | 8 | 8 |

  - The Tentacles grow stronger the longer the creature lives.
  - If a Sucker enters the Mystic Elevator, the Elevator does not work until the Sucker leaves.
  - Arms do not slow hero movement, but Suckers do.
- Head — Might 6
  - The creature's Head can attack heroes. It takes no damage when defeated in combat. The heroes will tell the traitor what happens in that case.
> Note: The book prints only a Might value for the Head.
> Resolution (official): a trait that isn't printed can't be attacked. The rulebook says you can't attack an opponent with a trait it doesn't have, so the Head can't be hit with Speed or Sanity attacks. Community answers agree that Dynamite (which affects only targets with Might and Speed) does nothing to it. They are split on whether the Revolver can still be used as a plain Might attack. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf (rulebook, Make an Attack), https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1243908, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2898670, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1191949

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Arms do not move and cannot attack or be attacked. Suckers can do both.
- If a Sucker defeats a hero in physical combat, the hero takes no damage. Instead the hero is grabbed and drops all items, which stay in that room. The Sucker then ends its movement.
- No other Sucker can attack a grabbed hero.
- When a Sucker starts a turn grabbing a hero, it moves 2 spaces that turn toward its matching Arm instead of moving normally. It may take any path back to the Arm. A Sucker carrying a hero cannot attack.
- If a hero grabbed by a Sucker starts a monster turn in the room with that Sucker's matching Arm, the hero is eaten and killed. Remove that Tentacle (Arm and Sucker) from the game.
- If a Sucker is defeated by any attack during a hero's turn, it is stunned and retreats: it drops any hero it is grabbing, and its token goes back into the same room as its matching Arm.
- The Bell has no effect on grabbed heroes. The Spirit Board has no effect on Suckers.

#### You must do this
- At the end of each of the traitor's turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are looking for the creature's Head to kill it. The traitor must stop them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside 3 triangular Might Roll tokens.

### Rules

#### How to Destroy the Creature
- The heroes must find the Head of the Tentacled Horror and destroy it, as follows.
- The hero carrying the Crystal Ball must use it to find the Head's location.
  - Gazing into it takes a Knowledge roll of 4+. A failed roll brings the negative consequences listed on the Crystal Ball card.
  - On a success, instead of searching for a card, roll 4 dice and look up the Head's room in the table below.
- After the Crystal Ball has been used to find the Head, it shatters; discard the card.
- If the room rolled has not been discovered yet, search the room stack for it and give it to the traitor, who connects it to any unexplored doorway on an appropriate floor. Then shuffle the room stack.
- Place a small monster token in that room to represent the Head.

| Result | Room |
|---|---|
| 0 | Larder |
| 1 | Kitchen |
| 2 | Organ Room |
| 3 | Chasm |
| 4–5 | Underground Lake |
| 6 | Conservatory |
| 7 | Crypt |
| 8 | Furnace Room |

> Note: The book does not say what happens if the rolled room is already in the house; presumably the Head token is simply placed there.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The haunt books only cover the undiscovered case, and players simply place the Head in the rolled room when it is already in play. Searched: BGG haunt 23 threads (1098087, 1152560, 1919508, 2265007, 2656317, 2898670, 1243908), the 1st-edition revised Secrets of Survival. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1152560, http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf

#### Special Attack Rules
- Arms cannot attack or be attacked. Suckers can attack and be attacked normally.
- If a Sucker defeats a hero in physical combat, the hero takes no damage. Instead the hero is grabbed by that Sucker and drops all items, which stay in that room. The Sucker's movement then ends.
- When a hero defeats a Sucker in physical combat, the Sucker is stunned and retreats: it drops any hero it was grabbing, and its Sucker token is moved to the same room as its matching Arm token.
- Arms do not slow hero movement; only Suckers do.
- The creature's Head can attack heroes.
- Whenever the Head would take damage, place a triangular Might Roll token in that room instead. When the heroes have placed all 3 tokens, they destroy the Head.

#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- This applies to a hero who is grabbed.
- A grabbed hero cannot use items but can still attack the Sucker that is grabbing him or her.
- If the hero defeats the Sucker, the Sucker drops the hero and retreats. For the rest of that turn, each room the hero moves into costs 2 spaces of movement.
- If the hero ties or is defeated, the hero takes no damage, but the hero's turn is over.

### Heroes win when
- They destroy the creature.

### What they know about the other side
- A Tentacled Horror is hunting the heroes.
- Each of its Tentacles is represented by an Arm token and a Sucker token.
- A hero pulled by a Sucker to its matching Arm will be killed at the start of the next monster turn unless rescued first.
- The Tentacles grow stronger over time.
