# 45 — Tick, Tick, Tick

Summary: A demolitionist traitor has strapped a time bomb to every hero and is building a Big Bomb; the heroes must defuse their bombs and kill the traitor first.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- If the traitor doesn't have the Dynamite card, the traitor takes it from the explorer who has it, or else searches the item stack for it, takes it, and shuffles that stack.
- Put a plastic clip on the Turn/Damage track at 0. It tracks time.

### Rules
#### The Big Bomb
- The Big Bomb is complete on Turn 12. It then explodes and everyone dies.
#### You Must Do This (at the end of the traitor's turn)
- Advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number (the first advance takes it to 1).
- Add that number to the number of heroes still alive, and roll that many dice (maximum 8 dice).
- On a result of 8+, the time bomb of the closest living hero to the traitor's left who still has an active time bomb explodes. That hero is killed, together with every other hero in the same room. All items and omens those heroes carried are destroyed (their cards are discarded).
#### Special Attack Rules
- The traitor cannot move, and must stay in whatever room they were in when the haunt began (working on the Big Bomb). If that room is the Mystic Elevator, the elevator no longer moves.
- Proximity trigger: after the end of the traitor's first turn, any explorer wearing a time bomb who enters the traitor's room, or a room adjacent to the traitor's room, explodes at once, along with all of that character's items and omens. The adjacent rooms don't need connecting doors. Every hero in the same room as the exploding explorer (and their items and omens) also explodes. The traitor is not affected by these explosions.
- Once a hero's bomb has been deactivated, that hero cannot explode from the traitor's die roll or from moving next to the traitor. The traitor must then either wait for the Big Bomb to go off or attack that hero if he or she enters the traitor's room.
- The traitor can use the Dynamite in their own room (instead of using it to attack an adjacent room with a connecting door), and is not affected by it.
> Note: "Explodes" for heroes caught by the proximity trigger is not further defined here; by the end-of-turn rule an explosion kills the hero and destroys the carried items and omens.
> Resolution (community): A proximity explosion has the same effect as the end-of-turn roll. The hero and every other hero in that room die, their items and omens are destroyed, and the traitor is unaffected. Only a hero's own failed defuse roll (2 or less) kills every explorer in the room, the traitor included. This comes from one detailed, unchallenged answer by a frequent rules answerer. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2141761

### Traitor wins when
- Either all the heroes are dead, or the traitor completes the Big Bomb.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor strapped a time bomb to each hero. The heroes are trying to defuse the bombs.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Each hero takes a pentagonal item token, representing a Time Bomb.
### Rules
#### How to Stop the Traitor
- At least one hero needs to have their Time Bomb defused so they can kill the traitor before the Big Bomb explodes.
- Once during their turn, a hero may attempt a Knowledge roll of 7+ to deactivate the Time Bomb strapped to them. If the hero has the Madman card, they need only 5+ on this roll.
  - If the bomb is deactivated, discard that hero's Time Bomb token.
  - If the result of the roll is 2 or less, the hero activates the bomb: all explorers in the same room explode and are killed, and all their item and omen cards are discarded.
- Instead of trying to deactivate their own bomb, a hero may attempt to defuse another hero's bomb, if both are in the same room and that hero gives permission. The same Knowledge roll of 7+ (5+ with the Madman) applies, as does the penalty for rolling 2 or less.
- The traitor will keep working on the Big Bomb until killed.
### Heroes win when
- The traitor is dead and at least one hero survives.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is a demolitionist who has rigged each hero with a Time Bomb. The heroes don't know how much time they have left.
- The traitor holds a trigger that can detonate the bombs, but only when the traitor is in the hero's room or a room adjacent to it. The adjacent room does not need a connecting door.
- The bombs are also ticking, so they might go off if enough time passes.
- The traitor is working on the Big Bomb.
