# 55 — She Is Not Amused

Summary: The traitor is revealed as Medusa, who petrifies people; the heroes must use an Image in the Mirror card to cut off her head, freeing petrified soldiers to help along the way.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer is still in the game but has turned traitor.
- If the Statuary Corridor is not yet in play, search the room stack for it and place it in the house. Then shuffle that stack.
- Put your figure in the Statuary Corridor.
- Put one small orange monster token (representing Statues) face-down in each room with an item symbol [horned skull icon] or omen symbol [raven icon].
- If the Armor card is not yet in play, search the item stack and discard pile for it and take it. Then shuffle the item discard pile into the item stack.
- Gain 2 Might and 1 Speed.
- Set aside triangular Might Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes.
- Set aside triangular Sanity Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes.

### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- If your attack deals physical damage, give that explorer a triangular Might Roll token. That explorer loses 1 Might at the end of each of his or her turns, except while petrified.
- On your turn, if no explorer is in your room, you may gaze: choose an exit of your room, and every hero in line of sight must succeed at a Sanity roll of 4+ or be turned to stone. Give each explorer turned to stone a triangular Sanity Roll token. That explorer may not move or take an action until no longer petrified.
- You may not attack and gaze on the same turn.
- You cannot attack a petrified hero, or a Statue that has not become a Hoplite.

> Note: the printed book doesn't say whether Medusa may attack a petrified hero or an unflipped Statue. The last bullet above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. She can attack neither; the FAQ adds the sentence to the end of the second Special Attack Rules bullet. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

> Note: This half does not say how a hero stops being petrified or loses the Might Roll token; that is presumably in Secrets of Survival. The Statue tokens are placed but not otherwise used in this half.

#### How you regenerate
- At the end of your turn, if your Might is below its starting value and you have not taken physical damage this turn from an explorer who has a mirror, gain 1 Might.

> Note: "starting value" is not defined further (the character's printed starting Might, or the value after this haunt's +2 Might).

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead or turned to stone.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are descendants of Perseus and must be petrified.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Allies
- Hoplite — Speed 5 · Might 5
  - Statues (face-down monster tokens showing an "S") become Athenian Hoplite soldiers when flipped face up (see How petrification works).
  - A Hoplite ignores all negative room and card text, and counts as a monster under the controlling hero's control while that hero moves it and attacks with it, but is otherwise not a monster.

> Note: the printed book says only that Hoplites ignore negative room and card text, leaving open whether they are monsters. The text above carries the official correction.
> Resolution (official): errata. The FAQ (which labels this haunt 59 by mistake) replaces the sentence in How Petrification Works with: a Hoplite ignores all negative room and card text, and is treated as a monster under your control while you move and attack with it, but is otherwise not a monster. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

### Rules
#### How petrification works
- Medusa can petrify heroes, turning them to stone.
- On their turn, a hero who is not petrified, in a room with a petrified hero, may attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to unpetrify that hero.
- Statues are face-down monster tokens that show an "S". Whenever Medusa takes physical damage, every explorer in a room with a face-down monster token may attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to unpetrify that Statue. On a success, flip the token face up; it is now an Athenian Hoplite soldier.
- At the start of their turn, a hero in a room with a face-up Hoplite can send it after Medusa.
- If a Hoplite reaches Medusa, it may make a Might attack. If it does not reach her, it stays where it is until a hero reaches it and sends it again.
#### Special attack rules
- Medusa can be killed only by an explorer who deals enough physical damage to kill the traitor while holding an Image in the Mirror card.
- While you have an Image in the Mirror card, add 2 dice to your rolls when attacking and when defending against Medusa. You also gain these dice against Medusa's gaze.

> Note: the printed book doesn't say whether the mirror's 2 dice also apply to the Sanity roll against Medusa's gaze. The last sentence above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. They do; the FAQ (which labels this haunt 59 by mistake) adds the sentence to the end of the mirror paragraph of Special Attack Rules. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
- Medusa can poison you, giving you a Might Roll token, which drains your Might. If you have one or more Might Roll tokens, remove them all when you gain Might from a card or a room.
#### Special event rules
- If you discover a room with an [spiral event symbol] and you do not have one of the two Image in the Mirror cards, roll 3 dice.
  - On 3 or more, you may search the event stack and its discard pile for an Image in the Mirror card instead of drawing an event, and put it in front of you. Then shuffle the event stack and its discard pile together.
  - That event card counts as an item from then on.
- If both Image in the Mirror cards are already in front of explorers, ignore this rule.

### Heroes win when
- You sever Medusa's head.

> Note: the heroes' book does not say how Hoplites are moved when sent after Medusa (e.g. their movement per turn), what happens when a hero is petrified, or exactly how a Might Roll token drains Might; those are presumably in the traitor's book. The book misspells "poison" as "poision".
> Note: "If you do not have one of the two Image in the Mirror cards" can be read as "you hold neither card"; the trailing clause (ignore the rule once both are in front of explorers) suggests a hero may get one only if they don't already hold one.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is Medusa, one of the Gorgon sisters, with venomous snakes for hair and the power to petrify with a glance.
