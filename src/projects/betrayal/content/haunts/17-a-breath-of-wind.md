# 17 — A Breath of Wind

Summary: A Poltergeist is flinging the house's junk at the heroes; they must find Candles and burn them around the floor where the haunt began to exorcise it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Place a small purple monster token (representing the Poltergeist) in the room where the haunt was revealed.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 4.
### Monsters
- The Poltergeist — Speed 3 · Might X · Sanity 4
  - Unlike a regular monster, the Poltergeist can pick up, steal, trade and drop any number of items, just as an explorer can. It ignores all the normal effects of the items it carries.
  - Its Might starts at 4. Each time it picks up an item, advance the Turn/Damage track by 1.
  - At the end of each monster turn, if the Poltergeist is in the Junk Room, Storeroom, Attic, Library, Wine Cellar, Research Laboratory or Operating Laboratory, draw an item card for it and advance the Turn/Damage track by 1.
  - Each time the Poltergeist loses an item, lower the Turn/Damage track by 1.
### Rules
#### Helping the Poltergeist
- You can help the Poltergeist by attacking your fellow explorers, or by finding or stealing items for it.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Each turn, the Poltergeist can make a separate Might attack against each hero it reaches, but never more than one attack against the same hero.
- When it attacks, the Poltergeist's Might equals the current value of the Turn/Damage track, to a maximum of 8.
- If its attack would inflict 2 or more points of damage, it can choose to steal an item instead (and that item adds to its Might).
- The Poltergeist is not harmed if the hero wins the combat.
- The Poltergeist is immune to Might attacks and cannot be harmed by the Revolver.
- If the Dynamite explodes in the Poltergeist's room, the Poltergeist drops every item it carries and is removed from play. It can re-form on its next turn (see below).
#### Re-Forming the Poltergeist
- At the start of each monster turn, the Poltergeist may choose to re-form in any room with an omen symbol [raven symbol].
- If it does, it drops any items it was carrying, and the Turn/Damage track is set to 3. The Poltergeist then appears in the omen room of your choice and continues its turn.
- If it re-forms in the Junk Room, advance the Turn/Damage track to 4.
> Note: The book does not say whether the Poltergeist may re-form while it is still in play, or only after being removed by the Dynamite; "at the start of each monster turn" reads as always available.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to exorcise the Poltergeist.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside several pentagonal item tokens to represent Candles.
### Rules
#### How to Perform the Exorcism
- The exorcism ritual requires burning Candles.
- Once each turn, a hero in the Kitchen, Dining Room, Chapel or Gallery can attempt a Speed roll of 3+ to find a Candle. (More than one Candle can be found in the same room.) On a success, place a Candle token on that hero's character card.
- A hero carrying a Candle may discard it in any room on the floor where the haunt was revealed and attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+.
  - On a success, place the Candle token in that room. No more Knowledge rolls may be made in that room.
  - On a failure, the Candle is lost; return the token to the set-aside pile.
- Once a Candle token has been placed in a room, the Poltergeist and the traitor can't pick it up.
- When the heroes have placed Candle tokens equal in number to the number of heroes when the haunt was revealed, they exorcise the Poltergeist.
- A hero with the Candle item card can use (and discard) that item instead of a Candle token when attempting the Knowledge roll. On a success, put a pentagonal item token in that room.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Poltergeist is immune to Might attacks and can't be harmed by the Revolver. The Dynamite does affect it (the Traitor's Tome describes what happens).
- Any hero in the Poltergeist's room may attack it with Speed.
  - If the hero defeats it, the hero inflicts no damage but can steal one item it is carrying.
  - If it defeats the hero, the hero takes 1 die of physical damage (instead of normal combat damage).
- A hero carrying the Ring, the Skull or the Bell can attack the Poltergeist with Sanity.
  - If the hero defeats it, decrease the Turn/Damage track by the difference in the rolls.
  - If it defeats the hero, the hero takes 1 die of mental damage (instead of normal combat damage).
### Heroes win when
- The heroes exorcise the Poltergeist.
### What they know about the other side
- They are trying to kill the heroes.
