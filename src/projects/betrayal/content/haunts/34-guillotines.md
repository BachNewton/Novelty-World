# 34 — Guillotines

Summary: Every explorer wakes up wearing a timed steel collar with a spring-loaded blade, and Keys hidden around the house unlock them two at a time; one explorer is secretly the traitor behind it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt uses the hidden traitor rules described on page 17 of the rulebook.
- All players read this haunt in the Secrets of Survival booklet.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Place pentagonal item tokens (these are Keys) in each of the following rooms, now or when the room is discovered: Attic, Catacombs, Chasm, Collapsed Room, Crypt, Furnace Room, Gallery, Junk Room, Operating Laboratory, Pentagram Chamber, Tower, and Vault.
- Take red monster tokens numbered 1 up to the number of players. Mix them and deal one face down to each player, number hidden. The explorer holding red token 1 is the traitor.
- Take pentagonal item tokens numbered 1 up to the number of players and give one to each player. These are kept face up, number showing.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0 with four or fewer players, or at 1 with five or six players. It tracks time.

### Rules
- This haunt uses the hidden traitor rules on page 17 of the rulebook. Every player reads this booklet; one of them is secretly the traitor.
- The traitor's own collar does not work, but the other players do not know this (from the "What the Traitor Hears" text).
- The traitor must reveal themselves if their explorer would be killed by a collar. Until then, the traitor may collect Keys and may even trade them to other players to earn trust.
#### How to Unlock a Collar
- A hero may take a Key token from a room after drawing and resolving any cards the room requires. Taking a Key is optional. The room timing sidebar below sets out when the Key can be taken.
- After finding a Key, an explorer cannot move further that turn, but may still try to use the Key or give it to another explorer.
- Each Key can unlock a collar only once; discard the token after use.
- An explorer carrying at least two Key tokens may announce, at any time during their turn, that they are unlocking their own collar or the collar of anyone else in the same room.
- An explorer cannot be traded a Key and use it to unlock their own collar in the same turn.
- Keys cannot unlock the collar of an explorer who has been decapitated.
#### Room timing for Keys (sidebar)
- Crypt, Furnace Room: take the Key after taking damage from the room's effect.
- Gallery: you must jump down into the Ballroom (which must be in the house) to get the Key.
- Collapsed Room: first make the roll to avoid falling. On a success you get the Key; on a failure you fall to the basement without it.
- Vault: the first explorer to end a turn in this room after the Vault is open gets the Key.
- Catacombs, Chasm, Tower: you must succeed at the roll to cross the room before you can pick up the Key. On a failure, you may try again next turn. On a success, you end up on the far side of the room, and must roll again to go back the way you came.
- Attic, Junk Room, Pentagram Chamber: to collect the Key, attempt the roll the tile requires as if you were leaving the room. On a failure, you take the stated damage and do not find the Key. Success or failure, you must still roll again if you try to leave the room.
- Operating Laboratory: the Key is inside you. You must roll 3+ with each of your traits and also take 2 dice of physical damage to get the Key. If any of the rolls fails, you do not get the Key but still take the damage.
#### You Must Do This
- At the end of each of the haunt revealer's turns, advance the Turn/Damage track by 1.
- Then every explorer whose numbered item token is equal to or less than the current turn number must roll 3 dice. If the result is less than the current turn number, that explorer is killed immediately.
- The book's example: with four players, suppose Professor Longfellow holds item token 1 and Zoe Ingstrom holds item token 2. Once two turns have passed, each of those two players rolls 3 dice, and whichever of them rolls 0 or 1 loses their explorer.
#### Removed collars
- A collar counts as removed when it is unlocked or when it detonates.
- A collar also counts as removed if its wearer was killed by other means, or if it is on a revealed or dead traitor.

### Heroes win when
- All the collars are removed and at least half the heroes (rounded up) are still alive.

### Traitor wins when
- More than half of the heroes are killed.

### What they know about the other side
- Someone has locked the explorers in collars with ticking timers that are likely to decapitate them soon.
- One of their companions is a hidden traitor who would rather see them headless.

> Note: The sidebar's "Collapsed Room" says a failed roll drops you to the basement without the Key; it does not say whether the Key can be attempted again later. The book does not state whether a traitor whose collar "would kill" them is revealed before or after the roll result is applied; it says only that they must reveal "if that explorer would be killed by a collar".
> Resolution (community): Reveal timing: two independent answers agree that the traitor rolls like everyone else, and only when the result would detonate the collar does it fail to close and the traitor reveal. So the reveal comes after the roll. Collapsed Room: no ruling found. The only related answer, a single reply, says a failed Key roll in the Attic, Junk Room or Pentagram Chamber can be retried on a later turn, and that the Key is not lost. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1205673, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1556941

> Note: The book uses pentagonal item tokens both for the Keys and for each player's face-up collar number, which takes some tokens away from the Keys.
> Resolution (designer): Bruce Glassco, the haunt's author, said on BoardGameGeek that the collar numbers were meant to be a separate set of numbered tokens, given out face up (purple monster tokens in his draft). Editing changed them to pentagonal item tokens, which leaves fewer tokens for Keys. Use a separate numbered set for the collars. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/748658, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2595826
