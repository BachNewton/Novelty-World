# 98 — Plastic Fantastic

Summary: The haunt revealer's mind has been absorbed into dozens of dress mannequins that come to life when the heroes fail to do things; the revealer runs the Mannequins but wins alongside the heroes if the Mannequins all leave the house.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but has NOT turned traitor; it has been absorbed into the Mannequins. Remove your figure from the house and discard your cards.
- Put a small monster token (representing a Mannequin) of any color, stunned side up, into each room of the house. Place them at random, without regard to which colors end up where.

### Monsters
- Mannequins — Speed 3 · Might 1 · Sanity 1 · Knowledge 1
  - Mannequins don't roll for movement.
  - They ignore all negative room features except Locks.
  - They are made of plastic and can't be damaged by physical attacks.

### Rules
#### On every hero's turn
- At the end of every hero's turn, check for provocations.
- For each action on the Provocations table that the hero did NOT do during their turn, state the provocation aloud, then roll 2 dice and flip that many stunned Mannequins of the listed color face-up. (Example: if the hero didn't attack, roll 2 dice and flip that many green tokens face-up.)
- You may choose any stunned Mannequins of that color to flip.
- If there aren't enough stunned Mannequins of that color to flip, then for each remaining Mannequin you couldn't flip, move a token of that color toward the hero who just took the turn.
> Note: The book doesn't say how far such a token moves toward the hero, or whether a face-up or stunned token of that color is moved.

#### Provocations

| If a hero does not . . . | Roll for the . . . |
|---|---|
| Discover a room tile | Red tokens |
| Move to a new floor | Orange tokens |
| Use an item or omen | Yellow tokens |
| Attack | Green tokens |
| Use all of his or her movement | Blue tokens |
| Leave a room with a monster | Purple and magenta tokens |

#### On the monsters' turn
- Move all stunned Mannequins a number of spaces equal to their Speed toward the Entrance Hall. A stunned Mannequin that is in the Entrance Hall with at least 1 space of movement left is removed from the house.
- Pick a Mannequin color. Until the next monsters' turn, don't check for provocations for that color.
- For each color of face-up Mannequins, choose a room containing one or more face-up Mannequins of that color, and move the face-up Mannequins of that color toward the nearest hero.
- Mannequins lure face-up Mannequins of any color they pass along the way; those newly joined tokens move with them toward the hero.
- After all Mannequins have moved, the face-up Mannequins in each room attack each hero in that room once.
- Stunned monsters are NOT turned face-up at the end of the monsters' turn.
> Note: The printed text says "choose a room that contains one or more Mannequins of that color" and then moves "the face-up Mannequins of that color"; it is not explicit whether only the face-up Mannequins in the chosen room move, or all face-up Mannequins of that color.

#### Special attacks
- All face-up Mannequins in a room make one collective attack against each hero in that room. Roll Might dice equal to the number of face-up Mannequins in that room (maximum of 8 dice).
- If a hero makes a mental attack against a Mannequin in the same room, you defend by rolling dice in the attack's trait equal to the number of face-up Mannequins in that room (maximum of 8 dice). If you lose, turn all Mannequins in that room face-down.

### Mannequin player wins when
- All Mannequins have left the house and at least one hero is still alive. In other words, you win when the heroes do.

### What you know about the heroes
- They're nice, ordinary people, but your Mannequins have gained a strange, formless sentience that is likely to be deadly for everyone.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Discard all weapon item cards and weapon omen cards, and draw the same number of non-weapon item cards.
### Monsters
- Mannequins — no stats printed on this page
  - They are made of plastic and can't be damaged by physical attacks.
  - Mannequins can be face up or face down.
### Rules
#### Special movement rules
- For purposes of movement, all the face-up Mannequins in a room count as one monster.
#### Special attack rules
- Mannequins can't be damaged by physical attacks (maximum of 8 dice).
> Note: "(maximum of 8 dice)" is printed in the same sentence as the physical-damage immunity; it presumably refers to the limit on dice rolled by the Mannequins in the attack below, but its placement is ambiguous.
- If you end your turn in a room with one or more face-up Mannequins, you may attack them with Sanity or Knowledge. The player with the Traitor's Tome rolls a number of dice equal to the number of face-up Mannequins in the room.
  - If you defeat the Mannequins, turn all the Mannequins in that room face down.
  - If you don't, you take the normal amount of mental damage.
#### Special item and omen rules
- If you draw a weapon from the item or omen stack, discard it and draw another card from the same stack.
### Heroes win when
- All the Mannequins have left the house and at least one hero is still alive.
### What they know about the other side
- The house is full of dull-eyed Mannequins that have absorbed the consciousness of the heroes' friend. Unused to society, they come to life at the slightest provocation.
- The friend (what remains of them) is still on the heroes' side, but will animate the Mannequins now surrounding them. For now the Mannequins are docile.
