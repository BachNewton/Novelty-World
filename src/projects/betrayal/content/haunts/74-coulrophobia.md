# 74 — Coulrophobia

Summary: The traitor is Knick-Knack the Clown, armed with deadly Gags; the heroes must escape through the locked front door, kill the Clown, or destroy all the Gags.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- You are Knick-Knack the Clown, and you tell knock-knock jokes.
- Put your figure in the Entrance Hall.
- You may reset any of your traits to 1 above their starting values.
- Set aside five pentagonal item tokens numbered 1 to 5; they are the Gags.
- Take five pieces of paper, number them 1 through 5, and write these words on them (or different ones in the same spirit):

| # | Gag |
|---|---|
| 1 | Acid-blasting seltzer bottle |
| 2 | Exploding whoopee cushion |
| 3 | Neck-breaking banana peel |
| 4 | Toxic cream pie |
| 5 | Strangulating rubber chicken |

- Close your eyes and have each hero pick one piece of paper and keep it in their pocket. If there are more papers than heroes, the heroes put the unchosen ones in the box where you cannot see them.

### Monsters
- Noodles the Dog — Speed 6 · Might 3
  - Noodles attacks normally and can carry one Gag at a time.
  - If you use the Dog to deliver a killing joke, it must use the Gag it is carrying.
  - The heroes may try to attack the Dog. The Dog cannot take damage, but it can be stunned or have its Gag stolen.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Use the Gag tokens to deliver the right joke death to each hero. Each hero has secretly chosen a personal joke (the paper they picked) they would just die for. Once during your turn, you can make either a knock-knock joke or a killing joke:
- Knock-Knock Joke: choose one of your remaining Gags and tell the heroes a knock-knock joke about it (see Knick-Knack's Knock-Knock Jokes). Each hero on your floor makes a Sanity roll. If your Gag's number matches a hero's paper, "you must laugh out loud manically" (as printed) and that hero needs 5+; every other hero needs 2+. Each hero who fails takes 1 point of mental damage.
- Killing Joke: if you or the Dog are in the same room as a hero, you can use one of your Gags. The Gag is destroyed. The hero must succeed at a Might or Speed roll of 5+ or take 1 point of physical damage and 1 point of mental damage. If the Gag number you chose matches the hero's paper, put that paper face-up in front of the hero. From that hero's next turn, the hero is laughing to death: at the end of each of their turns, the laughing hero takes 1 point of physical damage and 1 point of mental damage, and can no longer make attacks.
- When you would take damage, you may choose one of your Gags and destroy it to take no damage instead.
#### Knick-Knack's Knock-Knock Jokes
- You are terrible at knock-knock jokes. The book says they always go like this (its example uses the Strangulating rubber chicken): "Knock knock." / "Who's there?" / "Strangulating rubber chicken." / "Strangulating rubber chicken who?" / <sound of strangling noises>.

### Traitor wins when
- You kill two heroes, or all but one hero is no longer in the house and you kill that hero.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes have no sense of humor.

> Note: "If your Gag number matches that of the hero's piece of paper, then you must laugh out loud manically" — the "you" here appears to mean the matching hero (who must laugh and needs 5+), but the sentence's subject is ambiguous; it could be read as the traitor laughing.
> Note: The book does not say whether destroyed Gags (from killing jokes or from preventing damage) can no longer be used for knock-knock jokes; "remaining Gags" implies they cannot.
> Note: How the Dog comes to carry a Gag is not stated on this page.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Might Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes.
- Set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes.
- Each hero chooses one of the pieces of paper provided by the traitor; each paper represents a different Gag. If there are more papers than heroes, hide the unchosen ones in the game box.
  > Note: This is under "What you know about the bad guys" in the book, but it is an action the heroes take.
### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- The traitor will try to kill you with your own Gag.
- If you attack the Clown, you may use the Stealing Items rules to steal a Gag; the Clown chooses which Gag.
- You may also attack the traitor's Dog. A win stuns it; or, if the Dog is carrying a Gag, you may steal that Gag instead.
- When you successfully steal a Gag, you immediately destroy it.
#### How to leave the house
- The Clown has locked and chained the only door out of the house (and tied a bow around it).
- Once during your turn, a hero in the Entrance Hall may attempt either a Might roll of 5+ to break the chain or a Knowledge roll of 5+ to pick a lock. On a success, add a token of the matching type (Might Roll or Knowledge Roll) to the Entrance Hall.
- When the number of tokens in the Entrance Hall equals the number of heroes, the door is unlocked.
- If you start your turn in the Entrance Hall and the door is unlocked, you may escape.
### Heroes win when
- The last living hero escapes the house; or
- You kill the Clown; or
- All of the Clown's Gags are destroyed.
- However, the heroes lose if two of them are killed, no matter what else happens.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is Knick-Knack the Clown, who has prepared a number of deadly jokes (Gags) meant to slay the heroes.
