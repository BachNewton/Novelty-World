# 54 — Monster Mash

Summary: The house is hosting a ball for classic movie monsters, with the traitor as host; the heroes must banish enough monsters by confronting each with its specific weakness.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer is still in the game but has turned traitor.
- If the Ballroom is not yet in play, search the room stack for it and place it in the house. Then shuffle that stack.
- For each room on the Guest List currently in play, put that room's listed large circular monster token in it.
- If fewer than three monsters are in play, search the room stack for enough monster starting rooms to bring the total to three monsters and place them in the house. Then shuffle that stack.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- When you would discover a room, draw the next five rooms for the appropriate floor, choose one, and put the other four on the bottom of the room stack in any order.

#### Special attack rules
- You cannot be attacked.

#### Special movement rules
- When any room on the Guest List is discovered, by you or anyone else, put its listed monster token in that room.
- When you move a monster, if any hero is within its line of sight, you must move it toward a visible hero.
- If no hero is visible to the monster, you must move it by the shortest possible route toward the Ballroom, including through the Coal Chute, dumbwaiters, and so on. If a hero comes into the monster's line of sight while it moves, it must change direction toward that hero.
- Once a monster enters the Ballroom, it stays there for the rest of the game.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or at least four monsters are in the Ballroom.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes think they can find things in the house that will destroy your guests. You need to destroy them first.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Monsters
- Banshee, Crimson Jack, Demon Lord, Dracula, Frankenstein's Monster, Mummy, Witch, Zombie Lord (no stats printed in this book)
  - Defeating a monster with a normal attack (Speed and Sanity attacks included) only stuns it.
  - A monster is permanently banished if it is ever in the same room as a hero who has that monster's weakness omen card (see Guest list).

### Rules
#### Guest list
| Monster | Weakness | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Banshee | Spirit Board | She must return to the outer planes. |
| Crimson Jack | Photograph | You confront him with evidence of his crimes. |
| Demon Lord | Holy Symbol | It burns! |
| Dracula | Spear | Stake his heart! |
| Frankenstein's Monster | Girl | You appeal to the creature's gentler side. |
| Mummy | Book | You read his name from the Book of the Dead. |
| Witch | Cat | She wants her familiar back. |
| Zombie Lord | Vial | His features dissolve into goo. |

#### Special attack rules
- The traitor cannot be attacked.
#### Special movement rules
- You may never enter or leave the Ballroom unless you cannot move anywhere else.

### Heroes win when
- You have banished a number of monsters equal to half the number of explorers, rounded up.

### What they know about the other side
- A party is under way and the house is filling with monsters who want the heroes dead. It will get worse once the party really gets going.
- The heroes know each monster's weakness (the Guest list).
