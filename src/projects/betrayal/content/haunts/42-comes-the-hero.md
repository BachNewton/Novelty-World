# 42 — Comes the Hero

Summary: An invincible traitor is performing a ritual to open a gate to Hell; the heroes' only hope is a statue that comes to life when given the right item.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- Any of the traitor's traits that are below their starting value are raised to their starting value.
- If the traitor doesn't already have a weapon card (such as the Revolver, Axe, or Blood Dagger), search the item or omen stack, take the first weapon found, then shuffle that stack.

### Rules
#### How to Open the Gate to Hell
- The traitor must complete these steps in this order:
  1. Sacrifice (kill) one of the heroes using a weapon, then bring the body to the Catacombs, Chasm, or Pentagram Chamber.
     - A body is picked up the same way as an item, and then carried. Tip the hero's figure over and put it on the traitor's card to show the body is being carried.
     - While carrying the body, each room the traitor enters counts as 2 spaces of movement.
     - The Dog cannot carry a body.
  2. Once the body is in one of those rooms, the traitor can attempt a Sanity roll or a Knowledge roll of 4+ to open the gate to Hell. This roll may be attempted only once per turn.
#### Coping with Immortality
- The traitor's traits do not go up or down, even when the traitor gains or loses items, unless the heroes find some special way to lower the traitor's traits.
- Items cannot be stolen from the traitor.
- Explorers don't slow the traitor's movement.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The traitor can't be attacked, and takes no damage from card effects or room effects.
- The traitor can attack as normal, but takes no damage when defeated.

### Traitor wins when
- The traitor opens the gate to Hell.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to stop the traitor from opening the gate to Hell.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Place a pentagonal item token, representing the Statue, in the room where the haunt was revealed.
### Allies
- Animated Statue — Speed 8 · Might 8 · Sanity 8 · Knowledge 8
  - It helps the heroes (see Rules).
### Rules
#### How to Kill the Traitor
- The traitor can't be attacked or harmed by normal means; the heroes must use an unusual method (the Statue).
- The Statue's hand is outstretched, and its plaque reads "To Defeat the Undefeatable." The Statue is inanimate until a hero places an appropriate item in its hand during that hero's turn. When that happens, the item is discarded and the Statue animates.
- The item placed determines the Statue's form:

| Item | Statue Becomes. . . |
|---|---|
| Holy Symbol | Judge |
| Axe | Warrior |
| Crystal Ball | Seer |
| Book | Wizard |

- Once animated, the Statue keeps that form for the rest of the game. Tell the traitor what form it has taken.
#### The Animated Statue
- The Statue does not move normally. It must be moved by the heroes' mental powers: once during their turn, a hero in the same room as the Statue may make a Knowledge or Sanity roll, and may then move the Statue a number of spaces up to the result of that roll.
- When the Statue is in the same room as the traitor, it doesn't attack. Instead, at the beginning of the traitor's turn, it lowers one of the traitor's traits by 1. Which trait depends on its form:

| Statue | Trait Reduced |
|---|---|
| Judge | Speed |
| Warrior | Might |
| Seer | Sanity |
| Wizard | Knowledge |

- If the traitor attacks the Statue and defeats it, the Statue isn't stunned. Instead, the Statue can't lower a trait during its next turn. The Statue can still be moved to follow the traitor.
> Note: "its next turn" is as printed; the Statue's trait-lowering happens at the beginning of the traitor's turn, so this presumably means the next time that effect would apply.

### Heroes win when
- The traitor is dead.
### What they know about the other side
