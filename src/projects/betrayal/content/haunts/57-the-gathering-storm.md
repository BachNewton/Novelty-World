# 57 — The Gathering Storm

Summary: A storm is tearing the house apart room by room; with no traitor, the explorers must gather Tools and seal every entrance to the basement before the storm destroys the Basement Landing.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor, only heroes, who are all trying to escape the house. The rest of its rules are in Secrets of Survival.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The haunt revealer chooses an empty room on the roof (preferably far from any explorer) and destroys it (see How to destroy a room).
- Set aside the pentagonal item tokens (they represent Tools).
- Set aside six Obstacle tokens (they represent Destroyed Landings).

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor; all explorers play together against the storm.
#### How to find Tools
- You may give up some of your movement to search for Tools that you and the other explorers can use.
- Choose a number of dice equal to or less than your current Speed and roll them. On a 2+ you gain a Tool.
- Then subtract the number of dice you chose from your Speed for this turn. Example from the book: Zoe has Speed 4 and uses 3 dice to search, so she can only move one space this turn.
#### How to advance the storm
- At the end of each explorer's turn, except the turn in which the haunt is revealed, destroy every room that is adjacent to a room destroyed on an earlier turn (see How to destroy a room).
- If the Basement Landing is destroyed, the explorers lose.
#### How to destroy a room
- Remove all figures and tokens from the room tile, flip it over, and put it back where it was in the house.
- Tokens that were on the room are lost. Explorers that were on the room are killed.
- An explorer on the same floor as a room may discard a Tool to keep that room from being destroyed.
- The Grand Staircase, Foyer and Entrance Hall count as one room for this purpose.
- Mark each destroyed landing room with an Obstacle token.
- Once a whole floor has been destroyed, the next time you would destroy a room, instead choose the landing of the floor below that floor and all rooms adjacent to it.
- If you would destroy the Basement Landing, first check whether all entrances to the basement are sealed (see How to seal an entrance). If they are, the explorers win; if not, they lose.
#### How to seal an entrance
- During their turn, an explorer on a basement room that has an exit to a higher floor may discard a Tool to seal it.
- Sealing does not stop explorers from moving through the entrance; it stops only the storm.
- Entrances to the basement include the Basement Landing, the Coal Chute, a room with the Below Collapsed Room token, and any rooms with dumbwaiters and stairs that connect to the basement.
#### Special movement rules
- You may try to leave a destroyed room, but you must succeed at a Speed roll of 3+. If you fail, you fall to the landing of the floor below and take 2 dice of physical damage.
- You may choose to fail that roll on purpose, as a desperate attempt to get off your floor.
- You may discard a Tool to enter and leave destroyed rooms for the rest of your turn without making Speed rolls. While moving this way, treat every entrance on the destroyed tiles as open.
#### Completion chart
- When you finish this haunt, mark your explorers' completion of it in the chart after haunt 100 (printed in a red box beside the haunt number).

### Heroes win when
- Every entrance to the basement has a Tool token on it, sealing off the basement; anyone still in the house above is doomed.
- The explorers lose when all explorers are dead or when the Basement Landing is destroyed (unless, at that moment, all basement entrances are sealed, as above).

> Note: "Choose the landing of the floor below that floor and all rooms adjacent to it" is printed as one clause; it reads as "destroy that landing and every room adjacent to it."
> Note: "rooms with dumbwaiters and stairs that connect to the basement" is ambiguous between "dumbwaiters, and stairs" and rooms having both; it most plausibly means any room with a dumbwaiter or stairs leading to the basement.
> Note: sealing an entrance is said to be done by discarding a Tool, while the win condition says each entrance has "Tool tokens on them"; presumably the discarded Tool token is placed on the entrance.

### What they know about the other side
- There is no traitor in this haunt. It is the explorers against the storm.
