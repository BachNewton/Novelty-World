# 46 — The Feast

Summary: The traitor has lured the heroes into a house of cannibals; the heroes must shepherd the captive Victims out the front door or kill every cannibal.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- If the Dining Room isn't in the house, search the room stack for it, place it on the ground floor, then shuffle that stack.
- Place small purple monster tokens in the Dining Room, one per hero. They are the Cannibal Freaks.

### Monsters
- Cannibal Freaks — Speed 2 · Might 4 · Sanity 4
  - A Cannibal Freak defeated by an attack is killed instead of stunned.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Victims cannot attack. If the traitor or a Cannibal Freak defeats a Victim in combat, the Victim is killed, and the attacker can feast (see Feasting). A Victim that defeats its attacker inflicts no damage.
- The traitor and the Cannibal Freaks do not slow Victims, and Victims do not slow the traitor or the Freaks.
#### Feasting
- A killed Victim becomes a Corpse: flip its token to the stunned side. A killed hero's figure is tipped over.
- If, at the start of its turn, the traitor or a Cannibal Freak is in the same room as a Corpse or a dead hero, it can feast on that body, provided no living hero is in the room.
- Spending a whole turn feasting on a Corpse or dead hero (taking no other actions) adds 1 to each of the feaster's traits. Cannibal Freaks can raise their traits this way too (track increases on paper).
- Once a dead hero or Corpse has been eaten, remove it from play.

### Traitor wins when
- Either all the Victims are eaten or all the heroes are dead.
- If even one Victim escapes through the front door, the traitor can win only by killing all the heroes.

### What they know about the other side
- Victims captured by the Cannibal Freaks have just escaped from the Attic. The heroes will probably try to save them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If the Attic isn't in the house, search the room stack for it and place it on the upper floor, then shuffle that stack.
- Put small magenta monster tokens (representing Victims) in the Attic, equal to the number of heroes.
- All Victims start facing the same direction: turn each token so its tip points that way.
### Allies
- The Victims — Speed 2 · Might 3 · Sanity 3
  - The heroes are trying to rescue them; the player to the left of the traitor moves them (see The Victims below).
### Rules
#### The Victims
- At the start of their turn, the player to the left of the traitor moves the Victims.
  - Unless a hero is in the same room as a Victim, that Victim moves 2 rooms straight ahead. If it can't move straight ahead, it turns to the next exit on its left and takes that exit. This is the only way a Victim's facing can change.
- Victims react to house dangers and required rolls as if they were monsters. A Victim can't go through an unexplored door.
- A Victim stops moving if it enters the same room as a hero. A Victim won't move on its own while it's in the same room as a hero.
- If a hero begins their turn in the same room as a Victim, that hero can move with the Victim 2 spaces in any direction before continuing to move and take actions for that turn.
  - A hero can move only one Victim at a time, but a Victim can be moved several times in a round by different heroes.
- Cannibal Freaks and the traitor do not slow Victims, and Victims do not slow Freaks or the traitor.
- A hero may attempt a Knowledge roll (picking the lock) or a Might roll (breaking down the door) of 5+ to open the front door in the Entrance Hall. On a success, the hero draws an event card and ends their turn.
- On later turns, any hero can move out the front door from the Entrance Hall (at a cost of 1 space) with a Victim, to escape. A hero can come back into the house (again at a cost of 1 space) to bring out more Victims.
- Each Victim brought out of the house makes it harder for the traitor to win. Once a Victim has exited through the front door, remove that token from the game.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Victims and Cannibal Freaks are killed, not stunned, when defeated.
- Victims cannot attack. A Victim inflicts no damage if it defeats its attacker.
### Heroes win when
- The traitor and all the Cannibal Freaks are dead, or
- All the Victims and all the heroes escape the house.
- If even one Victim or hero is killed, the heroes can win only by killing all the bad guys.
### What they know about the other side
- The house is inhabited by deranged Cannibal Freaks, and the traitor lured the heroes here.
- When the traitor or a Cannibal Freak feasts on a Victim, that being becomes more powerful.
- The traitor and the other cannibals also want to feast on the heroes.
