# 100 — Let's Play a Game

Summary: A voice on a cassette tape traps the explorers in a house full of Challenges that is set to burn; each explorer secretly chooses to go it alone or to cooperate in a team, and must vanquish their Challenges before time runs out.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor; all players are heroes.
- The list of Challenges below is used by all explorers, but nobody reads it in advance. The rest of the rules for this haunt are in Secrets of Survival.
- When you reveal a Challenge, look up the entry with its number.
- If the entry tells you to flip the token or leave it face-up, you do NOT vanquish the Challenge. Otherwise, you vanquish the Challenge and put its token on your character card.
- Several entries distinguish between a "loner" and an explorer "on the team"; those terms are defined in Secrets of Survival.

#### Challenges
1. **"A hiss, a pop, and a javelin bursts from the wall."**
   - Attempt a Speed roll of 4+.
   - Fail: take 1 die of physical damage and flip this token.
   - Succeed: if the Spear is not yet in play, search the omen stack and discard pile for it and take it, then shuffle the discard pile into the omen stack.
2. **"A voice whispers, 'The price of this test is a patch of your skin.'"**
   - You may reset one of your physical traits to its lowest value above the skull symbol.
   - If you don't, flip this token, and if you are on the team, every other teammate loses 1 Sanity.
3. **"You see your reflection in the glass of an old clock, and watch yourself catch fire."**
   - Attempt a Sanity roll of 4+.
   - Fail: lose 2 Sanity and flip this token; your teammates each take 1 die of physical damage.
   - Succeed: if the Idol is not yet in play, search the item stack and discard pile for it and take it, then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.
4. **"A mechanical arm extends from the wall and pins you."**
   - Attempt a Might roll of 6+.
   - Succeed: gain 1 Might and move to an adjacent room.
   - Fail: you are trapped. Leave this token face-up while you are trapped, then flip it over once you are no longer trapped.
   - While trapped, you can't leave this room until you succeed at a Might roll of 6+, or until a teammate spends 2 spaces of movement and succeeds at that roll for you.
5. **"A large axe falls from the ceiling."**
   - Attempt a Speed or Might roll of 3+.
   - Fail: take 2 dice of physical damage and flip this token.
   - Succeed: if the Axe is not yet in play, search the item stack and discard pile for it and take it, then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.
6. **"A gun turns itself on your friend."**
   - If you don't have the Revolver, take it from another explorer, or search the item stack and discard pile for it and take it, then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.
   - Once you have the Revolver, attack an explorer within range using the Revolver. You may choose yourself, in which case you are both the attacker and the defender.
7. **"You decide who gets hurt, and how."**
   - If you are a loner: take 3 damage, divided however you choose among all your traits.
   - If you are on the team: attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+.
     - Fail: each other teammate takes 2 points of mental damage, then flip this token.
     - Succeed: deal either 5 points of physical damage or 5 points of mental damage, divided as you choose among your teammates.
8. **"A light dew touches your skin and fills you with dread."**
   - Attempt a Sanity or Knowledge roll of 5+.
   - Fail: lose 1 in the other mental trait (the one you didn't roll) and flip this token.
   - Succeed: gain 2 in the trait you chose to roll.
   - If you are on the team and have Challenge 8 on your character card, any teammate in your room rolls 1 fewer die on all Challenge rolls.
9. **"You uncover an empty box, and are compelled to fill it."**
   - Set aside all your items next to this room and leave this token face-up. Those items can't be picked up, and if Challenge 17 has been vanquished, discard them.
   - If you are a loner, lose 1 Sanity. If you are on the team, your teammates lose 2 Sanity, divided as you choose.
10. **"A panel slides away to reveal a pattern of numbers."**
    - Attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ to see whether you can work out the next number in the pattern.
    - Fail: take 1 die of mental damage and flip this token.
    - Succeed: if the Puzzle Box is not yet in play, search the item stack and discard pile for it and take it, then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.
11. **"Everyone is tested."**
    - Every explorer rolls 8 dice.
    - The explorer with the highest roll wins the Challenge and takes this token.
    - The explorer with the lowest roll reduces one of their physical traits to its lowest value above the skull symbol.
    - If the highest roll or the lowest roll is tied, flip this token; nobody is affected by this Challenge.
12. **"You learn how to give and take."**
    - Attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+.
    - Fail: take 2 dice of physical damage and flip this token.
    - Succeed as a loner: you may reduce one physical trait of an explorer to its lowest value above the skull symbol.
    - Succeed while on the team: you may reduce one of your own physical traits to its lowest value above the skull symbol to bring a dead teammate back to life, resetting that teammate's traits to their starting values.
13. **"There appears to be nothing here."**
    - Return this token to the box. It doesn't count as a Challenge.
14. **"The floor drops out from under you."**
    - Leave this token face-up. Move every explorer in this room to the Basement Landing (or to the Roof Landing if you are already in the basement); this ends your turn.
    - Any explorer who enters this room on a later turn may complete this Challenge.
15. **"Smoke pours from the vents."**
    - Lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number.
    - If you are on the team and haven't achieved your team victory, flip this token.
16. **"A maddening alarm resounds."**
    - You may choose another explorer to attack; that explorer then attacks you.
    - Alternatively, you may mix this token in with any others that are set aside to be placed when a new room is discovered; if you do, gain 1 Sanity.
> Note: The book doesn't say what happens to the token if you choose the attack option (by the general rule above, you would vanquish it, since no flip is mentioned).
17. **"You discover a full box."**
    - While this token is face-up, subtract 2 from all Knowledge rolls made in this room.
    - Attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+.
    - Fail: leave this token face-up.
    - Succeed: if Challenge 9 is face-up, take this token, Challenge 9, and any items next to that room.
> Note: The book doesn't say what happens on a success when Challenge 9 isn't face-up; by the general rule above, you would vanquish this Challenge.
18. **"You remember the aphorism 'blood for blood.'"**
    - Attempt a Knowledge roll of 6+.
    - Fail: flip this token.
    - Succeed: if the Sacrificial Dagger is not yet in play, search the item stack and discard pile for it and take it, then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.
    - Once the Sacrificial Dagger is in play, the explorer holding it must attack another explorer in any room.
> Note: It is not stated when the forced attack happens (e.g. each turn, or once); the text only says the holder "must attack another explorer in any room".
19. **"You awaken and all seems new."**
    - Put this token and your figure in the Entrance Hall. If you are on the team, also put a teammate of your choice in the Entrance Hall.
    - If this token is in the Entrance Hall at the start of your turn, you may reset your Knowledge to its lowest value above the skull symbol to vanquish this Challenge.
20. **"You crumple to the floor."**
    - Leave this token face-up and end your turn.
    - From then on, an explorer who starts their turn in this room can vanquish this Challenge.
21. **"You find out if crime does pay."**
    - Choose another explorer in any room and steal any number of that explorer's items (at least one).
    - Take 1 die of mental damage for each item you steal.
22. **"You trigger a series of potentially unfortunate events."**
    - Draw three event cards and follow their instructions.
    - If you are on the team, you can give any number of these cards to teammates of your choice, and they must follow those cards' instructions instead.
    - If any explorer loses 1 or more points in a trait during this sequence, flip this token.
23. **"You reconsider your allegiances."**
    - If you are an individual, you may join the team; if you don't, flip this token.
    - If you are on the team, you may leave the team and become a loner; if you don't, flip this token.
> Note: This entry says "individual" where others say "loner"; they appear to mean the same thing.
24. **"You profit off the hard work of others."**
    - You may make a Speed attack against any number of other explorers, in the order in which they will take their next turns.
    - These attacks deal no damage; whoever wins each combat takes a Challenge from the loser, if possible.
    - If you lose any of these combats, or don't attack anyone, flip this token.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- You may reset any of your traits to 1 above their starting values.
- Put the 24 Obstacle tokens (they represent Challenges) face down at random in rooms that contain no explorers. Spread them so that each floor has about the same number of Challenges, if possible. If you can't place all of them, set the rest aside.
- Open the Traitor's Tome to this haunt; the Challenges are described there.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 8 and put it in front of the haunt revealer. It keeps track of time.
- Each explorer takes a small red monster token and a small blue monster token. Review How to vote (below). Then each explorer secretly chooses one of the two tokens. When everyone has chosen, all explorers reveal their choices at the same time.
### Rules
#### How to vote
- If you vote red, you choose to work as a loner and must vanquish four Challenges on your own.
  - You may reduce the number of Challenges you need to vanquish by 1 for each explorer you kill.
  - When you make a physical attack, you steal an item if you win by 2 or more, in addition to dealing physical damage.
- If you vote blue, you agree to work as a team with everyone else who votes blue.
  - The team must vanquish a number of Challenges equal to three times the number of living teammates; this total goes down if a teammate dies.
  - If a team is formed, search the item stack and discard pile for these six cards, if they aren't in play yet: Amulet of the Ages, Ceremonial Robe, Chainsaw, Medical Kit, Pickpocket Gloves, and Rabbit's Foot. Shuffle those items and deal one at random to each explorer on the team, shuffling the rest into the stack.
#### You must do this on the haunt revealer's turn
- At the start of the haunt revealer's turn (even if the haunt revealer has died), lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number.
#### How to reveal and vanquish Challenges
- If you discover a new room and there are Challenges set aside (because not all tokens could be placed), put a Challenge face down in that room.
- While in a room with a face-down Challenge, you may spend 3 spaces of movement to secretly look at its number and preview that Challenge in the Traitor's Tome, then put it back face down.
- While in a room with a face-down Challenge, you may spend 1 space of movement to flip it over, reveal its number, and look up its entry in the Traitor's Tome to try to vanquish the Challenge. This ends your movement for the turn.
- If you vanquish a Challenge, put its token on your character card to mark your success.
- If you are on the team, you count as your own teammate for the purposes of Challenge text.
- Members of the team are not their teammates' opponents. If a teammate dies, discard that teammate's tokens.

> Note: the printed book doesn't say what happens to a dead teammate's vanquished Challenges, or whether teammates may attack each other. The last bullet above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. A dead teammate's Challenge tokens are discarded, and teammates can't attack one another to bring that about. The FAQ adds the bullet to the end of How to Reveal and Vanquish Challenges. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
### Heroes win when
- You vanquish all of your Challenges before you die or before the Turn/Damage track reaches 0.
- Anyone who has not achieved victory when someone wins or the haunt ends is killed in the fire.
> Note: The ending is headed "If (some or all of) you win", so several explorers (e.g. the whole blue team, or a loner and the team) can win. The page does not say whether the game ends as soon as the first explorer or team wins, or continues until the track reaches 0.
