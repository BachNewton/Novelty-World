# 86 — The Woods in the Cabin

Summary: The traitor turns out to be the living embodiment of an ancient tree the house is built around, and it wants only to grow, spreading roots, acorns and squirrels through the house.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Remove your figure from the house. You are no longer a person; you are the living embodiment of the Tree.
- Write down how many rooms are in the house.
- Discard every card you are carrying, even cards that can't normally be dropped. If any discarded card is a weapon, shuffle it back into its stack.
- Put the Tree House on top of the Foyer. If an explorer or anything else is in the Tree House, move it along with the Tree House. If the Tree House is not in play yet, search the room stack for it, place it on top of the Foyer, then shuffle that stack.
- If the basement has fewer than five rooms, place basement rooms from the room stack until it has five.
- Put one Plant token (a Rootstalk) in each of four basement rooms, and one small green monster token (a Root) in each other basement room. For these and all other tokens, if you run out of tokens at any point, don't place any more.
- Put one small orange monster token (an Acorn) in each upper floor room.
- Put small red monster tokens (Squirrels) equal to the number of heroes in the room with the Plant token connected to the Tree House.
> Note: The book does not explain which Plant token is "connected to the Tree House" or how that connection is determined; Secrets of Survival does not define it either. Owner to rule.

### Monsters
- Squirrels — Speed 8 · Might 1 · Sanity 6 · Knowledge 1
  - Squirrels move up to their full Speed; they do not roll for movement.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- Do these in this order:
  1. Put one Root in a basement room that has no Rootstalk.
  2. Put one Acorn in an upper floor room.
  3. Put Squirrels equal to the number of heroes in the room with the Plant token connected to the Tree House.
  4. Discover a room through any open door in the basement.

#### Special attack rules
- On the monsters' turn, your Squirrels may attack any hero who has an explorer token on their character card (that is, a hero who has attacked you or your Squirrels). They may not attack other heroes.
- If a hero attacks one of your rooms, that room has a Might of 4.
- Squirrels in the same room may combine their Might values into a single attack. If such an attack fails, each point of damage removes 1 Squirrel from that room.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or
- You have grown to ten undestroyed rooms more than the house had at the start of the haunt.
> Note: The printed wording is "you become ten undestroyed rooms larger than at the start of the haunt"; compare against the room count written down at setup.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are of no concern to a Tree: you won't bother them unless they bother you, and you aren't even aware of them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Each hero without a weapon draws a random weapon from the item stack, if any are in the stack. Then shuffle the stack.
- Set aside one matching explorer token for each hero.

### Rules
#### How to Cut Down the Tree
- Destroy the ground floor so the top of the house becomes too heavy for the trunk.
- This happens when no ground floor tiles remain except the Tree House, and the total number of rooms on the upper floor and roof exceeds the number of Roots in the basement.

#### Special Attack Rules
- Once on your turn, you may attack a ground floor room: make a Might attack against the room. If you defeat it, flip the room tile over. (For the Entrance Hall or Grand Staircase, instead put a random ground floor room tile face-down on top of it.) You may not attack the Tree House.
- You may attack a Root (a small green monster token). Roots can only be removed by using Dynamite on them or by dropping one of the very heavy Acorns (small orange monster tokens) on them:
  - Once on your turn, you may remove an Acorn in your room from the house. Then roll 6 dice; if the result equals or exceeds the number of rooms on the ground floor, remove a Root of your choice.
  - You may not remove a Rootstalk (the Plant token), which counts as a Root.
- If you are in a room with an Acorn and a Squirrel (small red monster tokens), you may make a Knowledge attack against the Squirrel. If you defeat it, remove the Squirrel and the Acorn from the room.
- The first time you do any of the actions above, put your explorer token on your character card.
> Note: The explorer token's purpose is given in the Traitor's Tome: Squirrels may attack only heroes with an explorer token on their character card.

#### Special Movement Rules
- You may move from a face-down tile into any adjacent room, or discover a room through any side of that face-down tile. (The book cautions against doing this more than necessary, since the goal is to destroy the ground floor.)
- If the Stairs from Basement are in play, they now lead to and from the Tree House.

#### After the haunt
- When you finish this haunt, mark your explorers' completion of it in the chart after haunt 100.

### Heroes win when
- They fell the Tree.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has become a giant, rapidly growing Tree.
