# 25 — Voodoo

Summary: The traitor has hidden voodoo dolls, each linked to a hero, in dangerous places around the house; the heroes must find and destroy every doll while enough of them survive.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0 to track time.
- Using "Voodoo Doll Descriptions", choose a doll for each hero. On a piece of paper, secretly write down which doll belongs to which hero, and which of that doll's two listed rooms it is in. Do not tell the other players where the dolls are.
  - If one of a doll's two listed rooms has been discovered and the other has not, the traitor must choose the discovered room. Otherwise the traitor may choose either room.
- Once every hero has a doll, read aloud to each hero the flavor quote for his or her doll from "Voodoo Doll Descriptions".
> Note: The book does not say whether two heroes may be given the same kind of doll; there are five dolls, one per possible hero.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The wording is unchanged from the 1st-edition revised tome ("Select a doll for each hero"). ### Rules
#### Voodoo Doll Descriptions
| Doll | Possible locations | Quote read aloud at setup |
|---|---|---|
| Wax Doll | Furnace Room, or the ovens of the Kitchen | "You're burning up!" |
| China Doll | Balcony, or the Tower | "The ground, far below, is waiting." |
| Stone Doll | Underground Lake, or the Graveyard | "Muck in your mouth. You're choking." |
| Glass Doll | Pentagram Chamber, or the Chapel | "Evil, evil everywhere. Unholy evil." |
| Rag Doll | Gardens, or the Conservatory | "Stabbing punctures. Dirt and blood." |

> Note: These quotes are the traitor's hints that the heroes use to work out which rooms hold dolls, so they are game data and are kept exactly.
> Resolution (unresolved): an editorial note, not a rules question, so no ruling applies. The 2020 Hasbro reprint prints the same quotes. Source: https://www.hasbro.com/common/documents/60D52426B94D40B98A9E78EE4DD8BF94/38E7C8028AA2455CBE6D25E68FF9C18E.pdf

#### Voodoo Doll Effects
- Wax Doll: the hero takes 1 point of physical damage. Quote: "Fire burns good and evil."
- China Doll: roll 4 dice. If the result is less than the turn number, the doll falls and shatters, killing the hero. Quote: "A strong wind pushes you."
- Stone Doll: the hero must attempt a Might roll. If the result is less than the turn number, the hero loses 1 point in each trait. Quote: "You are sinking into grime. So dark. So dirty."
- Glass Doll: the hero takes 1 point of mental damage. Quote: "Unholy presence, where once there was goodness."
- Rag Doll: the hero must attempt a Knowledge roll. If the result is less than the turn number, the hero takes 2 points of physical damage. Quote: "Blood red, rose death."

#### You must do this
- At the end of each of the traitor's turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number, starting with 1. At that time, every doll that has not been destroyed affects its hero (see "Voodoo Doll Effects"). Read each doll's effect quote aloud as it happens.

### Traitor wins when
- More than half of the heroes who began the haunt are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to find the dolls and destroy them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
- Explorers do not have to stop moving when they enter a previously unexplored room that has a symbol [raven symbol] [bull skull symbol] [spiral symbol].
  - A hero may explore as many new rooms as he or she likes, and draws a card only if the room where that hero ends movement has a symbol.
  - A hero must also draw a card when searching for a doll in a newly discovered room that has a symbol.

#### How to Destroy the Dolls
- First the heroes use the traitor's hints to work out which rooms hold dolls; then they find the dolls and destroy them using the steps below.
- The heroes may ask the traitor to repeat any of the hints.
- Move to a room you believe holds a voodoo doll. (The traitor's clues help identify which rooms have dolls.)
- In that room, attempt a Knowledge roll of 2+ to search for a doll.
  - On a success, ask the traitor whether the room contains a doll. The traitor must answer truthfully and say which doll it is.
  - A doll cannot be found unless the hero is in the right room.
  - A hero may search only one room per turn.
- A hero who finds a doll may destroy it automatically if it is that hero's own doll.
  - Any hero can find where any doll is, but each hero can destroy only his or her own doll.
- When a hero dies, that hero's doll is also destroyed.

### Heroes win when
- All of the dolls are destroyed and at least half (rounding up) of the heroes who began the haunt are still alive.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has hidden voodoo dolls throughout the house, each linked to a hero, in dangerous places.
- The dolls' effects get worse as the Turn/Damage track advances.
