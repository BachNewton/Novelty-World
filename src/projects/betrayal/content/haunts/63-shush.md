# 63 — Shush

Summary: The traitor reveals themself as an evil fairy who has stolen the explorers' voices in a box, and feeds on them while they search in silence for the Spellbooks that can stop it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Put the Box card (the Voicebox) next to the traitor's room.
- Choose a number from 1 to 6. This is how many uncursed Spellbooks the heroes must collect before they can hurt the traitor and destroy the Voicebox.
  - For each step from the chosen number up to 6, the traitor adds 1 to one of their traits; it need not be the same trait each time.
  - The remaining Spellbooks are cursed. Example: choosing 4 means the heroes must collect four uncursed Spellbooks, two Spellbooks are cursed, and the traitor may raise two traits by 1 each or one trait by 2.
- Put six pentagonal item tokens (Spellbooks) face down in different rooms.
- On a piece of paper, write down which item tokens are the cursed Spellbooks.

### Rules
#### The Restrictions on Explorers
- The heroes may not talk to each other until their voices return (see Interacting with the Voicebox).
- Once play resumes, every time a hero says anything out loud, that hero takes 1 die of damage to a trait of their choice.
- Chuckling or giggling does not count.

#### When a Hero Collects a Spellbook
- The traitor checks the number on the item token.
- If it is a cursed Spellbook, it explodes: the hero takes 1 die of physical damage, and the item token is removed from play.
- If it is an uncursed Spellbook, the hero takes the item token.
- When the heroes have all of the uncursed Spellbooks, tell them that a hero may pick up and open the Voicebox.

#### Special Attack Rules
- If a hero attacks the traitor before the heroes as a group have collected the required number of Spellbooks, resolve the attack normally but tell the hero the traitor takes no damage. The traitor can still damage the hero.

#### Interacting with the Voicebox
- Until the heroes have collected the required number of uncursed Spellbooks, nobody (the traitor included) can pick up and open the Voicebox.
- Once they have, a hero in the Voicebox's room can pick it up and open it.
- If the Voicebox is opened: remove the Box card from the game and tell the heroes their voices have returned. The traitor takes 1 die of damage to each of their traits. From then on the heroes damage the traitor normally, but the traitor does not have to tell them so.

> Note: the printed book never tells the heroes how the Voicebox is opened, and speaks of picking it up "or" destroying it. The text above carries the official correction: the traitor tells the heroes once they hold every uncursed Spellbook, and the Voicebox is picked up and opened, not destroyed.
> Resolution (official): errata. The FAQ adds the bullet telling the heroes when they may pick up and open the Voicebox, and rewrites Interacting with the Voicebox as above. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

> Note: "Put the Box card next to your room" presumably means the Voicebox sits in the traitor's room at the start. The page does not say what happens if a hero picks up the Voicebox without destroying it.
> Resolution (official): picking up and opening are one step; the corrected rule (above) has the hero pick up and open the Voicebox, and opening it is what returns the heroes' voices. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes must win but do not know how. The traitor holds the keys to their victory, and the heroes have to earn them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The heroes cannot speak, and neither can the players controlling them. The first hero player reads this half silently, gestures to the other hero players to wait, then passes the book to the player on their left (and so on).
### Rules
- Until the traitor says otherwise, the hero players cannot speak.
#### How to use the Spellbooks
- The heroes must collect the correct spells to open the Voicebox and regain their voices.
- Some Spellbooks may be cursed.
### Heroes win when
- They have regained their voices and killed the traitor.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is a monstrous creature that was wearing a human mask.
- The Box the traitor guards, the Voicebox, is covered in spells and is the source of the magic silencing the heroes.
> Note: The heroes' half gives no mechanics for Spellbooks or the Voicebox; those are presumably in the Traitor's Tome, and the traitor tells the heroes when they may speak.
