# 2 — The Séance

Summary: The heroes and the traitor race to hold a séance that summons a Ghost; whoever finishes first controls it, and the heroes then must either lay its bones to rest or destroy it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Set aside a small purple monster token (the Ghost).
- Set aside 1 triangular Knowledge Roll token and 1 triangular Sanity Roll token.
- If the Pentagram Chamber is not in the house, search the room stack for it and place it next to a basement doorway at least 5 tiles from you, or as far from you as possible. Then shuffle the room stack.

### Monsters
- The Ghost — Speed 4 · Sanity 6
  - You control the Ghost if you summon it first, or if the heroes summon it first but then fail their quest. You keep control of it even if your explorer dies.
  - On every turn you control it, the Ghost must move toward a hero and must attack if it is able to.
  - Only the Ghost can move through collapsed rooms. It can also move through walls as if they were connecting doorways, but not through floors or ceilings.

### Rules
#### How to Summon the Ghost
- You and the heroes are racing to summon the Ghost; summoning requires completing a séance.
- While you hold the Spirit Board, you may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ or a Sanity roll of 5+. Only one of these attempts is allowed per turn.
- On a success, take the token for the trait you rolled (Knowledge Roll token or Sanity Roll token).
- Once you hold both one Knowledge Roll token and one Sanity Roll token, you summon the Ghost.
- If you summon first, place the Ghost token in your explorer's room. If the heroes summon first, they will tell you what happens.

#### If You Summon the Ghost First
- Read aloud the Ghost's declaration: "I will have my revenge against the living!"

#### Collapsing the house (once you control the Ghost)
- At the end of your first turn controlling the Ghost, the house starts to collapse. The first room to collapse must be the Attic; if the Attic is not in the house, choose any unoccupied upper-floor room instead.
- From then on, at the end of each explorer's turn, tell that explorer to choose a new room to collapse.
- To collapse a room, flip its tile face down. A room may only collapse if it is adjacent to a room that has already collapsed; the two rooms do not need connecting doors. If a room collapses while occupied, every explorer in it is killed.
- When the whole upper floor has collapsed, collapse the ground floor next, starting with the Grand Staircase. Use pentagonal item tokens to mark the Grand Staircase, Foyer and Entrance Hall as collapsed. When the ground floor is gone, collapse the basement, starting with the Basement Landing.
- If a Mystic Elevator roll would send it to a floor with no uncollapsed rooms that have open doorways, the elevator does not move.

#### Special Attack Rules
- No one can attack until the séance has been completed. If the heroes control the Ghost, they will tell you their special attack rules.
- The Ghost makes Sanity attacks, which deal mental damage.
- Only a hero who has the Ring, or a hero in the Pentagram Chamber, can attack the Ghost, and only with Sanity.
- If the Ghost attacks and rolls lower than the hero, the Ghost is not harmed.

> Note: the collapse rules are printed under the Ghost's box and say "your first turn in control of the Ghost"; the book does not say whether the house collapses if the heroes control the Ghost.
> Resolution (designer (revised 1st edition)): the house does not collapse while the heroes control the Ghost. The designer's revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome puts the collapse rules under "If You Summon the Ghost First", and its Secrets of Survival sends the traitor to that section only when the heroes fail to bury the bones by Turn 5. Several posters in a BGG thread agree, with no one dissenting. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf, http://web.archive.org/web/20060110122045/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/84488, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2212358

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, no matter who summoned the Ghost first.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are also trying to summon the Ghost first. If they succeed, they gain control of it and receive a quest to complete. If they fail to summon first, they will try to destroy the Ghost instead.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
- Set aside the same number of triangular Sanity Roll tokens.
- Set aside a pentagonal item token (representing the Corpse).
### Rules
#### How to Summon the Ghost
- The heroes race the traitor to summon the Ghost by conducting a séance.
- Each hero can attempt a Knowledge roll or a Sanity roll of 5+, but only while in the Pentagram Chamber. A hero can attempt only one of these rolls per turn.
- Each time one of these rolls succeeds, put a Knowledge Roll or Sanity Roll token (matching the trait rolled) in the room where the haunt was revealed. When the heroes have placed tokens numbering half the number of players (rounded down), they have summoned the Ghost.
- If the heroes finish summoning before the traitor does, they control the Ghost (see the next section). If the traitor finishes the séance first, the traitor gains control of the Ghost.
> Note: The threshold counts "a number of those tokens", apparently Knowledge Roll and Sanity Roll tokens together; the book does not say so outright.
> Resolution (community): yes. Any mix of Knowledge Roll and Sanity Roll tokens counts toward the heroes' target of half the number of players, rounded down. Several independent BGG answers agree. One later post claimed the target is the full player count, which contradicts the printed "half". Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/841127, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/622488
#### If You Summon the Ghost First
- Read the Ghost's declaration aloud: "Bury my bones!"
- Put the Ghost token in the room where the last séance roll succeeded. It stays there until the heroes lose control of it.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip on 1. At the end of each later turn of the player who completed the séance, move the clip to the next number. The heroes have until the start of Turn 5 to bury the Ghost's bones.
- Once during each hero's turn, a hero in the Attic, Bedroom or Master Bedroom can attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ to find the bones. On a success, put the Corpse token on that explorer's character card.
- Carry the Corpse token to the Crypt or the Graveyard. While in one of those rooms, a hero can attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+, once during the turn, to find the right gravestone and bury the bones.
- While the heroes are doing this, the Ghost can't attack anyone.
- If the heroes have not buried the bones by the start of Turn 5, the traitor gains control of the Ghost, following the instructions in the Traitor's Tome. Once that happens, burying the bones no longer placates the Ghost; the heroes must destroy it instead.
#### Special Attack Rules
- No one can attack until the séance has been completed.
- While the traitor controls the Ghost, heroes can make only Sanity attacks against it, and only if they possess the Ring or are in the Pentagram Chamber. A successful Sanity attack destroys the Ghost.
- If the Ghost attacks a hero and is defeated, the Ghost is not harmed.
### Heroes win when
- They defeat the Ghost after the traitor has taken control of it, or
- They bury the Ghost's bones after they summoned it.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is trying to summon a Ghost before the heroes do.
