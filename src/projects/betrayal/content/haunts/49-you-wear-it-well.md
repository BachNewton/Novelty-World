# 49 — You Wear It Well

Summary: The heroes' souls have been torn out of their bodies, and a powerful Astral Spirit allied with the traitor wants to possess an empty body; as disembodied Souls, the heroes must destroy the Spirit.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- Put a small purple monster token, the Astral Spirit, in the traitor's room.
- Set aside triangular Sanity Roll tokens, one per player.
- The Astral Spirit has forced the heroes' Souls out of their physical bodies; their bodies are now unconscious.

### Monsters
- Astral Spirit — Speed 3 · Sanity 6 · Knowledge 6
  - It can move through walls as if they were connecting doorways, but not through floors or ceilings.
  - It isn't stunned when defeated.

### Rules
#### Defeating Explorers' Souls
- The Astral Spirit cannot attack an explorer's physical body. It can attack an explorer's Soul, but only using Sanity or Knowledge.
- "You" (the traitor) can attack a hero's unconscious body. The hero can't defend against these attacks: roll 2 dice and deal that much mental damage to the hero.
- If such an attack reduces a hero's Knowledge or Sanity to the skull symbol, the hero's Soul is destroyed, but the Astral Spirit can't inhabit that body.
> Note: The second rule is addressed to "you" while the surrounding rules describe the Astral Spirit, so it is not certain whether the traitor's explorer, the Spirit, or both may make these attacks on bodies. As written it reads as the traitor.
> Resolution (community): Only the traitor's own explorer can attack the unconscious bodies. The Astral Spirit attacks only Souls, using Sanity or Knowledge, and never bodies. Several independent answers read it this way, and it matches the heroes' booklet, which says the traitor can attack a hero's unconscious body. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/992783, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/117439
#### How the Spirit Inhabits a Body
- Once a hero's Soul has been destroyed (other than by an attack on that hero's unconscious body, above), the Astral Spirit can attempt a ritual to enter that hero's soulless body.
- Once during each of its turns, the Astral Spirit can attempt a Sanity roll against a hero's body in its room. It succeeds if the roll is higher than the starting Sanity of the explorer whose body it is trying to inhabit. Each success puts a Sanity Roll token next to that body.
- When a hero's body has Sanity Roll tokens next to it equal to the number of players, the Astral Spirit inhabits that body and the traitor wins.

### Traitor wins when
- Either all the heroes' Souls are destroyed, or the Astral Spirit inhabits the soulless body of a hero.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to destroy the Astral Spirit. Once they do, they can re-enter their physical bodies.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Each hero tips over their explorer's figure; that explorer is now unconscious. Place a small blue monster token in that hero's room to represent their disembodied Soul. Note each token's number so the heroes' Souls can be told apart.
- Set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal to the number of players, and an equal number of triangular Sanity Roll tokens.
- If there is currently no way to move between the basement and the rest of the house, search the room stack for the Stairs from Basement tile, put it in the house, and shuffle the room stack.
### Rules
#### Souls
- Each hero is now a disembodied Soul.
- A Soul keeps all of its hero's traits.
- A Soul can move through walls as if they were connecting doorways, but not through floors and ceilings.
- A Soul can move freely up and down through the Coal Chute, Collapsed Room, and Gallery.
- A Soul is not affected by any room text that mentions physical traits or physical damage.
- A Soul can use its items but can't trade them to other heroes, and cannot pick up or acquire new items in any way. If a Soul is destroyed, its items are destroyed as well.
- A Soul cannot explore new rooms.
- When attacking or defending, a Soul can use only Knowledge or Sanity.
- Anything that would inflict physical damage on a Soul inflicts mental damage instead.
- A Soul can't use the Skull or any weapon card.
#### How to Destroy the Astral Spirit
- The Astral Spirit can be attacked only with Knowledge or Sanity.
- Each time a hero's attack on it succeeds, put a Knowledge Roll or Sanity Roll token (matching the trait used) on that hero's character card.
- When the heroes have collected tokens equal to the number of players in the game, the Astral Spirit is destroyed.
#### Special Attack Rules
- A hero who attacks the Astral Spirit and loses takes no damage.
- If a hero defeats the traitor with a mental attack, the traitor is stunned but takes no damage.
- The traitor can attack a hero's unconscious body, inflicting 2 dice of mental damage. The hero can't defend against this attack.
### Heroes win when
- They destroy the Astral Spirit. Once it is destroyed, the heroes return to their physical bodies.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is working with a powerful Astral Spirit. If it can possess an unconscious body, it will become immortal.
