# 83 — In the Details

Summary: Everyone in the house has signed an infernal contract, and the devil will claim whoever holds the lowest Name; each player tries to be the last one left or to find a loophole.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor, only heroes. Each of you is trying to be the one who gets out of the contract.
- The rest of this haunt's rules are in Secrets of Survival.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Mix up Obstacle tokens (representing Names) numbered from 1 to the number of players plus two (so a four-player game uses tokens 1 to 6).
- Give each player one Name token in secret. Set the other two aside.
- The haunt revealer takes the first turn.

### Rules
#### How to Find a Loophole in the Contract
- The contract keeps shifting; your Name is sometimes on it and sometimes not. If you hold the lowest number among all players still in the game, you may have found a loophole.
- Before anyone can find a loophole, at least one explorer must have died (the devil won't leave empty-handed).
- After that, while in the Pentagram Chamber, you may attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to turn your Name token face-up.
  - If no one else holds a lower number, you have found a loophole.
  - If someone else holds a lower number, that player shows it to you secretly, and you are removed from the game (you vanish in a puff of smoke). Set your Name token aside.

#### How to Better Understand Your Position
- In the Library or the Study, you may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ to look at all the set-aside Name tokens and, if you want, swap one of them with yours.

#### How to Make a Bargain
- In the Chapel, you may attempt a Sanity roll of 5+ to swap Name tokens with any other explorer.

#### How to Play Dice with the Devil
- If you have the Letter in the Game Room, you may shuffle the Letter into the omen deck to randomize the Names in the contract:
  - Put your Name token out of play and roll 4 dice.
  - Every other living explorer rolls 4 dice. Anyone who rolls equal to or lower than you must put their Name token out of play; anyone who rolls higher than you may choose to do so if they want.
  - Then everyone without a Name token gets a random set-aside Name token, in secret.
> Note: The book uses both "out of play" and "set aside" for Name tokens without saying whether tokens put out of play join the set-aside pool (the Letter rule says you put your token "back out of play", which hints that they do). If they don't, there can be fewer set-aside tokens than explorers who need one (e.g. three explorers put theirs out of play with only two set aside). Owner to rule.
- If you draw the Contract event card, instead of following its instructions you may shuffle it into the event deck to randomize the Names in the same way.

#### Special Attack Rules
- If you kill another explorer, you may look at their Name token number and compare it to yours. Put the higher of the two out of play and keep the other one, face-down.
> Note: The book doesn't say what "put the higher of the two out of play" means for the killer if their own token is the higher one; it reads as the killer keeping the lower token face-down as their Name.

#### At the End of the Round
- At the end of each round, just before the haunt revealer's turn, every living explorer passes their Name token clockwise to the next explorer.

### Heroes win when
- You are the last person left in the house, or you find a loophole in the contract.

### What they know about the other side
- The "bad guys" are the other players: whoever does not hold the lowest number among all players is currently set to have their soul taken by the house's infernal powers.
- You are best off killing the others so that you are lowest by default, or waiting until the contract shifts your way.
