# 18 — United We Stand

Summary: The traitor has become a monster that ordinary weapons can't harm; the heroes must overheat the furnace and set the house on fire to destroy it, escaping if they can.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Take the Turn/Damage track and two plastic clips.
  - Set one clip along the top at 5; this is your new Might.
  - Set the other clip along the bottom at the number of explorers + 1; this is your new Speed.
- Your mental traits (Sanity and Knowledge) stay unchanged.
### Rules
#### Special Movement Rule
- Each turn, roll dice equal to your Speed; the result is your movement for that turn, replacing normal movement. You can always move at least 1 space, whatever you roll.
#### Special Attack Rules
- You take no damage from physical attacks.
- A hero using the Ring can attack you with Sanity. If that hero defeats you, instead of inflicting damage, he or she can move you in any direction as many spaces as the difference between the die rolls.
- If the Dynamite explodes in your room, you are stunned for one turn.
- If any heroes have escaped from the house, you can try to pull them back in:
  - You must be in the Entrance Hall, in a room with an outside-facing window (Grand Staircase, Master Bedroom, Bedroom, Chapel, Dining Room), or in a room open to the outside (Conservatory, Entrance Hall, Gardens, Graveyard, Patio, Tower, Balcony).
  - Make a Might roll against the Might of the strongest hero outside the house. That hero adds 1 to the roll for each other hero who is also outside.
  - If you roll higher, pull a hero of your choice back into the house, placing that hero in your room. You can then attack him or her normally.
  - You can repeat this several times in one turn, until you fail an attack.
- If you kill a hero, you absorb him or her into your body: increase your Might by 1 and reduce your Speed by 1.
> Note: The pull-back rule ends repetition when you "fail an attack"; the book does not say whether a failed pull-back roll or a failed normal attack on a pulled-in hero (or either) ends it.
> Resolution (community): Either failure ends it: the traitor keeps pulling heroes back in and attacking them until it fails a pull-back roll or loses an attack against a pulled-in hero. Two independent BGG answers agree (both note the book is poorly worded), none disagree. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2226196
### Traitor wins when
- You kill at least two heroes (absorbing them), then escape through the front door, or
- All but one of the heroes are dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The heroes want to kill you by melting your flesh.
- They also want to escape by leaving through the front door. You can pull them back in with your strength.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If the Stairs from Basement tile is not in the house, search the room stack for it and place it in the basement. Then shuffle that stack.
### Rules
#### How to Burn Down the House
- A hero must discover and move to the Furnace Room. Once there, the hero makes a Knowledge roll of 5+. On a success, the furnace is set to overheat.
- At the end of the next turn of the hero who set the furnace to overheat, the Furnace Room explodes. Turn its tile over so its back is showing. Any explorer in the room (including the traitor) is killed.
- From then on, at the end of every explorer's turn (including the traitor's), a new room catches fire, killing anyone in it. (Flip the tile over.)
  - A room can't catch fire unless it is adjacent to a room that has already burned. The adjacent rooms don't need to have connecting doors.
  - Dead heroes' players can't cause the fire to spread, but the traitor must always do so.
- When the fire reaches the Stairs from Basement, a later explorer can choose to destroy the Foyer. The fire then spreads from there to the Entrance Hall and Grand Staircase. (Mark these rooms with pentagonal item tokens to show they have been destroyed.)
- The fire can also spread from the Basement Landing up the Coal Chute, or from a basement room up to the Collapsed Room.
- When the fire destroys the Foyer, the Entrance Hall and the Grand Staircase, the house collapses, killing anyone left inside.
#### How to Escape the House
- An explorer can attempt a Knowledge roll (picking the lock) or a Might roll (breaking down the door) of 4+ to open the front door in the Entrance Hall.
- If the roll succeeds, that explorer draws an event card and ends his or her turn. On later turns, all explorers can move 1 space out the front door from the Entrance Hall to escape.
- The traitor can't initially move through the front door: the escaping heroes bar it behind them.
> Note: "initially" is not defined further; the book doesn't say when or whether the traitor may later use the front door.
> Resolution (community): Most BGG answers read it as: the traitor can't leave through the front door until it has absorbed two heroes (its own win requirement), after which it may leave with no roll needed. One poster calls the question genuinely open (auto-exit versus rolling to open the door, as heroes do), so this is a majority reading rather than a settled one. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2226196, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1362888
### Heroes win when
- The house fire kills the traitor.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor hungers for the heroes' flesh, and only burning down the house will kill the monster.
