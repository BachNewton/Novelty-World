# 40 — Buried Alive

Summary: The traitor has buried a nonplayer friend alive somewhere in the basement; the heroes use the Spirit Board to find the burial room and dig the friend out before they die.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0; it tracks time.
- Choose a basement room already discovered in the house and secretly write it down: the buried person is in that room.
- If the basement currently has fewer than five rooms (the Basement Landing counts), draw and place basement rooms until there are five, then write down which one is the burial room.

> Note: The two setup steps overlap: the first says to choose an already-discovered basement room, the second says to fill the basement to five rooms first and then record the burial room. Presumably the basement is filled to five before choosing.
> Resolution (designer): Fill the basement to five rooms first, then choose the burial room. The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome (Bruce Glassco's rewrite for Avalon Hill) says to place the extra basement rooms before writing down the burial room. There, filling was optional ("may"); the 2nd edition makes it mandatory. Source: https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/84488

### Rules
#### You Must Do This...
- At the end of each of the traitor's turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number, starting with 1.
- Then roll a number of dice equal to that number. The buried person takes that much physical damage.
- Keep a running total of this damage. The buried person can take 12 points of damage before dying.

> Note: "can take 12 points of damage before dying" doesn't state whether death comes at exactly 12 total or only beyond 12.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome has the same wording. Searched the official FAQ, the BGG rules forum (haunt 40 threads) and the web. Source: https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf

#### The Spirit Board
- The Spirit Board helps the heroes. The explorer currently carrying it cannot trade or drop it.
- If the traitor defeats the hero carrying the Spirit Board by 2 or more with a Might attack, the traitor may steal the Spirit Board instead of inflicting damage. If the traitor does so, the Spirit Board is immediately destroyed and its card is discarded.

### Traitor wins when
- The buried person is dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to rescue the buried person.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Might Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
### Rules
#### How to Rescue Your Friend
- Searching: each time a hero moves into a room, they may search it by attempting a Knowledge roll of 3+ in that room. On a success, they ask the traitor whether this is the burial room, and the traitor must answer truthfully.
- A hero can search a room only once during their turn.
- Digging: once the burial room has been found, a hero in that room may attempt a Might roll of 4+ to help dig up the buried person. A hero may attempt one such roll during their turn.
- Each time a hero succeeds at the digging roll, put a Might Roll token in that room.
- The buried person is saved when the number of successful Might rolls (Might Roll tokens placed) equals the number of players.
#### The Spirit Board
- Once the haunt begins, the Spirit Board can no longer be used to look at the top tile of the room stack. Instead, it can be used to find the buried person.
- The hero holding the Spirit Board can't voluntarily give it away, trade it, or drop it. If that hero dies, the Spirit Board drops with all that character's items as normal, and any explorer can pick up the Spirit Board along with the other dropped items.
- The hero with the Spirit Board can use it once during each of their turns, until the burial room has been found. When the burial room is found, discard the Spirit Board card.
- On a turn when a hero uses the Spirit Board to find the burial room, that hero can't move or take any other actions that turn.
- To use it, attempt a Sanity roll and consult this table:

| Die Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | No effect. |
| 3–4 | Move any explorer 3 spaces. |
| 5–6 | Heal the buried person. The traitor is tracking how much damage the buried person has taken: roll 2 dice and tell the traitor to subtract the result from the buried person's damage total. |
| 7+ | The traitor must announce the room where your friend is buried. |

### Heroes win when
- They find and dig up the buried person before he or she dies.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has buried a (nonplayer) friend of the heroes in a basement room, and secretly writes down which room.
- The heroes don't know which room, but know the person was in the house when the haunt began.
- The traitor is keeping track of how much damage the buried person has taken.
