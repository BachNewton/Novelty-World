# 70 — To Reach the Cosmos

Summary: The traitor has become a brain-sucking horror that uses tentacle-like Brainstraws to steal the heroes' Thoughts; the heroes must take all of the traitor's Thoughts.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Set aside all the small green monster tokens; they are the Brainstraws.
- Place one Brainstraw in your room, and one in an adjacent room up to three rooms away that has no hero in it. If no such empty room exists, you may put the tokens in your own room.
- Take one piece of paper per hero. On each, write a "Thought" for that hero. It can be what you plan to do after ascending to the cosmos, something about an earlier event in the game, or simply the first thing that comes to mind. Fold each paper so no one else can read it.
- On the outside of each paper, write a trait (for example, Knowledge). No trait may be used more than twice. For each Thought, raise its listed trait by 1.

### Monsters
- Brainstraws — Speed 3 · Might 5 · Sanity 5 · Knowledge 5
  - If a Brainstraw enters the Mystic Elevator, the Elevator stops working until the Brainstraw leaves it.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- You may make a Sanity attack against a hero in your room in place of your physical attack.
- A Brainstraw may steal one of a hero's Thoughts, picked at random, by succeeding in a Knowledge attack against that hero. After stealing, the Brainstraw's movement ends and it spawns another Brainstraw in its room.
- If a Brainstraw is defeated by any attack during a hero's turn, it is stunned and retreats: place it in your room.
- When a hero defeats you in mental combat in order to steal your Thought, and you hold another explorer's Thought, you may give the hero that other Thought instead.
#### Losing and Gaining Thoughts
- When you lose a Thought, lose 1 in the trait written on the outside of its paper.
- When you gain a Thought, gain 1 in its trait and read the Thought aloud.

### Traitor wins when
- You end your turn holding the Thought of every living hero.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes do not want you to ascend. They will try to cut down your Brainstraws, and may even try to take your own Thoughts.

> Note: "an adjacent room up to three rooms away" is printed as is; it reads as "a room up to three rooms away", with "adjacent" not clearly meaning only one step.
> Note: Setup says one Thought is written "for each hero", yet the special attack rules mention "your Thought" and "another explorer's Thought" being held by you; the book does not say whether you write a Thought for yourself.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Each hero takes a piece of paper and writes a "Thought" on it, from that hero's perspective (anything that comes to mind, or something from an earlier event). Fold the paper so no one else can read it.
- On the outside of the folded paper, the hero writes the name of one trait. That hero raises that trait by 1.
### Rules
- At the start of your turn, if you have no Thought, you take 1 die of mental damage.
#### Special attack rules
- You may make a Knowledge attack against the traitor. If you succeed, you steal one of the traitor's Thoughts, chosen at random.
- If a Brainstraw defeats you in physical combat while you have a Thought, you take no damage. If you have no Thought, you take normal damage.
- If you defeat a Brainstraw in combat, it is stunned and retreats: move the Brainstraw into the traitor's room.
- If you defeat the traitor in physical combat, you deal no damage, but you may move the traitor to any room that contains a Brainstraw.
- A hero may try to steal a Thought from whoever holds it by succeeding at a Knowledge attack against that player.
  > Note: The book says "whoever has it" without naming whose Thought; read literally, a hero may make this Knowledge attack against any player holding a Thought (including another hero).
#### Losing and gaining Thoughts
- When you lose a Thought, lower the trait written on the outside of that paper by 1.
- When you gain a Thought, raise the trait written on it by 1, and read the Thought aloud.
- You may give a Thought to another hero in your room without attacking. This counts as you losing that Thought and the other hero gaining it (trait changes and reading aloud apply).
### Heroes win when
- You have taken all of the traitor's Thoughts.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has become a brain-sucking horror and is trying to steal the heroes' Thoughts with Brainstraws.
