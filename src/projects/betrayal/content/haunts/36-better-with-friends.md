# 36 — Better with Friends

Summary: The traitor has lured the group into a house that is sinking into an underground swamp; the heroes must fetch the Rowboat from the Attic and escape together before they drown.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- Put a pentagonal item token in the Attic to represent the Rowboat. If the Attic is not in play, put the Rowboat token there when the Attic is discovered.
- Set aside 4 triangular Might Roll tokens.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0; it tracks time.

### Rules
- At the end of each of the traitor's turns, the traitor must advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number, starting with 1.

#### Flooding
- The house floods to different levels depending on the turn. Each time the traitor advances the Turn/Damage track to a new number, the traitor tells the other players which floors are flooded and whether each is partially or fully flooded.

| Turn | Flood Level |
|---|---|
| 1 | Basement partially flooded. |
| 2 | Basement fully flooded. |
| 3 | Basement fully flooded and ground floor partially flooded. |
| 4 | Basement and ground floor fully flooded. |
| 5 | Basement and ground floor fully flooded, upper floor partially flooded. |
| 6 | Whole house fully flooded for remainder of game. |

- Flooding affects all the heroes but not the traitor.
- The effects of flooding are defined in the Secrets of Survival booklet.

#### Special Attack Rules
- The traitor can attack the Rowboat once during their turn, and only with a Might attack.
- To damage the Rowboat, the traitor attempts a Might roll of 3+. Each time the Rowboat is damaged, put a Might Roll token on the traitor's character card.
- Once the traitor has accumulated 4 tokens, the next successful Might attack disables the Rowboat.

> Note: The book does not say whether the traitor must be in the same room as the Rowboat to attack it. It also does not spell out what counts as a "successful Might attack" after the 4th token; presumably it is another Might roll of 3+.
> Resolution (designer): The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome (Bruce Glassco's rewrite for Avalon Hill) says the fifth successful Might attack destroys the Rowboat. So after the 4th token it is one more successful Might roll of 3+, and a forum answer agrees that it takes five successful attacks. On the room question, the community answers agree that the traitor must be in the Rowboat's room to attack it. Source: https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/84488, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1745155

### Traitor wins when
- Either more than half of the heroes who started the haunt are dead, or the Rowboat is disabled.

### What they know about the other side
- The house and the area around it are sinking into an underground swamp, and the heroes will try to escape.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### Effects of Flooding
- Any explorer (the traitor included) who begins a turn on a flooded level of the house suffers these effects:
  - Partially Flooded: move 2 fewer spaces that turn.
  - Fully Flooded: move 4 fewer spaces that turn and take 2 points of physical damage. This damage cannot be prevented or reduced in any way.
- However flooded the house is, an explorer can always move at least 1 space each turn.
#### Slowing the Flood
- The traitor uses the Turn/Damage track to mark the passage of time.
- During their turn, a hero may drop the Medallion in a partially or fully flooded room to stop the sinking for one turn. The Medallion card is then discarded, and on the traitor's next turn the traitor does not advance the Turn/Damage track.
#### How to Escape the House
- The Rowboat is in the Attic. If the Attic is not yet in the house, the heroes must explore the upper floor until they find it.
- A hero picks up the Rowboat and carries it from the Attic to the Balcony or Tower, placing the Rowboat token on their character card while carrying it.
- The Rowboat is a heavy item: while carrying it, each room entered counts as 2 spaces of movement.
- Only one hero can carry the Rowboat at a time, but it can be traded. The Dog cannot carry the Rowboat.
- As soon as every living hero is in the Balcony or Tower with the Rowboat, the heroes escape. They cannot escape while any living hero is still in the house.

### Heroes win when
- At least half the heroes who started the haunt (rounding up) escape the house alive. No living hero may be left behind in the house when they escape.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor lured the heroes here to die.
- The house is sinking into an underground swamp, and the heroes will drown unless they escape.

> Note: The heroes' half does not define which levels are partially or fully flooded or when that changes; the traitor's half holds that table and the traitor announces it.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found, and none seems needed. The revised 1st-edition booklets split the information the same way, and the traitor announces each flood level.
