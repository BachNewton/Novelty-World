# 82 — Get a Clue

Summary: The traitor, a long-serving butler, has just strangled the master of the house with a rope; the police are on their way, and the heroes are trying to establish alibis and pin the crime on the butler.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Gain 2 Might.
- Put a plastic clip on the Turn/Damage track at the number equal to the number of heroes. This track keeps time.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- At the start of your turn, move the Turn/Damage track clip to the next number. When it reaches 12, the police arrive.

#### Special attack rules
- If any of your traits would drop to the skull symbol, lower it to the lowest value above the skull symbol instead.
- Heroes will set up their alibis in certain rooms of the house. You cannot damage heroes, or steal from heroes, while they are in their alibi rooms.

#### Special item and omen rules
- You may not pick up item cards, item tokens, or omens.
- You may, however, steal weapons and the Rope from heroes by using physical attacks.
> Note: This page doesn't say where the Rope starts or which rooms are alibi rooms. Secrets of Survival says the Rope starts with the traitor and lists each hero's alibi rooms. Neither book says what token or card represents the Rope.
> Resolution (official): the Rope is the Widow's Walk Rope omen card. The FAQ, on playing Widow's Walk with a 1st-edition copy, says this haunt's references to the Rope mean the Widow's Walk omen Rope, not the 1st-edition item of the same name. It doesn't say how the traitor comes to hold the Rope if someone else has the omen, or if it hasn't been drawn. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead, or
- The Rope is dropped in the Dining Room and the police arrive before the heroes still alive have their alibis.
> Note: Neither book says what happens if the police arrive while some living hero has no alibi but the Rope is not in the Dining Room; the heroes' win condition is not met then either.
> Note: The printed sentence ("all the heroes are dead or the Rope is dropped in the Dining Room and the police arrive before those left alive have their alibis") can be grouped two ways. The split above is the more natural reading; the alternative is that the police arrival / alibi condition applies to both branches.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes know you committed the murder and plan to tell the police so, along with why none of them could have done it.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If the Dining Room is not yet in play, search the room stack until you find it, place it in the house, then shuffle that stack.
- Put all heroes' figures in the Dining Room. Each hero is a dinner guest, determined by the explorer's color (see Dinner Guests).
- Set aside one explorer token for each hero.
- Put pentagonal item tokens in the Dining Room, one per hero. They are the potential murder weapons: 1) a Stiletto, 2) a Derringer, 3) a Candelabrum, 4) a Crowbar, and 5) a Truncheon. Use them in that order; with fewer than five heroes, not every weapon is used.
- The Rope is also a potential murder weapon, but it starts with the traitor.

### Rules
#### How to Establish an Alibi
- A hero establishes an alibi by taking one potential murder weapon (the Rope, or one from the pile in the Dining Room) to one of that hero's own alibi rooms.
- The hero then attempts a Knowledge roll of 5+ to "remember" a convincing story involving the hero, the weapon brought, and the current room.
- To avoid conflicting stories, every alibi must be established in a different room.
- When the alibi is established, put your explorer token and the item token or card in that room. The weapon may not be picked up or used until the police arrive.

#### Dinner Guests
| Color | Name | Alibi Rooms |
|---|---|---|
| Red | Mr. Rose | Ballroom, Entrance Hall, Game Room |
| Yellow | Lieutenant Lemon | Drawing Room, Entrance Hall, Study |
| White | Professor Pale | Conservatory, Entrance Hall, Kitchen |
| Green | Mr. Lime | Ballroom, Conservatory, Entrance Hall |
| Blue | Mrs. Sapphire | Drawing Room, Entrance Hall, Game Room |
| Purple | Miss Violet | Library, Entrance Hall, Study |

#### Special Attack Rules
- The Butler can be damaged normally but cannot be killed. Heroes can steal items from him with physical attacks as normal.
- While a hero is in one of their own alibi rooms, the traitor can't do any damage to that hero during an attack.

### Heroes win when
- Every hero has established an alibi before the police arrive.
- One of the alibis must use the Rope, which does not start in the Dining Room.
- If a hero dies before establishing an alibi, another hero may establish an alibi for the dead hero, using the dead hero's alibi rooms.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is the Butler, a deeply disgruntled employee. After strangling the house's owner, Mr. Dedman, in an argument over dessert dishes, he has snapped and the heroes are his next intended victims.
- The police are coming; when they arrive everyone needs an alibi, or the Rope will be pinned on them.
