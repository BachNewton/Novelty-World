# 64 — Chairman of the Board

Summary: The other explorers turn out to be Poltergeists haunting the house; the lone hero must escape through the front door before they strip away the hero's sanity, defending with a Spirit Board.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- All explorers except one are traitors; there is one hero. The traitors' figures stay in the house, but they are now Poltergeists.
- Put the hero's figure in any room in the basement or on the roof that is not cut off from the rest of the house by a barrier. If a Poltergeist is in that room, move that Poltergeist's figure to where the hero was.
- Get some pieces of paper.
- Look closely at the details of every room tile.

> Note: The page says "your explorers are still in the game, but you have all turned traitor" and refers to "the hero" throughout; it is inferred that the explorer who revealed the haunt (or whoever Secrets of Survival names) is the lone hero.

### Rules
#### Special Movement Rules
- Poltergeists can move through walls as if they were connecting doorways, but not through floors or ceilings.
- They can move freely up and down through the Coal Chute, Collapsed Room, and Gallery.
- They are not affected by any room text that mentions physical traits or physical damage.
- They cannot discover new rooms, and cannot use the Mystic Elevator or dumbwaiters.
- They can use their item cards and omen cards but cannot trade them to other explorers. They cannot pick up or acquire new items or omens in any way.

#### Special Attack Rules
- Poltergeists cannot make normal attacks. Instead, a Poltergeist must end its movement in the hero's room and throw something in that room at the hero:
  - Look at the room tile and write down the name of a thing pictured on it (for example, REFRIGERATOR). The name must be one or two words, at least six letters long, and not tricky (for example, not FRIGIDAIRE for the REFRIGERATOR in the Kitchen). The hero must not see what is written.
  - Then make a Sanity attack. The hero defends by a method described in Secrets of Survival. When asked a question after this, the Poltergeist must answer truthfully.
  - The book's tip: attack in rooms with lots of things pictured on the tile; the Creaky Hallway has "floorboards" and little else.
- If a Poltergeist deals damage, it may instead move the hero a number of rooms equal to the damage it would have dealt. This must be a legal move for the hero.
- When defending, Poltergeists may use only Sanity or Knowledge. Anything that would deal physical damage to a Poltergeist deals mental damage instead.

#### Special Death Rules
- Poltergeists cannot be truly killed, even by a successful exorcism.
- If a Poltergeist dies, remove its figure from the house as normal; it loses all its item cards.
- At the start of its next turn, it places itself in the Dungeon, Rookery, Study, or Theater and resets its traits to their starting values. It may not move on that turn.

### Traitor wins when
- The hero dies.

### What they know about the other side
- The hero is trying to escape. If the hero gets out through the front door of the Entrance Hall, the Poltergeists lose their chance to make the hero a ghostly resident.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The hero player leaves the room; the traitors stay with the house this time.
- Get a piece of paper to be the Spirit Board and write the letters A through Z on it with plenty of room between them. (A real spirit board, such as The Ouija Board, can be used instead.)
- On returning, take a pentagonal item token from the box to be the Planchette (the pointer used with a spirit board).
- Take the Spirit Board omen card from whoever found it.
### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- You may attempt exorcisms to kill Poltergeists instantly. Each exorcism roll requires a specific room or item card, and you can make one "exorcism roll" each turn.
  - Sanity roll of 5+: while in the Chapel, Crypt, or Pentagram Chamber, or while carrying the Holy Symbol or Ring.
  - Knowledge roll of 5+: while in the Library or Research Laboratory, or while carrying the Book or Crystal Ball.
- If the exorcism succeeds, one traitor in your room dies instantly.
#### How to use the Spirit Board
- When you are attacked, you roll no dice. You defend with the Spirit Board instead.
- The attacking Poltergeist writes down one thing shown on the room tile; it must be one or two words and at least six letters long.
- Study the room for things it could be. You may then move the corner of your Planchette around the Spirit Board to point at one letter at a time, as many times as your Knowledge value, picking letters you believe are in the thing the Poltergeist is attacking with.
- For each letter, the Poltergeist must say how many times it occurs in the answer. Each correct letter counts 1 point toward your defense value for each time it occurs in the word or phrase.
- After that, guess the word or phrase. If you are right, add 1 more point to your defense value.
- Example (from the book): with Knowledge 5 in the Kitchen, you point to F-R-I-G-E. If the answer was FRIDGE you defend with 5; if it was REFRIGERATOR you defend with 8 (3 Rs, 2 Es, 1 F, 1 I, 1 G); if you also guessed REFRIGERATOR you defend with 9.
- When you would damage a Poltergeist with the Spirit Board, you may instead move 1 room.
#### Special movement rules
- Poltergeists do not count as opponents for the purposes of counting movement.
#### Special omen rules
- The Spirit Board cannot be dropped, traded, or stolen.
### Heroes win when
- You move through the front door of the Entrance Hall.
### What they know about the other side
- All the other explorers are now Poltergeists, trying to turn the hero into a ghost to haunt the house with them.
