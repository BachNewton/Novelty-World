# 35 — Small Change

Summary: The traitor has shrunk the heroes to the size of mice and let Cats into the house; the heroes must find a Toy Airplane and fly out before they are eaten.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- With three or four players: put one small red monster token (a Cat) in the Entrance Hall.
- With five or six players: put one Cat token in the Entrance Hall and another in the room where the haunt was revealed.

### Monsters
- Cats — Speed 6 · Might 7 · Sanity 5 · Knowledge 5
  - Cats count each doorway as a space, the same as explorers do (see Being Small).
  - A Cat can catch only one hero at a time.

### Rules
#### Being Small
- All items and omens the traitor carries have shrunk too and work normally.
- The traitor cannot draw any cards. Discovering a new room that shows any of the symbols [spiral event symbol] [bull-skull item symbol] [raven omen symbol] ends the traitor's turn.
- Each doorway counts as a space, so moving through a doorway into the next room counts as 2 spaces. A figure may stop in a doorway space.
- Going up or down any stairs requires a Might roll of 3+. On a failure the traitor's turn ends, and they may try again next turn.
- The traitor cannot use the Collapsed Room or the Mystic Elevator.
- The traitor is not affected by the Gallery, Gymnasium or Vault.

#### Special Attack Rules
- The traitor cannot attack explorers (the Cats are meant to eat them).
- When a Cat defeats a hero, it does not inflict damage; instead it plays "cat-and-mouse" with that hero for a turn:
  - On that hero's next turn, the hero gets a chance to escape (the heroes' booklet explains how).
  - If anyone defeats the Cat before the Cat's next turn, the Cat drops the captured hero and is stunned.
  - Otherwise, at the start of the next monster turn the Cat swallows and kills the captured hero.
  - Captured heroes are not affected by the Bell.
- A Cat in the same room as the Toy Airplane can attempt a Speed roll of 7+ to bat it down. A Cat that succeeds can then immediately attack a hero in the Toy Airplane.
- The traitor can also attempt to knock down the Plane, which takes a Speed roll of 5+.
- The heroes can attempt to restart the plane on their turns.

> Note: The book says the traitor "can also attempt to knock down the Plane" but does not say whether the traitor must be in the same room as the Toy Airplane, or what happens to heroes aboard when the traitor (rather than a Cat) knocks it down.
> Resolution (designer): The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome, which the designer Bruce Glassco rewrote for Avalon Hill, says the traitor can attempt "the same thing" as a Cat in the Toy Airplane's room. So the traitor must be in the same room as the plane. The same passage says the heroes may restart the plane on their turns, so a downed plane is grounded and must be restarted, as after a Cat bats it down. Nothing official says what happens to the heroes aboard: one community answer treats them as out of the plane, and another says only that the plane must be restarted. Source: https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/84488, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1281868, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1701681

### Traitor wins when
- More than half of the heroes who began the haunt have been killed by Cats.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to escape the house in a Toy Airplane.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside a pentagonal item token to represent the Toy Airplane.

### Rules
#### Being Small
- Items and omens the heroes carry shrank with them and work normally.
- Heroes cannot draw any cards. Discovering a new room that has any symbol ends the hero's turn.
- Each doorway counts as a space, so moving through a doorway into the next room costs 2 spaces. A hero may stop in a doorway space.
- Going up or down any stairs requires a Might roll of 3+. On a failure, the hero's turn ends, but they may try again next turn.
- Heroes cannot use the Collapsed Room or the Mystic Elevator. They are not affected by the Gallery, Gymnasium, or Vault.
- Heroes need the Toy Airplane to leave the house.
#### Using the Toy Airplane
- The Toy Airplane is in the Bedroom, Master Bedroom, Storeroom, Attic, or Game Room. Once during their turn, a hero in one of these rooms may attempt a Knowledge roll of 3+ to search it. On a success, put the Toy Airplane token in that room.
- Once during their turn, a hero may attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to start the Toy Airplane. On a success, it stays on the ground until that hero's next turn. Meanwhile other heroes can board it, but any heroes in it can be attacked by Cats.
- While flying, the Toy Airplane has a Speed of 5. On their following turns, the hero who started it moves it using the Airplane's Speed instead of their normal movement. Like shrunken explorers, it counts doorways as separate spaces.
- Picking up a hero on the ground with the Toy Airplane costs 1 space of movement. When this is done, the hero in the plane with the highest Speed attempts a Speed roll:

| Die Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 4+ | You pick up the explorer. |
| 2–3 | You fail, but you can try again (counts as another space of movement). |
| 0–1 | You crash. The Toy Airplane is on the ground, and you must restart it. |

- The heroes cannot leave the house until every living hero has been picked up. Leaving costs 1 space of movement to exit through a room's outside-facing edge.
- Flying heroes can attack or be attacked only with the Revolver, Ring, or Dynamite.
- Flying heroes do not make Might rolls to use stairs. They can go up or down through the Collapsed Room and Gallery, and cross the Chasm without rolling or taking damage.
- The traitor cannot fly the Toy Airplane.
#### If a Cat Catches You
- A hero captured by a Cat may try to get away at the start of their next turn: choose any trait, and the hero and the Cat each roll that trait (a "contest"). If the hero rolls higher than the Cat, they escape and take their turn normally. Otherwise they remain captured and their turn is over.
- If another hero attacks and defeats the Cat, the Cat drops the captured hero and is stunned.
- Heroes currently captured by Cats cannot be picked up by the Toy Airplane.

### Heroes win when
- At least half the heroes (rounding up) use the Toy Airplane to escape through the Balcony, Gardens, Graveyard, Patio, Tower, or any room with an outside-facing window.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor shrank the heroes and let the Cats into the house. The Cats will try to eat them.

> Note: The win condition names specific rooms (Balcony, Gardens, Graveyard, Patio, Tower, or any room with an outside-facing window), while the airplane rule speaks of exiting through "a room's outside-facing edge". The book does not reconcile these two phrasings.
> Resolution (designer): The revised 1st-edition Secrets of Survival (Bruce Glassco's rewrite for Avalon Hill) joins the two phrasings. The heroes escape through the outer edge of one of the listed rooms (Balcony, Gardens, Graveyard, Patio or Tower) or of a room with an outside-facing window.
