# 76 — Back to the Past

Summary: The heroes are sent back to the night the house was cursed, as reincarnations of its builders, and must find and reveal their murderer, the traitor, hiding somewhere in the house, before time runs out.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Set aside nine Obstacle tokens; they are the Pentagrams. Set aside six small monster tokens of any color; they mark Flipped Landings.
- Remove your figure from the house: you act from behind the scenes.
- Choose a room in play to hide in. Write its name on a piece of paper and keep it secret.

### Rules
#### Special Turn Order Rules
- You take no turns of your own. You act during the heroes' turns instead.
#### You Must Do This on Each Hero's Turn
- On each turn, the hero asks you one yes-or-no question about your hiding room. You must answer truthfully.
- A question may only be about a specific room or a room's characteristics, limited to the names or characteristics of the room. A question cannot involve which floor the room is on, its position relative to other rooms, or the symbols or game text on the room.
- After you answer, the heroes may be able to rule out rooms. Roll 2 dice, then flip face-down up to that many rooms that the players now know are not your room, based on your answer. "Do not ever flip your room."
  - Example 1 (from the book): asked whether your room has only one door, you answered no, so the heroes can eliminate every one-door room. You may flip as many one-door rooms as you rolled.
  - Example 2 (from the book): asked whether your room has a table, you answered yes. You may flip as many rooms without a table as you rolled.
- To flip a room: remove any figures and tokens from it, flip the tile over, put it back where it was, then put the figures and tokens back.
- If you flip a landing, put a small monster token on the flipped room as a reminder of which landing it was. It is still treated as the landing for that floor.
#### How to Slow the Heroes
- At the start of each hero's turn, you may place one of the nine Pentagrams in any room. Entering a room with a Pentagram token costs one extra space of movement. Use them to slow the heroes on their way to your room, or to disguise where you are hiding.
- Each time a hero enters a flipped room, that hero takes 1 die of mental damage, so choose which rooms to flip wisely.

### Traitor wins when
- You complete the ritual by still being unrevealed when the Turn/Damage track reaches 0.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are reversing time to stop you from killing them. You must slow them down by hiding your location.

> Note: Who sets and lowers the Turn/Damage track is not stated on this page; the heroes' half sets it at 12 and lowers it on each hero's turn and on each wrong guess.
> Note: The flip rule reads as the traitor doing the flipping ("you can flip"), though the first sentence says "the heroes may be able to eliminate" rooms.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Starting with the haunt revealer, players take turns drawing rooms from the room stack and placing them in the house. You may not place a room if doing so would make it impossible to place another room. Continue until every tile is placed or no more rooms can be put in the house.
  > Note: The book doesn't say here whether these rooms are placed face down. The "face-down" rooms in the movement rules are the rooms the traitor flips face down after answering questions (traitor's half), so these are presumably placed face up as normal; this is interpretation.
- Put the heroes' figures in the Entrance Hall.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 12. It tracks time, which counts down.
### Rules
- On each of your turns, lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number. Then, if the traitor has not been found, the traitor may place an Obstacle token, representing a Pentagram, in any room.
  > Note: The book doesn't say at which point of the turn the track is lowered.
#### How to search for the traitor
- At the start of your turn, you may ask the traitor one yes-or-no question about where the traitor is. The traitor must answer truthfully.
  - Questions may only concern the names or characteristics of the room.
  - Questions may not involve which floor the room is on, or where it is in relation to other rooms.
  - You may not ask about symbols or game text on the room.
- At the end of your turn, you may declare that your room is the room where the traitor is hiding. If you are right, the traitor tells you so and the heroes win. If you are wrong, take 1 point of mental damage and lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number.
#### Special movement rules
- You can use any false door, as long as you go through the door's entrance side.
- When you enter a face-down room, take 1 die of mental damage.
- You may enter and leave a face-down room through any of its sides.
- Ignore any text that hinders movement, except for the Pentagrams placed by the traitor. Entering a room that contains a Pentagram token costs one extra space of movement.
### Heroes win when
- You reveal the traitor before the Turn/Damage track reaches 0.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor murdered the heroes' former selves, and is trying to collect the house's magic through a ritual that will go wrong (or already has).
