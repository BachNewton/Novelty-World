# 78 — The Devil's Name

Summary: An ancient Fiend freed from a stone coffer has possessed the traitor and wants to possess every explorer; the heroes must work out its five-letter true name from Tomes of Lore and speak it to banish it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Gain 5 Knowledge.
- Put six pentagonal item tokens numbered 1 to 6, representing the Tomes of Lore, in different rooms. Put at least one on each floor, but only one may go on the floor you are currently on.
- If you have the Chalk card, give it to a hero.
- Pick a number from 1 to 20 to get a 5-letter name from the Fiend's Name table. Write the name on a piece of paper. "Do not reveal this name to the heroes."

#### How Your True Name Works
- Each Tome of Lore can reveal one letter of your true name. A letter is revealed only once a hero spends all of their movement for a turn and successfully researches the Tome. When a hero's research succeeds, you must reveal one unrevealed letter of your true name (any letter, in any order). Each instance of a letter counts as a separate letter, even if your name repeats a letter.
- If a hero in your room speaks your true name, you are banished and the heroes win. If a hero speaks the wrong name to you, you possess that hero immediately (see Special Attack Rules).
- A hero may also speak a name to a possessed hero. If it is correct, that possession ends and cannot be regained. If it is wrong, you deal the guesser mental damage equal to the number of letters not yet guessed.
#### Special Attack Rules
- You may make a Knowledge attack against a hero in your room. If that attack reduces the hero's trait to the skull symbol, part of your immortal essence possesses the hero. Possessed heroes reset their traits to their starting values and become traitors.
- If any of your traits would drop to the skull symbol, lower it to its lowest value above the skull symbol instead.
#### Special Item Rules
- You cannot pick up Tomes of Lore or the Chalk. Possessed heroes can do both.

### Traitor wins when
- Every hero is possessed or dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes have found the Brimstone Square, a relic bearing the secret of your true name, which has the power to imprison you again. It holds many secrets, so the heroes need to find Tomes of Lore to decipher your true name.

> Note: "the number of unguessed letters" — this page does not define whether this means letters the guesser got wrong in their guess or letters not yet revealed by Tomes. The heroes' book words the same rule as "the number of unrevealed letters", which points to letters not yet revealed.
> Note: Research roll details (trait, target) are not given on this page; the heroes' half gives a Knowledge roll of 5+.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If the Chalk card is not yet in play, search the item stack and discard pile until you find it and give it to a hero. Then shuffle the discard pile into the item stack.

### Rules
#### How to learn the Fiend's true name
- When you enter a room that contains a Tome of Lore, you may take it. You can carry only one Tome of Lore at a time.
- On your turn, you may give up your movement to attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ to research the name in the Brimstone Square.
  > Note: The book doesn't say outright that you must carry a Tome of Lore to research, but a success discards the Tome, and the traitor's book says a letter is revealed only when a hero "successfully researches the Tome"; this is interpretation.
  - On a success, discard the Tome of Lore; the traitor must tell you one letter of the name (any unrevealed letter the traitor chooses).
  - On a failure, you may try again on a later turn.
- Each occurrence of a letter in the name counts as a separate letter, even if the name repeats a letter.
#### How to banish the Fiend
- Knowing the name is not enough; the Fiend must still be banished by speaking it.
- You must be in the same room as the Fiend or as a hero possessed by the Fiend, and speak the name you believe is its true name.
  > Note: The book's sentence reads "in the same room the Fiend, or a hero possessed by the Fiend" (a word appears to be missing); it's read here as "the same room as".
- Once on your turn you may speak a name to one traitor, whether or not all the letters have been revealed. Guessing from partial information is allowed but risky.
  > Note: The book says "to one traitor" here, though the targets described are the Fiend and possessed heroes.
- If you speak an **incorrect** name:
  - to a possessed hero: the Fiend deals you points of mental damage equal to the number of unrevealed letters.
  - to the Fiend: you become one of its possessed puppets.
- If you speak the **correct** name:
  - to a possessed hero: the possession ends, that hero is freed, and the hero becomes immune to future possession.
  - to the Fiend: you banish the Fiend.
### Heroes win when
- You banish the Fiend by speaking its true name.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has been possessed by an ancient entity that seeks to possess every mortal it meets, replacing their identity with its own blasphemous name. It wants to spread its consciousness to every explorer in the house, and then to the world.
- The Fiend has been imprisoned by the power of its own true name, which is encrypted in the Brimstone Square along with the names of many other spirits and dark gods.
- Six Tomes of Lore are hidden in the house. Each can reveal one letter of the true name.
- The Tomes are represented by pentagonal item tokens. The Fiend knows the Tomes are dangerous to it, so the heroes should get to them as fast as they can.
  > Note: The traitor's book has no rule for destroying Tomes: it never mentions the Furnace Room or the Kitchen, and it says the Fiend cannot pick up Tomes of Lore (possessed heroes can). How, or whether, a Tome is destroyed is not stated anywhere. The printed heroes' book said the Fiend tries to destroy the Tomes in the Furnace Room or the Kitchen; the bullet above carries the official correction.
