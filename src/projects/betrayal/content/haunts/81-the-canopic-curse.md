# 81 — The Canopic Curse

Summary: The traitor seeks the one canopic jar in the house that holds a mummified pharaoh's essence, and must carry it to the Pentagram Chamber before the heroes stop it; some heroes may secretly be extra traitors.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Before leaving the room, collect the Obstacle tokens (representing Curses) numbered 1 to 6 and take them with you. Put them face-down and shuffle them randomly.
- When the heroes are ready, call each hero out of the room one at a time to receive a Curse (see Assigning Curses and Traitorships).
- Bring the Curse tokens back with you. Put the large circular Mummy token in the Pentagram Chamber.
- Put one random Curse token face-down under the Mummy. Put the rest in the box so that nobody knows their numbers.

### Rules
#### Curses
| CURSE | EFFECT |
|---|---|
| 1 | You cannot pick up an even-numbered Jar. |
| 2 | You are also a traitor. You may pretend to have any one of the other curses. You want to take the correct jar to the Pentagram Chamber instead of the Entrance Hall. |
| 3 | Unless you end your turn in a room with an item symbol [item symbol] or omen symbol [omen symbol], take 1 die of mental damage. |
| 4 | You may not pick up a Jar from the floor that you were on when you got the Curse. |
| 5 | You are also a traitor. You may pretend to have any one of the other curses. You want to take the correct jar to the Pentagram Chamber instead of the Entrance Hall. |
| 6 | You can only pick up the highest-numbered Jar that is not being held. |

#### Assigning Curses and Traitorships
- Outside the room, give each hero one Curse token at random. The book says "Not all heroes may have the same Curse", and Curses 2 and 5 count as the same Curse for this purpose.
- Then call that hero into your room and describe their Curse. Repeat for every hero.
- A hero who gets Curse 2 or Curse 5 becomes a secret traitor. That hero reads this page and chooses another Curse to pretend to have, and must act as though under that Curse until revealing the deception.
- The secret traitor may reveal the deception on any of their turns, and must reveal it when the last Jar is picked up (if not before).
> Note: Ambiguous; owner to rule. The six tokens are all different, so the sentence only bites through Curses 2 and 5. Reading A, "no two heroes share a Curse": at most one hero can be a secret traitor. Reading B, "the heroes may not all have the same Curse": the heroes can't all be secret traitors (it matters with two heroes, who could otherwise draw 2 and 5), but two of three or more heroes may be. The book says the hero gets the Curse "outside the room" and is then brought "into your room", so the assignment happens out of earshot of the other heroes.
> Note: The book does not say what happens to a secret traitor once revealed beyond the goal stated in their Curse (bring the correct Jar to the Pentagram Chamber).

#### How to acquire a Jar
- You may take a Jar from a room at the end of your turn.
- After taking a Jar you can't move, but you can give the Jar to another explorer.
- You can't pick up more than one Jar in a turn.
- You may drop a Jar at any point during your turn. You can't pick up a Jar you dropped on that same turn.

#### How to know who has the correct Jar
- Once all six Jars have been collected, reveal the face-down Curse token under the Mummy. The explorer holding the Jar token with the matching number has the correct Jar.
- It immediately becomes that explorer's turn. If that explorer is a traitor, they must head toward the Pentagram Chamber.

#### Special attack rules
- Until the correct Jar is revealed, explorers may not attack an explorer who is holding a Jar. After it is revealed, they may attack normally.
- An explorer holding a Jar who takes damage in an attack must succeed at a Might roll of 4+ to keep the Jar. If the Jar is dropped, any explorer may pick it up normally.

### Traitor wins when
- You bring the correct Jar to the Pentagram Chamber, reuniting the Mummy with its essence.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are also trying to get the Jars, to stop you from claiming the power.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Take pentagonal item tokens numbered 1 to 6; they represent Jars.
- Put items 1 through 5 face-up in different rooms, chosen randomly throughout the house, with no more than two tokens on any floor. Choose unoccupied rooms with item symbols [item symbol] first, then unoccupied rooms with omen symbols [raven omen symbol], if possible.
- Give item 6 to the traitor.

### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- Every hero is cursed. The traitor calls the heroes into the other room one at a time and randomly assigns each a Curse listed in the Traitor's Tome.
- You must obey your Curse's rules at all times.
- You may not tell the other heroes anything about the nature of your Curse, and may not discuss why you are taking particular actions on your turn.

#### How to Acquire a Jar
- You may take a Jar from a room at the end of your turn.
- You can't move after taking a Jar, but you can still give the Jar to another explorer.
- You can't pick up more than one Jar during your turn.
- You may drop a Jar at any time during your turn. You can't pick up a Jar you dropped on the same turn.

#### How to Know Who Has the Correct Jar
- When all six Jars have been collected, the traitor reveals the Curse under the Mummy. The explorer holding the matching Jar token has the correct Jar.
- This immediately becomes that explorer's turn. If that explorer is a hero, they must head toward the Entrance Hall.

#### Special Attack Rules
- No explorer may attack an explorer holding a Jar until the correct Jar is revealed. After that, attacks are allowed normally.
- An explorer damaged during an attack must succeed at a Might roll of 4+ to keep hold of the Jar. If the Jar is dropped, any explorer may pick it up normally.

### Heroes win when
- They bring the correct Jar to the Entrance Hall.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has learned that one of the Jars in the house holds a great pharaoh's remains, which will give great power to whoever opens it in the Pentagram Chamber.
