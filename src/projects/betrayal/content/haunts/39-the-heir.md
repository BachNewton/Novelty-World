# 39 — The Heir

Summary: One hero is secretly the last heir of the Romanescu royal family; the heroes must get the heir onto the throne with the Spear and the Ring before the traitor's hidden Assassins kill them.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- If the Statuary Corridor is not yet in play, search the room stack until it is found and place it in the house, positioned to be as hard as possible for the heroes to reach. Then shuffle the room stack.
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0; it tracks time.
- The traitor has a number of Assassins hidden in the house equal to the number of players. Secretly write down the room each one is hiding in, under these restrictions:
  - Each Assassin must be hidden in a room currently in play.
  - No more than one Assassin may be in a room.
  - An Assassin cannot be put in an occupied room or in the Statuary Corridor.

### Monsters
- Assasins — Might 2
  > Note: The stat block heading is printed "Assasins"; everywhere else the text spells it "Assassin(s)".
  > Resolution (unresolved): no errata found. It reads as a plain misspelling of Assassins. Searched the official FAQ and the BGG rules forum. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf

### Rules
#### Using Your Assassins
- When a hero moves into a room holding an Assassin, the traitor may choose to reveal that Assassin. If revealed, the Assassin immediately attacks that hero, even if it is not the monster turn.
- That attack is a sneak attack: roll 2 dice and deal that much physical damage. The hero does not get to defend against it.
- After attacking once, the Assassin takes poison and is killed. Mark that Assassin off on the traitor's sheet of paper.
- Whenever a hero dies, the traitor may ask whether that hero was the heir; the player must answer truthfully.

#### You Must Do This...
- At the end of each of the traitor's turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number, starting with 1.
- At the end of Turn 3, new Assassins equal to the original number of players enter the house. Secretly write down where they hide. Each may go in any unoccupied room that does not already have an Assassin.
- At the end of Turn 6, place more Assassins exactly as on Turn 3.

> Note: The Turn 3 and Turn 6 placements don't repeat the setup's "room currently in play" and "not the Statuary Corridor" restrictions; they state only "any unoccupied room that doesn't already have an Assassin".
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome has the same wording. Searched the official FAQ and the BGG rules forum (haunt 39 threads). Source: https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf

### Traitor wins when
- The heir is dead.

### What they know about the other side
- One of the heroes is the heir, but the traitor does not know which one.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- The haunt revealer secretly picks one other hero (not themself) to be the heir.
- The haunt revealer writes that character's name on a piece of paper and shows it to the other heroes (not the traitor).
- If the heir dies, the traitor wins, so the heroes should keep the heir's identity secret.
### Heroes win when
- The heir is sitting on the throne while carrying both the Spear and the Ring.
- The throne is in the Statuary Corridor. The heroes win as soon as the heir enters the Statuary Corridor carrying both items.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has filled the house with hidden Assassins.
