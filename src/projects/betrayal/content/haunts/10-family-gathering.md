# 10 — Family Gathering

Summary: The Madman has raised his dead family as Zombies; the heroes must lure each Zombie into a room that mattered to it in life and trap it there.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The Madman has killed your explorer. Remove your explorer's figure from the house and put a yellow monster token (the Madman) in its place. Drop all your items.
- Take a number of blue monster tokens (Zombies) equal to the number of players. Put each one in a room with an omen symbol [raven symbol], no more than one Zombie per room. If there are not enough omen rooms, put each leftover Zombie in any room without an event symbol [spiral symbol].
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip on 0; it tracks the Madman's damage.

> Note: "Drop all your items" — the book does not say where they go; interpretation: they stay in the room where your explorer was, as with any dropped item.
> Resolution (official): dropped items stay in that room under an Item Pile token. The 2nd-edition rulebook says so for dropping items (p. 11) and for a dead explorer's items (p. 19). Source: https://instructions.hasbro.com/api/download/F3148_en-us_avalon-hill-betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill-second-edition-cooperative-board-game.pdf

### Monsters
- Zombies — Speed 2 · Might 6 · Sanity 2 · Knowledge 3
  - You move each Zombie freely until it has line of sight to a hero. At that point it must immediately move toward the closest hero it can see. If two seen heroes are equally close, you choose which one it moves toward.
  - Once a Zombie starts moving toward a hero, it keeps moving toward that hero for the rest of its turn. Once a Zombie cannot see any heroes, you may move it freely again.
  - At the start of a Zombie's turn, if another hero in line of sight is closer than the hero it chased last turn, it moves toward the closer hero.
  - A Zombie attacks as soon as it is in a room with a hero.
  - If a Zombie enters a room where the heroes can trap it, the heroes will tell you what happens.
- The Madman — Speed 3 · Might 5 · Sanity 5
  - The Madman "can take 5 points of physical damage before he is killed". Track this with the plastic clip on the Turn/Damage track. Damage he takes does not change his traits.
  > Note: the book does not say whether the 5th point of damage kills him or whether he dies only on damage beyond 5. The owner should rule on this.
  > Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. One BGG reply, citing the revised 1st-edition books, said the Madman dies when he takes the fifth point of physical damage. That is a single post, not consensus. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/548813

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to trap all the Zombies in special rooms around the house.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Trap Zombies
- Zombies are slow, and a Zombie always moves toward the closest hero that it can see.
- Zombies can be trapped in these rooms: Master Bedroom, Chapel, Conservatory, Game Room, Library, Attic.
- When a Zombie enters one of those rooms, or starts a turn in one, it must attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+. If the roll fails, the Zombie is trapped there: it makes no attacks this turn and stays peacefully in that room for the rest of the game without making any further rolls. Mark it by turning its token face down.
- Once a Zombie has been trapped in a room, that room can't be used to trap another Zombie.
> Note: "If it fails" refers to the Zombie's Knowledge roll of 4+ (made "to avoid being trapped"); failing it means the Zombie is trapped.
> Resolution (community): yes. BGG answers read a failed 4+ Knowledge roll as the Zombie being trapped, which also stops it attacking that turn, and no one disputes this. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1406356
### Heroes win when
- All of the Zombies are trapped.
### What they know about the other side
- The Madman and the Zombies want to kill the heroes.
- The Zombies can be trapped by luring them into the rooms that were important to them in life.
