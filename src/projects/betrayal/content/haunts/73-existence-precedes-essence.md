# 73 — Existence Precedes Essence

Summary: The traitor has befriended a small, hungry Head and plans to feed the others to it; the heroes can hug the traitor back to normal and then hug the Head, or kill them both.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Lower your Sanity to its lowest value above the skull symbol.
- Put the Fountain token and the large circular Head token in the room where the haunt was revealed.
- Set aside one matching explorer token for each hero.
- Take three pieces of paper and write these notes on them:
  - "Attack the closest hero."
  - "Join the traitor."
  - "Rejoin the heroes."

### Monsters
- Head — Speed 5 · Might 5

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Instead of attacking, you may hurl the Head if you are in its room. You may throw it into a room any number of rooms away in a straight line, including up and down stairs, the Balcony, or the Coal Chute. After throwing it, make a Might attack against a hero in that room.
- When a hero is killed, replace their figure with their matching explorer token.
- If the Head kills a hero, or comes across a hero's explorer token during its turn or while being thrown, put that explorer token on the Head. Tokens move with the Head, and heroes can steal them.
- While the Head carries any explorer tokens, it cannot attack on its turn, but it may defend.
- You cannot be killed until the Head is killed. The Head can be killed only by taking 5 physical damage in one attack; remove it from the game, and from then on you can be killed normally.

> Note: the printed book doesn't say whether, or how, the Head can die, though the heroes can win by killing both the traitor and the Head. The last bullet above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. The Head dies only when one attack deals it 5 physical damage (enough to bring its Might or Speed to 0), and the traitor can't be killed while the Head lives. The FAQ adds this as the final Special Attack Rules bullet. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
#### More New Friends
- If the Head or any explorer brings a hero's explorer token into the Fountain, that hero's body comes back to life as a reanimated monster with no memory of its past self. Reset that monster's traits to their starting values, give the monster the "Attack" note, and put the monster's figure in the closest room that contains a hero.
- If the hero the monster attacked then attacks the monster on their next turn, give the monster the "Join" note: the monster is now on your side. If "the monster doesn't attack the reanimated hero" (as printed), give the monster the "Rejoin" note: the monster goes back to being a hero on the heroes' side.
  > Note: The printed condition for "Rejoin" says the monster doesn't attack the reanimated hero, but the monster is itself the reanimated hero. It most likely means "if the attacked hero doesn't attack the monster on their next turn" (the opposite of the "Join" case); this is interpretation.
- After each step of this process, take the piece of paper back so more monsters can be reanimated.

### Traitor wins when
- Every hero has either been fed to your Head or turned into more new friends (see More New Friends).

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes disapprove of your friendship with the Head and are trying to tear the two of you apart.

> Note: The notes are written as "Attack the closest hero." / "Join the traitor." / "Rejoin the heroes." but referred to later as the "Attack", "Join" and "Rejoin" notes.
> Note: "After each step of this process, get the piece of paper back" — it is unclear exactly when the "Attack" note is returned (after the monster's attack, or after the hero's response).

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside Obstacle tokens equal to twice the number of heroes. They represent Hugs.
### Rules
#### How to hug someone
- To hug the traitor or the Head, make a Might attack.
- When hugging the traitor, add 1 extra die for each Hug on the traitor's character card.
- Any other hero within 2 spaces of movement may join in a group hug. Ignoring any text that hinders movement, move those heroes' figures into your room, and add 1 die to your roll for each extra hugger.
- A hug deals no damage, but it does allow the normal stealing of item cards from the one being hugged. For this purpose, explorer tokens count as items.
- To restore the traitor to normal, you must hug the traitor for a number of turns equal to twice the number of heroes. After each successful turn of hugging, put a Hug onto the traitor's character card.
- If the Head is by itself, you may hug the Head. If the traitor has been returned to normal, the traitor may join you in this hug.
  > Note: "By itself" is not defined; presumably it means the Head is in a room with no other monster or traitor.
#### Special movement rules
- If you hug on your turn, you may not move again that turn.
### Heroes win when
- You hug the traitor for enough turns to restore them to normal, and then together hug the Head; or
- You kill both the traitor and the Head.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is trying to feed the heroes to the traitor's new best friend, a small, polite Head.
