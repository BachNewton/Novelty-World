# 75 — Let It Glow

Summary: The traitor has become an Ice Queen freezing the house solid; the heroes must lock Thermostats to warm it back up or kill the traitor.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Set the Turn/Damage track to 1 with a plastic clip. It tracks temperature: each number on the track stands for 10 degrees Fahrenheit below zero.
- Set aside ten small orange monster tokens; they are the Snow Monsters.
- Gain 2 Might and 2 Knowledge.

### Monsters
- Snow Monsters — Speed and Might are each equal to the number on the Turn/Damage track (minimum 1).
  - There are ten Snow Monsters.

### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- At the end of each of your turns, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number; this is the house's temperature dropping. Announce the temperature after you do so.
- If the Turn/Damage track reaches 0, you take 1 die of physical damage at the start of your turn unless you are in the Balcony.
#### How to Create Snow Monsters
- Once during your turn, you may create a Snow Monster in any room with an omen symbol [raven symbol] by succeeding at a Knowledge roll of 5+. If all ten Snow Monsters are in the house, you cannot create any more.
- If you are attacked in a room containing a Snow Monster, you can make the hero attack the Snow Monster instead.
#### You Must Do This on the Monsters' Turn
- On the Snow Monsters' turn, for each face-up Snow Monster in a room with an unlocked Thermostat, you may roll a die. For each blank result, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are killed or freeze to death.
- Freezing: when the Turn/Damage track reaches 9 minus the number of heroes at the start of the haunt, the house is completely frozen and all heroes freeze to death. (Example from the book: with four heroes, the heroes die when the track reaches 5.)

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes don't like the cold and will try to stop the house from freezing over.

> Note: The track starts at 1 and only ever advances, yet a rule covers it "reaching 0". The book does not say what moves it back down; presumably the heroes' half lowers it.
> Note: "Thermostat" is presumably a token or room feature defined in the heroes' half; it is not explained on this page.
> Note: "you may roll a die" — the number of dice is not stated beyond "a die"; read as one die per Snow Monster.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside 7 pentagonal item tokens, which represent Thermostats.
- Put one Thermostat, number side up, in the Furnace Room. Then put one more in each room with an omen symbol [raven omen symbol] of your choice, until you run out of Thermostats or of omen rooms without a Thermostat.
### Rules
#### How to defrost the house
- Once on your turn, you may try to raise and lock a Thermostat in the house: attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to turn up the heat and lock it.
  > Note: The book doesn't say explicitly that you must be in the Thermostat's room, though "get to rooms with Thermostats" (in What You Know) implies it.
- When a Thermostat is locked, turn its item token number side down, then lower the Turn/Damage track to the next number.
- If any explorer discovers an omen room, put a Thermostat in that room if you still have one that hasn't been put into play.
### Heroes win when
- You kill the traitor; or
- You lock six of the Thermostats and lower the Turn/Damage track to 0.
### What they know about the other side
- A childhood memory trigger has turned the traitor into an evil Ice Queen who is trying to freeze the heroes to death. Her power comes from the cold. The house grows colder every minute and is covered in snow and ice, so time is short; the heroes must reach rooms with Thermostats to warm the house back up.
### After the haunt
- When you finish this haunt, mark your explorers' completion of it in the chart after haunt 100.
