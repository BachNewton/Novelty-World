# 41 — Invisible Traitor

Summary: The traitor has turned invisible and means to kill everyone; the heroes must track down and kill an enemy they can't see.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- On a piece of paper, the traitor secretly writes down the name of any one room currently in the house. That is where the traitor now is. The traitor's figure is then taken off the board.

### Rules
#### How to Move
- The traitor does not move a figure across the tiles. Instead, the traitor writes down the room where their movement ends, working that out with their Speed and the normal movement rules.
- Leaving a room that has a hero in it does not cost the traitor an extra space of movement (the same as monsters).
- The traitor can still discover new rooms, but placing a new room tile obviously reveals to the heroes where the traitor is.
#### Stealing Items
- Once per turn, if the traitor ends their movement in the same room as a hero, the traitor may attempt a Speed roll to steal an item from that hero.
- This is not a Speed attack, and the hero can't defend against it.

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 4+ | You take the item. |
| 1–3 | You fail, but you don't need to tell the players which hero you were trying to steal from. |
| 0 | You fail, and you must tell the players who you were trying to steal from. |

#### Special Attack Rules
- The Ring no longer lets the traitor make Sanity attacks.
- Every attack the traitor makes is a sneak attack, unless the traitor is attacking with an item. A sneak attack replaces the normal attack: the traitor rolls a number of dice equal to half the original number of heroes, rounded up, and the attacked hero takes that much physical damage. The hero can't defend against it.
- If a hero guesses (or knows) which room the traitor is in and attacks the traitor, that attack is resolved normally.
- On the same turn, the traitor may both attempt to steal an item and attack.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to find the traitor and kill them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### Fighting Blind
- If a hero survives an attack by the traitor, that hero may attempt a Knowledge roll at the end of the traitor's turn and consult this table:

| Roll | Result |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Nothing. |
| 3–4 | The hero knows if the traitor is still in the room. If the traitor left the room, that hero knows which exit he or she used. |
| 5+ | The hero detected the traitor's movement and knows which room he or she is in now. |

- Once during their turn, a hero may attempt to attack the invisible traitor. If the traitor is in the same room, the fight is resolved normally. If the traitor isn't there, the attack fails and the hero can't make another attack that turn.
- If a hero fires the Revolver or throws the Dynamite into a room where the traitor is, resolve that attack normally.
#### Skull and Spirit Board
- A hero carrying the Skull at the beginning of their turn may attempt a Sanity roll of 4+ to track down the traitor. On a success, the traitor must say which floor he or she is on.
- A hero carrying the Spirit Board at the beginning of their turn may attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to track down the traitor. On a success, the traitor must say what symbol, if any, is printed on the room he or she is in.
> Note: The book doesn't say whether the Skull and Spirit Board rolls can be used on the same turn by one hero carrying both, or whether these rolls count as an action.
> Resolution (official (1st-edition FAQ)): No ruling is specific to this haunt, but the 2nd-edition rulebook's Task Rolls rule (p. 13) allows only one roll toward a given task per turn, even when different rolls could achieve it. The official 1st-edition FAQ uses this very case as its example: a hero may make a Knowledge roll for a room or a Sanity roll for an item they hold, but not both, and not rolls for two different items. So a hero carrying both the Skull and the Spirit Board may make only one tracking roll per turn. The rolls are not a limited "action": the same FAQ says a hero may make a haunt-specific roll and also attack in the same turn. Source: https://media.wizards.com/2015/downloads/ah/FAQ_BHotH_102605-2.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2449430
### Heroes win when
- The traitor is dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has turned invisible and has decided to kill all the heroes.
