# 84 — Forget to Remember

Summary: The psychic spirit of a dead serial killer has lodged in the traitor's mind; a secret roll decides whether the traitor serves the spirit (disloyal) or quietly still sides with the heroes (loyal) while being unable to prove it.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- If your Sanity and/or Knowledge is below its starting value, raise it to its starting value.
- Secretly roll 1 die for each hero and total the results.
- Have each hero roll 1 die.
- If the heroes' total is less than your result, you are **disloyal**, and Richard Smith lives inside you.
- If the heroes' total equals or exceeds your result, your friendship with the heroes lets you resist the Psychic Spirit of Richard Smith, and you are still **loyal**. The heroes may not believe it. You still count as a traitor, and may use any of the traitor powers on page 17 of the base game rulebook, because you now carry a trace of psychic power.
> Note: The book says to roll 1 die "for each hero" and add them, then compare against the sum of the heroes' own single-die rolls; whether you reveal your total or your loyalty is not stated, but the roll is secret and the text implies the heroes don't know which you are.

### Monsters
- Psychic Spirit of Richard Smith — Sanity 6 · Knowledge 6

### Rules
#### You must do this on the monsters' turn
- Whether you are loyal or not, you must make the Psychic Spirit's mental attacks against the heroes.
- If a hero has the Crystal Ball, the Psychic Spirit attacks that hero. Otherwise it attacks the hero with the highest Knowledge.
- If the hero loses, the damage must be divided evenly between Sanity and Knowledge; any odd leftover point goes to whichever of the two the hero chooses.
- The Psychic Spirit never attacks you, loyal or not.
> Note: The book does not say whether the Psychic Spirit attacks with Sanity or Knowledge, nor whether it moves or where it is located.

#### Special attack rules
- Whether you are loyal or not, you may now make mental attacks using your Knowledge or Sanity against any explorer in your room. The targets decide how damage dealt to them is applied, so you must persuade them to take the kind of damage you want, whether you are loyal or not.
- The Psychic Spirit never takes damage from mental attacks.
- The heroes have learned how to damage what remains of the Psychic Spirit. If you are loyal, you must persuade them to direct that effort at the Crystal Ball: a hero must succeed at an attack against the room containing the hero holding the Crystal Ball to destroy it.
- If you are loyal and a hero kills you, that hero sees in your eyes that you were innocent and loses 2 Sanity. Since you were never possessed by Richard Smith, nobody wins.

> Note: the printed bullet doesn't say whether killing a loyal traitor wins the game for the heroes. The last sentence above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. It doesn't: nobody wins. The FAQ rewrites the last Special Attack Rules bullet to add this. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0

### Traitor wins when
- **If you are disloyal:** every hero is either dead or a monster serving the Psychic Spirit of Richard Smith.
- **If you are loyal:** you and the living heroes win when every living hero's Knowledge has been lowered to its lowest value or dropped to the skull symbol. Once that has happened, if the heroes destroy the Crystal Ball, you then lower your Knowledge to 1 to forget Richard Smith, which destroys his spirit.
> Note: The loyal condition reads as a sequence: all living heroes at lowest Knowledge (or skull), then the Crystal Ball destroyed, then you drop your Knowledge to 1. Secrets of Survival says a hero whose Sanity drops to the skull symbol becomes such a monster.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes don't grasp what Richard Smith was or what his power can do. You have glimpsed it and been permanently changed, and they won't learn what must be done unless you convince them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside six Knowledge Roll tokens.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Whoever has the Crystal Ball card can use it to make distance attacks with Knowledge. These attacks also activate the Crystal Ball as normal, so the attacker risks Sanity loss.
- The Crystal Ball is also valuable to the Psychic Spirit and to the traitor, so the traitor may go after it.
- You may use any method of dealing physical damage to drive Richard Smith's spirit out of a room. Make a Might attack against your room, treating the room as having Might 5 (or Speed 5 against cards such as the Dynamite).
  - This attack deals no damage. Instead, if you deal 2+ physical damage to the room, put a triangular Knowledge Roll token in it.
  - No more than two such tokens can be in a single room. If you run out of tokens, you may move one from another room.
  - Each Knowledge Roll token in a room lowers the Psychic Spirit's Sanity and Knowledge values by 1 die in that room.

#### What Happens When You Would Die
- If your Knowledge drops to the skull symbol, you become catatonic instead of dying, and you automatically roll a 0 on all physical and mental attacks.
- If your Sanity drops to the skull symbol, you become a monster that can make mental attacks on behalf of the Psychic Spirit, subtracting 2 from the Psychic Spirit's mental attack roll in such cases.
  - If you then become catatonic, you don't move, but you may attack explorers in the same room as you.
  - You are inhabited by the Psychic Spirit and must be slain to defeat the haunt.
- If your Might or Speed drops to the skull symbol, you die.

### Heroes win when
- They have slain the Psychic Spirit of Richard Smith by slaying every body it inhabits, or the traitor chooses to erase the last trace of Richard Smith from his or her memory.

### What they know about the other side
- The house belonged to a brilliant, mad killer, Richard Smith, who designed it to boost his psychic powers, hoping to keep his spirit in the physical world and preserve his memory.
- It backfired: his memory and spirit are now bound to the house. By entering the house, the explorers have in effect entered his mind, and the house wants to imprint Smith's mind onto theirs so he can live, and kill, again.
> Note: Secrets of Survival does not repeat the Traitor's Tome preconditions for the second branch (every living hero's Knowledge at its lowest value or the skull, then the Crystal Ball destroyed, before the loyal traitor lowers Knowledge to 1). The two books should be read together.
> Note: "slaying every body it inhabits" appears to include heroes whose Sanity reached the skull symbol, since they are inhabited by the Psychic Spirit and "must be slain to defeat the haunt."
