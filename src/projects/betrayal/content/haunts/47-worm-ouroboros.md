# 47 — Worm Ouroboros

Summary: The traitor has transformed into the Worm Ouroboros, a giant two-headed world-snake; the heroes must use the Skull to weaken and destroy both heads before it grows to full size.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Remove the traitor's figure from the game. The traitor is now a giant two-headed snake monster: the Worm Ouroboros.
- The traitor drops all their items in the room where the haunt was revealed. If the traitor had custody of the Girl, Dog, or Madman, the traitor devours them and they are discarded.
- Place the two large circular Ouroboros Head tokens in the room where the haunt was revealed.
- Set aside 16 small green monster tokens (Ouroboros Body).

### Monsters
- Ouroboros Head — Speed 1 · Might 6
  - On the traitor's turn, roll separately for each Ouroboros Head to find how many spaces it can move.
  - Each room an Ouroboros Head leaves gets one Ouroboros Body token, if that room had none. A room can never hold more than one Ouroboros Body token.
  - Heads can move through rooms that already have an Ouroboros Body token; no further Body token is added to those rooms.
  - Heads can't move through the Secret Stairs or Secret Passage, or into the Mystic Elevator.
  - Each Head can discover new rooms. Ignore any symbols on those room tiles. Discovering a room with a symbol does not end a Head's movement (if it can move farther).

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- The heroes can attack the Ouroboros Heads only once they have completed a special ritual.
- Each Head must be hit a number of times equal to half the number of players (rounded up); then that Head is destroyed. Track hits on the Turn/Damage track or on paper.
- Only the Ouroboros Heads can attack. Both Ouroboros Head and Ouroboros Body tokens slow heroes' movement. Heroes do not slow Ouroboros Heads.
- Ouroboros Heads can't be stunned.
- Ouroboros Heads aren't affected by Speed attacks (such as those made with the Revolver or Dynamite).
> Note: "Half the number of players" (not heroes) is as printed.
> Resolution (community): Play it as printed. "Players" counts everyone at the table, the traitor included, so in a 3-player game each Head needs 2 hits. BGG answers consistently treat "players" as everyone at the table and "heroes" as the traitor's opponents, both for this haunt and for the same wording in other haunts. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2942334, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1301975, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2058861

### Traitor wins when
- All 16 Ouroboros Body tokens have been placed inside the house.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to kill the traitor.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Kill Ouroboros
- Both Ouroboros Heads must be destroyed to kill the Worm Ouroboros. To destroy a head, follow these steps in order:
  1. Pick up the Skull in the room where the traitor dropped it.
  2. Carry the Skull into a room with an Ouroboros Head token. While carrying the Skull, a hero may attempt a Sanity roll of 5+ once during their turn to cast a weakening spell on that Head. On a success, lower that Head's Might to 5, making it vulnerable to attacks for the rest of the game. A Head can't be attacked until this spell has been cast on it.
  3. After the spell has been cast on an Ouroboros Head, it takes 1 hit each time a hero defeats it. Each Head is destroyed once it has been hit a number of times equal to half the number of players, rounded up. The traitor keeps track of the hits.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Ouroboros Head and Body tokens slow heroes' movement.
- Ouroboros Heads aren't affected by Speed attacks (such as those using the Revolver or Dynamite).
### Heroes win when
- The Worm Ouroboros is dead.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor has transformed into the Worm Ouroboros: a giant, two-headed snake with a very long, large body. If it grows fully there will be no stopping it, so it must be killed quickly.
