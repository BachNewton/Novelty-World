# 37 — Checkmate

Summary: Death has been called into the house and has challenged the heroes to a game of chess; they must beat it at its own game.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- Put a small purple monster token (Death) in the same room as a hero of the traitor's choice.
- Take 5 pentagonal item tokens to represent Holy Seals. Place one Holy Seal token in each of these rooms, either now or when the room is discovered: Vault, Crypt, Research Laboratory, Operating Laboratory, and Game Room.
- Tell the heroes there are five Holy Seals, but do not tell them which undiscovered rooms hold them.

### Monsters
- Death — Knowledge 8

### Rules
#### Death Must Do This...
- During the monster turn, Death plays chess against the explorer in its room who has the highest Knowledge.
- Both sides attempt Knowledge rolls, but Death cheats: after its first roll that turn, it rerolls any dice that came up blank. Each blank die is rerolled only once. (The book's example: Death rolls 8 dice and 2 show blanks; those 2 dice are rerolled, once.)
- Death and its opponent then compare results:
  - A tie: nothing happens.
  - Death's result is higher: it captures a piece, with the effect from this table, based on how much Death won by.

| Death Wins by | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Pawn: Each hero loses 1 Sanity. |
| 3–4 | Significant piece: Each hero loses 1 Might. |
| 5+ | Death grimly intones, "Check." Each hero loses 1 Sanity and 1 Might. |

#### Special Attack Rules
- Death cannot attack, and cannot be affected in any way except by being beaten at chess.
- The traitor cannot enter the room Death is in, and cannot affect a hero in that room in any way (for example, with the Bell, the Revolver or the Dynamite).
- The traitor cannot pick up Holy Seals, but can steal them from the heroes (see "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook).

> Note: The table only covers Death winning. What happens when the hero's result is higher is in the heroes' booklet; the traitor's side says only that if the heroes beat Death even once, the traitor loses.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found, and none seems needed: a hero's higher result is the heroes' win (checkmate), as the heroes' booklet states. Searched the official FAQ and the BGG rules forum (haunt 37 threads). Source: https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.
- Or: at the beginning of the monster turn, no hero is in the room with Death. (If no one is there, the heroes forfeit their game of chess.)

### What they know about the other side
- One of the heroes will try to beat Death at chess, using the Holy Seals to help.
- If the heroes beat Death even once, the traitor loses.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Beat Death
- Once during each of Death's turns, one hero in the same room as Death may attempt a Knowledge roll; Death rolls Knowledge too. The heroes checkmate Death when the hero's result is higher than Death's.
- If no one is in Death's room to play against it at the beginning of the monster turn, the heroes forfeit the chess game and lose.
- Some items in the house help the heroes beat Death's Knowledge result:
  - Heroes can pick up Holy Seal tokens. A hero carrying a Holy Seal may attempt a Sanity roll of 4+ to break it. A hero can break only one Holy Seal during their turn. Each broken Holy Seal makes Death roll 1 fewer die on its later Knowledge rolls, or 2 fewer dice instead if there are only three or four players.
  - The Book contains chess strategies: if the hero playing chess against Death is carrying it, they add 1 die (up to a maximum of 8 dice) to their Knowledge rolls.
#### Special Attack Rules
- Death cannot attack, and cannot be affected in any way except by being beaten at chess.
- Death does not slow hero movement.

### Heroes win when
- A hero checkmates Death by getting a higher result than Death on a Knowledge roll.

### What they know about the other side
- Death has challenged the heroes to a chess game.
- If no one is in the room to play against Death at the start of the monster turn, the heroes forfeit and lose.

> Note: The book says the chess roll is made "during each of Death's turns", so the heroes' Knowledge roll happens on the monster turn, not on a hero's own turn. Where the Holy Seal tokens are placed is not stated in the heroes' half.
> Resolution (community): The chess game is played only on Death's turns; Death cannot be engaged on a hero's turn. This is one unchallenged answer, and it matches the revised 1st-edition wording. The Holy Seal rooms are deliberately only in the Traitor's Tome: the traitor tells the heroes there are five, but not where. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1787054, https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/BetrayalHouseHill_v4.2.pdf

> Note: The two books differ on who plays Death. The Traitor's Tome says Death plays against the explorer in its room with the highest Knowledge; the heroes' booklet says one of the heroes in Death's room may attempt the roll. The owner must rule on whether the heroes choose their player or the highest-Knowledge explorer must play.
> Resolution (community): Weakly settled. The revised 1st-edition booklets keep both wordings, and nothing official reconciles them. Community answers read it the Traitor's Tome way: Death plays the explorer in its room with the highest Knowledge, so the heroes choose their player only by bringing their best one into Death's room.
