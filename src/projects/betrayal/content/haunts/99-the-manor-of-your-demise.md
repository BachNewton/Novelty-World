# 99 — The Manor of Your Demise

Summary: The explorers are sucked into a smoldering game box and must play a new game of Betrayal at House on the Hill against a timer, finding the Box omen before time runs out.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor; all players are heroes, all trying to find the Box.
- You lose when all explorers have been killed, or when the timer runs out.
- All remaining rules for this haunt are in Secrets of Survival.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Pick up every non-landing room tile and shuffle those rooms back into the room stack.
- Put each living explorer's figure in the Entrance Hall. Explorers who have already died do not rejoin the game. Keep your traits at their current values, and keep any item cards you currently have.
- Discard all other cards. If this would drop any of your traits to the skull symbol, lower that trait to the lowest value above the skull symbol instead. Shuffle every card stack's discard pile back into its stack.
- Set a timer to 30:00, or to half of the last time you set a timer for this haunt, whichever is lower.
- Start the timer and begin a new game of Betrayal at House on the Hill.
### Rules
#### Special damage rules
- Because you revealed a haunt in the previous instance of the game, your traits can now drop to the skull symbol. If this happens, you die and don't return to the game.
#### Special haunt rules
- Even though a haunt was revealed in the previous instance of the game, none has been revealed in this one. You must make a haunt roll at the end of your turn if you drew an omen card other than the Box.
- If a haunt is revealed in this new game, read this haunt again from the top.
### Heroes win when
- You draw the Box omen card in this new game of Betrayal at House on the Hill.
### Heroes lose when
- All explorers have died, or the timer reaches 0:00 and you have not found the Box.
