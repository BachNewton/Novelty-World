# 58 — Olly Olly Oxen Free

Summary: The traitor is possessed by the vengeful ghost of a girl who died playing hide and seek in the house; the heroes must find her hidden Dolls and burn them to drive her out.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer is still in the game but has turned traitor.
- Set aside pentagonal item tokens (representing Dolls) equal to the number of explorers.

### Rules
#### Special attack rules
- Heroes are always hiding when they are in a room. To find them you must make Knowledge attacks against them. Heroes you defeat this way are found and become "It": they become traitors and start seeking the other heroes.
- A hero who becomes "It" must drop a Doll if carrying one, and should read the Traitor's Tome.
- Heroes cannot deal damage to traitors.

#### Seeking
- When a hero successfully finds a Doll, give that hero an item token.
- You cannot pick up or steal a Doll.

> Note: How heroes find Dolls, and where the Dolls go, is not described in this half.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are found and become "It".

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are now playing your game, willing or not. They are trying to hide from you, and if you find them they become "It".

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside Knowledge roll tokens equal to the number of explorers.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- Once during your turn, in any room that has an [horned-skull item symbol] and no Knowledge roll token, you may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ to find where one of the girl's Dolls is hiding.
- On a success, put a Knowledge roll token in that room. No other Dolls can be found in that room.
#### Special attack rules
- You cannot deal damage to the traitor.
#### How to burn a Doll
- Once you have a Doll, take it to the Furnace Room to burn it.
- Each explorer may carry only one Doll at a time.

> Note: the heading says "You must do this on your turn", but the rule itself says you "may" attempt the roll. The book also doesn't say here how a found Doll (marked by a Knowledge roll token) is picked up; presumably the token is the Doll.

### Heroes win when
- You have found as many Dolls as there are explorers and burned them in the Furnace Room.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is possessed by a little girl who died in the house during a game of Hide and Seek and now wants revenge. The heroes don't want her to find them.
- The only way to stop her is to drive her spirit out of the traitor's body by burning the Doll "friends" she has hidden around the house.
