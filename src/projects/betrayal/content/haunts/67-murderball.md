# 67 — Murderball

Summary: A ghostly bully boy hands the traitor a slingshot and the Bloodstone, starting a deadly game of tag in which the role of traitor passes from explorer to explorer.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game, now as the traitor.
- If a hero has the Bloodstone card, the traitor takes it.
- If the Nursery is not yet in play, search the room stack for it and place it in the house, then shuffle the room stack.
- Set aside small red monster tokens (Kills) equal to twice the number of heroes.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- The traitor must attack the heroes with the Bloodstone, shot from the spectral slingshot: a Speed attack against anyone in line of sight. Using the Bloodstone does not lower the traitor's traits, so it should be used every time.
- If the traitor loses the attack, they take no damage from the failed attack. Instead the Bully spirit deals them 1 point of mental damage (for being a "Loser!").
- If the traitor kills the target, the traitor keeps the Bloodstone and remains the traitor for another turn.
- If the traitor attacked but did not kill the target, the traitor gives the Bloodstone and the Traitor's Tome to the target. The old traitor is no longer the traitor and may take the rest of their turn. The target becomes the traitor but may not attack the old traitor on their next turn ("No tag backs!").
- If the traitor does not make an attack on their turn, the Bully spirit deals them 2 points of mental damage (for "not playing right"). Then roll a die and pass the Bloodstone to the explorer that many heroes to the traitor's left (on a blank, the traitor keeps it). That explorer is now the traitor and receives the Traitor's Tome.
- If the traitor is killed by a hero, or loses the Bloodstone to a hero, that hero becomes the traitor and gets the Traitor's Tome, and the old traitor becomes a hero (see the Secrets of Survival).
- Whenever any explorer kills another explorer, the killer gets a Kill.

#### Special Item and Omen Rules
- The traitor may not pick up or use weapons while they are the traitor.
- The traitor takes no damage from using the Bloodstone, and may not drop the Bloodstone.

### Traitor wins when
- The traitor has three Kills, or
- Collectively, Kills equal to twice the number of heroes have been awarded. In that case every explorer with a Kill wins (for "playing the game right"), and the explorer with the most Kills, if there is a single one, is the "most-awesomest-winner".

> Note: "Roll a die" with a "blank" result refers to the game's 0-2 dice, where a blank is 0. The count is printed as "that many heroes to your left", but the receiver is called "the explorer"; the page does not say whether anyone (e.g. dead explorers) is skipped while counting.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are several targets who bleed nicely when hit.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes.
- Set aside triangular Sanity Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes.
### Rules
#### How to free the spirit
- Lure the spirit out of the Bloodstone by showing it more interesting, deadlier weapons: gather two weapons and drop them in the Nursery.
- For this haunt, the Dynamite counts as a weapon, as do the Axe, Blood Dagger, Boomstick, Chainsaw, Sacrificial Dagger, Revolver, and Spear.
- A hero may drop the Blood Dagger in the Nursery, but it is treated as if it were stolen.
- Once at least two weapons are in the Nursery, heroes can try to trick the Bully into leaving the Bloodstone: once during your turn, while in the Nursery, you may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ or a Sanity roll of 5+.
- On a success, put a token matching the trait you rolled (Knowledge Roll or Sanity Roll) in the Nursery.

> Note: the printed book doesn't say whether the Blood Dagger, which can't normally be dropped, may be one of the weapons dropped in the Nursery. The Blood Dagger bullet above is official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. It can, but dropping it is treated as if it were stolen (so the Blood Dagger's penalty for being stolen applies). The FAQ adds this to the end of the first bullet of How to Free the Spirit. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
#### If you die
- Drop all of your item cards and put your figure in the Nursery.
- Reset your traits to their starting values, then draw an item card. You do nothing else this turn.
- Continue taking turns as normal.
### Heroes win when
- The number of Knowledge Roll and/or Sanity Roll tokens in the Nursery equals the number of heroes; this frees the spirit from the Bloodstone.
### What they know about the other side
- A spirit, a mean little boy who is a Bully, has drawn the traitor into a game. The Bully loves violent games; his favorite involves hurling a stone at the heroes to see who bleeds best.
> Note: The "If you die" section implies a hero who dies is not eliminated but returns in the Nursery (the Bully's line says you can't quit). It does not say what happens if the death occurs on someone else's turn; "do nothing else this turn" is written as if it is your own turn.
