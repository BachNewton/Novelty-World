# 33 — An Invocation of Darkness

Summary: The traitor, driven mad by a sinister Book, is performing a ritual to summon an Elder God; the heroes must destroy the Book or stop the traitor first.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and has turned traitor.
- Set aside the large circular Demon Lord token; it represents the Elder God.
- Set aside 5 triangular Knowledge Roll tokens.
- If any of the traitor's traits are below their starting values, restore them to their starting values. Then, for each trait, roll 2 dice and increase that trait by the result.
- The traitor takes an extra turn immediately after the haunt is revealed. After it, play continues clockwise starting with the player to the traitor's left, as normal.

### Monsters
- Elder God — Speed X · Might 12 · Sanity 7
  - X (its Speed) equals the number of players when the haunt was revealed.

### Rules
#### How to Summon the Elder God
- The traitor must carry the Book to either the Chapel or the Pentagram Chamber.
- Once the Book has been brought to one of those rooms, the traitor must make Knowledge rolls of 5+. Each roll costs 1 space of movement.
- The traitor may still attempt these Knowledge rolls even after the Book has been stolen from them, but must still be in the Chapel or the Pentagram Chamber to do so.
- Each successful roll earns the traitor one Knowledge Roll token.
- When the traitor has accumulated 5 tokens, a portal opens in the traitor's room and the Elder God arrives there. It kills the traitor immediately:
  - Remove the traitor's explorer figure from the board.
  - Drop all items the traitor was carrying; they stay in that room.
- From the traitor player's next turn on, that player controls the Elder God and hunts the heroes with it.

> Note: The book does not say whether the Knowledge rolls may be attempted before the Book has first been carried to one of the two rooms. Read literally, the Book must be taken there first ("After taking the book to one of those rooms"), and only afterwards may the rolls continue without it.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. Searched the official FAQ, the BGG rules forum (haunt 33 threads) and community errata lists. Related: the traitor always starts the haunt holding the Book, because the Book omen triggers this haunt. A single unchallenged forum answer also says the traitor may make several of these rolls in one turn, 1 space of movement each, because the haunt overrides the once-per-turn rule. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2099242, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1807633

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to destroy the Book, to stop the summoning, or to banish the Elder God.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If neither the Chapel nor the Pentagram Chamber is in play, the player to the left of the haunt revealer searches the room stack for one of those two tiles and places it on a legal floor of the house, as far from the traitor as possible. Then shuffle the room stack.

### Rules
#### How to Destroy the Book
- Until the Elder God has been summoned, heroes may steal the Book from the traitor (see "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook).
- Once the summoning has happened, the Book lies in the room where the traitor performed the ritual, and it can be picked up as normal.
- To destroy the Book, carry it to the Furnace or the Chasm and spend 1 space of movement to throw it in. This destroys the Book and banishes the Elder God.

### Heroes win when
- The Book is destroyed, or
- The traitor is killed before finishing the summoning ritual.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is using the Book to summon a hideous Elder God that will consume the world, starting with the heroes.
