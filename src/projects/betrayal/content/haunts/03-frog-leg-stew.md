# 3 — Frog-Leg Stew

Summary: A Witch who has made herself invulnerable is turning people into Frogs; the heroes must find a mandrake root and use the spellbook to make her mortal, then kill her.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Set aside a small red monster token (the Cat) and 4 small orange monster tokens (Frogs).
- Place the large circular Witch token in the Entrance Hall.
- Place one pentagonal item token (a Root) in each of the Conservatory, the Larder and the Kitchen — either now, or when each of those rooms is discovered. Do not announce which undiscovered rooms will receive Root tokens.
- You cannot pick up Root tokens.

### Monsters
- The Cat — Speed 3 · Might 3 · Sanity 2
  - It enters play when the first explorer becomes a Frog (see below).
- The Witch — Speed 4 · Might 3 · Sanity 6
  - She is currently invulnerable: she cannot be attacked.
  - She cannot make normal attacks, but she can cast one spell each turn.
  - She cannot pick up items.

### Rules
#### The Witch's Spells
- The Witch may cast one of the spells below on each monster turn.
- If you take the Book from the heroes, you may also cast either of the first two spells (Skin of Frog or Breath of Dragon) on each of your own turns.
- Skin of Frog: target any hero in the Witch's room. The Witch and the hero each make a Sanity roll. If the Witch's result is higher, the hero becomes a Frog:
  - The hero drops all items.
  - Replace the hero's figure with a Frog token.
  - The hero's Might and Knowledge drop to their lowest values above the skull symbol.
  - A Frog cannot attack, draw cards or discover rooms.
  - An explorer who is not a Frog can pick up and carry a Frog as if it were an item. A carried Frog can do nothing.
  - Neither you nor the Witch can attack a Frog.
- Breath of Dragon: target any explorer in the Witch's line of sight (an unbroken straight line of doors) or in her room. It deals 2 dice of physical damage, which cannot be resisted or reduced.
- Wings of Raven: the Witch moves herself to any room in the house (choose the room and place her token there). Alternatively, if she is in your room, she can cast it on you instead (moving you).

> Note: for Wings of Raven cast on you, the book says only "she can cast this spell on you instead"; by the spell's wording that moves you to any room in the house.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. Searched the official 1st-edition FAQ and the BGG haunt 3 threads (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/840676, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1704452, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2044332, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/28774, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/327418). The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome has the same wording. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf

#### You Must Do This During Your Turn
- When the first explorer becomes a Frog, place the Cat token in the room where the haunt was revealed. On the next monster turn it moves toward the nearest Frog.
- If the Cat is in a room with a Frog and beats it with a Might attack, the Cat eats the Frog, killing that hero.
- The Cat ignores Frogs that are being carried.

### Traitor wins when
- Every hero is either dead or a Frog.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes have the Witch's spellbook (the Book card). You should take it from them, especially as they may use it to overcome the Witch's invulnerability.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules
#### How to Kill the Witch
- The heroes must use the spellbook (the Book card) to cast a "Form of Mortal" spell on the Witch, which makes her vulnerable to attacks. The spell needs a mandrake root, represented by a pentagonal item token.
- Killing the Witch takes the steps below, in order. Each hero can attempt only one of these steps per turn.
  1. Find a mandrake root. When a hero discovers a new room that has a mandrake root in it, the traitor places a Root token in that room. Some Root tokens may also be in rooms that were already discovered.
  2. In a room with a Root token, a hero can attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to dig up the mandrake. On a success, put the token on that hero's character card.
  3. A hero who has a Root token and the Book, and is in the same room as the Witch, can attempt a Knowledge roll of 6+ to cast Form of Mortal. On a success, that hero can then attack the Witch normally. Once the spell has been cast on her, any successful attack kills her. On a failure, the hero keeps the Root token and can try again next turn.
> Note: The mandrake root is called both "a pentagonal item token" and a "Root token" on this page; they appear to be the same token.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found, and no source treats them as different tokens. In the 1st edition the Root was its own orange token; the 2nd edition replaced it with a generic pentagonal item token. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060110122045/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1857789
> Note: Step 3 says "you can then attack the Witch normally" after a successful cast, which reads as allowing the attack in the same turn, though each hero may otherwise attempt only one step per turn.
> Resolution (official): yes, the attack may come in the same turn. The 2nd-edition rulebook (Make an Attack, p. 13) lets you both take a haunt-specific action and attack in the same turn. Avalon Hill's 1st-edition FAQ rules the same way. The one-step-per-turn limit applies to the haunt's steps, not to attacks. Source: https://instructions.hasbro.com/api/download/F3148_en-us_avalon-hill-betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill-second-edition-cooperative-board-game.pdf, http://web.archive.org/web/20151204092838/http://www.wizards.com:80/default.asp?x=ah/faqs/hoth
#### Frogs
- A hero who is turned into a Frog drops all items and discards any companions. That hero's Might and Knowledge drop to their lowest values above the skull symbol.
- A Frog can't attack, draw cards or discover rooms.
- An explorer who is not a Frog can pick up and carry a Frog as if it were an item. A Frog can't do anything while it is being carried.
- A hero who has the Book and is in the same room as a Frog can attempt a Knowledge roll of 4+ to turn the Frog back into a human. The restored hero's traits return to their starting values.
> Note: "Traits return to their starting values" does not say whether this means all four traits or only the Might and Knowledge that were reduced.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. One 1st-edition-era BGG reply suggested restoring the lowered traits to their starting values. No source addresses whether Speed and Sanity also reset. The revised 1st-edition Secrets of Survival has the same wording ("traits return to their starting levels"). Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/28774, http://web.archive.org/web/20060110122045/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Witch is invulnerable and can't be attacked until Form of Mortal has been cast on her.
- Explorers can attack the Cat when it appears.
### Heroes win when
- They kill the Witch.
### What they know about the other side
- The Witch has cast a spell that makes her invulnerable.
- She can also turn people into Frogs.
