# 93 — Owl's Moving Castle

Summary: The traitor has become an Owl and wants everyone to be Owls while the house walks off a cliff, its sections falling away; the heroes switch between Human and Owl form to survive and must turn the traitor back into a Human.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but has turned traitor.
- If any floor is more than two tile lengths away from every edge of the table, shift that whole floor so that at least one of its tiles is exactly two tile lengths from an edge.
- Place your figure in any room on your floor.
- Flavor instruction: stretch every "oo" sound into a very long "oooo" while playing, because you are an Owl.

### Rules
#### Acting as an Owl
- You can fly. You may move onto tiles that have been flipped face-down, and across empty (non-existent) tile spaces between sections of the house, provided you leave and enter through any side of a room that has a window or is an outside room.
- Each empty space where a room would be costs 2 spaces of movement.
- While flying you must stay on the same floor, and you must end your turn in a room inside the house.
- Your Speed is double the value shown on your character card. When you make a Speed roll you roll at most 8 dice.
- You take no damage from falling (for example from the Balcony, Collapsed Room or Mystic Elevator).
- You can't use item cards or omen cards.

#### Moving the house
- At the end of your turn, roll a number of dice equal to the number of heroes. You may move up to the rolled number of room tiles toward the edge of the table.
- The Grand Staircase/Foyer/Entrance Hall counts as three room tiles and must be moved together, all at once.
- To move a room tile: remove all tokens from it, flip it face-down, and place it next to a room tile on the table.
- If placing the tile adjacent to a room tile that sits on the table's edge would put it off the table, it falls off the cliff and no longer exists. Return that room to the box.
- You may not move a room tile that has other tiles on all four sides. A tile can be moved only if at least one of its sides is not adjacent to another tile.
- You may not move a tile if doing so would split a floor into separate parts, unless both resulting parts contain rooms with windows and/or outside rooms. The list of rooms with windows and outside rooms is in this expansion's rule sheet.
- You may move a tile that has a hero on it. That hero immediately becomes an Owl if not one already; this does not cost the hero any Sanity.
- If a tile with a hero on it falls off the table, that hero's Sanity drops to its lowest value above the skull symbol, and the hero becomes an Owl, placed in the room you tried to place the fallen tile next to.
- You can move the landings, but only if at least half of their doorways are not connected to other rooms.
- If a landing leaves the table, any card or tile that refers to that landing instead refers to a room on that floor, chosen by the explorer whose turn it is.
- A room that is the only room on its floor can't be moved.

#### Special attack
- Once per turn, while you are an Owl, you may "persuasively hoot" at the heroes in your room.
- Against each hero in your room, make your choice of a Might attack or a Sanity attack.
- If you win against a hero, that hero may split the damage between Might and Sanity however they like.
- If the damage would drop a hero's trait to the skull symbol, the hero instead becomes an Owl (if not one already), and that hero's Sanity drops to its lowest value above the skull symbol.

### Traitor wins when
- All heroes are dead, or
- every living hero has no explorer tokens on their character card or figure and is stuck as an Owl for good.
> Note: The traitor's half never explains the explorer tokens; the heroes' rules use them to track transforming between Human and Owl (see How to transform).

### What you know about the heroes
- The heroes are all Owls just like you. They can be Humans or Owls, but they don't want to be Owls, and they don't want you to be one either.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Put three of your matching explorer tokens on your character card.
### Rules
#### How to transform
- You may transform from a Human into an Owl by losing 1 Sanity and discarding one explorer token from your figure. If losing that Sanity would drop you to the skull symbol, discard one of your explorer tokens instead of losing 1 Sanity.
- You may transform from an Owl into a Human by moving one of the explorer tokens from your character card to your figure. If you have no explorer tokens on your character card, you can't transform into a Human and remain an Owl for the rest of the game.
#### How to act when you're an Owl
- You can fly: you may move onto flipped-over tiles, and across non-existent tiles between sections of the house, when you enter and exit through any side of a room with a window or an outside room. Each space where a room would be counts as 2 spaces of movement. You must stay on the same floor and must end your turn on a tile in the house. This expansion's rule sheet lists the rooms with windows and the outside rooms.
- Your Speed is double the value on your character card. When you make a Speed roll you roll at most 8 dice. You take no damage from falling, as happens in the Balcony, Collapsed Room, or Mystic Elevator.
- You can't attack, and you can't use item cards or omen cards.
- If transforming into an Owl would drop your Sanity to the skull symbol, discard one of your explorer tokens and leave your Sanity at the lowest value above the skull symbol.
- You can hoot as much as you like.
- Every "oo" sound you make becomes a very long "oooo" while you are an Owl.
#### How to act when you're a Human
- You can't enter tiles that have been flipped. You may leave a flipped tile through an edge that connects to a door or window on an unflipped tile. If you can't leave your room without crossing a flipped tile, you must transform into an Owl if you want to move.
- You can still hoot as much as you like.

> Note: the printed rule says only "You cannot walk on tiles that have been flipped", which leaves open whether a hero who landed on a flipped tile as an Owl can leave it as a Human. The text above carries the official correction.
> Resolution (official): errata. An Owl may land on flipped tiles; as a Human you can't enter one, but you may leave one through an edge that connects to a door or window on an unflipped tile. The FAQ rewrites the sentence in How to Act When You Are a Human. Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
#### Special attack rules
- While you are a Human, you can make a Knowledge attack against the traitor if the traitor is an Owl. This attack deals mental damage. When the traitor's Knowledge is reduced to the skull symbol, reset it to one step above the skull symbol; the traitor turns back into a Human.
- Before making a physical attack as a Human while the traitor is an Owl, you must first succeed at a Speed roll of 4+, or the traitor flies out of reach. If you fail that roll, you can't attack again this turn.
> Note: the printed book doesn't say whether the Knowledge attack deals damage, or what happens if the heroes kill the traitor. The sentence "This attack deals mental damage" and the kill win below are official errata.
> Resolution (official): errata. The Knowledge attack deals mental damage, just as a Might attack deals physical damage; and killing the traitor wins. The FAQ adds the damage sentence after the first sentence of Special Attack Rules and changes You Win When to "you kill the traitor or turn the traitor back into a Human". Source: https://www.dropbox.com/s/3y34f1s2tn6o4mx/Widow%27s%20Walk%20FAQ%20KB%2012.6.161.pdf?dl=0
### Heroes win when
- You kill the traitor, or turn the traitor back into a Human.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor is very happy to be an Owl and wants all of you to be Owls forever.
- The traitor's house is slowly moving toward the edge of a cliff. When parts of the house fall, they are lost forever, and the only way to survive while in such a place is to transform into an Owl.
### After the haunt
- When you finish this haunt, mark your explorers' completion of it in the chart after haunt 100.
