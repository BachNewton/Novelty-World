# ★ — Seasons of the Witch

Summary: The meta-haunt that ends Widow's Walk. It can only be played once every explorer has been through the four seasonal haunts. The witch Magdalena Gunchester traps the heroes in her house for a year, with a new ordeal each Month, and those who survive all twelve Months must defeat her in the Widow's Walk.

> Note: Credited "by Mike Selinker, inspired by a story by Nikolaus and Oliver Davidson". There is no traitor. The haunt is printed in three parts, and play follows them in this order:
> 1. **The season gate** (Secrets of Survival, a "STOP" page): it decides whether the haunt can be revealed at all. See the first part of the Heroes half.
> 2. **The heroes' setup and standing rules** (Secrets of Survival): see the rest of the Heroes half.
> 3. **The Months** (Traitor's Tome): the heroes' rules for each Month. The haunt revealer reads them one Month at a time, and no one may read ahead. See the Traitor half.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

> Note: This half has no traitor section. The Traitor's Tome holds the heroes' rules for each Month instead. Each Month box opens with italic story text, which is left out below.

### Monsters
- Lioness (large circular Cat token, Month 3) — Speed 6 · Might 5
- Cubs (small red monster tokens, Month 3) — Speed 4 · Might 3

### Rules
#### How Months work
- Each round of hero turns is one Month. It starts at the start of each of the haunt revealer's turns and lasts until the end of that round (the end of the Month).
- Read the Months below in order. Read only the current Month's description.
- If a hero named in a Month's description is in play, that hero gains the listed ability for that entire Month.

#### Month 1: January
- You may board up windows and outside rooms that aren't false features: you may put an Obstacle token (representing Boards) in any room you are in this turn that has one of those features. A full list of rooms with those features is in this expansion's rule sheet.
- **Vivian Lopez:** You gain 3 spaces of movement this turn.

#### Month 2: February
- At the start of your turn, take 1 die of physical damage for each room within 4 spaces of movement from your hero that has a window or outside room and does not contain Boards.
- **Missy Dubourde:** At the start of this Month, each hero gains 2 Might.

#### Month 3: March
- Put the large circular Cat token (representing the Lioness) in the Upper Landing, and a small red monster token (representing one of her Cubs) in each other landing.
- Then each of them moves to attack the nearest hero; you may choose when there is a tie. Then remove the tokens.
- The Lioness and Cubs stats are under Monsters above.
- **Jenny LeClerc:** Add 2 dice to all heroes' rolls to defend this turn.

#### Month 4: April
- For each room connected to the Underground Lake, roll 3 dice and put that many small green monster tokens (representing Snakes) in the room.
- One at a time, each Snake moves up to 4 spaces toward the closest hero, but must stop if it enters a room that has no Snakes in it.
- After all the Snakes have moved, any hero in a room with one or more Snakes must succeed at a Speed roll of 5+ or take 1 die of physical damage and a matching explorer token.
- Leave the Snakes in the house.
- **Father Rhinehardt:** At the end of your turn, set aside all explorer tokens.
> Note: "A matching explorer token" presumably means one of the hero's own explorer tokens, which the heroes' Setup sets aside; they count in May and June.

#### Month 5: May
- One at a time, each Snake moves up to 4 spaces toward the closest hero, but must stop if it enters a room that has no Snakes in it.
- After all the Snakes have moved, any hero in a room with one or more Snakes must succeed at a Speed roll of 5+ or take a matching explorer token.
- Then each hero with one or more explorer tokens takes 1 die of physical damage.
- Remove the Snakes from the house.
- **Brandon Jaspers:** At the start of this Month, each hero gains 2 Knowledge.

#### Month 6: June
- At the start of the turn of a hero who has one or more explorer tokens, that hero takes 1 die of physical damage, then sets aside all of their explorer tokens.
- At the end of the Month, each hero in a room with a Plant token (representing Food) must succeed at a Speed roll of 5+ to chase away the rats. Set aside the Food from any such room where a hero failed to chase the rats away.
- **Darrin "Flash" Williams:** At the start of this Month, each hero gains 2 Speed.
> Note: The Food (Plant tokens) is placed in the heroes' Setup.

#### Month 7: July
- You may spend 3 spaces of movement in the Gardens, Kitchen or Tree House to plant Food: attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+. If you succeed, put a Plant token in the room, if one is available.
- At the end of each hero's turn, that hero must attempt a Might roll. If the result is not higher than the number of spaces of movement the hero spent this turn, the hero takes the difference as physical damage.
- **Professor Longfellow:** On your turn, from anywhere, you may put a Plant token in the Wine Cellar without spending any movement, if one is available.

#### Month 8: August
- Smoke fills the Coal Chute, the Furnace Room, and rooms with event symbols [spiral event symbol]. At the start of the Month, any hero in one of those rooms, or in a room connected to one of them, takes 1 die of physical damage.
- At the end of the Month, at least one hero in the Furnace Room must succeed at a Knowledge roll of 5+ to fix the furnace. If no one does, set aside the Smoke token (representing Coal).
- **Heather Granville:** If the Coal is set aside this turn, put it back in the Furnace Room.
> Note: The Smoke token (Coal) is placed in the Furnace Room in the heroes' Setup.

#### Month 9: September
- At the end of the Month, each hero in a room with Food must succeed at a Sanity roll of 5+ to drive off the swarm of locusts. Set aside the Food tokens from any such room where a hero failed to drive off the locusts.
- **Peter Akimoto:** At the start of this Month, each hero gains 2 Sanity.

#### Month 10: October
- A quake hits the basement. At the end of the Month, for each non-landing room in the basement, roll 2 dice. If a blank comes up on either die, remove that room tile and any tokens in it from the house.
- Any hero in a destroyed room must succeed at a Speed roll of 4+ to move to the Basement Landing; otherwise, that hero dies.
- Reconnect any unconnected rooms to the house, if you can.
- **Ox Bellows:** Choose a room; do not roll the 2 quake dice for that room.

#### Month 11: November
- At the end of the Month, remove from the house one Food token for each hero, plus the Water and Coal tokens. For each token you can't remove, each hero takes 1 die of mental damage.
- **Zoe Ingstrom:** At the end of the Month, you remove 2 fewer Food tokens from the house.
> Note: The Water (Fountain token) is placed in the Underground Lake in the heroes' Setup.

#### Month 12: December
- Put a small monster token of any color (representing a Bat) in each room.
- In each room you are in this turn that has windows or an outside feature (see this expansion's rule sheet) and doesn't contain an Obstacle token, remove the Bats in that room and in any connected rooms on that floor.
- You may spend 3 spaces of movement to remove an Obstacle token in your room.
- At the end of the Month, the Bats deal 1 die of damage per Bat on a floor to each hero on that floor, divided among traits of your choice. Then remove the Bats from the house.
- **Madame Zostra:** After the Bats are placed, remove six Bats of your choice.

#### After Month 12: No more Months
- Put all your figures in the Widow's Walk.
- Starting with the haunt revealer, each hero must take a turn attacking the Witch using any trait. If you defeat her, put the matching Trait Roll token next to the Witch.
- You can't choose a trait that matches a Trait Roll token already next to the Witch until all four types have been placed.
- Keep taking turns attacking until you are all dead, or until you have placed a number of Trait Roll tokens equal to the number of heroes who started the haunt.
- The Witch's stats are in the heroes' half (Magdalena Gunchester, under Monsters).
> Note: The Speed Roll token was left off the printed Widow's Walk token sheet.
> Resolution (official): use an item token to mark a successful Speed attack against the Witch (the FAQ also offers a downloadable replacement token sheet). Source: https://media.wizards.com/2016/downloads/widows_walk_faq.pdf

### Heroes win when
- After Month 12, you place a number of Trait Roll tokens next to the Witch equal to the number of heroes who started the haunt.

### Heroes lose when
- All of you are dead.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### The season gate (the "STOP" page)

> Note: This page carries the "HAUNT ★" emblem but no haunt title; its only heading is a large "STOP" sign. It comes before the haunt itself, which starts on the next spread.

- As each explorer plays the four haunts listed below, mark the space for that explorer and that haunt in the chart.
- When you would reveal the haunt marked with the [star symbol] in the haunt chart:
  - If every explorer in your group has played all four haunts listed below, turn the page and go on to the next haunt, and prepare for a longer haunt than usual.
  - Otherwise, ignore this haunt roll: put down this book, shuffle the omen card you drew back into the omen stack, and keep playing.
- Whenever you start a new game, if all of your explorers have encountered the four haunts listed above, you may decide together to encounter the Witch, the culmination of your journey through the houses on the hill. If you decide to, then when you would trigger the haunt, skip the haunt chart and go straight to the next page.
> Note: The first rule says "all explorers in your group have played all four haunts," the last says "all of your explorers have encountered the four haunts"; both appear to mean the same per-explorer requirement tracked in the chart.

### Setup
- Set up the Turn/Damage track with a plastic clip at 0 and put it in front of the haunt revealer. It tracks the Months you are trapped in the house. January is about to begin.
- If any of your traits are below their starting values, raise them to their starting values.
- Note your hero's birthday on the Calendar (it is also on your character card). Each hero gains a special ability on surviving to see their birthday month.
- Take turns placing a room tile from the room stack until every room is in the house. To place one, choose an open doorway on an appropriate floor, then flip the tile and place it. If you ever can't place a room, you may rotate room tiles as needed to open a doorway, as long as you don't cut off one part of the house from another.
- Put the large circular Witch token (it represents Magdalena) in the Widow's Walk.
- Put a Plant token (it represents Food) in each of the Dining Room, Gardens, Kitchen, Larder, Tree House, and Wine Cellar.
- Put the Fountain token (it represents Water) in the Underground Lake.
- Put the Smoke token (it represents Coal) in the Furnace Room.
- Set aside the large circular Cat token, the small monster tokens of every color, the Obstacle tokens, the triangular Trait Roll tokens, and each hero's matching explorer tokens.
- The haunt revealer reads January's section of the Traitor's Tome and takes the first turn. Do not read ahead in the Traitor's Tome.

### Monsters
- Magdalena Gunchester — Speed 8 · Might 8 · Sanity 8 · Knowledge 8
  - Magdalena does not take turns or move from the Widow's Walk.
  - When a hero exits the Widow's Walk, Magdalena attacks that hero.
  - If she is stunned, flip her face up at the start of the next hero's turn.

### Rules
#### You must do this on the haunt revealer's turn
- At the start of the haunt revealer's turn, advance the Turn/Damage track to the next number. Then the haunt revealer reads only that Month's section of the Traitor's Tome.
- If you advance the Turn/Damage track past 12, read the final section (After Month 12).

#### Special movement rules
- When you end your turn, treat your room as though you had just discovered that room tile.
- If you use a special room feature, such as the Larder or the Vault, that requires marking it with an explorer token or similar token to show it is exhausted, you may not use that feature again after doing so, even if you used it before the haunt started.

### Heroes win when
- At least one of you survives twelve Months in the house and defeats Magdalena.

### What they know about the other side
- Magdalena is a witch and the sole surviving heir to the Gunchester fortune, a family of arms dealers. Imprisoned by her husband, General Wilhelm Gunchester, she went mad, gained witchcraft, turned the family's homes into houses of horror (the four seasonal haunts: trees in spring, storms in summer, owls in autumn, ice in winter), and killed Wilhelm. Once a pacifist, she now means to end humanity's violent ways, and has decided the explorers will stay in Gunchester House for a whole year.
