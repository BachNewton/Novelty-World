# 8 — Wail of the Banshee

Summary: The traitor has summoned a Banshee with the Spirit Board; the heroes must complete an exorcism in the house's holy and learned places before its wail kills them.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Place the large circular Banshee token in your explorer's room and choose which way it faces (use the arrow printed on the token).

### Monsters
- The Banshee — Speed 8
  - It usually moves by its own pattern. It always moves exactly as many spaces as its Speed roll.
  - Each time you are able to move the Banshee, roll 2 dice and consult the table:

| Die Roll | Banshee's Move |
|---|---|
| 0 | Put the Banshee in any room up to 8 tiles away. It does not pass through any other rooms this turn. |
| 1 | Choose which room the Banshee moves into first. For the rest of its movement this turn, it always turns left, if possible. |
| 2 | Choose which room the Banshee moves into first. For the rest of its movement this turn, it always moves straight ahead, if possible. If the only options it has are turning left or right, determine randomly which way it goes. |
| 3 | Choose which room the Banshee moves into first. For the rest of its movement this turn, it always turns right, if possible. |
| 4 | This turn, you control the Banshee's movement, but its wail can affect only one explorer once. |

  - Turning works by "hugging" a wall: e.g. if it must turn left but cannot, it goes straight; if it cannot go straight, it turns right; if it is in a dead-end room, it leaves the way it came in.
  - The Banshee cannot affect, or be affected by, other figures' movement. Like any monster, it cannot discover new rooms.
  - If the Banshee enters the Upper Landing, the Collapsed Room, the Gallery, the Foyer (when Stairs from Basement is in play), or a room with Secret Stairs, Secret Passage or Wall Switch tokens, you choose whether its next move takes it to the corresponding room elsewhere. If you move it there, you also choose which way it faces in the new room.
  - The Banshee cannot use the Mystic Elevator.
  - The Banshee cannot be attacked.

> Note: the movement table is indexed by 0-4, which matches the possible totals of 2 Betrayal dice (each die 0, 1 or 2). Interpretation: the Banshee's normal monster Speed roll (8 dice for Speed 8) sets the number of spaces and the separate 2-dice roll sets the pattern; the book says only "as many spaces as its Speed roll" and "roll 2 dice to determine how it moves".
> Resolution (official): the 2nd-edition rulebook (How Monsters Work, p. 18) says a monster moves by rolling dice equal to its Speed, and the result is the number of spaces it can move. So "its Speed roll" is the 8-dice roll, and the separate 2-dice roll picks the movement pattern from the table. Source: https://instructions.hasbro.com/api/download/F3148_en-us_avalon-hill-betrayal-at-house-on-the-hill-second-edition-cooperative-board-game.pdf
> Note: "facing" matters for left/right/straight; the book does not say how facing updates as it moves, beyond implying it faces its direction of travel.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The BGG threads on this haunt (https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/85897, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1828516, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1810982) cover repeated wails and staircases, not how facing updates. The revised 1st-edition Traitor's Tome has the same wording. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/20060614164031/http://www.wizards.com:80/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf

### Rules
#### The wail
- If the Banshee passes through or stops in a room containing an explorer, it wails: every explorer in that room must make a Sanity roll:

| Die Roll | Effect |
|---|---|
| 6+ | Take 1 die of mental damage. |
| 3–5 | Take 2 dice of mental damage. |
| 0–2 | Take 4 dice of mental damage. |

- You are immune to the wail while you have the Spirit Board. If you lose it, the wail can affect you.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes plan to silence the Banshee.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Knowledge Roll tokens equal to the number of players.
- Set aside the same number of triangular Sanity Roll tokens.
### Rules
#### How to Banish the Banshee
- The heroes must perform an exorcism before the Banshee kills them all. It takes a number of successful "exorcism rolls" equal to the number of players.
- Each exorcism roll needs a specific room or item, and is either a Sanity roll or a Knowledge roll. Each hero can attempt only one exorcism roll per turn.
  - Sanity roll of 5+: while in the Chapel, Crypt or Pentagram Chamber, or while carrying the Holy Symbol or the Spirit Board.
  - Knowledge roll of 5+: while in the Library or Research Laboratory, or while carrying the Book or the Crystal Ball.
- On each successful exorcism roll, put a Sanity Roll or Knowledge Roll token (matching the trait rolled) on the item card or room tile used for that roll.
- Once any hero has successfully used an item or room for an exorcism roll, no hero can use that item or room for the exorcism again. (For example, after a successful Sanity roll in the Chapel, no one can use the Chapel again.)
- When the heroes have placed tokens equal to the number of players, the Banshee is banished.
#### Special Attack Rules
- The Banshee can't be attacked.
- Stealing the Spirit Board from the traitor makes the traitor vulnerable to the Banshee, but does not give immunity to the hero carrying it.
### Heroes win when
- They perform the exorcism and banish the Banshee.
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor used the Spirit Board to summon a deadly Banshee.
- While carrying the Spirit Board, the traitor is immune to the Banshee's wail.
