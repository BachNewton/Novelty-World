# 28 — Ring of King Solomon

Summary: A portal from Hell has opened and Demons are pouring into the house; the heroes must use King Solomon's Ring to defeat the Demon Lord.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer stays in the game and is now the traitor.
- Choose which room holds the portal from Hell. It must be an unoccupied room with an event symbol [spiral symbol] at least 4 rooms away from the closest explorer. If no eligible room is more than 4 rooms away, choose one as far away as possible.
> Note: The book says both "at least 4 rooms away" and "If there isn't an eligible room more than 4 rooms away"; it is unclear whether exactly 4 rooms away qualifies.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The 1st-edition revised tome has the same mismatch ("at least four rooms" against "more than four rooms"). Searched: BGG haunt 28 threads (1258689, 95997, 95957, 66317, 609022, 1787662, 1565472, 1700760, 2036274, 2079251, 26643). Source: http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_TraitorRev_120105.pdf
- Put the large circular Demon Lord token in the chosen room. In the same room, put small yellow monster tokens (Demons) numbered from 1 up to the number of heroes when the haunt was revealed (maximum 4).

### Monsters
- Demon 1 — Speed 3 · Might 5 · Sanity 5
- Demon 2 — Speed 4 · Might 4 · Sanity 4
- Demon 3 — Speed 5 · Might 3 · Sanity 3
- Demon 4 — Speed 6 · Might 2 · Sanity 2
- Demon Lord — Speed 2 · Might 7 · Sanity 7

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- A Demon may steal the Ring from the explorer carrying it instead of dealing damage. (See "Special Attacks" on page 13 of the rulebook.)
- A Demon carrying the Ring cannot use it, trade it to another Demon, or drop it. An explorer who defeats that Demon in physical combat by 2 or more may steal the Ring back.
- Speed attacks (such as those using the Revolver or Dynamite) cannot be used against the Demon Lord.

#### Each Demon must do this
- On the monster turn, each Demon must always move its full speed toward the closest hero it can attack, using the best route possible, and attacks during its turn if it can.
- If several heroes are equally far away, the traitor decides which one the Demon moves toward and attacks.
> Note: This rule is headed "Each Demon"; it does not say whether it also applies to the Demon Lord.
> Resolution (community): yes. Players treat the Demon Lord as a Demon, so both the move-toward-the-closest-hero rule and the Ring-stealing rule apply to it, and Secrets of Survival speaks of "any other Demon" after naming the Demon Lord. Several independent answers agree. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2036274, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/609022, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1258689

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to kill the Demons.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Rules

#### Special Attack Rules
- A hero making a Sanity attack against the Demon Lord with the Ring adds 2 to the roll result.
- The first time a hero carrying the Ring defeats the Demon Lord, it is stunned; the second such defeat destroys it.
  - If the Demon Lord attacks the hero carrying the Ring and loses, that also counts as one of the two defeats.
- A hero who makes a successful Sanity attack with the Ring against any other Demon gains control of that Demon.
  - A hero may control more than one Demon.
  - On that hero's later turns, he or she can move controlled Demons and attack other Demons or the traitor with them.
  - If another hero takes the Ring, that hero controls any conquered Demons as well.
  - If the Ring is dropped or stolen by the traitor, all controlled Demons become uncontrolled.
- A hero without the Ring who defeats a Demon or the Demon Lord stuns it as usual.

### Heroes win when
- They destroy the Demon Lord, which means defeating it twice while carrying the Ring. Each of those attacks can use Might or Sanity.

> Note: "If you make a successful Sanity attack against any other Demon using the Ring" gives control only on a Sanity attack; the +2 bonus is stated only for Sanity attacks against the Demon Lord, though the win condition allows Might or Sanity for the two defeats.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The 1st-edition revised Secrets of Survival has the same split: control only on a Sanity attack, +2 only on Sanity attacks against the Demon Lord, and either trait for the two defeats. Players confirm that both defeats must be made while holding the Ring. Source: http://web.archive.org/web/2010/http://www.wizards.com/avalonhill/rules/BHH_SurvivalRev_120105.pdf, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2079251

### What they know about the other side
- Demons are entering through a portal from Hell and want to kill everyone.
- Different Demons have different physical and mental traits.
