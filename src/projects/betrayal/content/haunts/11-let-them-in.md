# 11 — Let Them In

Summary: The traitor and the Madman are opening the house's windows to let Specters in; the heroes must banish the Specters by exorcism or by beating them one at a time with Sanity attacks.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- You lose any bonuses the Madman card gave you. Set the Madman card aside.
- Place a small yellow monster token in your room; it represents the Madman.
- Place a small purple monster token face down in the Entrance Hall and in every room that has an outside-facing window. These are Specters waiting outside the house.
  - If a room's window is false (the tile is placed so the window faces another wall), do not put a Specter token there.
### Monsters
- Specters — Speed 4 · Sanity 6
- The Madman — Speed 7 · Might 7 · Sanity 7
### Rules
#### How To Let the Specters In
- The Specters get in only when windows, or the front door (in the Entrance Hall), are opened. Both you and the Madman can open them.
- Opening a window or the front door costs 1 space of movement.
- When the front door or a window is opened, turn the face-down Specter token in that room face up. A Specter can move and attack on the same turn it is turned face up.
- If a room with an outside-facing window is discovered later in the game, put a face-down Specter token in it.
- If a window becomes false (blocked by another room) before its Specter has been released, remove that face-down Specter.
- Face-down Specters do not affect heroes moving through their rooms, and they are not affected by the Bell or the Spirit Board.
#### The Madman Must Do This ...
- On the monster turn, the Madman moves by the shortest route possible to the closest face-down Specter token, then opens the window in that room (or the front door).
- Once every face-down Specter has been turned over, the Madman keeps exploring the house, looking for rooms with outside-facing windows.
- The Madman can discover new rooms. He still ignores all room features, and he does not stop or draw cards when he enters an unexplored room with a symbol. Rooms he discovers this way count as explored for other explorers.
- Once "all the room tiles listed above are in the house" and all available Specters have been activated, the Madman can attack.
> Note: The book refers to "the room tiles listed above", but no list of room tiles appears on this page. The likely intent is "once all rooms with outside-facing windows are in the house" (the Madman's exploring goal just above), but the book does not say so.
> Resolution (community): "The room tiles listed above" means the rooms with outside-facing windows (the Madman's exploring goal), not the whole room stack; once those rooms are all in the house and every available Specter is activated, the Madman stops exploring and may attack. Several independent BGG answers agree, and the rulebook glossary names the window rooms (Bedroom, Grand Staircase, Master Bedroom, Chapel, Dining Room). Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2947787
#### Special Attack Rules
- Specters can attack, and can be attacked, only with Sanity attacks.
- If a hero carrying the Ring makes a Sanity attack against a Specter and defeats it, that Specter is banished.
- The Madman cannot attack heroes until all the Specters are inside the house, but he can defend himself if attacked.
> Note: This condition ("all the Specters are inside the house") is worded differently from the one in The Madman Must Do This ("all the room tiles listed above are in the house and all available Specters have been activated"). The book does not say whether these are meant to be the same condition.
> Resolution (unresolved): no ruling found. The one BGG answer that touches it (thread 2947787) restates the attack condition as the two-part one (window rooms all in the house, all available Specters activated) without addressing the differing wording. Searched: BGG Rules and General forums for this game (all Haunt 11 threads), the Avalon Hill Betrayal FAQ (1st edition, last revised 2005), the Widow's Walk FAQ. Source: https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/2947787, https://boardgamegeek.com/thread/1681879
### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.
### What they know about the other side
- If the heroes succeed with an exorcism, they will banish the Specters.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- Set aside triangular Sanity Roll tokens equal in number to the number of players.
- Set aside the same number of triangular Knowledge Roll tokens.
### Rules
#### How To Perform An Exorcism
- An exorcism banishes all of the Specters. It needs a number of successful "exorcism rolls" equal to the number of players.
- Each exorcism roll needs a specific room or item, and is either a Sanity roll or a Knowledge roll:
  - Sanity roll of 5+, made while in the Chapel, Crypt or Pentagram Chamber, or while carrying the Holy Symbol or Ring.
  - Knowledge roll of 5+, made while in the Library or Research Laboratory, or while carrying the Book or Crystal Ball.
- Each hero can attempt only one exorcism roll during his or her turn.
- Each time an exorcism roll succeeds, place a Sanity Roll token or a Knowledge Roll token (matching the trait used) on the room tile or item card used for that roll.
- Once the heroes have used an item or room in a successful exorcism roll, no hero can use that item or room for an exorcism again. (Example from the book: after a successful Sanity roll in the Chapel, nobody can use the Chapel again.)
- When the heroes have placed a number of these tokens equal to the number of players, the Specters are banished.
#### Special Attack Rules
- A hero who has the Ring can make Sanity attacks against a Specter. If the hero defeats it, that Specter is banished.
- If a hero defeats a Specter when the Specter attacks the hero, the Specter is stunned.
- While the Madman is focused on letting in the Specters, he won't attack explorers. He can still defend himself if attacked.
### Heroes win when
- All the Specters are banished, either through an exorcism or by defeating each of them individually with Sanity attacks (using the Ring card).
### What they know about the other side
- The traitor and the Madman are trying to let in the Specters.
- The Specters will try to kill the heroes. Specters can't be attacked physically.
