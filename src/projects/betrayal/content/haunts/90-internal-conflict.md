# 90 — Internal Conflict

Summary: The traitor has just been promoted to Chief Intern and, badge in hand, sets out to eliminate the competing interns (the heroes) while the grumpy full-time employees wait for their coffee.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- Your explorer stays in the game but is now the traitor.
- Take one pentagonal item token, representing your Badge. While you have the Badge, you can order the other Interns around.

### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- If you discover a room with an event symbol [event symbol: spiral], put an Employee in that room before you draw your event.
> Note: The Employee token type is not specified on this page; Secrets of Survival uses Obstacle tokens and places one in each event-symbol room at setup.

#### How to deliver Coffee
- To deliver a Coffee, end your turn in the same room as an Employee. Remove that Employee from the house and put it on your character card.
- If you succeed at delivering a Coffee, raise your Speed by 1.
- If you fail to deliver a Coffee, lower your Sanity by 1.
> Note: This page doesn't define failing to deliver a Coffee. Secrets of Survival words the same rule as "if you deliver a Coffee on your turn ... If you don't, lower your Sanity by 1", so a turn without a delivery is read as a failure, for the traitor too (interpretation).

#### Commanding the competition
- At the end of your turn, you may move any number of your competing Interns elsewhere in the house. For each Intern you want to move, roll 1 die and carry out the matching action from the chart below.
- If one of these effects would make you draw a room tile, treat that room as if it had already been discovered.

| Roll | Order | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | "Get me some office supplies!" | Draw the next room from the room stack, place it in the house, and put the Intern in that room. |
| 1 | "Deliver these memos!" | Move the Intern to any room on the floor above or below his or her current position. |
| 2 | "Attendance is mandatory, but only for you!" | Move the Intern to any discovered room a number of spaces away up to his or her current Speed, ignoring room effects on the way. |

> Note: "Treat it as if it had already been discovered" is read as: the new room's discovery effects (e.g., drawing a card for its symbol) do not trigger. Setup says you can order the other Interns around "as long as you have the Badge", so this power presumably requires holding it.

### Traitor wins when
- You are the last intern alive.

### What they know about the other side
- Your promotion to Chief Intern puts your former friends under your command. They envy you and may try to take your Badge.
- The full-time Employees dislike you as much as the other Interns, more so without their Coffee; keeping the other Interns occupied might soften them.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If any floor has fewer than five rooms, place rooms from the room stack until every floor has at least five rooms.
- Put one Obstacle token (representing a full-time Employee) in each room with an event symbol [spiral event symbol].

### Rules
#### You Must Do This on Your Turn
- If you discover an event room, put an Employee in that room before you draw your event cards.

#### How to Deliver Coffee
- To deliver a Coffee, end your turn in the same room as an Employee. Remove the Employee from the house and put it on your character card.
- If you deliver a Coffee on your turn, raise your Speed by 1. If you don't, lower your Sanity by 1.

#### How to Get Promoted
- You may try to steal the Chief Intern's Badge by defeating the Chief Intern by 2 or more when making a Might attack, or by looting it from the Chief Intern's dead body.
- Whoever wears the Badge automatically gains all of the Chief Intern's powers and can now read the Traitor's Tome. If still alive, the explorer who lost the Badge is demoted to a regular Intern.
> Note: The book implies that whoever takes the Badge becomes the traitor-side Chief Intern and the former Chief Intern becomes a hero, but doesn't state the team switch outright beyond "demoted to a regular Intern" and "can now read the Traitor's Tome."

### Heroes win when
- They are the last intern alive.
> Note: Read literally this is a per-player goal ("you are the last intern alive"), so heroes appear to compete with each other too, not only with the Chief Intern.

### What they know about the other side
- The Employees love bossing the interns around, get especially grumpy without their Coffee, and accept no excuses.
- One of the heroes' former friends impressed the boss and was promoted to Chief Intern, and can now give them orders too.
