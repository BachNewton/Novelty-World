# 24 — Fly Away Home

Summary: The traitor let a swarm of bloodsucking Bats into the house and they have already killed the traitor; the heroes must drive them off with the pipe organ and kill those still clinging to explorers.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Setup
- The traitor's explorer is dead. Remove the figure from the game.
- Set aside 24 small red monster tokens (Bats). Put 3 of them in the Tower or Attic. If neither room has been discovered, the haunt starts with fewer Bats.
- Put 3 Bat tokens in the Chasm or Catacombs, under the same conditions.

### Monsters
- Bats — Speed 5 · Might 2 · Sanity 1
  - More Bats enter the house throughout the haunt.
  - During the monster turn, roll a number of dice equal to the number of players, and set aside that many Bat tokens.
  - During the monster turn, Bats enter the house. Bat tokens may be put in the Tower, Chasm, Conservatory, Entrance Hall, Gardens, Graveyard, Patio, Tower, Balcony, and any room with an outward-facing window (Grand Staircase, Master Bedroom, Bedroom, Chapel, and Dining Room).
    - If one of those window rooms is placed so that its window is false (faces another wall), a Bat token can still be put there.
    - Only one Bat token can be put in each of these rooms, unless there are more Bats than rooms; in that case the traitor chooses which rooms get an extra Bat token.
  - Bats may keep moving after they enter the house. Entering counts as moving 1 space.
  - If the traitor runs out of Bat tokens, no more Bats can enter until some other Bats die. There can never be more than 24 Bats in the house at once.
> Note: "Tower" appears twice in the list of entry rooms as printed.

### Rules
#### Special Attack Rules
- Bats do not attack normally. Instead, roll 1 die for each Bat attacking a hero. On a roll of 2, that Bat latches onto the victim: put the Bat token on that hero's character card.
- Bats that are not attached do not affect hero movement. Each Bat attached to a hero makes that hero move 1 fewer space that turn. A hero can always move at least 1 space.

#### Each hero must do this
- At the start of each of his or her turns, a hero takes 1 point of physical damage for each Bat token on his or her character card.

### Traitor wins when
- All the heroes are dead.

### What they know about the other side
- The heroes are trying to find a way to drive off the Bats.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- If the Organ Room is not in the house, search the room stack for it and put it in the house. Then shuffle the stack.

### Rules

#### How to Drive Away the Bats
- The heroes must start the pipe organ in the Organ Room to confuse and drive off the Bats, then kill the Bats that remain.
- The steps must be done in the order listed. Each hero may attempt only one step per turn.
  1. Move to the Organ Room. Once there, the hero may attempt a Might roll of 5+ to start the pipe organ.
  2. Once any hero has started the pipe organ, a hero in the Organ Room may attempt a Knowledge roll of 6+ to make the right noises.
     - Success drives away all unattached Bats and also stops any more Bats from entering the house.
     - A hero whose hobby is Music may attempt a Knowledge roll of 5+ instead.
  3. Finally, attack and kill any Bats attached to heroes.

#### You Must Do This
- At the beginning of each hero's turn, that hero takes 1 point of physical damage for each Bat attached to him or her.
- A hero wearing the Armor takes 1 less damage from that total.

#### Special Attack Rules
- A hero may attack, using Might, a Bat attached to himself or herself or to another hero. A Bat defeated this way is killed instead of stunned.
- Unattached Bats do not affect hero movement.
- A hero moves 1 fewer space on his or her turn for each Bat attached to that hero, but can always move at least 1 space.

### Heroes win when
- They drive away the Bats by playing the pipe organ in the Organ Room, then kill any Bats attached to explorers.

### What they know about the other side
- The traitor let bloodsucking Bats into the house.
- The Bats have already killed the traitor and now want to kill the heroes.
