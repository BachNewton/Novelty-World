# 92 — Ghost at the Finish Line

Summary: There is no traitor; a ghostly former sprinter called the Runner challenges every explorer to a race through the house, rewarding gifts that match his secret tastes, and whoever places the last room tile wins.

## Traitor (Traitor's Tome)

### Rules
- This haunt has no traitor. All players are heroes, each trying to be the one to find the last room in the house.
- All remaining rules for this haunt are in Secrets of Survival.

## Heroes (Secrets of Survival)

### Setup
- None of the players is a traitor, but the heroes are all competing against each other to win this haunt.
- Note the total number of item cards and omen cards each hero has in front of them. Then shuffle every card from explorers' hands and/or discard piles back into their respective stacks.
- Put the large circular Ghost token (it represents the Runner) in the Gymnasium. Set aside seven other random large circular monster tokens.
> Note: The Ghost token was left off the printed Widow's Walk token sheet.
> Resolution (official): you may use the Banshee token in place of the Ghost token (the FAQ also offers a downloadable replacement token sheet). Source: https://media.wizards.com/2016/downloads/widows_walk_faq.pdf
- Each player takes three pieces of paper and secretly writes one attribute of item and omen cards on each. An attribute can be anything that is not too specific, e.g. "Is a weapon," "Starts with C," or "Cannot be dropped." Pick something general that fits more than a few cards. Place the notes face down without discussing them.
- Randomly choose six notes and read them aloud; these are the Runner's criteria. If any of them match, discard the duplicate and draw another note. (If you run out of notes, write another one.)
- Deal each hero the same number of item cards and omen cards they had before.
### Monsters
- Runner (large circular Ghost token) — no stats printed
  - The Runner cannot be damaged.
  - When he makes a Sanity attack, his Sanity equals the number of large circular monster tokens in his stack, counting himself.
### Rules
#### You must do this on your turn
- At the start of your turn you must give the Runner a gift from any room in the house, if you are able to: discard one item card or omen card (even a card that can't be dropped).
- Then check how many of the Runner's criteria the discarded card matches. The number of matches grants you a ghostly power from the Criteria Matches table.
- Example from the book: discarding the Girl could match "Has just one vowel" and "Rhymes with an animal" (that is, "squirrel"), for 2 matches.
- If you can't give the Runner a gift, treat it as giving a gift with zero matches.
#### Criteria Matches

| Matches | Power |
|---|---|
| 0 | You are attacked by the Runner. |
| 1 | You have 1 more space of movement this turn. |
| 2 | You have 2 more spaces of movement this turn. |
| 3 | Discovering a new room with any symbol [spiral symbol] [horned skull symbol] [raven symbol] [question mark symbol] does not end your movement this turn. |
| 4+ | Once during this turn, you may teleport to any room. You may also choose one of the other powers on this table. |

#### Special attack rules
- Explorers can't damage other explorers, but they can steal item cards from each other.
- The Runner can't be damaged.
- If you can't give the Runner a gift at the start of your turn, he makes a Sanity attack against you, with Sanity equal to the number of large circular monster tokens in his stack (including himself). After that, put one large circular monster token under the Runner, unless his stack already holds eight (including him).
> Note: The 0-matches table row ("You are attacked by the Runner") and the "can't give a gift" rule (which counts as zero matches) both lead to the Runner's Sanity attack. The book only states the token-adding step under the "can't give a gift" wording; whether a gift that is given but matches nothing also adds a token to his stack is not stated explicitly, though the "treat it as zero matches" equivalence implies the same attack.
### Heroes win when
- The final room tile is discovered. If it ever becomes impossible to place any more rooms in the house, the explorer who placed the last tile wins.
### What they know about the other side
- There is no traitor. The Runner is a ghost who wants gifts resembling the things he loved in life, and he grants ghostly powers according to how closely each gift matches what he wants.
